# Foundation #18 — original bilateral public-office context, not invented treaties

**Source authority:** Beastbound Unified Master Prompt §34 (multiple sovereign governments, changing political pressures, histories and foreign policy), the earlier already verified original diplomatic transition history (`world/simulation.js`) and actual independent constitutional office and mandate lineage (`world/government-offices.js`). This bridge reuses those exact engines and records. It cannot create diplomacy, people, or laws by defining a different status or faction system.

## What this connects

The original world independently adjusts the relationship between two existing Atlas polities during each actual monthly world tick. Its existing one independent tension draw can produce a genuinely recorded war, truce, strain, peace or practical cooperation. Each changed status is preserved with source-sealed original numeric context and real actual world RNG. A truce does **not** prove that named sovereigns negotiated or legally signed a document.

Separately, each Atlas polity already has a real governing office, a real present incumbent and a source-verifiable chronological history of its actual governmental mandate renewals and changes. There was previously no immutable link between *which two governments actually existed at the date of a genuine geopolitical change* and the original archived diplomatic transition.

## Original date-matched two-sided government evidence

For newly recorded independent relationship status changes, the existing original `diplomaticAudit.transitions[].source` now optionally contains `officesAtChange`: **exactly the two actual polities already in that pair**, each with its original Atlas constitutional office, governing body, form of government, actual incumbent and mandate number at the event. It also source-identifies the actual original initial mandate-audit anchor hash and latest real mandate renewal/change source hash at the same date. This information is taken from **existing original `Gov.historicMandates`**, not newly invented envoys.

The original transition SHA covers that civic context as well as its existing actual economic/war/one-world-RNG evidence. `Realm.verifyDiplomaticOfficeContexts(w,key)` verifies the *full original diplomatic audit* and both independent government registers, reconstructs the exact historical officeholders by their recorded mandate number/day, and cross-checks original office, governing institution, anchor and mandate hashes. An imagined minister, false historical sovereign, forged or mismatched polity, changed mandate or invented source is rejected even if a fraudulent outside actor recalculates only the outer diplomatic SHA. **Subsequent real changes of government do not rewrite historical officeholders.**

The historical internal `Realm.diplomaticTransitionHistory(w,key)` view includes `originalOfficesAtChange` only after that combined source is verified. Earlier original diplomatic records that predate this extension have `null`, signifying **not recorded**, not an arbitrary reconstruction with the *current* modern incumbent. Old campaign saves with their actual original diplomatic audit remain valid. No new public-status API or remote omniscient player knowledge is introduced. Players continue to learn international changes only through the existing original delayed foreign courier/publication process.

**Important boundary:** this is evidence of who held each civic office *when the independent relationship state changed*. It is emphatically **not** evidence that either individual consented to war, authorised negotiations, recognised a border, ratified an accord, signed a treaty, bound a population, spent treasury assets, held an embassy, granted succession, or decided that status transition. Those require separate actual events, representative authority and independent bilateral assent. No extra civic, envoy, player, beast or world dice roll was added; the exact existing relationship change and six-day world clocks remain untouched.
