# Adaptive independent NPC ambitions — 48 existing people react to changing conditions

This is the next concrete implementation of the [Living World Change Charter](LIVING_WORLD_CHANGE_CHARTER.md). The original Master Prompt and Table Rules require actual independent people, believable projects, persistent history, and growth and failure, not a fixed plot that exists for the protagonist. **The specific vocational action categories and their numbers below are newly authored limited regional mechanics**, not invented quotes from canon or proof that a named person has visited a site. Established campaigns and Jory's separate facts remain unchanged.

## Not another list of static researchers

The earlier `world/agent-initiatives.js` had three specific named professionals undertaking three specific practical projects; `world/knowledge-diffusion.js` could spread already published methods along three real trade routes. Those mechanisms remain unchanged. **`world/adaptive-ambitions.js` now tracks all 48 existing named professional NPCs**, in all 24 countries, with their existing career, home state, employing institution and original personal `life.goal` preserved. Every actor may independently choose a *contextual practical goal* when it is genuinely relevant to their profession and surrounding conditions. No player must purchase sponsorship, accept a guild quest, awaken a magical technique, or belong to a noble family before such people can act.

A limited first set of grounded goals:

- **Habitat care:** a field specialist can direct limited monitoring and erosion-prevention work toward an actually stressed established region. The real ecosystem's pressure and quality determine priority. A successful funded practical trial reduces pressure by up to four points and increases quality by up to two, **not** resurrecting species or claiming a comprehensive restoration.
- **Route safety:** a field specialist may prioritise an actual risky or disrupted trade route attached to their country. If work succeeds, that route's risk falls by up to four points. This **does not** end a real war, override a blockade, secretly teleport the worker, guarantee reopening or grant the player a safe journey.
- **Civic records:** a civic professional may choose a practical service-record audit in a country with genuinely weakened legitimacy. Successful work modestly improves the real employing institution's public reputation; only real public government bodies can additionally yield up to one point of legitimacy. An academy's routine audit cannot pretend to be a democratic mandate, a royal succession or a legal ruling.
- **Practical teaching access:** a sufficiently experienced professional can independently improve a local organisation's practical workshops when its actual training capacity leaves room for improvement. A genuine successful project grants a small capacity/reputation improvement, **not** automatic player membership, a free teacher, a secret family trait, or a cultivation level.

These priorities are **calculated from changing world facts**. A person can choose a project, spend money and time on it, find the problem solved by other events, switch to more urgent work, become delayed by an actual injury or closed institution, or stop after prolonged setbacks. A deliberate choice uses a separate persistent world randomness stream, so a need does not become an obligatory destiny and the protagonist cannot pick NPC success. This is a first model for authentic practical goals; it is **not** generalized AI personality, autonomous romance, succession, private psychological mindreading, world-spanning political treaties or universally novel supernatural disciplines.

## Work, consequences, revisions and failure

Every fourth **genuine world month** an actor has a chance to act on an existing or contextually relevant goal, staggered by identity so all 48 do not act in the same month. The timing is a limited simulation cadence, not a restriction on what a player can attempt in the world. A selected project takes **three actual funded work sessions** at the actor's existing active institution, with **2–3 real budget units spent per session**. An independent d20 practical trial then uses the NPC's actually earned professional rank and the organisation's real capacity and national stability against DC 14, with critical successes/failures counted honestly. The genuine target values and budget before/after, real rolls, method, actor, date, original trigger and outcome are stored in a SHA-256 historical receipt.

A verified limited success changes only an established ecological, transport or institutional quantity that corresponds to the actual project. Failure spends time and resources **without applying a successful outcome**. Two blocked sessions cause lost preparation; four blocked sessions can cause a project to be abandoned. If the original need resolves or another genuinely urgent need arises, a person's plan can **change without retroactively refunding costs or erasing the previous work**. Existing personal goals are retained; current ambitions are sealed on the NPC record, then cleared when the goal is genuinely completed or abandoned. The world may therefore develop in both helpful and unhelpful directions even while the player explores elsewhere.

## Character knowledge and traceable accomplishments

`world.ambitionsStatus({})` is **strictly read-only**. It shows only real *local* published accomplishments or publicly reported failed vocational attempts, each supported by the original world record hash and chronicle notice **after its eight-day reporting delay**. It never reveals private next goals, plans that were revised before publication, NPC secret intentions, economic metrics, unpublished trial rolls or faraway research simply because it happened. Naming a successful expert in an actual published journal helps the world remember who performed noteworthy work rather than merely attributing developments to generic guilds.

This feature does not write or backfill imaginary past events in existing saves. Its records are seeded lazily when real world-month processing occurs; a read-only status request does not advance the world or persist anything. The world clock continues determining meaningful changes even when the player never speaks with a given professional. A private Project chat must keep original witness/roll provenance and show no mechanical gain until the engine confirms it.

## Important limitations

This is **a first adaptive ambition loop for practical professional intervention**, not a complete human society. The new goal-selection mechanism does not already create individual families, birth/death, emotional bonds, named personal rivals, political candidacy, court succession, new cultivation techniques, private spell invention or player coauthoring. Existing `agent-initiatives` and `knowledge-diffusion` continue to supply **separate verified innovation** and international transfer rather than granting arbitrary magic on civic success. Future additions should respond to credible causes, original world rules and source-attested individual choices, with free player paths and genuine independent failures.

`tests/adaptive-ambitions.test.js` checks all 48 actual NPC records, deterministic long-run evolution, authentic budgets and trial checks, contextual environmental help and road-risk improvement, civic authority limits, failures, resource delays, revisions without refunded investment, no invented magical progression or government takeover, local-public-notice privacy and file-backed read-only idempotency. Full GitHub Actions CI remains mandatory. No active campaign save is modified.
