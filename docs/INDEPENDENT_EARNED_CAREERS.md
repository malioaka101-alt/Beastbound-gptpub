# Genuinely earned independent careers — foundation-first people (7 November 2026)

**User's standing rule:** Progress is determined by **what an individual actually does, understands, achieves, and can physically or spiritually support**, shaped by aptitude and potential. Time is a record of when actions happened, **never a required age, years-served, minimum practice count or sixday waiting gate**. “Lifelong mastery” is how demanding great competence usually proves to be, not a timer to fill. The original Unified Master Prompt, Table Rules and established campaign facts retain priority.

## Why this is a world foundation, not a prewritten NPC roster

The engine can already create new individually persistent people in any simulated region in response to real ecology, trade and community needs. They previously had personal finance, fatigue, travel and choice, but **no source-verified professional career growth from their actions**. A named occupation did not provide actual earned competence. The new generic module `world/person-careers.js` gives those **existing emergent individuals** an independent, persistent **professional practice record** rather than assigning a global list of talented masters.

A generated citizen's career contains a personally determined latent **professional aptitude** (independently sourced from their own creation), original professional situation, real resolved work, distinct proven methods, practical competence from 0 Untrained through 5 Grandmaster, and source-bound work and earned qualification receipts. It is **not** a lawfully issued licence, Guild credential, magical discipline, bloodline, acquired secret technique or inherited status. With actual source-backed biological parents, inheritance may legitimately matter; without them no individual can be assigned an invented magical ancestry by a profession string or byname.

## Actual work changes the person and their community

When a person **independently chooses real livelihood work** during world simulation—or freely agrees to a locally witnessed work opportunity—the engine selects a plausible task from the person's work and the **current local ecological and economic conditions**, using their **own RNG, never the player's**. General kinds of supported tasks include ecological inspection and habitat repair, practical water care, goods provisioning and storage, local trade work, infrastructure repair, supply coordination and mediation. These are flexible work mechanisms, not a final list of every profession or a declaration that every creation is already manufactured.

An authentic personal roll measures the specific work's **difficulty** against the person's independently generated aptitude, demonstrated experience, current fatigue and environment. A meaningful success can give professional practice credit; particularly challenging, strongly successful or exceptional work can award more, and a talented individual can gain considerably faster. Failures earn **no automatic mastery** and carry believable costs, including fatigue or financial strain. Some successful ecological and civic work also **slightly improves actual saved local pressure, habitat quality or civic stability**; ordinary trade work can produce a modest legitimate livelihood. Work and decisions survive checkpoint reload, with each receipt tied to the real original person.

Professional advancement is tied to **cumulative independently earned work**, harder successful challenges and distinct methods, not an NPC's biological age, passed sixdays, job title, fixed labour quota or mandatory teacher. Later qualifications need genuine *varied demonstrated ability* and stronger outcomes, but the module imposes **no compulsory number of days, years or counted sessions**. Within any day an individual can potentially perform another credible piece of work when actual physical capacity, duties and opportunity allow. This is subject to meaningful world consequences, not a right to infinitely repeat free success rolls.

There is **no fabricated human power** from this module. A competent independent artisan does not automatically have an Arcane Circle, Meridian Gate, healing spell, soul mastery, beast contract or family technique. These are separate systems with separate source-verified breakthrough, training and consent requirements. Existing professional Guild licences remain culturally and legally independent from personal skills.

## Actually witnessing an independent person

When a generated person is *truly locally present* at a working site, `social.observeLocalPersonWork({npcId})` allows the player's character to spend genuine scene time **observing** a real work opportunity. The individual decides freely whether to participate. If they decline, the player sees the refusal and the shorter real-world interaction. If they work, the person's **own** mechanics determine the job and outcome. A player cannot submit the NPC's achievement, success, talent, rank, training, location, hidden recipe or contract consent. The scene can reveal only the observed type of work, whether its practical goal succeeded, and any directly witnessed local consequences.

`social.witnessedPersonWorkStatus({npcId?})` shows **only the player's actual previously witnessed work scenes**, not private offscreen occupations, invisible aptitude scores, undisclosed formal ranks, lineage or relationships. Read-only status must neither mutate a save nor roll private dice. Observing skilled work does not automatically teach the technique, raise the player's rank or grant ownership of the worker's goods.

This first work system is part of the **open-ended living-world machinery**, not a complete census or a full universal NPC invention and education simulator. Further work should ground nonhuman sapient careers, medical treatment, scholarship, apprentice relationships, family-origin advantages, organisations that train knowledge, and original devices in **their actual projects, materials, inherited abilities where appropriate, knowledge and personal autonomy**. No artificial universal profession or population ceiling.

## Honest current coverage

Supported now: new people pursue ordinary bounded professional work independently; actual job checks and attempts persist; success and aptitude influence personal rank; outcome can affect source-grounded local ecology/civic state; genuine local observation, free refusal and personal memory; verified save/replay and strict non-magical status boundaries. **Not yet implemented**: general research project invention by NPCs, all specialised school progression, full individual human births and inherited aptitude transmission, independent household families, full political careers, sapient beast professions, universal player-to-NPC teaching and any automatic high-tier discipline from job income.

Mandatory regression: `tests/emergent-careers.test.js`. No active player save is edited during development.
