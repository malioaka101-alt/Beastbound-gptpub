# Master Prompt #14 — Beast-Origin lineage integration foundation (draft)

**Authority:** original Unified Master Prompt §§18 and 24; prior authenticated Origin research, biology, ecology, social rights, health, energetic and human evolution records. **Next sequential foundation after #13**, whose code and 151-group regression were merged as PR #45.

## Reuse instead of a second power ladder

The original Origin Pattern is deeper than DNA; it can include living anatomy, energetic patterns, soul resonance and developmental instructions. Human and beast organisms remain distinct and require genuinely compatible physiology. The pre-existing `rules/beast-origin-foundations.js` deliberately supports **non-invasive ecological hypotheses only**: identified habitat, diet and ecological role are not actual demonstrated lineage materials or permissions to seed a trait. Preserve that boundary.

This draft adds `rules/beast-origin-lineage.js` as a **source-specific extension** to the existing `origin` rule API, not a replacement for the existing physical Origin load, human evolution, meridian, soul, body, ecology or social ledgers. Each potential trait originates from an **independent GM-authored specific Origin Pattern source** in `s.table.originTraitSources`; the player rule API cannot manufacture those attestations or choose its own lineage effect, rank, biological load, consent or roll.

The source identifies a unique trait and route, original bodily mechanism, energetic and spiritual requirements, metabolic restrictions, physical conflict limits, load assessment and **documented ownership/rights status**. Named route examples mirror §24 — ancestral, lineage transfer, imprint, origin cultivation, relic and synthetic — without prescribing universal species, abilities, schools or teacher/tuition gates. A third party's material or sapient beast's body cannot be treated as available simply because the researcher saw the organism. Missing acquisition rights remain **unresolved**, not implied consent.

## First executable vertical slice: exact anatomical evidence and four screens

`origin.lineageMap` studies the **same individually recorded trait** across fresh witnessed anatomical observations. Each attempt consumes original Stamina, advances the world clock, resolves the real d20, keeps a source-hashed roll receipt and accumulates actual progress. Earlier non-invasive ecological maps may inform what the GM investigates but never suffice for this source. Reused observation or a changed source does not supply a new roll.

When mapping is genuinely completed, `origin.lineageScreen` separately assesses the original human **physical**, **energetic**, **spiritual** and **trait-conflict** demands. These are *individual observations*, not a universal permission check or a requirement that everyone possess a Mana Core. Real existing capacity, possible load conflicts and currently recorded soul/human stability shape risk cautions. Independent genuine checks may support promising, caution or inconclusive results. They can identify a useful biological opportunity without consuming a donor, requiring a teacher, seeding tissue, granting powers or incrementing a human stage. `origin.lineageStatus` displays only mapped, already source-attested information and results; saved receipts are checked for source and roll integrity.

## Stages III–VIII: original human biology, not an automatic beast ability

The follow-on `rules/beast-origin-integration.js` extends **the same specific individual's** `s.table.originLineageStudies[sourceId]` with an `integration` ledger. Unlike the old prototype's generic eight-step UI, the `origin.lineageIntegrate` action derives the next actual stage from the sealed previous record; it does not allow the caller to pick a step, a convenient die result, a power name, an energy reward or an unearned human evolutionary stage.

Before **stage 3 Trait Seeding**, the GM must have independently witnessed and attested `s.table.originLineageAuthorisations[sourceId]` with the exact source seal, independently checked original material identity and legal/consensual rights. Rights may be honestly endogenous to the actual human, documented informed donation, a licensed held relic or physically authenticated self-synthesis; witnessing an animal or purchasing arbitrary stock does not certify its biological identity. Donor use requires **explicit observed informed consent**; a GM-authored attestation is not a player-supplied permission check. Sources can be genuinely self-derived without compulsory mentors, money or animal samples. For material integration, the actual already-owned `origin_sample` is removed through the **shared real inventory executor**; failed seeding also consumes the material and repeated attempts require a new physical source. Inherited or demonstrated own-body processes instead record the witnessed endogenous origin and do **not** manufacture item stock.

The independent human Origin load is reserved **once**, on genuine successful seeding, against the original `s.player.origin.load/capacity`. The single original candidate is then observed through **4 Rejection Window, 5 Stabilisation, 6 Expression, 7 Adaptation and 8 Personal Evolution**. Every stage requires an independent measured human-specific bodily response, actual metabolic cost, a physical failure countertest, time, real Stamina, a bounded original dice check and save-verifiable source evidence. Rejection check failures cause real **wound-system injury**, recorded pressure strain and persistent rejection risk; severe other failures may also cause injury. `origin.lineageCare` documents independent real clinical treatment, costs time/Stamina and lowers recorded pressure only when the actual clinical check succeeds; **it never removes an existing physical wound for free**. Normal medical action must treat actual wounds. The integration cannot silently bypass an unresolved strain or pretend a previously failed result never happened.

A successful **Expression** supports only demonstrated bodily functions, not a copied species ability. The first executable example links one source-observed individual *human bodily-footing* expression to an **already prepared local footing check** using `origin.lineageFooting`, with independent prior witness, one original d20, +1 narrow physical support, one real Stamina and no repeated attempt. It explicitly **cannot stack** with the previously tested Body Refinement tissue aid on the same check. This is an adapter example, not a list of all possible Origin organs: later entirely different sources can be described and researched, while numerical effects require real matching physical mechanisms and verified action adapters rather than fabricated bonuses.

After independent Adaptation and a genuine **Personal Evolution** trial, the original record keeps its actual reserved Origin load and one source-specific **human** stable trait identity. The record does not automatically set `s.player.humanEvolution.stage`, award the beast's original ability/technique, install nonhuman organs, imply an owned animal, or advance unrelated Meridian/Arcane/Soul levels. A completely bespoke ability without an existing executable adapter remains a documented individually witnessed biological expression, **not** an automatically resolved combat/terrain effect.

## Validation and acceptance limits

`tests/beast-origin-lineage-source-mapping.test.js` verifies steps I–II, separated original source and four axes, no seeding from ecology and preserved original human evolution. `tests/beast-origin-integration.test.js` exercises source-specific eight-stage completion using observed endogenous ancestral biology, independent real checks, original Origin load conservation, personal human expression on an existing terrain check, no double-roll or source stacking, retained save/tamper checks, and real material conservation under independent consented donation. It also confirms that missing ownership/consent cannot turn a research hypothesis into an Origin trait.

The disposable test sources are **examples**, not universal official lineages, species, price requirements or power templates. Not all imaginable beast-derived traits, organs, social rights or human evolution branches have finished specific action adapters. GM-specific existing physical systems and genuinely established world law remain authoritative. Foundation #14 remains unchecked until exact-head full regression and final acceptance; no real campaign save was altered.
