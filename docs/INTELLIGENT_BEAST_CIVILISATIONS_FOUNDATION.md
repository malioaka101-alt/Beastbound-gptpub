# Intelligent beast civic life — foundation #07

**Canon:** Unified Master Prompt §§12, 15, 34 and 43. Intelligent beasts are independent persons with varied biological communication, kinship, law, customs and resource interests. Neither human-style government nor ownership is universal.

**One existing world:** Author new civic facts on `s.table.realm.countries[realBeastPolityId].beastCivic`, not in a second civilisation simulator. The two current canonical beast polity references are Reef Cantons and Old Tracks Beast Commonwealth; these are *examples*, not an exclusive universe or finite institution roster. Already established atlas rights, real country welfare/legitimacy, physical ecosystems, source-witnessed `sapientLife`, live `emergentGroups`, `emergentHouseholds`, existing diplomacy and GM-created lived places remain authoritative.

### Independent GM-authored culture

`world.beastCivicAuthorInstitution` accepts an original named institution and kind, mandate, communication language, sensory medium, decision rule, source and visibility. It records a charter inside that actual independent beast country's saved records. A school, court, seasonal assembly, sanctuary council or another original kind is not restricted to a few menu options; **no resident, member, official, organisation salary, power, bond, legal decree or language comprehension is created by authoring**.

The local read-only `world.beastCivicStatus` reveals known public charters, personally witnessed public proceedings and established public household headcounts only in the actually visited polity or a previously GM-established place there. It cannot expose distant hidden councils, private meetings, secret officials, personal aptitudes or a guaranteed nonhuman language understanding.

### Recorded actual persons, households, offices and treaties

`world.beastCivicProceeding` requires an earlier, specific *in-world story event* with the exact source identity `personally-witnessed-beast-civic-proceeding-v1`, a genuine local place, distinct sourced participants with independent `sapientLife`, a concrete subject, basis, outcome and privacy. Freeform issues include policy hearings, rituals, court cases, education charters, diplomacy and local decisions. Describing a proceeding doesn't perform an unverified election, settle a real combat, create a citizen or grant powers.

A claimed public office further requires the actual named individual to be independently witnessed **accepting that precise office**. The resulting public record is evidence of a role, not proof of unconditional obedience, title inheritance, combat aptitude, compensation or life-long service. Bilateral treaty terms require an already established pair of real polities and evidence of **both** jurisdictions accepting the same text; the resulting factual source is attached to the existing country diplomacy connection. It does not silently change war, border permissions, market rates or party consent.

`world.beastCivicHousehold` requires a separate original witnessed household scene: two or more genuinely existing, independent sapient beast persons, each personally accepting a specific cohabitation, with a nonduplicate living household ID. Residents are linked to their *existing individual* source hashes and the original event, not spawned. This creates neither a marriage, biological bloodline, property ownership, legal kinship, romantic promise nor bound creature. No active member may be double-assigned to another live household without a separately supported departure.

### World continuity and safety

The existing `world/simulation.js` monthly country tick checks already authored civic charters for real ecological or legitimacy stress and records an agenda-level review flag when material conditions change. This is independent institutional *attention*, **not** invented votes or persons making decisions offscreen. Real civic outcomes still require separate sourced decisions. Source-hashed charter, proceeding and household histories survive normal export/import, while duplicate source events cannot be replayed as multiple decisions.

Existing country relationships, living citizen agency, group membership rules and public law remain unmodified: later foundations can extend civic resource accounting, member recruitment, courts, titles, treaty enforcement and institutions when actual observed consequences warrant it. There is no arbitrary language gate on the GM's power to create cultural facts, and no automatic power/wealth from belonging.

The whole regression suite must succeed on the exact #07 head before the checklist is checked.
