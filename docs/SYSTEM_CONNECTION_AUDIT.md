# Beastbound — connecting the existing systems before adding more (13 November 2026)

**Authority:** Original `Beastbound_Realms_Unified_Master_Prompt.md` §§19, 35–36, 39, 41–50, 54, 57; `docs/MASTER_PROMPT_DEPTH_CHECKLIST.md`; `reference/Beastbound_Table_Rules.md`. This is a **review of code that currently exists on `main`**, not a new mechanical subsystem, permission framework, universal progression formula or exhaustive fantasy content list. Neither apparent conceptual similarity nor the same object mentioned by two systems implies permission to merge their separate rules or grant an effect.

## Consolidation audit: shared proofs, independent rules (13 November 2026)

**Duplicate monetary verification:** `world/person-careers.js` and `world/independent-local-purchases.js` each checked the same before/after Scales conservation independently. Their actual existing receipts now reuse the **pure** `rules/core.js` conserved-Scales check. Actual person choice, payee identity, work completion, seller stock, demand, money movements, and persistence remain in their original owners. No additional ledger, payment, RNG or buyer is created.

**Duplicate ownership adapter:** built instruments and paid equipment already publish their own verified `ownedAsset` source. `rules/material-handoffs.js` now consumes their shared `kind/owner/quantity/originalRoot/source` result instead of reconstructing separate provenance labels. Only the owning system determines whether the item exists. A quote, proposal or document never creates goods, magic, legal title or consent.

**Shared physical conservation:** source-authorised player sales, independently funded resident purchases and separately accepted person-to-person material gifts now use the same finite two-account physical-unit invariant in `rules/core.js`; existing `conservedScales` uses the identical arithmetic with its own denomination and balance limits. Each original owning system still verifies source, presence, stock, choice, provenance, time and persistence. No new transfer permission or item menu.

**The original person-custody balance is checked end-to-end:** new voluntarily accepted/refused handoff receipts, genuinely wage-funded independent purchases and sixday ration consumption now retain their real item before/after evidence **inside the existing material account and original receipts**. The same account verifier rejects an unexplained extra current holding for different people and goods, and keeps older receipt formats compatible. No new balance ledger, player-visible private stock, automatic consent, goods or currency.

**No discontinuous stock under receipt replay:** the original existing personal custody verifier now walks the actual chronological gift, funded purchase and ration-use records as one per-item before/after chain. An individually source-hashed row cannot silently skip unowned stock between two receipts; old pre-provenance rows remain readable when they precede witnessed history, but may not be introduced in the middle to hide a forged gap. Existing actor, consent, market and privacy checks remain the source of authority.

**Original material handoff accepts actual quantities:** The existing `rules/material-handoffs.js` player-to-person operation and `scripts/chat-turn.js` freeform material interpretation now carry a source-owned positive whole quantity, rather than fixing every volunteered bundle to one unit. An independently present recipient still decides; the existing owner and recipient balances conserve that exact original good, the source receipt is durable, and the player sees only the witnessed decision, not a private inventory. Earlier single-item receipts and caller operations remain valid. The two already-established examples of finite rations and gathered reeds demonstrate the common path without authoring a new item or merchant. No bulk-transport, general NPC sales, legal transfer rights or arbitrary-item generator is claimed.


**Original custody also proves bundle property:** The existing `rules/material-handoffs.js::ownerStock` property attestation now recognises an independently accepted source-reconciled gift of **any actually offered finite quantity**, not only one unit, as its historical physical ownership root. Original receipt identity remains the source; registering the observed object creates no stock, grants no deed or private disclosure, does not reroll consent and leaves historical one-item saves valid. The accepted two-reed example exercises this genuine cross-system connection with the pre-existing recipient and materials. No new asset ledger, recipe, merchant or legal transfer rule.

**Independent seller, real goods and real cash:** An existing independently lived and physically present person may now decide whether to sell genuinely held original material back to the player. The existing `rules/material-handoffs.js` account loses exactly the sold units; the original player inventory gains them. The original player Scales purse pays the original `world/person-careers.js` citizen cash account, and `world/independent-local-purchases.js` cross-verifies both original receipt mirrors, independently chosen decision and the citizen's wages, spending and sale proceeds. A real owner source (an accepted gift or genuine prior funded purchase) is required; the original `E.RESOURCES` reference value is the only initial supported source-backed individual asking price, **not** a universal market price. Refusal has no payment or transfer. Independent public merchant quotes, regional demand and final legal title remain their own authorities; no seller, funds, goods or labour is generated by a sentence. One existing material-account and citizen-market structure is reused, not a new wallet/merchant ledger.

**Accepted shared #19, with genuine limits:** an employment agreement is not a completed paid duty, expressed demand is not a funded purchase, a physical asset observation is not a legal title, and resource reference values are not universal market prices. Original seller-specific constraints do not prohibit creating future legitimate sellers or goods once their sources exist. The accepted foundation reuses correct independent people/stock/Scales/work/consent witnesses across unlike assets and actors, with all four required CI checks green on merged PR #103. No magic, legality, tax, licence, beast compliance, resale right, commodity quality or hidden funds may be inferred; separate systems retain their authority.

## Integrate first where overlap already exists

**1. Shared work proof completed for #19 (reusable in #24).** PR #95 merged after its full regression/final gate, joining existing material-conserving medicine, construction and repair source witnesses. These remain the same source when #24 later handles genuine tool quality, wear or medical lifecycles. Do not reopen #19 for optional craft catalogue growth.

**2. Authentic trade and custody links completed for #19.** Merged PRs #99–#103 connect actual person ownership balances, original multiunit gifts, witnessed GM property observations and independently chosen personal sales to the unchanged original player currency/inventory and citizen cash accounts. The existing provisioner's independent funded customer trade, real wages, accepted service terms, paid demand and separate equipment-source purchase evidence remain distinct but verifiable in one economy. Further seller, good, price or legal rights support requires actually established source evidence, not guessed balances or a new global merchant ledger.

**3. Connect world consequences, not world engines (#18 / #20 / #22 / #25).** Work, rescue, environmental damage, nonhuman consent, organisation and legal effects must each reach the **same real person/location/date/world history**. Keep distinct NPC decisions, clinical realities and local law. Repair an observed broken handoff rather than manufacture a general synthetic 'world consequence' pathway.

**4. Keep cross-discipline interfaces narrow (#16 / #23 / #24).** Existing coherence, partnership, combat, equipment and body/Origin mechanics can observe one another's *verified capabilities* but must never substitute one cultivation structure for another or infer compatibility from a label.

## How a merger or adapter earns its place

1. Name a **real cross-system event** and identify the state owner already responsible for it. If no live event fails, prefer no code change.
2. Prefer calling or extracting a pure shared validator from the **existing authoritative module**. Keep event-specific checks and independent character choices in their own owners.
3. Verify old save compatibility, exact original RNG/roll count, resource conservation, world tick timing, source hashes, player knowledge/privacy, NPC/beast consent and replay.
4. Write a **common-invariant regression** in the existing test group (plus one unlike source-backed example if needed); do not add a subsystem and tests for every object.
5. Reassess checklist acceptance immediately after essential shared connections pass. **No more optional feature PR chains as blockers.**
