# Unified living character mechanics — staged integration (18 November 2026)

## Goal and source authority
Every individually consequential Beastbound person (including elder, clan leader, ordinary handler, scholar, cultivator or ruler) must possess a persistent, causally explainable mechanical identity. Reuse the existing **living** modules; do not introduce a universal NPC level or GM-selected combat/rank shortcuts. The unabridged Master Prompt and the original distinct power-system source owners are authoritative, including the six core attributes, original potential facets, profession advancement and the Meridian / Arcane / Soul / Body / Rift / Beast-Origin / human evolution / willing beast partnership separation.

This work began when a deliberately fabricated non-canonical travelling clan elder could be portrayed in dialogue but had no source-backed mechanical profile. **The testing scene is not Maddoc's canon**. Never import the fabricated elder, her attendants, clan, beast, powers, or invented local facts into Maddoc's real campaign.

## Existing source owners — DO NOT rebuild

- `world/emergent-people.js`: individually witnessed GM people and causally emerging people; source-sealed identity, life RNG, career, presence, encounters.
- `world/independent-person-lives.js`: their independent daily interests, study, ordinary practice, relationships, fatigue, choices, focus changes. Its familiarities are explicitly **not** supernatural advancement.
- `world/notables.js`: existing 48 named professionals, earned professional practice ranks and **preparatory** system-specific study toward verified milestone events. An uncompleted trial is not a breakthrough.
- `world/agent-initiatives.js`, `world/adaptive-ambitions.js`, `world/person-presence.js`: autonomous work, priorities, schedules and local presence.
- `rules/character.js`: original player six-attribute and multidimensional potential definitions. Use consistent scales but keep private NPC potential hidden unless legitimately discovered.
- Original `rules/` discipline modules own actual stages, anatomy, abilities, techniques, costs, consent, injury, elemental effects and event results. A new NPC title, occupation or family never grants them for free.

## Phase 1 — implemented on feature branch

`world/person-mechanical-foundations.js` gives each **new** `emergent:person_N` a deterministic, independently seeded six-attribute natural baseline and 14 original latent potential dimensions. Each profile binds to the person's actual sealed birth identity and world seed. No player RNG, clock, wages, magic, equipment, techniques, combat stats or beast consent are spent or awarded.

`world/emergent-people.js` attaches this foundation during BOTH causal emergence and source-witnessed GM introduction. `mechanicalFoundationVersion:1` in the original birth source makes the new foundation mandatory during verification; deletion or alteration is rejected. **Older saved people are not silently backfilled** or treated as invalid. Their history and provenance remain intact.

`gmDossier(w, actorId)` is an **internal GM-only** read-only bridge: for newly created independent actors it displays those sealed baseline traits alongside the real career rank and actual earned discipline/technique arrays; for older established notables it displays **only** their existing professional and discipline-preparation fields. Missing facts stay unknown. There is deliberately NO ordinary player-facing rule action exposing an NPC's private potential, aptitude or unknown rank.

`tests/living-npc-mechanical-foundations.test.js` creates a **disposable** clan elder in an isolated generated test world; rejects GM-selected scores, asserts identity and profile integrity, privacy, original ranks unawarded, player economy/RNG/time unchanged, deterministic save roundtrip, tamper rejection and a legacy-notables read-only bridge. Original regression suite remains authoritative; PR CI must pass before main merge.

**Phase 1 does NOT complete full NPC builds.** Natural baseline scores are *not* final current combat attributes (age, wounds, physique, training and equipment affect those), and no actual discipline rank or beast has been fabricated for the elder. The capability to display an NPC's current complete combat profile requires source-specific work in later phases.

## Foundation source-verification cost hardening

Long-running world regressions repeatedly verify the same person's source identity. The Stage 1 foundation now caches the **expected deterministic source derivation** by original world seed, actor ID and sealed origin hash, with a bounded 4,096-entry cache. Every verification still compares the actor's entire current profile with the expected record. Public `build()` returns a separate copy, so mutating that copy cannot corrupt future expected values or permit false verification. No saved schema, original rank, source witness, player dice or game-clock mechanics change. The disposable clan-elder regression checks cached-copy isolation and live source tamper rejection. This is a performance safeguard, not a migration or power grant.

## Stage 2A — source-owned earned capability evidence (follow-up branch)

The next tested increment adds `earnedEvidence(w,actorId)` and embeds its result in the private `gmDossier` bridge. It verifies the individual's original identity, `person-careers` receipts/promotions/methods and, when present, the independent-life source ledger before giving the GM an individual evidence snapshot. The preserved professional skill rank and earned work checks are **ordinary professional competence**, not power or a universal cultivation level. Independently accumulated study familiarity is explicitly **not** supernatural advancement.

For existing notables the view distinguishes original professional standing, preparation for a system-specific trial and actual unawarded breakthroughs. It preserves recovery-month information without inventing detailed wounds. Every power family remains explicitly unresolved at this stage: Meridian stage and gates; Arcane core/circles; Soul phases; Body cycles; Rift steps; Beast-Origin integrations; human evolution; and partnership synchronisation. Current adjusted attributes, techniques, injuries, equipment, genuine beast bonds and combat effects stay **unknown** until original source owners prove them.

Unverified legacy `practicedDisciplines` and `knownTechniques` arrays are deliberately **not** promoted into verified GM dossier powers. No public rule action exposes this private evidence. The disposable Stage 2A test verifies save replay, normal witnesses, null-vs-zero, career and life tamper rejection, no world mutation and no earned stage by social role.

**Unfinished:** Stage 2B must bind individual discipline-specific original events, real techniques, equipment/ownership, wounds/age-adjusted current attributes, and origin-provenance for legacy actors. Stages 3–6 are still outstanding. No NPC combat capability or discipline award is inferred by the Stage 2A evidence view.

## Stage 2B.1 — current ordinary professional capability from original work (stacked)

The original `world/person-careers.js` now supplies a **read-only** `currentCapability(person)` projection. It shares `baseWorkReadiness` with the **actual** original `work()` resolution, including earned profession competence, the original personal aptitude and the current original fatigue penalty. Proven methods list the original lifetime recorded successes and available recent signed actual work receipts. No new dice, time, purchases or caller-selected ability modifiers are added, and the actual work owner's local DC, independent choices, peer support, innovations and effects still govern the outcome.

`world/person-mechanical-foundations.js` links that projection as `earnedEvidence.currentMundaneWork`, inside the **GM-only** dossier. A social title, career description, study interest, or unverified legacy techniques never supplies a new ability. The current modifier is an **ordinary profession work** baseline, not a cultivation, spellcasting, combat, universal NPC skill or public knowledge rating. Wounds, equipment bonuses, current combat statistics, full technique capability and voluntary relationships remain unknown until separately sourced. No assumed attendance or willingness appears in the projection.

`tests/living-npc-current-professional-capability.test.js` creates a disposable genuine witnessed worker, authentic witnessed duty and one original career work check. It verifies that the pre-check projection matches the exact original resolved work modifier (without extra assistance), that current fatigue and real success receipts update correctly, and that this remains private, replayable and tamper-rejecting without changing player rolls or clock.

**Still outstanding:** systematic age/condition/wound/gear ownership, actual source-proven magical disciplines and techniques, all other Stage 2 scope and later stages. This increment never awards supernatural proficiency or retrospectively fills an older person's history.

## Stage 2B.2 — existing owner-verified physical materials (stacked)

The original `rules/material-handoffs.js` now provides an **internal-only, read-only** `privateHoldingEvidence(world, actorId)` projection. It validates an existing, already established individual custody account using the original `validateAccount` owner and extracts only current ordinary material quantities supported by its original handoff, genuinely funded purchase, independently chosen sale, or consumption receipts. No account is created during inspection; a person with no custody account has **unknown** possessions, not an empty inventory.

Each recorded holding retains its latest original receipt hash, source kind, current balance, and whether its earlier acquisition chain is fully witnessed in retained receipts. Legacy balances without adequately witnessed proof are counted as unverified item types, **not** converted into a confirmed object, free equipment, weapon, armor or specialist reagent. The report is private to the GM, never a standard player status display; ownership is not consent to transfer, ability to use, readiness to fight, or proof of currently worn equipment.

`world/person-mechanical-foundations.js` bridges this owner view as `earnedEvidence.physicalMaterialCustody`; the distinct `equipment.verifiedItems` remains **null** pending authentic source-owned tools/gear and combat-ready condition. `tests/living-npc-material-custody-evidence.test.js` uses a disposable actually emerging person and real freely accepted physical item transfer to test source proof, unknown-vs-zero, replay, private projection, and forged-stock rejection. No Maddoc campaign save, player RNG, power stage, technique or beast relationship is modified by these GM-only reads.

**Still unresolved:** currently equipped gear and weapon effects, individual wounds and age-adjusted combat stats, original Meridian/Arcane/Soul/Body/Rift/Beast-Origin milestones and techniques, evolution and beast partnership. No power is inferred from a profession or held materials.

## Stage 2B.3 — real workplace strain, recovery and limitations (stacked)

The private `earnedEvidence.physicalCondition` projection now includes the **original** `person-careers.workplaceSafetyStatus(person)` incident history plus each original career safety receipt hash. The same living person's recorded fatigue and recovery status are used to mark the original career-work recovery block: a recovering person cannot be compelled to perform another original work action. The source's own strain, near-miss or controlled-exposure records are durable actual work aftermath, and later independent recovery does not erase the historical event.

This does **not** assert a clinical injury, missing limb, HP deficit, wound-box count, pain diagnosis, inability to participate in every other activity or change to supernatural powers. `wounds` and `currentAttributes` remain `null` pending genuine individual source-owned physical and combat records. No new condition roll, recovery shortcut, wage, teacher, equipment, combat advantage or stage is awarded.

`tests/living-npc-original-workplace-condition.test.js` creates a disposable independently living worker who encounters an actually rolled hazardous work attempt, witnesses the original source-owned safety consequence, and verifies the GM-only condition, exact source receipt, original recovery gate, replay, tamper rejection and subsequent genuine sixday recovery. No Maddoc campaign file is used.

## Stage 2B.4 — original sapient-beast personal experience bridge (source reuse)

**No new progression, species, stat or personal achievement system.** Sapient beasts who have already been genuinely encountered and have an original `world/sapient-beast-persons.js` record already possess their own `world/earned-individual-practice.js` receipt-verified autonomous work. The private character dossier can now read that original individual evidence using `gmDossier(world, 'sapient:'+beastId, gameState)`. Because beast individuals live in the original game state's `beasts` table, the third argument must be the **matching same game state**, not a different campaign; the read itself must not mutate state.

The existing original sapient-person verifier checks identity, original encounter and earned-practice source chain. The dossier only projects original source hashes, personally earned real-work domain points, demonstrated practical methods, the recorded success/receipt hashes, and evidence of the original species. The points represent their specific **mundane independently chosen work**, **not** beast grades/levels, human career ranks, Meridian or Arcane stages, combat stats, contracts, language, automatic techniques or implied cooperation. Without an original encountered personhood record, the individual remains unknown. A later voluntary partnership or evolution must use its **own existing source owner** and consent evidence.

`tests/living-npc-original-sapient-evidence-bridge.test.js` uses a disposable real engine wilderness encounter and subsequent independent original sixday work. It verifies source-owned earned outcomes, unchanged beast level/techniques, no forced bond, no public leak, no read-time dice or clock, replay stability, wrong-campaign rejection and original record tamper rejection. No campaign save is touched.

## Stage 2B.5 — already living starting relatives, without invented kinship

An existing `world/origin-relatives.js` person can now be inspected through the same internal `gmDossier(world, 'origin-kin:'+personId, matchingGameState)` bridge. It reuses the **original independent relative** source and `Kin.status(gameState)` family-continuity verifier; no relative is created, backfilled or summoned by reading. The matching-game-state requirement prevents mixing the player's family and another campaign's NPC records.

The private evidence contains the original relative's sealed origin hash, exact founding household role, any separately established kinship, the original personally confirmed relationship statement (if one exists), and source-verified independently chosen sixday activity receipts. An old `guardian` role is **not** biological `mother`, `father` or `aunt` unless the player explicitly established that relationship in the original continuity record. This bridge neither rewrites sealed source records nor overrides an established player-confirmed family fact. Private funds and fatigue remain GM-only; a relative's attendance, consent, independent status, wounds, combat attributes, techniques and supernatural stages are not silently assumed from their family role.

`tests/living-npc-original-kin-evidence.test.js` creates only disposable new-campaign relatives, tests source role/correction separation, actual original sixday decisions and source receipts, private/public boundaries, replay, tampered source and activity rejection, old saves without actors, and mismatched campaign rejection. It does not load or write Maddoc's actual save.

## Stage 2B.6 — voluntary original living household evidence (source reuse)

No new social, family, marriage, inheritance or household system has been added. The existing `world/emergent-households.js` owner already establishes **two independently consenting real adults** only after sustained original contact and source-witnessed co-presence, and records later actual separation or independently agreed reunion. The existing `world/emergent-people.js` person record starts with no family and receives its original home ID **only** from that owner.

The emergent-person GM-private `earnedEvidence.chosenHousehold` field reads a real existing `person.family` home **only** after calling `emergent-households.valid(world,home)`, including its original founders, origin hashes, contact provenance and life-transition signatures. A person without such an owner-backed home remains `null` (no confirmed voluntary home), and a forged reference or rewritten separation/reunion is rejected. The original current `shared` or `apart` value remains **GM-private**. The owner deliberately publishes only historically witnessed formation, not private current arrangements.

Founders are not automatically spouses, lovers, blood relatives, parents, hereditary heirs, permanent co-residents, obligated workers, consenting partners in a beast contract or supernatural cultivators. The bridge cannot confer common funds as private assets, magic, combat modifiers, rights or presence. `tests/living-npc-original-household-evidence.test.js` reproduces genuine original independent contact, free original household formation, original separation and save replay using disposable worlds. It tests both founders, a stranger, public privacy, non-inferred ancestry, no GM-read mutation and forged source rejection. No real saved campaign is loaded or changed.

## Stage 3A — actual existing wound combatant for a currently witnessed independent human

This is the first **real current-condition action**, not another status projection: `health.linkWitnessedHuman` registers an eligible, actually present `emergent:person_N` as a persistent combatant through **original** `rules/unified.js` → `rules/build.js:humanStats` → `rules/combat.js:register/makeCombatant`. It uses the individual's original source-sealed six natural attributes, original human ability modifier convention, existing unarmoured Medium human formula and original Light/Serious/Critical wound boxes, with **no chosen combat numbers**. Its result uses the original observed-combatant public interface, not private NPC Toughness, Defence, hidden aptitude or combat wounds. A new witnessed registration consumes **no die or world time**. Repeating registration cannot refresh wounds or reroll a profile; its source is tied to original birth hash, foundation seal and real independent local shift.

This capability requires an **actual same-scene physical witness** naming that particular NPC and establishing they are *uninjured, unarmed and unarmoured*, not just a nickname, household, reputation or guild office. The individual must be a source-backed adult, currently active, with genuinely verified physical presence and no unreconciled original workplace incident or unverified legacy techniques. A prior unknown-age/legacy/unknown-injury individual cannot be retroactively assumed healthy; a current on-scene observation is an **authored GM fact**, not proof of historical medical diagnosis. Unusual size, worn equipment, clinical injury, actual age modifications or active techniques must be handled by their **original owners** before registering a combatant. The original build formula's nonnegative Stamina requirement is enforced without inventing a floor, bonus or new human stat scale.

The existing `rules/combat.js` can already apply real attacks and persistent wounds to that registered combatant; `combat.status` shows only **observed condition** for non-player humans, and the save preserves genuine wounds and incapacitation. This increment **does not** unlock arbitrary player-initiated attacks, independently acting hostile-human fights, combat recovery or automatic NPC Meridian advancement. Those require original `combat.exchange`/action authority, independent actor choices, ongoing injury/world-life reconciliation and the existing discipline owners. In particular, the original NPC's `world/emergent-people.js` daily work routine does not yet treat the combatant wound state as a livelihood gate: do not declare injured NPC combat fully integrated with the sixday simulation until that bridge is tested and committed. This is a bounded, source-valid **entry into the actual combat engine**, not a second combat system.

`tests/living-npc-original-wound-combat-entry.test.js` creates source-generated, disposable local adult individuals and original unarmed physical witnesses, asserts exact existing formula values, denies caller-selected stats or fake presence/health, invokes the original attack and wound model once in a disposable test, checks persistent injury without second registration healing, private observed condition, replay and no changes to player money, rank, RNG/time at registration. No real save is read or written.

## GM behavioural contract

When introducing an ordinary new person, narrate naturally and record a witnessed identity when appropriate. **Do not pretend the introduction alone supplies earned magic or a clan genealogy**. When the plot requires a powered NPC to use a real discipline, inspect source-owned capabilities first. If no evidence exists, establish the legitimate cause/owner of the capability, not a chosen stat block in prose. Distinguish *GM-private world facts*, *Maddoc's knowledge*, and *individual speaker knowledge*. Keep varied age-appropriate dialogue and natural conversational momentum, including Maddoc's witty, fiery, curious, thoughtful ten-year-old voice, without forcing player input after every trivial remark. Never use any temporary test NPC as an actual witnessed world event.
