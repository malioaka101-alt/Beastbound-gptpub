# Independent human–sapient beast terrestrial journey — source-owner acceptance plan

**Scope:** Unified Living Character #177, draft PR #188; unrelated to deferred mechanical redesign #189. **Status:** acceptance design only; no independent terrestrial journey is implemented by this document.

## Exact original source gap

The original engine supports linked-location travel at 75 minutes per leg (145 when travelling to the ruins), deducting 4 player Stamina; active encounters and battles block player travel. The separate lived-places journey owner supports GM-adjudicated established destinations and hazards but explicitly does not grant NPC arrival.

Real native sapient meetings occur in native habitats such as the forest. The only original accredited individual Grade exam facilities are crossing and academy, both with **empty native spawn lists**. Independent humans have individually verified home-region work shifts; witnessing a person in a forest is not evidence of their later attendance at a distant academy. Sapient beasts independently depart on sixday boundaries. Therefore an existing relationship and sufficient wages do not establish co-presence at a facility. An independent journey owner is a hard prerequisite for a genuine paid Grade examination.

## Required original mechanical ownership

1. **Identity and present body:** Source-verified human, actual Combat wound/Stamina owner and current shift; the sapient beast's native encounter, original individual identity, welfare and actual local presence. No invented clean Combat status.
2. **Real route:** Original linked exits and the original elapsed time, each leg separately, with source-known origin and destination. Longer or unlinked routes require established original GM travel adjudication, not a point-to-point teleport.
3. **Separate consent:** A fresh actually witnessed route proposal with purpose, destination, demands, risk, return freedom and communication. Resolve independent human and beast decisions from each person's personal RNG and current boundaries. No caller-selected outcome, scheduled compulsory return or generic legal permission.
4. **Costs and risks:** Conserve original human Stamina and independently established beast exertion. Wounds, fatigue, hunger, hazards, weather and changing ecology must remain meaningful. A refusal or impassable route changes no location or fee.
5. **Temporal presence:** Source-seal departure and arrival minutes, original individual IDs, old/new shifts, source location and actual costs. The original earlier forest interactions remain historically true while both people are absent from the forest after travel. No second body or simultaneous professional shift.
6. **Encounter continuity:** Resolve departure from a genuinely existing encounter without turning a freely departing sapient person into property, erasing their agency or moving the player. The player has a separate original travel decision and knowledge boundary.
7. **World ticks and return:** Independent offscreen work, original Rift-plane absence, beast sixday departure, non-native facility ecology, and later voluntary return must not double-count a person in two locations. A visit is not permanent relocation, guaranteed availability, or guaranteed ongoing consent.
8. **Reciprocal replay:** The human and sapient beast retain source-sealed decision, travel, identity, expense, interval and return receipts. Save/import and historical-at-time position are independently verifiable. Resealed fabricated locations, earlier arrival, changed RNG choices or unspent Stamina are rejected.

## Dependent Grade and contract milestone

After actual independent arrival at crossing or academy, the existing Grade owner must still require 100 individually earned Scales, a pre-established numeric **individual** calibration (never species rarity), separately understood human and sapient examination consent, its original 120-minute check and sixday cooldown. A paid service does not prove a successful grade test. A real Grade does not grant a contract: Willingness of at least 50, original Soul-owned capacity, separate informed sealing assent and the original d20 remain necessary. None of those steps grants ownership, affection, synchronisation or an automatic shared technique.

## Required acceptance tests

- A native independently willing pair actually travels one original connected leg, with correct bodily costs/time and two genuinely sourced decisions; later genuine presence at the facility is observed.
- Human refusal and sapient refusal separately cause no teleport, check, Grade or inferred future assent.
- Wrong route, unresolved encounter/combat, absent adult, departed beast, unsafe physical condition, insufficient original Stamina, forged prior location and unverified hazards reject progression.
- Correct historical location before/after every leg; no simultaneous jobs, local presence, native forage, remote power practice or NPC combat elsewhere.
- Real return, actual original sixday departures, changing welfare, player separate travel choice and expiration of social terms.
- Identical save/import results and independent tamper rejection for resealed decision, minute, shift, beast identity, source payment or return.
- A genuinely paid and independently accepted **positive** Grade examination at a real facility across disposable original worlds; no guaranteed acceptance or roll.
- A contract only after all separate original readiness, Source Grade and sealing requirements; no default bond from escorted travel.

3. Genuine temporary attendance and independently accepted return integrated with world days, witness knowledge and commerce.
4. Original actual Grade and then magical contract/Sync positive longitudinal acceptance.

**No existing campaign saves altered. No merger or ready-for-review status until the complete source-owner and six-shard acceptance tests pass.**
