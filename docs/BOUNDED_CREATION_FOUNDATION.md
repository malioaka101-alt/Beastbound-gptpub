# Open-ended creation: bounded designs before inventions become real

**Implementation status (7 November 2026): A genuine concept/effect-envelope and persistent design-dossier foundation, not unrestricted item crafting, spell authoring or technique activation.** The original Unified Master Prompt and Table Rules are authoritative, particularly Master Prompt §§9, 19–26, 32–36, 43 and 54 and `docs/FOUNDATION_FIRST_WORLD_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Why

The player should be able to propose something **novel** without being restricted to a fixed list of named recipes and spells. But the **name or description is not a source of magical power**. A device described as “unlimited healing” cannot automatically become one, nor can someone who has never cultivated a system declare a Rank IX technique because the title sounds powerful.

Creation is important throughout society: lighting, transport, communication, animal welfare, agricultural irrigation, legal and voluntary contract support, medical diagnostics, research instruments, ecological monitoring, architecture, production, small arts, and real combat systems. A maker needs to understand how and why a creation functions. An advanced practitioner may have a strong source advantage without simply copying every learned technique or ignoring scarcity, safety and the consent of collaborators.

## Playable first step

`creation.evaluate({...})` checks a **freeform purpose and original name** against a mechanically defined proposed mechanism. It is **strictly read-only**, even in private file-backed turns. `creation.draft({...})` records that technical proposal after **35 actual in-world minutes and 1 Stamina**, preserving a stable ID, owner, local origin and original proposed scope. A draft remains **unproven**. `creation.status({})` or `creation.status({id})` reports only the player's personally recorded concepts and their missing prerequisites. Drafting does **not** grant an item, usable spell, learned technique, XP, special privilege, hidden discovery or proven safety.

The caller provides an original `title`, `purpose`, `medium` (technique, device, tool, medicine, ritual, formation, infrastructure or shared work), `system` (mundane; Arcane, Meridian, Soul, Body, Rift, Origin; runes, formations, alchemy, healing or partnership), and bounded `function`, `magnitude`, `range`, `area`, `duration` and `novelty`. A proposed input-material list can name real required quantities but **cannot supply material Grades, claimed magical properties, unlimited yields or preselected outcome**. The skill, prerequisites, budget, research grade, expected difficulty and costs come exclusively from the engine's actual formulas and the creator's saved progression.

**Functional primitives are effect constraints, not an exhaustive inventory of possible inventions:** sensing one physical signal, sending a bounded signal, warding one hazard, shaping existing material, controlled local motion, superficial repair, ordinary work support and a bounded strike. Other novel processes, effects, traditions and real-world applications can be investigated and granted new safe compositional adapters when grounded. The practical *purpose* is fully freeform, but it cannot silently exceed the bounded primitive. No single phrase turns basic sensing into thought-reading, a physical tool into infinite energy, a signal into universal instantaneous communication, a local aid into teleportation, or superficial healing into resurrection.

## Strength and feasibility

1. **Actual source-derived effect envelope:** the proposed Magnitude (0–7) maps through `rules/build.js`'s `effectGrade` (Grade I–IX as appropriate). Grade measures a desired effect, not an item that exists or a fully known magical technique. Range, area and duration remain explicit; no unlimited radius or everlasting no-maintenance effect can be claimed by descriptive text.
2. **System-specific mechanics:** Arcane shapes use the real `spellCost` formula for minimum Circle, mana, complexity, casting DC and load, and compare against genuinely completed Circle history and current reserves. Meridian harmful forms can be given only a *provisional* `qiTechniqueCost` estimate, never a free gate or mapped circulation route. Soul, Body and Rift proposals identify separately earned stage/anatomy limitations instead of treating every discipline as an identical numbered tier. Beast-Origin integration requires genuine compatibility and capacity; runes, formations, alchemy and healing need appropriate separate technical sources; partnership work needs an individually willing actual other participant.
3. **Knowledge and research:** existing `rules/research.js` novelty rules determine provisional **lead skill rank, research Grade, DC, work track and per-sixday material budget**. The submitted operation cannot declare a lower DC or fake a successful test. Only previously recorded and source-matched independently replicated research can count toward a design's specific technical proof; a similarly titled manuscript or unrelated breakthrough does not. Even matched academic research does not automatically finish an item's fabrication or a practitioner's learning.
4. **Material, workshop and welfare reality:** an item needs actual identifiable material, an appropriate safe worksite and a tested manufacturing method; quantity, material Grade, compatibility, energy and upkeep need independent evidence. Nonconsensual beast-as-equipment design cannot grant a partner's power or contract assent. Temporary, durable or permanent effects have different structural obligations and credible failure modes.
5. **No unnecessary gatekeeping:** a *small ordinary mundane* design can be investigated by a novice without mandatory academy fees, academic replication or a preselected teacher. It still needs a real prototype, materials and a competent physical trial before it operates. Exceptional or newly discovered magical technologies require correspondingly stronger proof. This is **difficulty and causality**, not a rule that creation requires a fee or guild membership.

## Illustrative designs, not fixed recipes

- A small reed-based indicator of local floodwater change is a mundane tool with a *sensing* scope. It needs real reed, calibration and a physical trial; it does not magically detect hidden soul currents.
- A collaborative sanctuary signal device uses a *signal* mechanism to communicate within established reach. It still needs participants to cooperate, is neither a compulsion spell nor a way to broadcast instantly across continents.
- A healing prosthetic, medicinal mixture or contract-stability ward may be worthwhile, but requires the *specific biological or spiritual mechanism*, a suitable tester and welfare safeguards. The word “healing” or “contract” cannot skip those.
- An Arcane transportation apparatus needs a demonstrable *movement* effect, valid Circle, range, material Grade and energy; it cannot summon unverified Rift Fold Step or teleport without anchors.
- A combat spell is allowed in principle, but its harm, range, targets, timing, energy and counters require the same evidence and cost discipline. It is **one of many uses**, not the default output.

## What is still not implemented

This is a **design-feasibility and persistent concept framework**. It deliberately does **not yet** allow the player to manufacture an arbitrary new device, cast a custom spell, automatically activate a proposed technique, create a contract, declare an invention universally feasible, or fabricate magical materials. Follow-up work must link designs to source-verifiable local experiments, manufacturing trials, failures, actual items/techniques, maintenance, skill training, independent replication and legal/social consequences. Until then the current protected `craft.fieldPoultice`, field equipment, and existing earned spell/technique procedures remain the only individually implemented creation/application routes they already support.

More future content should be driven by general capability mechanisms, **not** hundreds of hard-coded recipes. There are no arbitrary permanent population or invention limits; any newly justified effect primitive must still have a bounded, testable mechanical implementation, separately derived from prose.

Mandatory regression: `tests/creation-design.test.js`; no active campaign saves edited. The test checks proposed multi-use creations, source-limited rank and cost derivation, actual system history, refusal of forged power, material quantities and absent evidence, private draft identity and save/reload, read-only feasibility/status and file-backed duplicate turn safety.
