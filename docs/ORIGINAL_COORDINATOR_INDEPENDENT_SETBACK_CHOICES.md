## One original coordinator, one genuine original setback, individual agency

A player-founded local group can already invite real people, record their independent consent, witness actual member views, and **independently offer a limited, voluntarily accepted group operational coordinator role** through `G.offerCoordination`. The role's original `coordinationMandates` source preserves the real accepted/declined choice, time limit, membership and scope. It is not a government job or a compulsory authority.

The original monthly `G.month` work resolver can later perform the real witnessed player's previously attempted group action and independently determine **one genuine work success/failure and actual operating cost**. If the original resulting **failed** group review is recorded in the original `w.emergentGroups.history` for the same real group, month and original world tick, and its coordinator is still independently serving and locally part of the group, `GroupMemberAgency.month` may privately trigger **one independent decision** using the already existing member agency RNG. The coordinator may continue or voluntarily resign from **their own role**. No decision is supplied by the player and a failed original group project does **not** guarantee resignation.

The same original group now contains optional `coordinationReviews` receipts. Each retained source seals the actual original failed work result (including its original actual chance/draw, source event ID, group and day), the original accepted coordinator mandate/hash, original consenting individual, actual historical day, group setbacks and unchanged prior accomplishments, and one independently replayable personal RNG draw. It refuses fake setbacks, duplicate decisions for one review, or continuing to make decisions on an old term once the member resigned. Older saves without reviews remain source-valid and are not retroactively populated with invented leadership crises.

`G.coordinatorStatus` now truthfully returns the last serving real delegate as-of the requested historical day, with an early voluntary resignation ending that person's bounded mandate **only as of the original choice day**. Before the real decision, the actual original coordinator remains recorded; after a voluntary resignation, the original founder remains historically the founder and can again organise/offer another independent, time-limited volunteer role. There is **no automatic successor, forced group vote, impeachment, money transfer, government title, changed consent, instant public news or expropriation**. The existing group member roster, funds, real work results, hours, influence and time are otherwise unchanged.

This remains an **internal, privately evidenced** world decision; no new remote public notice is emitted and no player-facing omniscient role-surveillance API is added. The existing source and world tick can support later actual personal communications rather than assuming the player knows a distant NPC's private choice.
