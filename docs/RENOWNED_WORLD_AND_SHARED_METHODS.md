# Beastbound — Renowned world history, public techniques and civilian power paths

> **World-foundation layer, not a complete world atlas or an active campaign.** The existing authoritative Master Prompt §§19–25, 31–38 and 41–45 defines what the world requires. `world/atlas.js`, `world/mythic.js`, `world/social-houses.js`, `world/notables.js` and `world/simulation.js` already define the six continents, 24 polities, institutions, houses, old conflicts, sites, trade and independent ongoing political/ecological life. This document adds *new, selective public-history authoring* consistent with that prior canon. A historical attribution is **not** a verified supernatural rank, family gene, accepted pact or presently living NPC. No active campaign currently exists.

## The established shape of history

The **Age of First Paths** predates national borders: rifts and migratory beasts shaped routes and the earliest shared shelters. In **Valedryn**, the **Estuary Wars** ended in the **Seven Estuary Treaty**, helping establish passage rights across disputed river and ferry lands. Later **Sahriel** aquifer compacts preserved the political power of communal wells and their custodians. In **Threnor**, the **Longwinter Covenant** allowed seasonal rescue refuges to serve nonhuman migrants as well as human households. The **Ash Years** in **Kareth** brought down an old volcanic empire, after which forge republics, labour protections and rift licensing evolved. In **Oruvan** the **Grazing and Passage Accord** protects negotiated herd routes, while **Islarin**'s reefs and islands have living nonhuman citizens and active treaty councils. The present century sees these duties contested by security, extraction, family privilege, scholarship and commerce.

The regional traditions already exist as daily customs, not an afterthought: Reedmarch tide boards and lantern drifting; Crownspine witnessed rescue apprenticeships; Sahriel's First Cup oath; Whitewake open ice refuges; Kareth Cooling Night; reef signal songs and sea-envoy seating; and Open Peak's independent highland service. Stories about spiritual and magical figures remain evidence-limited where original records are missing.

## A few institutions known at the world's highest reputational levels

**Reputation has domains**: being the best-known healing house is not being the strongest fighter; a famous war college is not a superior soul engineer; a legendary beast senate is not a beast-ownership organisation. The following are a selective slice of renowned institutions and the *existing* families associated with their local histories:

- **Briarhall Great House & Orchard College**, Asterwyn: medicine, cultivation-related clinical care, orchard hospitals and debated private constitution records. Family responsibility for aid does not imply hereditary healing gifts.
- **Stonepath Rescue Clan & Gate Monastery**, Crownspine: 108-Gate anatomy, safe circulation, body resilience, storm rescue and public mountain refuge. Schools can teach, but are not compulsory gateways to individual cultivation.
- **Emberhand Foundry Kin & Forge University**, Free Forges: Arcane architecture, runes/formation theory, safe glass and forge work, alongside labour disputes after the Ash Years.
- **Starlens Archivist Line & Aurora Academy**, Icehollow: long aurora observations, soul safety, identity-preserving rites and arguments over public access to the sky archives.
- **Sealwarden Frontier Line & Ashglass Rift Wardens**, Ashglass March: boundary observations, rift containment, permits, evacuation routes and responsibility for public safety maps.
- **First Cup Custodian Kin & Well Collegium**, Sahriel: aquifer protection, water examination, glass and medicinal alchemy in a rights-governed desert economy.
- **Shellsong Reef Kin & Reef Consistory**, Islarin: sovereign nonhuman citizens, translation, patient consent and the living reef archives.
- **Openpeak Scholar Households & Open Peak School**, Oruvan: independent human refinement without mandatory beast partnerships; highland teaching and bridges, with private household books but no automatic inheritance.
- **Redwall Officer House & Redwall War College**, Iron Citadel: defensive unit geometry and supply systems amid civil–military disputes.
- **Many Tracks Kin-Council & Council of Many Tracks**, Old Tracks: interspecies law, protected migration and voluntary diplomacy.
- **Marshglass Lineage & Lumenfall Annex**, Reedmarch: public field diagnostics and cautious Beast-Origin research contrasted with restricted household records.

These are **renowned in particular fields**, not an invented world-wide 1–11 strength ranking. Their resources, disagreements and political situation continue moving through the original Atlas, houses and institutional world simulation.

## Four ways an ability or technique exists

| How the world knows it | What the player can infer | What the engine actually permits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Common/publicly documented technique or procedure** | The name, working principle, usual risks and where it is taught or practised are common culture | Requires the **original earned anatomy, study, materials and actual engine action**; knowing a formula's name does not grant it |
| **Restricted institutional, guild, house or clan teaching** | A family or institution may be known to possess folios, skills or exclusive duties | No private formula, bloodline right, social admission, permit or ability is granted by a surname or by reading its public history |
| **Individual original creation** | A practitioner may experiment and develop something uniquely theirs | The existing `creation.draft`, personal development ledger and source-based action adapters determine their **actual** effects; no universal preset ultimate technique |
| **Legend or disputed ancestral account** | A history, miracle or figure has public cultural influence | Original attribution and supernatural stage may be unknown; it is **not evidence** that a current person can reproduce it |

Within `world/renown-and-practice.js`, **19 shared methods** include implemented engine procedure links *only where those actions already exist*. Runes, magical formations and advanced alchemical powers remain explicitly **theory-only** until real implementations exist. Restricted private procedures are separately tracked, without copying a secret into a public catalogue.

## Actual engine connection: ordinary work changes society through a verified record

A human can narrate a natural action such as *“I examine the riverbank from the old ferry, test the flow against my own circulation, and prepare a careful note for anyone responsible for the crossing.”* The GM may then choose an appropriate existing local professional procedure based on the real scene and character, or tell the player why the proposed effects exceed available knowledge.

The new `world.culturalPractice` **internal rule adapter** supports seven physically located, source-backed fieldwork examples: ordinary ferry surveying; first-Gate flow inspection; genuine Third-Circle laboratory calibration; source-verified Soul Threading self-stability practicum; real Organ Cycle uphill rescue drills; earned Rift Anchor boundary auditing; and noninvasive Beast-Origin ecology follow-up from an actual local mapped specimen. It uses the existing **Core.check**, attribute/skill ranks, fatigue and discipline reserves, and World time and sixday/month simulation. It does **not** automatically cast the associated strike, summon a patient, create a beast partner, repair a levee, manufacture a medical cure or award wages or rank.

A successful attempt becomes a dated source-verified **local field report**, visible through the existing `world.news` once actually published. At a later real sixday tick, the **existing relevant institution** can independently acknowledge or file the report based on its **current** budget and capacity. If it acts on the report, its actual reputation can improve slightly. This is not a forced response, public office, employment, education, handover of private notes or free social influence. Anyone without powers can also do useful factual civic work.

The public history layer appears in `world.heritage` and feeds the pre-existing `world.realmOverview` and `world.atlas` institution views, so culture is part of the world the GM already narrates rather than a separate detached menu. The local work record is visible via `world.culturalPracticeStatus`; neither read-only view changes dice or underlying story. **Players speak and act naturally; the GM resolves these internal routes invisibly.**

## Unfinished scope, deliberately

This first milestone is a short background focused on already established political and mythical anchor points, not a complete world roster. New named historical figures remain retrospective, and no peak-stage living person has been assigned an unverified combat stat block. The broader ability traditions and advanced real industrial skills can be expanded *as specific witnessed sources, disciplines, material workflows, professional qualifications and NPC consequences become playable*. More world background should grow from events and cultures without making players the centre of history.

The canonical Master Prompt and Table Rules always outrank this supplement. All named institutions, homes and regions refer to existing Atlas identities, while houses and organisations continue changing independently. No old Aerin/Jory campaign secrets or established campaign facts have been imported.
