## Correction, 7 November 2026 — mastery is performance, NEVER elapsed time

**User clarification overriding any older pacing wording below:** “Lifelong” describes how demanding mastery usually is, not a calendar or age prerequisite. No minimum days, years, number of sessions, rest period, age, fixed teacher or training schedule may be used to *unlock* the degrees or bridges. Progress follows actually resolved difficulty/quality, meaningful applied work, successful method diversity, earned skill, individual aptitude/potential and credible discoveries. Chosen actions do have realistic time/effort costs and the independent world continues ticking, but **idle time alone contributes zero advancement and an exceptional breakthrough can be earned promptly**. A serious failure consumes capacity, not a mandatory sixday wait. This statement takes precedence over earlier time-target descriptions and preserves all original Master Prompt power and consent conditions.

**Legacy skill PP correction:** `engine.skillGain` now awards actual action-based Practice Points without the prior sixday ceiling; `ppPeriod` and `ppThisSixday` remain for compatible historical reporting only. Rank 4/5 still require separately recorded actual trials, no self-asserted titles. `tests/earned-practice.test.js` verifies same-sixday rank gains from real practice and no idle-calendar rewards.

## Evidence criteria

- **Tested:** a real operation changes a disposable save, respects prerequisites, records time/resources/risks and persists; it does not imply all advanced paths exist.
- **Partial:** some reproducible data or code exists, but material gameplay mechanics remain absent.
- **GM-led:** primarily narration/knowledge/agency instruction, not automatically enforceable by JavaScript.
- **Unimplemented:** no working private-chat procedure; do not invent dice, powers or outcomes.

## High-impact correctness changes in this audit

1. **Actual capability profile:** seed-reproducible physical, energetic, mental and spiritual *current* facets, distinct from fourteen sealed potential dimensions. Assessment is a source-attributed check with time, cost and truthful inconclusive outcome. No free potential score or universal F–EX rank.
2. **Protected per-discipline advancement:** old automatic `train meridian` etc are rejected by the private runner. The compiled test-backed 40/72/100/150-point source tracks and unique early rites use the canonical check, real calendar, fee, Insight and wound consequences. Gate practice tracks **each numbered Gate** with sound/perfect/flawed quality and the 36/72/108 milestone requirements. No unrelated power system advances automatically.
3. **Evolution atomicity:** a mechanically successful event cannot mutate the beast into a name without anatomy, new needs, magical organs and source-bounded abilities. A pre-authored form profile is mandatory **before** dice/catalysts, then applied in the same persisted turn; blocked or unknown branches remain unable to succeed without the conditions. No duplicative rolls.
4. **Safe local CLI:** `bridge.js` routes actions through the protected chat-turn executor and requires stable IDs; it no longer allows direct prototype power training or destructive campaign overwrites.
5. **Game saves:** the active Ivar save is preserved at 5 Leaffall 1153 AC, 08:30. Initialising capability profiles is a separate, auditable, idempotent change; it must not advance time or modify an unrelated save.

## Not yet complete — blockers before claiming full parity

- **Arcane:** Circle II–IX construction, spell memorisation, prepared load, actual cast legality/interference and backlash.
- **Meridian:** Stage IV–IX breakthrough conditions, all forced-current scar remedies, gate-specific medicinal requirements and technique repertoire.
- **Soul / Body / Rift:** advancement after the first phase/cycle/step, durable injury/identity/realm consequences, special techniques and recovery.
- **Beast-Origin and human evolution:** 8-phase integration, full provenance/compatibility rejection, human-form anatomy application and evolution crises.
- **Partner system:** ten-stage relationship practice effects, spontaneous agency, actual shared techniques and symbiotic evolution.
- **World:** deep legal courts, settlement logistics, character-driven politics, ancient planar truths, named histories, fully authored bestiary profiles.
- **Skill growth:** player skills, paid professions, contests, qualifications, clinical examination accuracy and talent improvement not fully integrated with all supernatural systems.

**Release rule:** This is a substantial mechanical upgrade and a rigorous audit, **not** a claim that all 57 sections are implemented. A missing procedure stays disabled rather than being replaced with a generic level-up or a fictitious roll.
