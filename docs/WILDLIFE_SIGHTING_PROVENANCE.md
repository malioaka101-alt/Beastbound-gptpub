# Witnessed individual wildlife observations

The live private-chat `world.observe` route and the natural commands `observe`, `inspect beast` and `watch beast` now inspect **only an actually encountered, physically present wild animal** in its recorded local habitat. A request cannot generate a free new species, choose a taxonomic source, select a 1-minute survey or set its own identification DC.

A valid source is the currently pending encounter in `s.encounter`: the beast must already exist, have a genuine habitat exposure to the current authored region, and belong to that region's established spawn ecology. The encounter must be non-threatening, unresolved, unpartnered and non-owned; sapient beings require person-aware interaction rather than an animal identification procedure. The action may be attempted **once per individual**, whether the identification succeeds or fails. A threatening creature needs a real survival decision; a partner or owned animal needs a distinct humane care/knowledge process.

The check is the already established **Wisdom + earned Beastcraft** non-invasive identification, at the existing provisional DC 10. It consumes five real in-world minutes, rolls only once and stores the original d20 event hash in `table.wildlifeObservationEvidence[beastId]`. Success may record that **particular actually encountered** animal in `s.sightings`; failure records an inconclusive observation without a guessed species. The read-only `world.sightingStatus` shows only **independently verified** species/locations, never unmeasured Grade, hidden powers, hidden evolution requirements, numeric potential, contract consent or research truth. Historic unsourced prototype sightings are not automatically counted as newly verified guild census observations; task progress is based on the recorded verified individual encounters.

This corrects a prototype route that allowed a caller-supplied source and survey duration to generate arbitrary biome sample taxa and treat them as established sightings. That old `world/simulation.js::observe` is still available to isolated, deterministic world-ecology tests, but is not a playable independent sighting.

**Scope limitation:** The underlying prototype still has a small authored local species set and a basic seed-driven encounter system. This fix does not implement full world-scale distribution, migration, reproductive ecology, complex sapient social interactions, or the entire source bestiary. Sightings are evidence of seeing a present animal, **not** of taming it, identifying every biological ability, learning a combat profile or unlocking its evolution.

Regression tests: `tests/wildlife-observation.test.js`, including real engine exploration and disposable file-backed chat turns. No campaign checkpoint is modified.
