# Foundation #18 — Living group purpose, not a fixed organisation catalogue

**Source:** Unified Master Prompt §§33–34, 42; Table Rules; existing living group, independent people, real time, and source-witness rules. Original canon and campaign facts prevail. The GM creates the ideas; the engine checks history and mechanical effects.

## Why this connects existing foundations

The shared living association registry already lets world events and real people create organisations, recruits only independently consenting members, tracks their changing opinions, roles, task attempts, operating balances, failure and inactivity. Its historical founding charter is intentionally immutable. That original founding claim should remain true, while the organisation's *current publicly stated purpose* can change.

This slice uses the **same registered organisation**, **same world clock**, **same original story-event witness** and **same public release discipline**. It does not create another group roster, group currency, RNG, universal permit, progression gate, class menu or alternate world simulation.

## Playable example: a founder restates their own purpose

The genuine locally present founder of an already witnessed informal group calls the existing rule dispatcher action **social.restateGroupPurpose** with freeform groupId, purpose and reason.

This takes **25 real world minutes**, using the original time/world-tick flow, not an invented persuasion roll. Every revision is an append-only purposeHistory row: the previous purpose and source hash, original story-event witness, identified founder, true world minute/day, freely authored reason and changed purpose, with the original **two-day local publication delay**. Historical charter.duty remains the real founding claim, while the existing social.groupsStatus uses the current statement for its founder and released statements for other observers.

The original founder can read **social.groupPurposeHistory** without consuming time or currency. This declaration is explicitly **not** evidence that independent group members agreed, that any project succeeded, that government granted a licence, that the group owns another person's assets, or that it gained a treaty or supernatural ability. Individual members can disagree or depart through their already established distinct decision systems. This is a declared new direction, not automatically a collective mandate.

## Different seed examples, same expandable mechanics

These three **disposable regression examples** demonstrate the shared mechanism; they do not limit future world content:

- A field ecology circle changes from local observation to river-access monitoring, then shifts again to seasonal maps.
- A voluntary artisan cooperative changes its stated focus to practical repairs and shared design knowledge; no completed repair, physical tool, wage or stock appears from the words.
- A wildlife-welfare circle changes purpose to habitat watching and communication, without changing beast consent, rights, behaviour or ecological facts.

New guilds, temples, chosen-family communities, research schools, sects, beast-led associations and unfamiliar collectives remain possible without adding names to code. The new trusted **registerPurposeRevisionAdapter(origin, check)** and **revisePurposeFromEvidence** are backend extensions of the existing world registry pattern. Independent NPC-formed groups can eventually revise their own purpose only with a **genuine verified actor decision source**. This example does not manufacture such decisions. Unknown sources fail closed.

Original monthly group tasks still spend only original funds, use original effort and original independent rolls. Public notes of their activity now use the actually declared current purpose rather than an outdated founding description. The changed purpose never retroactively proves the success of unrelated work.

## Evidence and limitations

The **group-evolving-declared-purpose.test.js** regression runs three freely authored disposable associations, repeated amendments of one original group, original source-chain verification, real time, delayed public visibility, no added members, money or dice, read-only historical inspection, save export/import and tamper rejection even after outer hash recalculation. It leads the full original regression inventory.
