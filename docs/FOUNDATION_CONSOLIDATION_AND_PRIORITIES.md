## 7 November — one voluntary physical handoff across people and materials

The world now supports an ordinary general **offer/give/hand real carried item to a physically present human** using the original named/independent person-presence, item conservation, independently chosen acceptance, actual six-minute world time, world-owned recipient inventory and a saved verifiable handoff. This unifies different previously disconnected foundations: commerce produces actual owned goods, people can accept or decline them, and a later independent tick may consume genuinely held provisions as part of their personal life. An accepted item does **not** become a market quotation, friendship, forced obligation, teacher or wage. Giving to a named person can no longer be silently misparsed as feeding a wild animal. An actual wild creature retains the distinct original independent animal-care route. All canonical physical items and genuinely available human identities share the same operation; no merchant-specific gift script is required.

See `docs/FOUNDATION_A_PERSON_TO_PERSON_MATERIAL_HANDOFFS.md` and `tests/voluntary-person-material-handoffs.test.js`. Keep the broader game grounded in actual available sources and choices. This is **not** universal arbitrarily priced sale/barter, instant work contract, trade inventory or full person hunger simulation. Continue to consolidate where a specific cross-system playable gap exists, not to proliferate a new subsystem for each material. Active Aerin state remains untouched.


## Reversible ordinary habitat work: the SAME real plants can be harvested or replanted (7 November 2026)

**Core principle:** The existing `world/simulation.js` native-source stocks now support **both** verified extraction and a player's attempted native planting, rather than one-off gathering scripts or an invented separate botanical economy. The original established Whisperfen marsh reeds and Ashwind ironbloom can be gathered, carried and **returned to their actual source habitat**. Each planting costs **one actually owned unit**, physical labour (40 minutes, 3 Stamina), and one independently resolved **Survival** check grounded in current ecology. An attempted replant does not grant any immediate resource, instant environmental improvement or guaranteed successful propagation; a successful viability check creates a source- and roll-hashed **pending planting** on the same pre-existing world-owned source. The next actual sixday tick independently decides whether the plant establishes, fails when habitat conditions have worsened, or finds its local patch already full. Previously established natural regrowth still occurs, separately and within the same finite habitat stock cap.

In normal chat, player intentions `plant reeds`, `replant marsh reed` and `plant ironbloom` resolve to the same source-validated `world.replantMaterial({item})` procedure. There is **no mandatory prior survey, paid teacher, licence, fee, daily planting quota or player-selected success/roll**. A person may reasonably try to propagate material from their own bag even though surviving roots/seeds cannot be guaranteed. Only native source-authenticated materials are currently simulated; this does not claim all plants can be propagated from detached material or that nonnative transplanting is harmless. Current world source proves where native material exists; a separate future evidence-backed case may establish other propagation methods. A read-only `my plantings` / `world.plantingStatus` response shows only attempts personally made at the **current location** and what their real local site currently records, not remote seed survival or private offscreen data. Full turn-ID replay returns the original single work and original die.

**Code:** Reuses existing `rules/reed-harvest.js` material and source validation, `rules/unified.js` atomic item movement, original `rules/core.js` d20, `rules/world.js` world-time, and the original finite `world/simulation.js` growing sites. No new magic, material creation or parallel skill ladder. Old saved sites lacking `plantings` remain readable and lazily support new work. Each world planting carries a hash of its original sealed attempt source, plus a personally witnessed result; a single specimen can never establish twice on successive ticks. No editor changes to Aerin's checkpoint. `tests/native-habitat-material-restoration.test.js` exercises both native species, genuine costs, independent later growth, ecological failure, no item/stock duplication, unsupported placement refusals, read-only status and private turn-ID idempotency; all earlier trade, gathering, crafting and medicine tests stay mandatory.

**Cross-system usability, not another engine layer:** The existing protected `creation.draft` dossiers, `creation.prototype` work, `creation.calibrate` / `creation.measure` real instrument use, `creation.repair` with owned replacement stock, original `craft.fieldPoultice`, and `health.balm` now connect through normal in-world text. ## 7 November — physically reusable crafting, real alternative clean workspaces

The original ordinary field poultice, genuinely invented reed surface-water indicator, and field repair of an owned worn indicator now share **one internal `rules/physical-work.js` material-and-effort executor**, rather than reimplementing resource subtraction, stamina expense, original Crafting roll, time advance, witness hashes and source journal independently. The caller establishes a **real existing recipe or independently sealed invention**, correct environment, materials, health and costs; the common executor preflights all materials atomically, checks real stamina, commits precisely one material spend and one authentic Crafting roll, retains the original source witnesses even as narrative history rotates, then advances the actual world. It does **not** expose an unsupported player-selectable recipe, automatic device, output, choice of DC or magical technology. This is an actual consolidation across three distinct previously playable crafting operations.

The exact original poultice recipe now works at **either** sourced local clean medical venue: **Lumenfall Annex laboratory/clinic** (original), or **Morrowfen Crossing infirmary** (already an authored location facility). The optional second worksite means a fieldworker may return to the Crossing infirmary instead of being forced to travel to the academy for a straightforward common poultice. No teacher, fee, additional profession rank or arbitrary timetable is required; real access, cleanliness, safety, actual materials and the engine-owned Crafting result are still decisive. Other work must genuinely establish its own appropriate tools, rights and hazards. Legacy poultice first-attempt/day records retain original shape and old Lumenfall provenance.

`tests/shared-physical-work-journeys.test.js` verifies **paid provisions + authentic fen harvest → original poultice at the real Crossing infirmary**, as well as `creation.draft → genuine physical prototype assembly → first calibration → fresh real reed gathering → physical repair`. Existing poultice/prototype/harvest/trade/regression tests remain mandatory. Invalid material plans, mismatched raw output claims and same-turn replay are refused without free advancement. This is a shared actual-life mechanism, **not** three new siloed mini-systems. The active Aerin checkpoint must remain untouched.


## 7 November — reusable material flow, not one-off task systems

**Latest player correction:** Build general in-world capabilities that serve multiple related situations. A named merchant, marsh-reed harvest, healing balm and voluntary wildlife food are **examples**, not separate siloed engines or a complete list of allowed creative actions. Preserve relevant differences in habitat, craft, economic rights, biology and independent decision-making.

**Concrete code consolidation:** The existing `rules/unified.js` now provides **internal-only** `planItems(s, deltas)` and `applyItems(s, plan)` for canonical physical resources. A multi-item plan validates ALL actual quantities, authenticity of resource keys, scarcity, carrying limits and current inventory **without changing the save**. Applying the plan verifies that no affected item changed in between, transfers the exact verified deltas atomically and retires the plan so it cannot be replayed. No rule-request operation grants raw access to these helpers. They don't make a resource available; each consuming system must still prove its **specific source and decision**.

The very same internal material flow is now used by **six genuinely different causal activities**: real in-person ration purchases, source-witnessed fen gathering, actual two-input field medicine and resulting healing balm, independent wild-animal food acceptance, voluntary partner provisioning, and Light-wound balm consumption. Nonstackable provenance-bound weapons still use their appropriate item/action checks. The ordinary wildlife offer grammar now resolves **existing resource names** (including original named item labels and naturally plural nouns) rather than one branch per feed phrase, with the animal's actually present identity and original independent dietary decision still verified.

**Coverage proof:** `tests/shared-material-flow.test.js` validates two-item atomic changes, insufficient materials, invalid quantities and source-nonexistent items, inventory capacity, stale/reused plans, unchanged player RNG and no exposed arbitrary item-minting rule. Existing end-to-end tests for merchant purchasing, real harvesting, medicine crafting, wild feeding and beast care remain in required CI; wildlife tests additionally exercise generalized resource-name and creature-target parsing. Development never modifies Aerin's save.

**Honest remaining work:** This DOES NOT make every item gatherable, tradable, edible or craftable merely because it appears in the canonical resource list. The current world still has few real stock-owning suppliers, authored material sources and genuine recipes. Expand these through actual ecology, owner choice and physical tools, then connect them to the **existing shared transfer mechanism**. Do not multiply another merchant/gather/food mini-system for every new item, material, species or location. The GMs can adjudicate other plausible methods using source-consistent world facts instead of treating the current text parser as the limit of player freedom.

## Cross-system playability pass — 7 November 2026

**User direction:** Stop repeatedly expanding one narrow mechanic. Prioritise useful interactions across an actual scene: truthful information → free choice → source-grounded NPC or beast activity → engine-confirmed consequence → safe checkpoint. Add no separate registry, role gate or general-purpose subsystem merely for architectural completeness.

**Implemented without another engine layer:**
- The existing private `scripts/chat-turn.js` now answers ordinary `look`, `scene`, `who is here`, `routes`, `help`, `status`, `inventory` and `journal` as genuinely **read-only** actions. A harmless glance does not consume time, advance the world, spend a turn ID on a roll, or overwrite bounded evidentiary log history.
- Local scene awareness reuses already established `E.LOCATIONS` routes, actual current encounter, `world/person-presence.js` individual presence and player-known focus; no hidden person's goal, relationship, power, automatic consent or unseen discovery leaks to the player. Named daytime public professionals are not reported as permanently on duty at night.
- Ordinary `talk to <real name> about <freeform question>` now matches the actual named person before parsing the subject. A canonical NPC's previously authored public statement may be heard, but the question is **not** treated as proof that they answered it specifically, offered a contract or revealed a secret. Newly generated present people retain their existing independent response path.
- `docs/CHAT_EXECUTION.md` no longer offers retired prototype quest payouts or illicit caller-selected check DCs as copyable actions. Real checks require an independently witnessed precommitted `checkId`; source-scoped actions and honest mechanical results remain the standard.

**Honest gaps:** A readable scene view is not a new vision power or a generated census. It does not create full social conversations, arbitrary craftable goods, guaranteed shopping stock, universal travel routes, advanced beasts or powers. NPC schedules are still coarse where no actual hours are sourced; do not invent daily whereabouts. Future work should favour new verified **playable journeys across existing systems**—for example wildlife observation and limited gathering to real crafting or honest trade, or real embodied ability use in a scene—over a fifth overlapping knowledge/society subsystem. Aerin's live save remains untouched.

## Continuation: eliminate artificial learning gates without new subsystems

The existing `discipline.practice` now accepts 15–480 genuine work minutes (with fatigue, actual world time and native checks), while keeping legacy sixday calls as optional historical compatibility. When `source` is absent, independent practice is the natural default; the older established course is available explicitly, not required. Brief assistance never automatically charges an entire course fee or borrows a course-level check advantage just because an unverified institution name was supplied. `discipline.status` exposes these choices to the player.

Existing `npc.converse` and `witnessedTheory` check **actual heard statements and subject-relevant established professional knowledge**, not a fixed two-NPC whitelist or an invented teacher's magic stage. An author-established broad scholar can know and discuss more than one field even without personally practising it. This modest factual theory benefit applies where relevant to early practice, breakthrough, Meridian Gate work and Second Arcane Circle structural design. It cannot compel voluntary hands-on lessons or pass a high-stage teacher's powers directly to a learner. The first local NPCs remain examples, not a universal list of authorised educators.

`world/notables.js` no longer interprets high institutional resources as proof a higher-ranked teacher exists. Genuinely capable senior mentors, unusual books, inspired research and direct demonstrations should only receive different and potentially larger benefits when specific **individual source evidence, voluntary sharing, appropriate comprehension and actual circumstances** are available. Avoid speculative teacher registries.

Tests: `tests/flexible-discipline-practice.test.js`, `tests/contextual-theory-guidance.test.js`, `tests/arcane-second-circle.test.js` and existing source-parity, world and private runner suites. No campaign progression was fabricated.

## Superseding teaching clarification — optional accelerators, two distinct skills

Real helpers **may** improve understanding, speed, safety, access, reliability, technique or experimentation where contextual. **No generic teacher, high-stage mentor, textbook, licence, tuition or time gate is compulsory.** A person may be exceptionally skilled in practice but a poor instructor, or never have cultivated yet hold extensive and valuable theoretical knowledge. Judge a real advisor's knowledge and communication separately from personal advancement; judge the student's comprehension and hands-on results separately from the teacher's achievements. Sapient people and beasts retain full independent consent. Read `docs/OPTIONAL_TEACHING_AND_THEORY.md` for the standing rule and the first verified classroom-independent, source-backed theoretical assistance in `rules/disciplines.js`. A teacher's rank is not a proxy for breadth of understanding.


**Decision, 7 November 2026:** Prefer the smallest interoperable systems that make the Master Prompt's living world playable, instead of continually adding standalone mechanics for every conceivable social, cultural or magical situation. The original `reference/Beastbound_Realms_Unified_Master_Prompt.md` remains authoritative; the foundation-first architecture and later player corrections explicitly oppose arbitrary compulsory teachers, fees, calendar or age gates.

## 2. Essential progression: focused vertical repair

The original Master Prompt §20 describes 12 Primary Meridians, 108 individually worked Gates in Lower/Middle/High bands, real gate quality, deviation and structural milestones 36, 72 and 108, **not** a universal six-day wait or a required academy lesson.

`rules/disciplines.js` now permits **independent Gate work at any currently modelled physically suitable location**, given an awakened and authenticated existing primary-meridian structure, ordinary safety/health, and the person's own verified prior Gate records. `route:'established'` remains an optional supported choice where actually available; it is not compulsory. The default route is based on whether an external source is provided. A player cannot supply a success flag, quality, DC, or milestone proof.

A real practice attempt uses `minutes` (default 180; allowable 15–480) rather than automatically advancing six days per attempt. The duration is genuine elapsed effort, **not** a minimum number of days or sessions before unlocking a Gate. The authoritative d20 check determines work progress, risks and quality; forced current still risks flaws and deviations. On Gates 36, 72 and 108 the existing band DC is increased for structural difficulty (+3), and previous individual Gate records are checked before any roll. A completed transition is recorded with identity, context and day, not accepted from a caller boolean.

This is **only a repair to the existing Stage II–III Gate path**. It does not implement Meridian Stages IV–IX, invent medicines/techniques, override documented dangerous anatomy, or turn every world location into an ideal clinic. The legacy historical fields remain readable. Matching old Gate records gives save-consistency evidence, not proof against someone with direct write access to every save object.

## 4. Genuine gaps — build only when relevant

**High priority for playable breadth**
- Finish **Second Circle** simultaneous-channel technique use and validated preparation/load; extend **Arcane Circles III–IX** with distinct earned construction geometry, actual spells, risk, resources and bounded combat/noncombat effects. The Second Circle's source-backed structural capacity is now implemented, but these further effects are not.
- Higher **Meridian Stages IV–IX** with Dantian Sea, meridian-web, forced-current scars, actual techniques and physiology, without transferring them from Arcane ranks.
- Progressive **Soul / Body / Rift** stages beyond the verified early milestones, with their own anatomy, risks, consequences and real capabilities.
- Actual **Beast-Origin integration and human evolution**, conditional on individual compatibility, safety, consequences, physical/source proof and genuine consent where relevant.
- Fewer fixed, procedure-shaped training durations in other older operations when the canon only requires real effort and demonstrated competence.

**High priority for integrated play**
- Use existing scene, individual, body, resource and time data for genuine **discovery → practice → technique → equipment/ability use → consequences → saved history** journeys.
- Keep player knowledge separate from secret world state. Support freeform intent with a tested native action or a truthful GM adjudication; do not substitute a made-up roll.
- Before generalizing unrelated local services, test one actual player journey from local opportunity through earned progression, risk or choice and a verified saved checkpoint.

**Defer unless actual play exposes a need**
- A second universal relationship engine, prewritten world social census, mandatory teaching marketplaces, invented schools/fees, exhaustive language mechanics, artificial species/clan inventories and a new universal power ranking.
- Specialised cross-species education, social institutions or ancient magical procedures without a witnessed individual, concrete local situation, compatible biology and an actual mechanical effect to implement.

**Development acceptance:** every addition should remove a real gameplay blocker, meaningfully integrate existing capabilities, or repair a demonstrable inconsistency in the Master Prompt. Otherwise record a limitation and leave it unbuilt until causally needed.

**Campaign checkpoint protection:** `saves/aerin_marsh.json` must not be changed by development or by the disposable integration tests. No new earned progression, relationship or lore is added to Aerin until a real in-world action is executed and its checkpoint acknowledged.
