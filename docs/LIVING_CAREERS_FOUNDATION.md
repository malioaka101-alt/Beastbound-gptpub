# Foundation #17 — Living careers, authored work, and independently earned qualifications (draft)

Authority: original Unified Master Prompt §§32–33, 47–48; the original Table Rules and established histories take priority. **This is not a profession roster, mandatory career ladder or requirement for paid instruction.**

## Reuse first

Existing `world/emergent-people.js` creates autonomous individual workers, chooses actual work/rest/relocation, tracks local presence and preserves their original living biography. Existing `world/person-careers.js` already resolves genuine personal d20 work, local ecology/civic/trade effects, fatigue, independently earned practice and broad demonstrated competence; `world/simulation.js` already ticks their world and stores real institutions and conditions. Source-backed worker agreements, groups, research, provisioner stock, player economic resources, teaching and knowledge already have their own ledgers. **No new economy, career XP, third-party workforce simulator or job-level currency is introduced here.**

## First expandable slice: an authored local duty belongs to an existing person's career

This initial extension adds `career.authoredDuties` **inside the same verified career record**. A GM can describe an independently established, source-witnessed local task with arbitrary natural-language role and goal, a machine-addressable unique method, an existing person, a real originating region/day and optionally a **genuinely existing active institution in that person's polity**. This is compatible with an independent vocation: institution membership is never inferred from a job opportunity. The mechanical adapter references one of the original **ecology, civic or trade** work contexts. For limited physical outcomes it can explicitly point to already implemented habitat-repair or water-management; other invented effects are not silently granted. The engine, not the GM's adjective, calculates difficulty from genuine conditions and resolves the actual actor's existing individual rolls.

Authored descriptions do **not** confer skill, a formal licence, a master title, employment membership, an agreed wage, a supernatural power or a guaranteed outcome. The work remains among that individual's ordinary options, and the **existing independently chosen livelihood decision** determines if and when they actually undertake it. If the person changes region, an earlier local assignment cannot be used remotely. When a referenced institution stops being active, its local task cannot continue as if the employer still operates. Genuine attempts produce the same original career work receipts, income/fatigue rules and existing environmental effects, while durable assignment-specific proof hashes retain the entire sequence after the normal 24-receipt career window rotates.

A later observed change can retire a particular duty through a separately source-noted retirement, preserving its historical source and every accomplished receipt. Changes to authored titles, location, source, mechanics and completed work are checked by the same existing per-person verification; reopening or reshaping is a newly sourced role, not a stealth edit of the old one. Existing saved people lacking the new optional field can migrate upon first legitimate authored job without erasing their earlier career.

## Two intentionally non-exhaustive examples

* **Wetland culvert care:** a regionally established individual may undertake newly noticed drainage repair and demonstrate a specific practical method. Its narrow effect uses the existing habitat-pressure repair operation. The role alone does not heal the landscape; only genuinely successful source-resolved work can do that.
* **Storehouse provision auditing:** an individual may investigate real storage records and supplies as a distinct trade practice. It produces observed professional work and normal livelihood consequences; it does not create stock, conjure goods, open a guild, sign a contract or grant unseen economic facts.

The engine recognises these not by a pre-approved name list, but by the **same `authorDuty` and original work/verification machinery** that would hold other locally credible services. Future true medical occupations, school work, transport, beast-related duties, craft specialisations and politically changing public jobs can be added with proper domain-specific actual-world effects. A useful example is neither a universal cost/limit nor a compulsory starting profession.

## Second source-bound slice: earned and locally witnessed practical attestations

A genuinely completed ordinary work receipt may be recognised by an *existing active local institution* through a separately GM-witnessed decision of an *actual institution-affiliated named professional*. This is documented in the individual's **same original career** as an `attestations` entry. The first adapter, `issueAttestation`, verifies the previous original personal success receipt, actual issuer/representative, scope of the documented method and day, and seals a copy of the genuine successful work source so its proof survives bounded 24-entry receipt rotation. No artificial XP pool, universal exam, required apprenticeship, fixed credential catalogue or compulsory profession rank is introduced.

A document can have an independently stated expiry; no fixed global qualification-renewal term is imposed. The GM may source a genuinely separately witnessed `revokeAttestation` event, retaining the original accomplishment and recording the change. A later *new* attestation needs new existing-source work and a new witnessed decision; rewriting the old document is rejected. Only a legitimately source-witnessed local scope is recorded. In particular, this initial `attestations` registry is **not** legal licensure, medical authorisation, guild membership, formal employment, automatic prestige or supernatural permission. Legal scope and issuer powers must be established by appropriate actual institutions and jurisdictional rules before granting such effects.

A second disposable full-suite-first test `tests/living-career-attestations.test.js` checks an actual worker's original independently rolled success, a truly existing institution and affiliated professional witness, truthful refusal for fabricated achievements/issuers, real save/reload and 40 subsequent career receipts, expiry, revocation without erasing prior skill, and source corruption refusal. The intended suite grows from 168 to **169 test groups**. **This second slice remains staged/unverified pending exact-head CI; #17 remains unchecked.** Existing local voluntary work agreements remain the separate valid consent path, and original institutional abstract budget points cannot be converted into personal Talons or contracted wages.


## Third connected slice — independent work-offer decisions and real attempted fulfillment (unverified draft)

A source-authored duty is an opportunity, not a command. `offerDuty` may present a **specific previously sourced local duty**, a **currently active existing institution**, its named **actually affiliated professional**, a risk found among the **actual region's recorded hazards**, an explicitly documented safety plan, a bounded scope, and a *personally independently issued* opportunity. The already existing individual worker decides for themselves from their **own existing RNG**, individual aptitude, fatigue and local ecological pressure. The GM cannot supply accepted/rejected, a salary or an assumed higher rank as parameters. An individual can decline, and a same-day offer to that same person for that same duty cannot be rerolled by changing the request ID.

An acceptance **does not compel the worker to perform the duty**. The original `world/person-careers.js` still chooses among real independently available work and rest. If and only if that actual career work is selected while the local offer remains valid, the original work's single already-existing d20, livelihood changes, fatigue, ecological/civic/trade consequences and genuine receipt are used. The accepted offer merely links the full verified actual attempted work evidence into the **same personal career**; it does not create extra dice, skill points, contract income, magic, guild membership, or free goods. On refusal or on expiry it adds no authorized forced work. A sourced voluntary ending retains prior evidence and does not erase the person's earned skill or previous actions. A change of region or a suspended issuer stops the local duty from being selected, even if historical agreement proof remains intact.

The documented hazards and care provisions are truthful records of the negotiated expectations, **not blanket immunity or automatic workplace injury rolls**; specific actual hazard incidents and insurance/regulatory liabilities need further canonical work/health/law adapters. The institution's **abstract funds** and the individual's **abstract livelihood funds** are not Talons/Scales; hence this example deliberately **does not introduce a wage or currency conversion**. Actual paid employment must first establish a legitimate transferable physical payer-owned balance, same-unit worker wallet and genuine paid-work agreement. Otherwise one would mint money from a budget label.

`tests/living-work-independent-offers.test.js` is placed first in the **170-group** full regression inventory. It covers actual existing workers, a valid existing issuer, an original individual RNG decision, refusal and anti-reroll protection, actual regional dangers, no chosen acceptance or salary, ordinary original work receipts, replay, expiry, valid withdrawal and source tampering. This is staged as a draft; **full exact-head CI is still required and #17 remains unchecked**. All roles and terms are illustrative open-ended source-authored examples, not a fixed profession count, employment queue, mandatory school or universal time cap.


## Fourth example — voluntary professional peer learning, grounded in original work (10 November 2026, draft)

The GM may establish a meaningful professional demonstration from an actual previously successful independently earned work method. `world/person-careers.js::observePeerGuidance` connects **two real currently active individual workers actually present at the same genuine saved local shift**, using source-verified existing NPC presence rather than a fictional mentor. A genuine prior personally earned original work receipt establishes that this *particular person* can perform the *particular practical method*. The learner may already pursue their own suitable work independently; a teacher is strictly optional. The teacher decides with their **own individual RNG**, without caller-selected agreement or repeated same-day consent rerolls. Individual physical fatigue changes on a real accepted shared work demonstration; refusal creates no forced attendance, wages, rank or technique.

An accepted demonstration is a **limited situated practical insight**, not an immediate increase in learning level, an employer contract, inherited bloodline, Circle, medicine licence or universal skill. If the learner later **independently chooses a real originally valid task using the same method while the locally witnessed shift remains relevant**, the original real career work resolves using its **one pre-existing personal roll**, work aptitude, fatigue, environmental difficulty and consequences, with a one-use bounded **+1 practical guidance modifier**. It is consumed whether the honest task succeeds or fails. This numeric one-use example is *not* the global maximum on real pedagogy, nor an obligatory 6-day apprenticeship curriculum. Other learned techniques require separately source-authenticated actual-world effects and relevant original competence/biological disciplines. Records retain the mentor's original demonstrated work source, independent decision witness, actual shared location, one supported authentic learner work receipt and immutable provenance after normal journal rollover and save re-import. This work stays in the **same** original individual careers, not a parallel education engine.

New disposable `tests/living-career-peer-guidance.test.js` is first in the required **171-group** full CI suite. It covers real independent civilian source IDs, actual shared saved shift presence, honestly witnessed previously successful methods, no chosen consent or double RNG, optional refusal, genuine single subsequent work roll, one-use support even on ordinary failure, source persistence and tamper rejection. **Unverified draft until exact-head full CI succeeds. #17 remains unchecked**, with payer-backed wages, more nuanced safety obligations and the general legal scope of licences still outstanding. No active campaign saves altered.


## Fifth bridge — real physical-currency compensation, without creating a second economy (10 November 2026, draft)

A first genuinely *funded* career payment connects one actual operating merchant account to one existing individually living professional's single source-verified successful service. The preexisting `world/simulation.js::localProvisioner` already holds a named owner's physical **Scales**, finite provisions, historical purchases and actual wholesale expense. The new `world/person-careers.js::payVerifiedTradeWork` uses a separately GM-witnessed **one-off** local payment decision by that merchant to compensate an actually present autonomous worker for a previously proven **successful** same-region **trade service**. The merchant must have its original verified seller identity, location, original work evidence, an actual source-described seller decision naming that individual, and **sufficient real Scales**. No scheduled wage, future contract, compelled worker action, or fictional hiring/salary is assumed. An independently accepted institutional work offer can continue to evolve separately; an abstract guild operating budget is *never* retroactively reinterpreted as Scales.

The same **actual currency unit** is used at both ends. The finite original seller `fundsScales` account decreases by exactly the amount added to the existing emerging worker's new `cashScales` field. Personal `funds` livelihood capacity, institution `funds` budget points and player `coins` are unchanged. Historic individuals with no currency field remain at zero Scales by default; there is no retroactive free salary. The worker's original career stores the original work source, one genuine signed compensation, real payer, unit, original balance before/after, amount and witnessed seller decision. A matching complete record lives in the **original merchant's own ledger** and is cross-verified. The original work hash cannot receive a second payment, even after the bounded original work journal rotates. No customer goods, tax exemption, welfare credit, magical technique, affiliation or other asset appears because a monetary payment was recorded. Both histories and the worker's wallet are preserved by the actual existing checkpoint.

This first named provisioner is a **verified exemplar of the same-unit conservation bridge**, not a fixed hardcoded ceiling on future employers, industries, salaries or payment arrangements. Additional shopkeepers, farms, estates, temples, governments or beast institutions can author legitimate payer-owned currency accounts, needs, witnessed choices, voluntary agreements and services using the same source/debit/credit receipt pattern; they must first establish truthful available balances, rather than mint money by labelling an abstract resource as wages. The foundational first route proves a conserved actual pay event; it is **not yet** a recurring employment contract, automatic pension, tax and insurance system, proof of seller interest in every task, or a legal right to work.

The disposable source-correct `tests/living-career-real-merchant-payments.test.js` checks an actual already existing physical merchant float and stock, a living original civilian source/shift, real independently resolved successful work, source-witnessed decision, exact debit-credit conservation, own actual wallet, duplicate/counterfeit rejection, no fabricated payout from guild funds or player Talons, both original ledgers, save reload and historical receipt rotation. It runs first in **172 full test groups** after the peer-guidance group. **Draft, not exact-head CI-verified or merged. Foundation #17 remains unchecked.** Full safety medicine response, legal credential authority, genuinely negotiated standing work/pay agreements and cross-occupation employer adapters still remain.
