## Original Stage IV source and responsive living-web anatomy

The existing `rules/disciplines.js` remains responsible for earned Foundation/Awakening, all 108 individually recorded Gates, three Lower/Middle/High milestone receipts and real Stage IV Dantian Sea formation. New `rules/meridian-living-anatomy.js` **only extends `s.table.powerPhysiology.meridian.advancedNetwork`**, not a separate cultivation engine. It requires the same source-backed prior Gate anatomy, completed Sea work, original source date and current Dantian presence.

`meridian.livingWebMap` accepts an original GM-authored route name and purpose, a physically credible set of unique numbered Extraordinary/Major/Minor segments, actual opened Gate numbers, Dantian centre, pressure load, neighbouring interference, risk, documented treatment path, and a **previously witnessed local individual-anatomy event**. The anatomy classes and counts are canonical, but route names, symptoms, professional focus and techniques are **not fixed catalogs**. Overlapping routes cannot each claim an individual channel, and a failed authoring request cannot allocate phantom structures. A source map alone creates no Qi, opened Gate, stage, stored energy, learned skill or spell.

`meridian.livingWebWork` uses actual Qi, Stamina, duration and the original check. Difficulty reflects the mapped route's source-defined load, size and existing flawed Gates, while the player's established Wisdom, Cultivation and genuine Dantian purity contribute. Each real attempt appends its own sealed source, roll, cost, earned integration and real-world time. Severe failure can persist as a recorded original route-pressure deviation, reducing actual Meridian stability; it does not silently manufacture permanent organ loss or a magical illness. Progress cannot be forged by changing route.integrity independently of resolved check receipts.

`meridian.livingWebTreat` requires an existing pressure injury and a *separate actual witnessed clinical diagnosis and intervention*, genuine treatment time, Stamina and a Medicine check. Success records limited physical recovery; a failed treatment still consumes actual effort. Historical injuries and care are retained, with pending injury state derived from original success receipts. Being too weak for pressure practice does not disqualify the character from seeking treatment.

`meridian.livingWebStatus` displays only the character's actual personally mapped routes and real coverage. In the current draft, independently integrated route segments count toward Stage V network coverage only if their injury is resolved. Full integration of all canonical 8+36+72 segments is an **anatomical verification goal**, not an auto-award of Stage V or 116 mandated sessions. The GM may map original connected subnetwork bundles, subject to physically credible load and risk; there is no compulsory teacher, paid school, calendar-gate or fixed medical style.

## Stage VIII — bounded, portable Living Domain (draft)

`rules/meridian-living-domain.js` extends the **same** `advancedNetwork` with `domainPlans`, `domainTests` and `domainPulses`. The prerequisite is an authentic Stage VII tissue and Meridian integration, the physical source of an individually earned circulation route, and an actual local scene. The GM may author any personally measured field principle and its credible environmental supports and counters—not select from a menu of prescribed school forms. The observed radius is bounded by the real individual's current Qi-flow capacity and stability and becomes more demanding at greater ranges; it is **not a universal fixed-metre domain cap**.

Individually observed environmental practice earns source-checked experience through real Qi, Stamina, pressure, engine checks and time. A separate measured-range countertest is required for Stage VIII integration and capacity recalculation using `Build.meridianProfile(8)`. Current Qi is conserved, never refilled. An actual Stage VIII practitioner can try `meridian.domainPulse`, a short, costly and independently contestable local field. It can collapse on a genuine check. A previously learned field may **travel with its owner** when its unchanged measured radius and compatible new environment are **actually witnessed**; initial training location is not an ownership zone or arbitrary travel restriction. No domain may compel NPCs, command beasts, claim an entire territory, automatically defeat opposition or produce unearned spells/attacks.
