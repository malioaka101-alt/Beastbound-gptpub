# Real professional relationships — evidence, consent, rivalry and change

## Source and limits

The Beastbound Unified Master Prompt and Table Rules require **independent NPC agency, real development, research, personal relationships, social mobility, rivalries, recoverable failures and histories grounded in actually established facts**. The [Living World Change Charter](LIVING_WORLD_CHANGE_CHARTER.md) expressly rejects frozen roles, automatic friendships, obligatory patronage or legendary powers merely because time passes. This patch implements a first **individual professional relationship** loop between the two *already established* background professionals in each of the original 24-polity world atlas. The original source did **not** name these precise professional collaboration programs, event delays, roll DCs, or pair them as friends. They are explicitly **new regional simulator authoring**, not assertions of their private feelings, births, romances or supernatural techniques.

## Individually acting people, not automatic faction friendships

The world already tracks 48 genuine named public-service and field specialist NPCs in `world/notables.js` and contextual, independently executed vocational ambitions in `world/adaptive-ambitions.js`. A source-hashed `verified-effort` result from an actual NPC's paid d20 practical test, with a true material change to local ecology, passage risk, civic service or training, may be locally **published** with its usual eight-day release lag. Only then can a relevant same-polity colleague respond. Every first encounter is prompted by an **actual published and evidence-verified accomplishment**, not a pre-written quest, family crest, ethnicity, gender, player presence, romance flag or arbitrary elapsed-month friendship score.

For each of the existing 24 home polities, the two named professionals are **different people**, who may independently react to another's released practical achievement in one of three ways:

- **Offer joint limited vocational work**: the colleague can send a genuine work invitation through their existing local professional network. The original practitioner has **a separate independent response**, no sooner than one real sixday later, and may decline because of interest, institutional closure, resources or injury. Rejection creates no automatic personal hatred, marriage, guild obligation or permanent hostility. **Both must agree** to participate.
- **Independently compare a method**: the colleague may instead initiate their *own* practical comparison without claiming that the original author agreed to join, was physically present or endorsed the alternative. The original tested work remains valid; a different alternative may also prove useful. This builds evidence-based professional competition, **not automatic animosity or character mind-reading**.
- **Decline to engage**: an independently established person may have other priorities. Neither NPC is forced into a story scene because a roll or the protagonist prefers an outcome.

A persistent relationship record tracks how many **verified** joint achievements, independent comparisons, failed attempts and voluntary responses actually occurred. It does not assert emotional intimacy, family membership, ownership of knowledge or a permanent inherited rivalry. A future actual published accomplishment can create a new opportunity after prior refusal or failure.

## Real costs and results, with no cloned NPCs

When two people agree to work together, **both** must remain healthy enough and their actual employing institutions or verified self-founded cooperative must be active and have the resources. They devote three genuine sixday-supported research sessions, costing **two budget points per person per session**. If both belong to the same organisation, it really bears both people's costs; no funds are conjured. Sustained closure, injury or shortage causes delay and eventual abandonment, retaining the expense of any prior funded preparation. After three funded sessions, an independently drawn persistent world d20 with their existing professional ranks and available institutional training is tested against a modest actual DC13 joint-work check. Success modestly improves practical training capacity of the actual participating institution(s) by at most one point, and the historic receipt gains a verified limited cooperative result. Failure grants no achievement.

An independent comparative project instead costs **two real budget units** per session at the *critic's own* employer, uses three sessions and a separate DC15 test. A successful alternative grants at most one training-capacity point to the actual competitor's employer. It does **not** revoke the original researcher's earlier verified work, steal their special technique, remove a family entitlement or force their consent. A failed comparison consumes genuine time/resources and produces no improvement. Both positive and negative results may be reported publicly after eight days. Thus strong old authorities can be surpassed by credible new professionals, while challenges can fail, successes can coexist and previous collaborators can later compete. Social relationships are changeable through *deeds*, not static faction labels.

The world peer ledger `table.realm.professionalPeers` records the exact existing person IDs, original published source, separate consent if relevant, individual institution resource costs, actual skill, single d20 trial and the event's original before/after capacity. Evidence is SHA-256 witnessed and powered by a **persistent private world RNG**, never player combat rolls. All actions execute on the **actual sixday world tick**, even when the protagonist never meets these characters. Nothing automatically grants a player cultivation gate, arcane spell, inherited physical trait, nobility, marriage, pet or special affiliation.

## Only publicly supported information is visible

`world.peersStatus({})` is strictly **read-only** and reports only locally published, source-hashed, appropriately delayed two-person professional achievements or failed verification reports. It does **not** reveal unpublished invitations, private responses, secret mutual trust, budgets, unobserved encounters, speculative romances or distant nation's office politics. The full ledger exists for GM world simulation only. A visible joint report is not a claim to have met these people, witnessed their conversation, or been offered their training.

Existing save data is not rewritten during status reads. The peer ledger is created on actual future sixday ticks, with no fabricated earlier partnerships. The source and its public bulletin must both be verifiable before independent colleagues begin their correspondence. A changed world can produce new links, failed comparisons, cooperation and alternative techniques; there is no forced story choice or automatic permanent status quo.

## Incomplete and next priorities

This is a **professional** social loop, not the complete individual relationship ecosystem. In future, real friendships, family disagreements, rivals competing for resources or office, teaching apprenticeships and independent intelligent-beast researchers need their own established people, circumstances, agency, communications and consent. Courtly romance, marriage, descendant birth and death, succession, binding patent law and new supernatural disciplines are *not* implemented by this operation. Expansion should preserve ordinary people's open-ended paths without forcing guild membership or noble approval.

`tests/professional-peers.test.js` and `tests/professional-peers-longrun.test.js` verify real original outcomes, the two distinct authentic professionals in each polity, independent engagement or abstention, informed bilateral work consent, institutional expenses, successful and failed individual comparisons, blocked projects, reusability of honest public achievements, player magic/rank invariants, local news delays, deterministic multi-year simulation, strict private-status protection and file-backed no-write reads. No active campaign saves are edited.
