## What is actually recorded

A player may now discuss their freely authored **specific practical association task** with an **actually present independently consenting member** of a genuinely founded voluntary group by `social.groupTaskRequest({groupId,npcId,task,untilDay})`. The task text describes the player's own proposal; there is no approved job menu or arbitrary universal mentor/fee/required rank. The original world calendar advances 18 real minutes, requiring physical presence across the conversation. The real **existing person-agency RNG** determines the individual decision (accepted, still considering, or declined). The caller may not choose or forge that outcome.

The original story event contains source-verifiable player founder, group origin, member acceptance, actual person ID, place/region, date and expiration, bounded independently replayable agency draw, freeform proposed objective, and explicit restrictions against forced service, legal appointment, wages, resources or instant results. An optional `voluntaryTasks` array in the original group holds the **immutable witnessed reply** and actual individual membership source hash. It is not a contract and cannot compel time from another consenting adult. An absent group, present nonmember, duplicate daily request, missing person, future/postdated witness, forged decision or ended membership cannot create consent. Earlier saves with no `voluntaryTasks` remain unchanged and source-valid.

A later original `world/group-member-agency.js::month` might organically decide that this actual active local member freely volunteers its ordinary **existing 90 minutes**, with its original ordinary one roll, independently sourced location/skills and unchanged success/failure/labour/effects. **Only if that genuine original voluntary work really happens** may the result be attached to one still-active independently accepted task as an append-only `taskServices` source. That source captures the ORIGINAL world journal event and original group hash, original roll/DC/outcome/member and prior witnessed voluntary task. If the member declines, departs, is distant, does not independently volunteer, or the original task expires, then there is **no task completion**, no secret synthetic work and no obligated extra roll. Subsequent original monthly volunteering is not repeatedly attributed to one old request.

No new funds, extra skill advancement, political status, group influence, job income, law, legal office, disciplinary power, arbitrary sign-off or power over nonmembers is awarded. Other real independent member service can still happen naturally without a task: it has not been converted into a forced guild-work system. This explicitly conserves the same group member-work counters, service minutes, existing world RNG and original group `e.funds` account accepted in verified PR #67.

The original player founder alone can read personally witnessed responses and any actual source-matched later work through `social.groupTasksStatus({groupId})`, a **read-only chat-turn action**. It does not reveal secret unknown distant people, fabricate commitment for undecided members, or write a save.
