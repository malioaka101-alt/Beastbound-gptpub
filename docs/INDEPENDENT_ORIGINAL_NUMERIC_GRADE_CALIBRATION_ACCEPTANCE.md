# Beastbound — Original Independent Numeric Individual Grade Calibration Acceptance

**Scope:** Unified Living Character System, Issue #177 / draft PR #188. This is a **mechanical acceptance specification**, not an awarded Grade, new general power ladder, completed examination, or amendment to the deferred progression/potential/combat redesign (Issue #189).

## Original canon and current engine boundary

The Unified Master Prompt §§7–8 distinguishes **species grade** (general species power and rarity band), **qualitative individual grade** (the particular current creature), **level**, **evolutionary form**, and **multidimensional potential**. None is a numerical substitute for another. The Beastbound Table Rules likewise forbid equivalence among beast Grade, species grade, level, human cultivation and synchronisation.

The existing `rules/bond-grade.js:gradeReference` demands a **pre-established numeric Grade of 1–9 for the same beast and current evolutionary form** before its original paid d20 examination. It currently validates the supplied `gradeCalibration` object's numerical range, timestamp, identifying species, qualitative individual grade, form and text source. That structural requirement is **not by itself a source-owned method to generate, measure or verify the individual Grade**. The GM must never manufacture the object, choose a favourable number, copy species rarity, level, potential, another person's Grade or a sample fixture to make the exam pass.

The positive original travel/work assessment [44905835040](https://github.com/malioaka101-alt/Beastbound-gptpub/actions/runs/44905835040) passed seven workflows at `f79369e`, with **three** individually physically arrived visitors each personally receiving **137 Scales** from the existing real merchant after a voluntary successful original career work check. New read-only `bond.npcGradeFunding` validates reciprocal worker/merchant source payments, current matching original visitor and beast at the examination facility and the individual 100-Scale fee; it spends nothing and certifies no Grade. **This closes the source-funding gap, not calibration or actual examination.**

## Separate source-owned pre-examination measurement required

Before `gradeCalibration` becomes authoritative for a protected sapient individual, the eventual diagnostic owner must establish the following *actual prior events*, all private unless separately observed by the player.

1. **Institutional legitimacy.** A genuinely existing, accredited beast-Grade examiner and functional diagnostic method at an original crossing/academy facility must be verified against the world source. A guild registrar, profession label or convenient place name is not automatically an accredited examiner.
2. **One real individual and one physical body.** Use the exact existing `s.beasts[beastId]` species, identity, current form, original contact/personhood source and two-sided original travel receipts. Neither a species template nor the player's unchanged position establishes the subject's presence.
3. **Independent informed diagnostic permission.** The protected sapient being freely chooses whether to undergo the noninvasive measurement; an accompanying human's separate choice does not override it. Existing travel, relationship, merchant employment and future formal-exam assent cannot be repurposed as diagnostic consent. Document refusal as a valid outcome without a new number.
4. **Genuinely measured biological and magical evidence.** Record source-supported individual anatomy and current energy/organ functions with method limitations, welfare, actual ecology, existing injuries, calibration uncertainty and the current physical form. Reuse the original individual biology/combat assessment owners where applicable, not new hidden universal stats. A measurement is an observation, not an innate technique, evolution or reward.
5. **An explicit independently validated Grade 1–9 reference rubric.** The current prototype has **no verified general source rubric** mapping individual diagnostic readings to numerical institutional Grade. Such a rubric must be established for the relevant species/organ system or validated comparison standard *before* a numeric outcome may be assigned. A rarity code, `individualGrade` label, level, potential, human rank, raw attack number or unrelated test fixture is not such a reference. Do not silently add a universal power score.
6. **Immutable, cross-checkable proof.** The diagnostic receipt should bind the existing beast ID, exact form, the authenticated facility/assessor, observed source and time, separate sapient decision, measured parameters, rubric/version, computed or independently certified numerical result, uncertainty, and a verifiable historical source hash. It must verify through save/load and reject resealed fabricated readings or a future-dated or changed-form reading.
7. **No reward from observation.** Measuring a person does not move or own them, restore Combat Stamina, mint Scales, unlock Grade certification, grant a contract or add synchronisation. Any actual measurement cost, time and risk require their original mechanical owners rather than a fictitious free action.

Only after a genuinely prior, source-verifiable calibration exists can the **separate original paid examination** proceed: 100 Scales debited from the same person's conserved cash ledger, two new independent informed examination choices, real location and welfare, actual 120 minutes, original DC `12 + 2×individual numeric Grade`, original earned Wisdom/Beastcraft and privately resolved d20, sixday cooldown after a failed examination. A successful exam confirms measured Grade; it still does not establish a bond, ownership or Sync.

## Acceptance cases still required

- Reject an attractive but unsupported `gradeCalibration={grade:9,...}` field, species-derived Grades, old-form readings, made-up accredited staff and a copied Grade from another beast. A source string alone is not diagnostic proof.
- A real living sapient person may decline a diagnostic. Earlier mutually accepted travel, work or companionship does not force measurement or formal examination.
- Record one **genuinely** corroborated individual diagnostic (not merely a synthetic fixture) with source-aware measurement, normal original time/welfare costs and a justified Grade rubric; prove the result survives export/import and fails independent tampering.
- Verify an original work-paid 100-Scale fee on **that same** separately willing person at the real facility, one freshly voluntary beast examination and one genuine engine check. Record both failure/cooldown and success without selecting the dice or the response.
- Preserve personhood, exact monetary conservation, no second body, no simultaneous home wages, no implicit contract or inherited consent, and player/private knowledge separation.
- The full six-shard source-owner regression and cross-system positive histories are **not complete**. Do not mark the independent numerical Grade examination, contract or Sync accepted until their own evidence is actually observed and verified.

**Order:** institutional/rubric/source design → independently consented diagnostic and sealed numeric calibration → already proven real individual fee funding → separately accepted 120-minute original Grade examination → independent sealing/Sync, if later earned. The deferred #189 progression/potential/combat redesign remains distinct.

## Original source-only specimen inventory and fail-closed Grade boundary — 21 November 2026

In the current draft, `bond.npcGradeSpecimenEvidence` provides **GM-private, read-only existing engine facts only** for a genuine independently arrived human–sapient pair at the established facility, requiring the exact verified original worker/merchant and terrestrial-visitor history. Its separate individual beast identity, form, qualitative grade, life/energy values and real present condition are useful *source inventory* for choosing a future physiological assay. They are **not diagnostic instrument readings, an institutional examiner, an independent assay, numerical Grade, or evidence of consent to one**. No body, source currency, time, player status, original RNG, relationship or grading record changes. A caller cannot specify readings or a numerical Grade.

The current `bond-grade.js:gradeReference` now **fails closed for a verified sapient-person record**, even if an arbitrary `gradeCalibration` object superficially contains a valid number, descriptive text and minute. This is intentional: until a new original source owner truly validates a historically witnessed species-appropriate biological/energetic assay, accreditation, mutually informed diagnostic acceptance, and pre-existing independent reference rubric, a free string or 1–9 number cannot unlock the separately paid exam. This is a source-authenticity gate, **not a positive calibration implementation**. Legacy isolated examination arithmetic and non-sapient Grade law are otherwise preserved.

The real original 137-Scale visitor source test now asserts true individual physically present anatomy inventory, zero side effects, rejected caller-specified grades, and forged prior numerical calibration refusal, including with a superficially legitimate source string. Preserve ordinary player-knowledge privacy, no conversion from qualitative grade, and original player mechanics. Actual accredited measured source, positive paid exam, contract and Sync remain future #177 acceptance items; #189 remains deferred.
