# Foundation #11 — source-earned Arcane higher Circles and original spell architecture

**Master Prompt §21 and the original Arcane system remain authoritative.** A Mana Core, structural Mana Circles, prepared spell formula, learned spell, verified cast and a changed organ are separate things. Having a Mana pool or calling an effect an Arcane spell cannot substitute for original source-based construction.

## Implemented draft bridge — Fourth Circle Dynamic Architecture

The verified previous Circle III Matrix Formation (`rules/disciplines.js`) is the prerequisite. `rules/arcane-dynamic-architecture.js` stores its new `dynamicFourth` history **inside `s.table.arcaneArchitecture`**, retaining the original Third Circle's matrix geometry and the real player's Mana Core. Original local GM-authored observations anchor a freely described fourth-channel design, its interference, actual channel response, countertest and bodily safety. New `arcane.dynamicMap`, `dynamicWork`, `dynamicRepair`, `dynamicComplete` and read-only `dynamicStatus` perform actual checks with real Mana, Stamina and one world clock.

Successful practice earns source-backed structural integrity points, severe failures cause bounded persistent core strain and real Core Stability loss, and a separately witnessed repair costs real energy/time and resolves by its **own** engine check. Only a successful physically independent live reconfiguration test awards Circle IV. Its `Build.arcaneProfile(4)` updates the original mana capacity, intake, channel count, load tolerance and prepared architecture; **it does not refill current Mana or teach any spell**. No arbitrary teacher, fee, sixday waiting period or forced academy route. A theorem or a person's expert explanation may inform a GM-authored exercise, but practitioner rank is not itself teaching ability.

## Original multi-channel formulae

`rules/arcane-spell-grammar.js` stores personally GM-authored formulae and real learning receipts **inside the same `s.table.arcaneArchitecture.formulae`**. Each has a unique original name, source witnessed physical geometry, measured channel IDs, interference and limitations. Its real budget comes from `Build.spellCost`: range, area, duration, load, complexity, minimum Circle, Mana, targets and casting DC. It cannot claim unobserved mage or beast anatomy.

The steps are intentionally distinct: `arcane.formulaMap` records the described design without granting magic; `arcane.formulaLearn` repeats genuinely witnessed formula experiments and earns knowledge through actual checks and paid Mana, Stamina and time; a genuinely learned pattern becomes a new record under the original `s.table.arcaneTechniques`. `arcane.formulaCast` currently only performs a **bounded noncombat sensory/geometry demonstration** with source witnesses, a real cast roll and Mana cost. It does not invent attack damage, defence, healing, injury repair, beast mimicry, teleportation, uncontrolled emotion or NPC obedience. Severe failures can leave persistent formula interference, and `arcane.formulaRepair` requires a new physical diagnosis and a separate paid original check.

The spell-grammar budget is a language for future source-specific effect adapters; the ability to design an unproven effect is not an automatic right to use it. An actually learned damaging or medical spell needs its own valid source, target and combat/health adapter. This restriction is physical consistency, not a mandatory finite menu of creative spells.

## Advanced original Circle V–IX source foundations (draft)

`rules/arcane-higher-circles.js` extends **the original `s.table.arcaneArchitecture.higherCircles` and the single genuine Mana Core**—there is no new independent power ladder or rank currency. Each personally authored design requires the previous actual earned Circle and its source history, real existing channels, a newly witnessed **stage-specific** physical observation, an independently testable interference limit and safety response, then actual engine rolls and energy/time expenditure. Six **earned structural points** trigger a distinct original formation trial; they are not six compulsory lessons, a teacher, a course, an academy fee or an age lock. Independent severe failures can produce lasting Arcane structural strain and Core Stability loss. Repaired structure requires its own original observed diagnosis, genuine Mana/Stamina/time and a separate check. Current Mana is never refilled at breakthrough; Circle capacity and channels come from `Build.arcaneProfile(stage)`. Formed states retain immutable original-source check receipts and the previous Circle seal. A new Circle supplies **architecture, not spells**.

- **Circle V — Field Projection:** observed local Mana output, measured range, ambient counter-pressure and a bounded field response; the Circle does not seize land or passively damage anyone.
- **Circle VI — Living Lattice:** physically examined **existing** human tissue and nerve response, biological load and independently bounded overload. Merely declaring a rare organ or evolved beast trait cannot create it.
- **Circle VII — Astral Architecture:** witnessed astral/spiritual-layer measurements and personal identity safeguards; this neither reads private thoughts nor automatically advances Soul cultivation.
- **Circle VIII — Law Interpretation:** a genuinely sealed law from the **existing cosmology ledger**, separate from hypotheses, plus personally witnessed local physical law manifestations and limits. Studying it does not rewrite it.
- **Circle IX — World Circuit:** a physically established local place and genuinely observed external Mana network with bounded isolation, preparation and feedback failure sources. An original Circle IX is not an automatic city-control, realm-walking, teleportation or spell licence.

An individual who understands an unusual method or meets a willing expert may develop a distinct original design; source, energy and actual verification govern results, not a preset catalogue or automatic mentor requirement. Cross-system techniques and combat, soul, medicine, environmental transformations, beast functional analogues and additional specialist capabilities still require **their own** physical sources, legal/consent circumstances and effect adapters. Such adapters should be developed only when actually needed by coherent play, not manufactured as a checklist of free spells.

## Open-ended spell research versus executable effects

`Build.spellCost` remains the true spell family / range / area / duration / complexity / load / Mana grammar. After the draft broadening, **original offensive, medical, warding, environmental, magical analogue and World Circuit designs may be mapped and genuinely studied** if the body and existing Circles can physically support their structural budget and their unique source has been established. A World Circuit formula requires a genuinely earned Circle IX, and a beast-pattern analogue requires an actual observed mechanism; those are **real provenance**, not fees or paid teachers.

Only the small source-witnessed *noncombat geometry/sensory demonstration* currently has a generic execution route through `arcane.formulaCast`. A speculative damaging spell may be learned as formula theory without silently acquiring an executable damaging attack. Real combat and other effects need matching source-specific world/combat/medical systems, targets, contests, casting risks and consequences. The spell concept and personally invented name never override this physical distinction.
