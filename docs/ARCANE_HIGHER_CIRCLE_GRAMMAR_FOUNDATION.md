# Foundation #11 — source-earned Arcane higher Circles and original spell architecture

**Master Prompt §21 and the original Arcane system remain authoritative.** A Mana Core, structural Mana Circles, prepared spell formula, learned spell, verified cast and a changed organ are separate things. Having a Mana pool or calling an effect an Arcane spell cannot substitute for original source-based construction.

## Implemented draft bridge — Fourth Circle Dynamic Architecture

The verified previous Circle III Matrix Formation (`rules/disciplines.js`) is the prerequisite. `rules/arcane-dynamic-architecture.js` stores its new `dynamicFourth` history **inside `s.table.arcaneArchitecture`**, retaining the original Third Circle's matrix geometry and the real player's Mana Core. Original local GM-authored observations anchor a freely described fourth-channel design, its interference, actual channel response, countertest and bodily safety. New `arcane.dynamicMap`, `dynamicWork`, `dynamicRepair`, `dynamicComplete` and read-only `dynamicStatus` perform actual checks with real Mana, Stamina and one world clock.

Successful practice earns source-backed structural integrity points, severe failures cause bounded persistent core strain and real Core Stability loss, and a separately witnessed repair costs real energy/time and resolves by its **own** engine check. Only a successful physically independent live reconfiguration test awards Circle IV. Its `Build.arcaneProfile(4)` updates the original mana capacity, intake, channel count, load tolerance and prepared architecture; **it does not refill current Mana or teach any spell**. No arbitrary teacher, fee, sixday waiting period or forced academy route. A theorem or a person's expert explanation may inform a GM-authored exercise, but practitioner rank is not itself teaching ability.

## Original multi-channel formulae

`rules/arcane-spell-grammar.js` stores personally GM-authored formulae and real learning receipts **inside the same `s.table.arcaneArchitecture.formulae`**. Each has a unique original name, source witnessed physical geometry, measured channel IDs, interference and limitations. Its real budget comes from `Build.spellCost`: range, area, duration, load, complexity, minimum Circle, Mana, targets and casting DC. It cannot claim unobserved mage or beast anatomy.

The steps are intentionally distinct: `arcane.formulaMap` records the described design without granting magic; `arcane.formulaLearn` repeats genuinely witnessed formula experiments and earns knowledge through actual checks and paid Mana, Stamina and time; a genuinely learned pattern becomes a new record under the original `s.table.arcaneTechniques`. `arcane.formulaCast` currently only performs a **bounded noncombat sensory/geometry demonstration** with source witnesses, a real cast roll and Mana cost. It does not invent attack damage, defence, healing, injury repair, beast mimicry, teleportation, uncontrolled emotion or NPC obedience. Severe failures can leave persistent formula interference, and `arcane.formulaRepair` requires a new physical diagnosis and a separate paid original check.

The spell-grammar budget is a language for future source-specific effect adapters; the ability to design an unproven effect is not an automatic right to use it. An actually learned damaging or medical spell needs its own valid source, target and combat/health adapter. This restriction is physical consistency, not a mandatory finite menu of creative spells.
