# Source-owned operational sites for real organisations

## Confirmed gap, not an academy-only simulator

Beastbound's existing voluntary association **already** has an immutable founding witness, actual members, independently reviewed work, its own finite operating funds, signed income and expenditure audits, and original world ticks. Its site may be a canonical Atlas location or an actually witnessed GM-established place. However, the original work attempts were drained after each monthly review; the group could not retain a verified, location-linked work history once the short world journal rotated. Calling a hall an "academy" did not connect it to physical work or facilities.

This source-owned bridge keeps one permanent **historical context receipt inside the original group record** for each freshly witnessed activity when the **existing** monthly group-work review actually runs. It is not a separate building/inventory/work simulator, extra money source, second dice check or new generic rank.

## Original sources and effects

- `social.groupContribute` still spends **the same 45 minutes** and, when requested, the player's **actual Talons** into the original association's balance. The original story event now also carries its real `siteMinute` and, for GM-created locations only, the original immutable `world/lived-places.js` founding witness. A contribution remains only an attempted activity—no completed construction.
- The original `world/emergent-groups.js::month` still chooses the independent result once, spends its real bounded group funds and retains its original treasury expense review. Only **after that existing review** does it retain a sealed site-operation receipt containing the actual attempt and original independent result. The **shared original review cost** remains the single original group expense, even if multiple attempts were reviewed in the same cycle. No second roll, additional expenditure or status grant is permitted.
- The original `social.groupSiteWorkStatus` reads the source-verified original group-site records for its actual local founder. Its records distinguish attempted physical work from the **original limited group review**. A favourable review does **not** prove that a particular building was constructed, research was completed, a beast trained, a student taught, or any unverified facility made usable.
- Cross-entity checking ensures the place's founding source still exists in the same saved world and was established **before** the recorded activity. Historical canonical Atlas locations remain accepted without fabricated GM-place provenance; older contributions without the new site-minute proof still resolve through their old group month code and are not silently granted new facility facts.
- Records keep original group identity, exact physical location, original action/event and independent review hashes. Replaced founding or review evidence is rejected. Old short journal entries may rotate without erasing the retained original review.

## Boundaries and next phases

A functioning voluntary study circle might now have documentary proof of attempting physical work at a real hall and paying real group operating costs. That is useful **operational history**, not actual legal title, exclusive room access, purchased materials, functional teaching equipment, employee contracts or academic accreditation.

Physical buildings, purchased goods, ownership and maintenance still require their original construction, custody, market and property source owners. Individual teachers, researchers and apprentices remain autonomous and use existing professional work and earned training. A later institution integration must combine those genuine sources, not call a site-use receipt proof of unbuilt classrooms.

The end-to-end `tests/gm-group-source-owned-operational-sites.test.js` tests a new GM hall, self-declared group, a genuine 45-minute funded contribution, the existing independent 30-day world review and actual treasury cost, persistent source receipt, read-only status, exact save replay, canonical site compatibility, short-journal rotation and tampering with place/review/action sources. No live campaign saves are touched.
