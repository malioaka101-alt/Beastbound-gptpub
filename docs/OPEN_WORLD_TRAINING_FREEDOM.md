# Power-system freedom — real-world routes, not compulsory tuition

The Beastbound Master Prompt §19 establishes **separate, anatomically grounded human disciplines** with distinct energy pools, risks, stages and ability systems. §48 requires **actual effort, instruction or knowledge, appropriate conditions and time**. It does **not** state that every human must join one particular academy, pay a universal fixed course fee, obtain permission from a named teacher, or follow only one route to power. Books, family practice, mentors, sects, individual discovery, field experience and self-directed study may all exist; their credibility and consequences depend on the world and actual events.

The previous engine prototype accidentally turned **one paid instruction example** into a universal toll for every sixday discipline practice session and every initial breakthrough. This update adds a positive **`route:"independent"`** alternative, keeping the previous `route:"established"` workflow intact for old saves and existing paid training.

## What the new path really does

A player can now choose `mode:"rule", system:"discipline", operation:"practice", params:{"system":"meridian","method":"breath","route":"independent"}` or analogous recorded methods for Arcane, Soul, Body and Rift. The system retains the original specific track, skill and attribute, exact `C.check` progress, capped values and ordinary failures. **No tuition is charged** for the independent route, while training still consumes **six real in-world days**. Its fixed check is **+2 DC** compared with known instruction, reflecting absence of specific expert feedback; other methods and failure complications (including injuries and recovery interruptions) remain possible. The actual source is generated from the **existing location and method** by the engine, never from a caller's invented teacher, magical book, long-term favour or unstated financial arrangement. A precommitted event records the method, site, track, duration and difficulty before any roll, with a source-linked hash in `table.independentTraining`.

No specific fee or coach is required to practise **your own breath, body, soul attention, or Mana Core theory** at a genuinely visited existing location where the action can occur. Such study is not an assertion that a particular teacher, tool or manual is present. A player is still unable to execute a sixday calm practice while an immediate beast encounter or fight is unresolved, or after being incapacitated. A bonded creature's sixday independent needs must have credible separate care; a player cannot quietly skip them. **Rift Sense** differs: its early independent training still requires an authentic nearby spatial anomaly, currently the Riftglass Outpost. The actual state limits where the character can perform the chosen activity, not the availability of a scripted quest NPC.

The separate **`discipline.breakthrough`** now supports the same `route:"independent"`. It requires **all the original anatomical and earned advancement prerequisites**: complete track points, two genuinely practised methods, recovery cooldowns, the system-specific construction requirements and any actually spent earned Insight. It replaces mandatory paid rite supplies with a source-linked **unassisted rite at the actual existing site**, charging **zero tuition** but applying **+3 additional DC** versus the supported assisted rite. A failure remains a failure, with the original setbacks, cooldowns and lasting damage risks. Progress cannot be invented by a string claiming that somebody previously taught the character.

The response from read-only `discipline.status` reports available routes, independent study difficulty, current location eligibility and tuition. The preceding `route:"established"` calls, with their old fees/parameters and existing success mechanics, remain valid. There is **no migration of previously completed milestones or power**.

## Real-world interpretation

**Learning opportunities are context, not global locks.** A local teacher may ask for a fee, accept service or friendship, refuse particular students, give a discount, teach in a different place, or share only a family method. A recorded manual may allow self-training, an experienced wanderer may exchange lessons for supplies, and an emerging practitioner may gradually experiment independently. The GM should narrate and distinguish these opportunities as independent actors. **Do not quietly award the same unearned rank** merely because a possible route is described.

The new engine route is deliberately a first broad foundation, **not yet** a complete dynamic teacher/sect/manual economy: the word `independent` refers to actual basic self-directed practice, not a claim to have acquired a rare resource, specialist instruction, a copyrighted secret technique, new inheritance, or the ability to bypass an advanced breakthrough. For wilderness training periods the separate existing provision/shelter systems are not yet fully unified with these sixday sessions; future work should explicitly account for meals, fatigue, weather and camp costs rather than treating six days in a dangerous swamp as magically safe. Private story narration should avoid presenting unsupported camps as facts.

## Verification

`tests/open-learning-routes.test.js` runs genuine sixday independent practice in all ordinary foundational disciplines, checks that Rift sensing cannot happen at an ordinary settlement, confirms full independent real breakthrough progression, source-event hashes, random failure risks, zero forced tuition, retained paid route and file-backed idempotency. The full engine suite remains required. No real user campaign saves are modified.
