# Foundation #16 draft — optional clinically witnessed Meridian healing intent

Authority: original **Unified Master Prompt §§20 and 26**. Healing and Regenerative Arts may integrate anatomy, original energy pathways, genuine repair and controlled regeneration, but must respect ordinary patient agency, medical limits and the existing physical wound model. Professional healing competence need not imply a combat tier.

This **first particular technique** is source-witnessed Meridian-assisted *self-treatment of one existing Serious wound* at a genuinely available safe clinic or infirmary. A player request does not establish a technique or patient outcome by itself. Its independently observed scene must describe the actual consenting human wound (canonical Light/Serious/Critical boxes), original serious tissue anatomy, existing first Lower Meridian Gate and mapped route, ordinary clinical baseline, measured source-specific Qi-assisted cellular response, independent adverse circulation countertest, patient consent, actual existing balm and sterile site, finite aftercare and contraindications. The original player is the only patient in this first adapter. Treatment of independent NPCs, beasts, Soul injuries, missing organs, Critical injury, infection, magical contamination, incompatible physiques and other paths must use separately observed source-specific procedures and patient/guardian consent, not be inferred.

`rules/specialist-healing-intent.js` uses original `meridian-qi-strike.gate` source verification, *actual existing medical and cultivation skills*, canonical `Combat.get(s,'player').wounds` and established `health.balm` item/transaction. The particular initial operation requires **one genuinely held existing healing_balm, 3 real Qi, 4 living Stamina, 90 minutes of real world time and exactly one original engine d20**. The result cannot be selected in parameters. On success, **one Serious wound becomes one residual Light wound**; aftercare remains necessary, with a separate ordinary balm or rest. On ordinary failure the Serious wound remains. On severe failure tissue strain adds an actual Light wound through the existing combat wound record, rather than inventing another invisible HP pool. No extra spell, technique, mana, meridian stage, Soul rank, evolution, immunity, automatic organ or species change is granted. The original 6-day hospital care path is not restricted or made compulsory by this optional high-uncertainty specialist method.

The familiar numbers and first-gate use are **only this personally witnessed initial medical approach**, not a general healing cap, required teacher or tuition fee, universal mandatory Meridian rank or ban on valid herbs, physician methods, spiritual healing or advanced regeneration. Expanding it requires authentic independent anatomical/cosmological world evidence and end-to-end original material and wound-model tests.

A new disposable `tests/specialist-healing-intent.test.js` exercises actual player injuries caused through the canonical combat system, independently observed consent/anatomy, original energy/balm costs, exact uncertainty, residual Light after success, ordinary balm aftercare, live save integrity, forged check/health rejection and narrative log rotation. It is staged separately from #49 until the existing 165-test exact-head Weapon regression completes, so the current CI is not spuriously cancelled. **#16 remains unchecked and unmerged**, and this draft is **not yet tested**. No active campaign save is touched.
