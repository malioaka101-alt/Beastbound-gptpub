# Verified common field poultice crafting — authentic clean medical workspaces

Beastbound supports professions, healing, resource economies and genuine mechanical consequences. The prototype `craft field_poultice` was previously blocked in private play because it took a fixed roll without an independently established clean worksite. This is a **narrow replacement for one known common recipe**, not generic alchemy or unlimited transmutation.

## Source and scene

The existing `engine.js` prototype defines a field poultice recipe of **two Marsh Reed plus one Clean Rations → one Healing Balm**, with **70 in-world minutes**, **Intelligence/Crafting DC 12** and loss of used inputs on failure. The authored Lumenfall Annex (`academy`) has real medical laboratory/clinic facilities; Morrowfen Crossing (`crossing`) already has an authored **infirmary**. The same ordinary known recipe can be prepared at a genuinely clean working surface in either established medical facility. Neither source grants a player an invented private laboratory, magical sterilization or the authority to use unrelated institutional equipment. Other source-scoped benches, kits, recipes and quality controls must be independently established.

A naturally phrased `mode:"act", action:"craft field_poultice"` (also `craft field poultice`) or protected `mode:"rule", system:"craft", operation:"fieldPoultice", params:{}` is now supported **at either of these two real clean medical facilities**, with no current combat/beast threat, no disabling Serious/Critical injury and sufficient Stamina. The operation checks actual saved inventory **before any die**: at least 2 `marsh_reed`, 1 `clean_rations` and 5 Stamina, of which it expends 3. It fixes its own DC, stat, actual materials, worksite, time and standard output; the caller cannot request `DC:1`, free ingredients, a miracle draught or a rare catalyst.

## Committed outcome

The engine first logs the genuine authored clean workplace, original fixed ingredients, recipe, DC and 70-minute work interval as a concrete **witnessed physical event**, then spends the inputs and makes one honest d20 check using the character's recorded Intelligence and **earned Crafting rank ×2**. The real result is persisted with its scene and original check event SHA-256 hashes, time, stock changes and corresponding capped original Practice Points (2 success, 1 failure). On success, exactly **one ordinary Healing Balm** is created. On failure, all spent inputs remain lost and the spoiled poultice cannot be used. The full work interval advances independent world clocks. No second quality roll, free HP healing or unsupported skill advancement occurs.

There is **no arbitrary one-per-day crafting cap**: every distinct batch requires fresh genuine materials, bodily work and one real check. The older first-of-day receipt remains immutable for historical save compatibility. The file-backed chat turn's stable ID prevents rerolls and duplicate stock awards. The read-only `craft.status` displays this public recipe, workspace availability, known supplies and today's attempt state. A player can obtain one genuine marsh reed from a verified successful fen survey and subsequent sustainable `world.harvestReeds`, not a free rare-resource roll. The initial fieldworker character also has two real starter reeds.

This does not imply unrestricted player access to every laboratory, magical potion compounding, individual compound pharmacology, the legality of selling healing balms, actual clean bench employment, food chemistry realism or a complete guild apprenticeship. Source-specific recipes and tool standards are still needed for other trades.
