## What the world already does

Once they actually exist, the **same original** `groupMonth` handles member counts, worker transfers between original incumbent and new group, operational funds, suspension, recovery, reversible relative standing and monthly independent rolls. No player chooses who wins or authorises other people's payroll. A published ordinary field report cannot establish foreign-office authority, hereditary power, a signed contract or a new type of cultivation.

Previously the original independent work journal `w.agentInitiatives.records` rotated to the last 200 reports, and each named professional's `history` to its latest 20 entries. Thus important older original organisational founding, suspension, reopening, competition lead change or reversal could disappear from accessible long-run historical context. This was not a new world decision; it was a short-lived record.

## Durable source continuity, without new actions

The original professional project may now optionally retain `organisationMilestones` for only five kinds of **ALREADY ACTUALLY RECORDED** original source reports: `new-organisation`, `group-suspended`, `group-reopened`, `local-competition-changed` and `local-competition-reversed`. The evidence copies the original full source-sealed report, its original ID, actor, sponsoring existing institution, original regional polity, actual day, original parent/group linkage, original independent numeric context and original delayed release day.

`verifyMilestones(p)` checks the complete original report hash and source, allowed event kind, original group and professional, original chronology, copy hashes and unique references. The creation of a historical copy does **not** draw extra RNG, fund an organisation, transfer members, generate new society, rerun trial work, grant legal office, invent a leader, or modify original successes/failures. The original relative-standing reversal can occur because of a genuine group suspension **even if raw relative member/reputation numbers are still higher**; no fictional majority vote or numerical mandate is claimed.

Old saved games with no `organisationMilestones` remain compatible and are **not** retroactively populated with reports that were pruned; the durable history truthfully begins at the next genuine original recorded milestone. This is not a claim to reconstruct unknown history or every past member's personal consent.
