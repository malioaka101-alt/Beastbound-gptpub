# Real wild-beast ecological learning — foundation for earned nonhuman growth

**Design authority:** Original Unified Master Prompt §§7–12, 18 and 19; Table Rules §§3.8–3.9; standing player clarification that **achievement, aptitude and potential—not age, a minimum number of sessions or years trained—determine progression**. This is the first defensible individual-beast *ecological learning* mechanism, **not** a generic beast-level, magical-technique, combat, evolution or sapient-beast career system.

## Individuals and biology before rankings

The existing `world/wild-lives.js` tracks genuinely **engine-witnessed** non-sapient wild individuals and real **source-backed wild-born** descendants. Their actions are naturally determined by their own physical needs, ecological conditions and independent RNG. A sixday marks a possible opportunity for independent action; **elapsed time never grants expertise**. The new `world/beast-ecological-learning.js` attaches a private, persistent learning record only when such an actual existing animal completes verifiable biological activities. The source binds the creature's ID, species and witnessed-life or proven-birth provenance, and successful practice receipts are chained across checkpoint reloads.

The first two practice capacities are intentionally **ecological, not human-style careers**:

- **Foraging competence:** actual physically available nourishment. An animal must genuinely obtain food, in an area whose food conditions support foraging. The individual's potential and current ecological difficulty determine whether a successful activity provides a new insight. Authentically experienced foragers may eventually become **modestly more efficient** at satisfying hunger from a real later forage: at most **four additional hunger points**, not unlimited energy, resources, healing or guaranteed success. Without food, no learned foraging power creates a meal.
- **Habitat navigation:** a real journey from one location to an **adjacent species-compatible reachable habitat**. Movement may provide an insight, determined by independent aptitude and the actual habitat's pressures. Proven navigation experience can sometimes help an animal **prefer a more viable already-connected habitat** rather than moving blindly, while retaining its own decision and environmental uncertainty. Practice cannot teleport, cross incompatible biomes, open Rift steps, or convey the animal's position to the player.

Original individual potential dimensions (**growth, adaptability, intelligence**) matter, and a genuine newborn's source-backed Origin Pattern can affect its physical aptitude where actually inherited. Unknown parentage is never invented. A newborn cannot automatically acquire adult independent skill; established biological dependence remains relevant.

Each genuine event is assessed separately against ecological challenge and the individual's current demonstrated proficiency, with **harder appropriate checks at higher expertise**. Exceptional results and real potential can make rapid gains, without any minimum age, number of training days, sixday waiting, paid mentor or forced owner. Failures and resting yield no mastery points, and real experience can change survival performance rather than merely decorating a stat card. Duplicate use of the same witnessed physical event is rejected.

The ecological skill bands are **private practical competence**, not beast Level, individual or species Grade, Soul phase, evolution Maturity, universal power magnitude, hereditary social caste, combat rank or a human professional qualification. The engine does not manufacture arbitrary new supernatural techniques or grant an existing species ability better damage for ordinary foraging.

## Ethical and knowledge boundaries

This animal routine specifically **excludes S3+ sapient persons**, owned beasts and current partners **from new wild-only training**. An animal's earlier genuinely earned ecological memories remain valid if its relationship or personhood later changes; they are not erased, but no person or partner may be managed through the independent-wildlife training route. Sapient beasts can form communities, families, schools, invent technology, cultivate and make independent choices; those are separate person-level practices with their own verified society, bodily anatomy, rights and source-backed progression, not a coerced application of animal instincts. The present implementation does **not** claim to have built that broader sapient nonhuman system yet.

The player observes only genuine wildlife behaviour during a real existing encounter. The creature's off-screen learning, exact private potential, routes, aptitude, ability conditions and acquired proficiency remain hidden; player status/history does not expose them by magical remote query. Player dice, partnership, social consent, ownership, wealth, professional ranks and secrets are not altered by wild activity. Player-visible hints should appear only through a later real observation, source-backed inference or witnessed consequence, not an omniscient panel.

## What has and has not been implemented

**Now grounded:** real independent biological behaviour, a source-attested personal skill history, separate ecological skill learning from actually completed forages and compatible movements, limited practical nutritional improvement, individual physical/potential differences, no time-based promotion and private deterministic replay. The verified animal development histories are save-native, not externally generated dossiers.

**Not yet grounded:** learning all species' magical techniques, animal hunting and predation training, general life/death simulation, autonomous S3+ beast societies and professional practice, general neonatal care, new skill-driven beast evolution event checks, complete multi-species ecology, and power-system rank breakthroughs. Extend with **individually tested action mechanisms and source-verified outcomes**, not hard-coded global beasts, guaranteed stages or inventions that work by their names.

Mandatory regression: `tests/beast-ecological-learning.test.js`; no active Aerin or Jory checkpoint edited.
