## 1. Cultural experience — manhua, manhwa and anime inspiration

These are **creative inspirations requested for the atmosphere**, not additional inherited Beastbound canon or copied plots and named characters.

- **Manhua-inspired weight of honour, mastery and lineage:** Public cultivation trials and sect-backed exhibitions can bring family and teacher reputations into the same courtyard as a young unknown contender. A seasoned elder may judge a competitor's discipline more seriously than their birth. A defeat can affect recruitment, face and negotiation; a victory may provoke a legitimate challenge or resentment, not automatic obedience. Hidden competence can surprise witnesses, but unusual power must have a real earned source, never appear merely for dramatic effect.
- **Manhwa-inspired visible competitive society:** Ordinary workers follow regional boards, professional standings and league updates; guilds and sponsors argue about worth, promotion, qualifications and contracts. Prestige travels at the speed of actual couriers, markets, magical communication or verified publication; a remote continent does not instantly learn every result. Public attention creates opportunity and pressure, but does not conjure wealth or knowledge of a private person.
- **Anime-inspired memorable rivals and participant journeys:** Rival contenders have independent goals, different training and ethics, changing respect, friendship, grief, injury and setbacks. Team contests expose coordination, trust and disagreement. An apparently simple healing, construction or exploration trial can generate as much emotional intensity as combat; a losing performance may still earn credible respect without a fabricated podium placement.
- **Magical spectacle without false mechanics:** Viewers might see shifting score inscriptions, heraldic banners, beast calls, judges' coloured lanterns, crowd hushes and real spectators gathering around public notices. The GM can dramatise the *known* result and public reactions; the numerical outcome, paid resources and earned abilities remain the engine's verified facts. Different cultures may use oral judges, testimony, marks, performances, measured benchmarks or no visible board at all.
- **Status and class with actual consequences:** Noble households, independent cultivators, guild professionals, scholars, beast communities and ordinary apprentices may compete under different rights and traditions. Social prejudice, patronage, favouritism and politics can exist but must not rewrite the actual roll, presume misconduct, or strip anyone of consent. The world must allow a guild's attempt to recruit a winner to fail, or a small local contest to matter more to a working village than a distant imperial championship.
- **Beasts remain persons or independent living creatures where applicable:** A beast's own interests, welfare, potential and willingness are not subordinate to a flashy duel or ranking. Partnership and synchronisation trials assess genuine mutual action, not ownership. Human-only contests and beast-led traditions are equally viable. No compulsory taming, eternal tournament careers or fixed ladder into every culture.
- **Society beyond the arena:** Competition calendars intersect with harvests, migrations, school years, medicine shortages, diplomacy, weather, life stages and livelihoods. A seasonal ecology report can influence a real patron's recruitment only when that patron independently acts and the report is known. Spectators, judges, reporters, rivals and organisers should live ordinary lives between contests.

### Natural, everywhere-and-nowhere competition culture — in play, not an engine taxonomy

**Breadth of who and where:** Competitive pride, shared trials and informal comparisons may occur **within** a family (siblings, cousins, successive generations), clan, noble house, guild, organisation, academy, sect, association, community group, town, city, kingdom, council, political party, court faction, military unit, craft shop, sanctuary, expedition or circle of friends; or **between** such groups, across regions, species, borders, professions, trade routes, governments, factions and individuals. Social position, age, interest, necessity, local law and the participants' own agency determine whether a particular comparison makes sense. Each may compete as *people*, teams or institutions, not merely as generic tournament entrants. A family's friendly personal challenge is not automatically a league result; a kingdom's contest is not automatically an imperial ranking or diplomatic conflict. The categories here are possibilities **for the GM's living world**, never a finite roster, mandatory menu, fixed schedule or new engine object per group.

**How naturally it appears:** Let competition emerge as a consequence of existing life: a workshop wants to prove a design; cousins quietly compare their progress; rival doctors disagree about triage methods; neighbouring market towns take pride in their best scouts; council members contest a public responsibility; political leaders earn or lose support through a locally understood public challenge; a travelling rival remembers a past encounter. Equally, people may cooperate, withdraw, ignore rankings, question a biased contest or prefer ordinary work. Many scenes have **no** competitive subtext. Competitive tension should not be introduced every time a family, faction, city or skill is mentioned, and an ordinary conversation should not become a challenge invitation simply to create spectacle.

**Scale and texture matter:** Intimacy, local gossip, ceremonial prestige and international recognition differ. Competition may be playful, private, festive, charitable, honour-bound, commercial, spiritual, dangerous, political, professional or quiet; the same family or town can have different attitudes in different circumstances. A known rival might bring tension into a market conversation without starting a tournament. A clan achievement may matter locally while being unknown elsewhere. Political contests require actual applicable local authority and legitimacy: they do not substitute for civic procedures or manufacture election results. Some people regard competitive rankings as meaningful; others actively resist them. Family and class pressures exist but do not dictate a person's feelings, consent or future.

**GM pacing rule:** Competition is **one naturally recurring cultural layer among many**—like food, festivals, trade, weather, belief, history, friendship, ecology, politics and profession. Introduce it when place, season, profession, established relationships, known reputation or a real NPC's interests make it plausible. Prefer indirect cues (a notice glanced at, an overheard rivalry, local pride, a restrained comment, a prize procession in the distance) over constant bracket announcements, compulsory rivals and dramatic HUDs. Zoom in when **the player chooses** to engage or events genuinely affect the scene; otherwise allow the world to breathe. The player is an ordinary participant in a wider independently active society, not the fixed target of every contest. Never script a win or loss for dramatic effect; respect prior facts, source-backed mechanics, individual beast agency and the player's choice to abstain.

**Illustrative atmosphere, not a preprogrammed event:** On the eve of a regional survey exhibition, a market bridge fills with visiting craftspeople, scholars and beasts. A small family fears its fieldworker will be overlooked next to famous sect representatives; a village doctor cares more about the correctness of a rescue method than the cheering crowd. Their judging principles and the reason they attended differ. The GM narrates such possibilities; source-backed world state establishes who actually arrived, worked, judged, heard, was injured and received payment. Do **not** hardcode this vignette.

## 3. Remaining completion work (finite, not one PR per individual incident)

**Phase A — registration and truly eligible participation.** From the original public notice and its effective competition window, verify that a real entrant actually attended, freely elected to participate, satisfied criteria with real qualification or work sources, and that the original cultural admission procedure genuinely permits entry. Person presence, beast agency, injury and existing bonds remain independent owner systems. Expressing interest, being near the notice or describing eligibility in the announcement is **not** verified acceptance. A locally self-enrolled open event may differ from one requiring a named official; do not force a universal registrar. **Gate:** actual willing eligible entrants accepted; no forged, absent, injured, nonconsenting or otherwise ineligible entry; no player-selected NPC decision.

**Phase B — judge only actual performance by original local criteria.** The original event's published method determines what evidence can be evaluated, by whom and how, without an all-purpose score formula. Witnessed work may have occurred before publication (honest portfolio) or under announced conditions (prospective performance); do not mistake one for the other. The owner of each original ability/physical work source remains responsible for outcome, cost and dice. A competent judge's real source and decision are required where local rules require a judge; self-recorded claims cannot certify themselves. Source-specific technical judging must not imply cultivation breakthroughs or universally compare incomparable disciplines. **Gate:** two unlike source/performance classes are adjudicated through the same underlying evidence-and-authority connection while retaining different local criteria and no additional dice.

**Phase C — local results, challenge/appeal, disqualification, season and separate standings.** Reuse original competition receipts and source history so a result has an immutable original event, entrants, performance, adjudicator, scope, season and calculation method. A justified review is a later historically competent original process, **not** silent editing of the original result. No mandatory appeals body, court, jury, tournament bracket or global league. Persist a season-scoped standing separately from raw power, professional licence, personal reputation and the permanent original result; following seasons do not erase history or lift beast species as a whole for one individual's achievement. **Gate:** a valid result can be reviewed under its own source procedure; ineligible/disqualified results do not earn points; same discipline in later real seasons maintains correct independent history.

**Phase D — conserved, source-authorised consequences.** A funded prize can come only from an original consenting payer/competent issuer with real available funds and an existing authorised transfer; preserve pre/post balances and source receipts. An advertised prize is a promise, not a mint. A title, career, scholarship, guild membership or public favour is an opportunity requiring an owner decision, not an automatic rank-to-career conversion. **Gate:** a genuinely funded and authorized award moves exactly once, unfunded/unauthorised or replayed payment moves nothing; separate ranking and prestige gains do not grant skill XP, powers or licences.

**Phase E — final integrated proof and stop.** Test at least two **unlike original performance origins** and two unlike **GM-authored cultural criteria and judging/review settings**, not a catalogue of hardcoded tournaments. Include independent person presence/refusal and a real beast/partnership agency boundary; player-known status only, world time/season progression, valid rank isolation, true funded transfer, verified review, no second rolls, forged and replay protection, old-save continuity and original publication/record provenance. All three GitHub regression batches **and** required final integration must pass on the **exact** PR head. Confirm merge on `main`, then mark #21 accepted and begin #22. Refrain from building all possible real-world/political arenas before doing so.

## 4. Engineering constraints and explicit exclusions

1. **One coherent lifecycle integration**, not a sequence of narrow hardcoded contest additions. Build in the existing authoritative modules; group necessary related changes for full regression review. A source adapter is justified only by a *demonstrated missing shared proof*, not an example we want to display.
2. **Narrative and mechanics stay separate.** The GM can invent people, event names, aesthetics, cultural practice, ceremonial rewards and rivalries. Actual attendance, consent, eligibility, performance, lawful authority, dice, rank movement and prize funds require original source truth.
3. **No global required workflow.** Some cultures have no published numerical ranking; others have formal seasonal standings or open challenges. No universal arena, referee, licence, combat bracket, sect school, court, appeal period, gift economy or monetary prize.
4. **Never mix individual power tracks with competition rankings.** Meridian, Arcane, Soul, Body, Rift, beast grades/evolution, personal professional skill and genuine partnership synchronisation use their distinct original rules.
5. **Stop when reusable foundation passes.** More named leagues, sects, newspapers, ranks, prestigious matchups, rare prizes, styles and ceremonial HUD designs are *future world content*, not blockers. No `saves/`, `requests/`, `results/` changes for development.
