# Foundation #21 — competition and ranking culture: existing source-owner audit

**Active after #20's bounded acceptance on 13 November 2026.** Authority: the original `reference/Beastbound_Realms_Unified_Master_Prompt.md` §§37–38, `docs/MASTER_PROMPT_DEPTH_CHECKLIST.md`, `docs/PROJECT_CHAT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`, `docs/SYSTEM_CONNECTION_AUDIT.md` and the original GM/engine division. This is a **source audit, not a new event or ranking engine**. #18 remains paused; do not fill its organisational/office gaps by assuming a universal competition council.

## Original foundation criterion

“GM creates events, entrants and qualification sources; engine handles announced criteria, actual eligible performance, judging, appeals, seasons, ranks and rewards.” The original Master Prompt demands many **culturally different** combat, noncombat, professional, creative, ethical, intellectual, team, human, individual-beast and reciprocal-partnership events. It separates standings by discipline, creature, partnership, profession, region and institution. A public ranking is **not** a universal measure of worth, an automatic licence, a power rank or a qualification in unrelated work.

## Genuine shared gap, before any implementation decision

The current playable competition code hardcodes **one original field assessment** and its score formula, date window, registrar and point destination. The other descriptors in `engine.js::COMPETITIONS` are **illustrations**, not proof of independently running tournaments. There is no verified *general connection* through which a GM-authored, jurisdiction/culture-specific **announced event's exact published criteria** can consume actual eligible **different** entrants' independently earned performances, preserve a historically competent source-specific judgment and challenge/review, and transfer **originally funded** consequences into the correct existing separate records. A made-up `judgeScore`, past claim of participation or new string in a ranking object must not create prestige, qualifications, trophies or currency.

**What does not warrant more code:** adding an arena, new event title, fixed event menu, generic contest die, universal global leaderboard, guessed NPC opponent totals, one compulsory judging authority, automatic beast participation, arbitrary score-to-cultivation conversion or a new duplicate wallet/calendar. The GM owns novel competition concepts and cultural traditions. A description may remain unverified until its existing source owners support the claimed mechanical result.

## Narrow next verification, not a commitment to build

1. **Trace** one independent contestant's source (person or unowned beast agency, physical attendance, eligibility), the original announced event criteria, an already executed work/check/performance, the legitimate local adjudicator, source-authored appeal terms and the existing relevant standalone ranking/qualification and funded reward owners. Confirm which links already share usable historical identifiers or provenance and which genuinely do not.
2. **Test the absent invariant on the original engine**, using disposable saves and at least unlike source-authored judging contexts if that is needed to prove true generality. Check that an event's terms *precede* performance; an unqualified or unwilling entrant, forged result, unannounced event, repeated submission, later edited criteria or unfunded prize cannot generate a result. Reject with no partial currency, rank, reputation, RNG, or time mutation.
3. **Reuse a minimal connection** in the original event/score/person/reward owners only if one shared missing connection is demonstrated. Keep source-specific scoring, sanctions and appeal protocols open-ended and culturally different; genuinely independently selected entrants and judges are not puppets of the GM's requested winning outcome.
4. **Acceptance gate:** demonstrate the expandable verified criterion across authentic source origins and seasons, original time and result replay, independent choices, contest/qualification separation, protected consent and real resource conservation. Require the existing exact-head **three regression batches and final integration** before merge and status acceptance. Then move on; do not catalogue competitions.
