**Active after #20's bounded acceptance on 13 November 2026.** Authority: the original `reference/Beastbound_Realms_Unified_Master_Prompt.md` §§37–38, `docs/MASTER_PROMPT_DEPTH_CHECKLIST.md`, `docs/PROJECT_CHAT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`, `docs/SYSTEM_CONNECTION_AUDIT.md` and the original GM/engine division. This is a **source audit, not a new event or ranking engine**. #18 remains paused; do not fill its organisational/office gaps by assuming a universal competition council.

## Original foundation criterion

“GM creates events, entrants and qualification sources; engine handles announced criteria, actual eligible performance, judging, appeals, seasons, ranks and rewards.” The original Master Prompt demands many **culturally different** combat, noncombat, professional, creative, ethical, intellectual, team, human, individual-beast and reciprocal-partnership events. It separates standings by discipline, creature, partnership, profession, region and institution. A public ranking is **not** a universal measure of worth, an automatic licence, a power rank or a qualification in unrelated work.

## Genuine shared gap, before any implementation decision

The current playable competition code hardcodes **one original field assessment** and its score formula, date window, registrar and point destination. The other descriptors in `engine.js::COMPETITIONS` are **illustrations**, not proof of independently running tournaments. There is no verified *general connection* through which a GM-authored, jurisdiction/culture-specific **announced event's exact published criteria** can consume actual eligible **different** entrants' independently earned performances, preserve a historically competent source-specific judgment and challenge/review, and transfer **originally funded** consequences into the correct existing separate records. A made-up `judgeScore`, past claim of participation or new string in a ranking object must not create prestige, qualifications, trophies or currency.

**What does not warrant more code:** adding an arena, new event title, fixed event menu, generic contest die, universal global leaderboard, guessed NPC opponent totals, one compulsory judging authority, automatic beast participation, arbitrary score-to-cultivation conversion or a new duplicate wallet/calendar. The GM owns novel competition concepts and cultural traditions. A description may remain unverified until its existing source owners support the claimed mechanical result.

## Narrow next verification, not a commitment to build

1. **Trace** one independent contestant's source (person or unowned beast agency, physical attendance, eligibility), the original announced event criteria, an already executed work/check/performance, the legitimate local adjudicator, source-authored appeal terms and the existing relevant standalone ranking/qualification and funded reward owners. Confirm which links already share usable historical identifiers or provenance and which genuinely do not.
2. **Test the absent invariant on the original engine**, using disposable saves and at least unlike source-authored judging contexts if that is needed to prove true generality. Check that an event's terms *precede* performance; an unqualified or unwilling entrant, forged result, unannounced event, repeated submission, later edited criteria or unfunded prize cannot generate a result. Reject with no partial currency, rank, reputation, RNG, or time mutation.
3. **Reuse a minimal connection** in the original event/score/person/reward owners only if one shared missing connection is demonstrated. Keep source-specific scoring, sanctions and appeal protocols open-ended and culturally different; genuinely independently selected entrants and judges are not puppets of the GM's requested winning outcome.
4. **Acceptance gate:** demonstrate the expandable verified criterion across authentic source origins and seasons, original time and result replay, independent choices, contest/qualification separation, protected consent and real resource conservation. Require the existing exact-head **three regression batches and final integration** before merge and status acceptance. Then move on; do not catalogue competitions.

## First #21 verified-source seam — actual published terms must predate the source performance

**Narrow implementation under full regression review, not acceptance of #21.** The first defect in the original `rules/field-competition.js` was not a missing tournament label: a correctly shaped `table.competitionNotices` row could be mistaken for a genuine published public notice, and an authentic but *later* notice could retroactively attach its already announced conditions to an earlier report. An additional invented contest would not repair either invariant.

The existing Copperwood guild `competition.notice` now keeps its **original engine-emitted public announcement event**, a stable hash and its exact actual world minute inside the **same existing notice row**. On result judgment and read-only status, the competition module checks that the original registrar and venue, event name, scheduled day, time and announced fee/reward match that source; while still present, the original log witness must match. The original event copy survives legitimate narrative-log rotation. A truly historic old-format notice can still be used when its original event is present in the log; an absent source is **not** an acceptable surrogate. This neither invents an independent registrar's choice nor gives the GM arbitrary event dates.

The actual eligible field report's **source-time must follow the issued notice**, not merely fall within the same sixday. This uses the original resolved survival check and existing ranking/qualification/wallet update, with no second die, new grade, invented rival, player-selected judging score, independent reward or changed contest category. Disposable tests reject a forged complete bulletin, an after-the-performance announcement, a changed archived publication, a shifted timestamp and a missing original event without altering RNG, coins or ranking. They retain an authentic pre-announcement, legacy source and save replay and the ability to read the archived bulletin when short logs rotate.

**The #21 shared gap is still wider than this safeguard.** Current real contest adjudication remains the original single field assessment only. Future event creation, independent entrants and their participation decisions, source-specific scoring and appeals, seasonal separate standings and **actually funded** prizes have not been mechanically supplied by a hash. The original guild fieldwork notice and actual individual practice/physical-work owners must be reused before inventing another list of events. Any actual new mechanic must prove a *shared* missing contract across unlike real performance sources and preserve separately governed skills, welfare, consent, ranking identity and money. #21 stays **active/open**.


## #21 — preserve the original *judgement*, not just the announcement

**Next audited missing shared invariant:** the legacy `competitionReceipts` row and `player.competitionRecord` lacked any original source reference that could demonstrate that the recorded points, qualification, fee and prize corresponded to the already verified terrain check and the announced event. The prototype's general save import verifies broad save structure, not this specific historical relationship. As in real work, payments and civic decisions, a recorded claim must remain distinct from source-verified performance. This is a source-integrity gap in the **existing** owner, not a reason to add another competition type.

Within the original `rules/field-competition.js`, judged results now preserve a source-sealed, **already independently resolved** original check/roll hash, exact original publication source hash, actual world decision minute and the original score, result classification, points, entry fee, pre/post player purse, separate fieldwork ranking and guild reputation. The same existing `table.competitionReceipts` row keeps the original source and hash, while the existing personal `competitionRecord` points back to it; no competing ledger is created. A read-only verification recomputes the judge's deterministic **existing** category thresholds and checks the preserved original roll's real source, original field check, original publication, one-use receipt and actual record. It detects later edited judgment data or a separately falsified ranking receipt, survives ordinary narrative log rotation and works after save export/import. It neither consumes a second roll nor changes the established scoring, rewards or existing world time. Pre-existing old-format awards remain **legacy reports**, not retroactively signed or silently upgraded.

**Boundary:** verifying an existing player's historical fee/prize delta is **not** proof that the issuing institution had an independently funded prize purse. Existing provisional event reward constants are unchanged, and future genuinely funded prizes must connect to an original actual owner balance and conserve that transfer. This additional integrity step does **not** establish independent rivals' attendance, voluntary registration, different cultural score criteria, lawful appeals or multi-season standings. Those remain the larger *shared* missing foundation. Do not build a new judicial, economic or universal competition subsystem merely to add another example; #21 remains open until those cross-source links are genuinely verified.

**Test evidence (pending full exact-head CI/merge):** `tests/field-competition.test.js` now includes tampering with the original performance hash, result source seal, adjudicated score, paid fee accounting, report use and source-linked personal record; it also checks unmodified ranking/currency and RNG on failed reads, rotating narrative logs, old historical award formats and export/import. Full regression and merge are required before this is recorded as verified implementation.
