# BEASTBOUND REALMS — THE STARTING WORLD
## A common-knowledge world for the beginning of every story

**Status:** authored and source-linked public setting layer, 14 November 2026. It sits **inside the original Beastbound world**. It is neither a separate realm, a new beginner-only campaign, a miniature substitute for the six-continent setting, nor a fixed starting location.

The **Starting World** is the world an ordinary person might have grown up hearing about: names on maps, the major kingdoms and other states, familiar houses and guilds, schoolbook history, the most discussed forms of cultivation, beast families from markets and field guides, public festivals, dangers, professions and disputes. It supplies recognizable common references **before** the GM introduces a unique character and unique opening. The foundations continue simulating an older, much deeper world beneath those familiar names.

> **The central distinction:** common knowledge is not omniscience. Not every person knows every continent, state or school. An ordinary local resident generally knows their neighbours and home polity more intimately than a distant kingdom. A caravan guide may know roads that a court scholar does not. A scholar may know meridian theory without having opened a single Gate. Someone can have heard of a beast family without ever seeing or correctly identifying an individual. Public claims about legendary bloodlines are *not verified activated powers*.

### What already exists underneath this layer

The original authored Atlas contains **six continents, 24 polities, numerous regions, public traditions, 48 institutions, nineteen trade routes, and major historical periods**. The established households include **28 historically chartered families and clans**, in addition to original new associations and living people. The original bestiary contains a large **generative species capacity**, not hundreds of thousands of individually witnessed or hand-written species. All human disciplines retain their separate progression mechanics. The public Starting World *references those same owners*; it does not seed a second country, lineage, economy, population, magic track or calendar.

The first physically connected, already playable local engine map remains **Morrowfen Crossing, Copperwood Verge, Whisperfen Wetlands, Ashwind Foothills, Lumenfall Annex and Riftglass Outpost**. Other countries are fully established world-scale polities, but a public map does **not** imply that every distant city currently has mechanically traversable streets or verified local NPC encounters.

## 1. The world that ordinary people recognize

### The six continents

| Continent | Public impression | What ordinary tales emphasize |
|---|---|---|
| **Valedryn** | Wet valleys, orchard crowns, mountains and harbour leagues | Canal rights, trade in grain and medicine, guild surveys, wilderness sanctuaries |
| **Sahriel** | Aquifer deserts, sandstone roads and monsoon gardens | The sanctity of shared wells, caravans, desert patrols, pollinator orchards |
| **Threnor** | Fjords, northern forests, ice roads and auroral observatories | Winter rescue, migration covenants, archives and disagreements over hunting grounds |
| **Kareth** | Basalt forge cities, hanging terraces and guarded boundary lands | Labour craft, rift safety, volcanic engineering and contested resource permits |
| **Islarin** | Maritime assemblies, reef settlements and storm islands | Inter-island trade, sea-person rights, reef care and typhoon shelters |
| **Oruvan** | Open grasslands, plateau settlements and multi-species courts | Grazing passage, independent cultivation, herd councils and border disputes |

The world has no single capital, world emperor, universal law or all-powerful beast-taming guild. Even well-known countries differ radically in constitutions, language, beast rights and everyday custom.

### The most familiar countries and public seats

These are **actual established countries** from the original Atlas; the Starting World does not invent extra crowns.

**Valedryn**

- **Reedmarch Compact** — *Morrowfen Crossing*. A canal and sanctuary council society whose ordinary argument is how ferry rights, food, flood protection and beast-migration routes can coexist. The March Council is a civic body, not the Naturalists Guild.
- **Asterwyn Crown** — *Bright Orchard*. A monarchy associated with orchard estates, relief obligations and court precedence. Even a celebrated court family is not automatically the ruling officeholder or the source of a player's bloodline.
- **Crownspine Holds** — *Stonecairn*. Highland confederation, cliff rescue and protected nesting grounds. Stone routes and monastic training are known without automatically granting a martial technique.
- **Mirror Gulf League** — *Saltglass*. Merchant republic and multilingual port culture known for arbitration, seaworthy craft and legal debates about nonhuman personhood.

**Sahriel**

- **Sahri Well Covenant** — *Isset Wells*. A water council in which access to shared wells is a public concern rather than the property of whoever is strongest.
- **Red Dune Sultanate** — *Marqesh*. A hereditary court and desert military society, publicly associated with patrols and restrictions on wild capture.
- **Copper Stair Charter** — *Stepsend*. Caravan guild federation, route beacons, travel agreements and rescue debts.
- **Pearl Bight Republic** — *Palmwake*. Monsoon orchards, medicine and pollinator sanctuaries with agricultural land-use arguments.

**Threnor**

- **Whitewake Jarldoms** — *Skelvik*. Coastal confederation known for cold-water rescue and maritime treaties.
- **Icehollow Monastery Lands** — *High Lantern*. Monastic council, auroral astronomy and migration records.
- **Winterwood Commons** — *Cedarwatch*. Forest councils balancing timber and den routes, with practical communal winter shelter customs.
- **Frostmarch Protectorate** — *Coldgate*. Frontier administration and emergency permits whose security policy does not erase ordinary civil protections.

**Kareth**

- **Free Forges of Kareth** — *Anvil Harbor*. A guild republic, renowned for craft schools and labour disputes in basalt industry.
- **Sky Terrace Union** — *Featherstep*. Suspended farms, aerie access and cliff rescue in a federated community.
- **Ashglass March** — *Sealwall*. A guarded rift frontier with containment laws and evacuation obligations.
- **Sunforge Principality** — *Goldflame*. Princely court, solar ore and strategic species protections. Famous family affinity stories are not verified spells.

**Islarin**

- **Tide Assembly** — *Waveroot*. Island assembly famous for seafaring and a public voice for independent sea peoples.
- **Reef Cantons** — *Living Atoll*. A **beast polity** whose citizens possess civic rights; its own constitutional order is not a human court costume.
- **Stormpeak Freeholds** — *Rainspire*. Storm harbours, protected flying-beast corridors and weather-oriented training.
- **Blackcurrent Harbours** — *Deep Chain*. Merchant league, cargo contracts, shipwrights and visible pressure for labour-law reform.

**Oruvan**

- **Grassroad Confederacy** — *Tallgrass Moot*. Pastoral confederation with negotiated seasonal routes and recognition of sapient herd leadership.
- **Skyvillage League** — *Windfall*. Highland communal republic and independently practising cultivators.
- **Iron Citadel Dominion** — *Redwall*. Military oligarchy, ore roads and contested resource claims, not a universal human military ranking system.
- **Old Tracks Beast Commonwealth** — *Many-Nest*. Another independent **beast polity**, defending cross-species personhood and the right not to be compelled into a pact.

A person may know these names from maps, merchants or songs yet know little of the current ministers, military posture, private archives, exact regional law, shortages, treaties or household relationships. Those are the living original world systems' responsibility.

Family names are **public cultural references**, not a compulsory class structure or a promise of inheritance. These 14 names are drawn from the already-authored families; many other named and emergent families exist.

| **First Cup Custodian Kin** (Sahri Wells) | Well care and water ledgers | Shared access versus trader pressure |
| **Pathlight Caravan Clan** (Copper Stair) | Route beacons and rescue obligations | Escort costs versus open roads |

The public world includes titled families, ordinary shop households, professional lineages, elected service kin, merchant clans, chosen-kin councils and independent people with **no** major family name. A person's surname cannot force an NPC to acknowledge kinship, lend money, teach forbidden theory, join a marriage, open an office or grant a magical constitution. Families contain independently acting individuals whose relationships and duties can change over time.

At **Morrowfen Crossing**, residents commonly recognize Reedward, Fenwake, Marshglass and the Reed Clan Families. Their chartered civic duties and public elders are local familiarity, while private genealogies and power claims remain separate evidence.

## 3. Well-known guilds, academies and public organizations

An ordinary person recognizes institutions by **what they do**, not by a universal tier. The Starting World includes public recognition of the Naturalists Guild, Lumenfall Annex, the March Council, Orchard College, Gate Monastery, Saltglass Exchange, Well Collegium, Route Keepers, Ice Rescue Brotherhood, Forge University, Ashglass Rift Wardens, Tide Assembly House, Reef Consistory, Herd Council and the independent beast parliament.

These are examples from a larger existing institutional network. Apprentices, self-taught researchers, veteran travellers, retired professionals, rulers, elders, unbonded craftspeople and sapient-beast delegates can all exist outside these organizations. A famous academy need not admit the player, demand a standard fee or provide the right teacher. An organization's public brief is not proof of today's staffing, resources or internal decisions.

Work commonly includes naturalist surveys, boat repair, animal welfare, sanctuary service, healer assistance, route maintenance, smithcraft, agriculture, cartography, law, ecological record-keeping, safe containment, beast translation and ordinary commerce. A job has actual duties, time and resources when genuinely undertaken.

## 4. What everyone has heard about the different power systems

There is **no universal cultivation ladder**. A stranger may know the names below while understanding little of their actual mechanisms.

1. **Meridian Cultivation:** Qi runs through living circulation anatomy; the **108 Gates**, **12 Primary Meridians**, **8 Extraordinary Meridians** and **3 Dantian centres** are familiar terms in medical and martial theory. Opening a Gate is a real structural achievement, not an automatic generic power level. Meridian stages I–IX follow their own logic.
2. **Arcane Circle Architecture:** Mages stabilize a Mana Core and construct Circles with formulae, geometry and energetic costs. First Circle, Second Circle and higher architectural concepts are recognized, but nobody gains a spell by being near a tower. Arcane progress is **not** Meridian progress.
3. **Soul Cultivation and Soul Engineering:** Spiritual perception, identity protection, soul-thread work and consensual resonance. The seven Soul phases are separate from spell circles. No ordinary soul user can casually read minds, own another's soul or raise the dead.
4. **Body Refinement:** Whole-body cycles develop muscle and tendon, marrow, organs, senses, blood and more. A dedicated body refiner may be capable without any bonded beast or mana circle.
5. **Beast-Origin and Lineage Arts:** Scholars, healers and families discuss inherited and integrated beast traits, the Origin Pattern, rejection and adaptation. A rumour of dragon blood is not proof of an expressed ability. Consent and safe sourcing matter.
6. **Rift Practice:** Surveyors know about boundary pressure, rift sensing and anchor marks; more advanced spatial practices are a matter of serious expertise and danger, not a publicly available shortcut.
7. **Specialist arts:** Runes, formations, alchemy, magical biology, medicine, weapon schools, healing and field crafts develop on their own original materials, skills and research.
8. **Beast partnership and synchronisation:** This is **not a human discipline**. A beast's consent and personal development remain independent; partnership techniques arise through earned cooperation and compatibility.

**Human evolution** is another layer. Bodies, minds, souls and supernatural structures may change through compatible development; discipline progress, technique mastery, human evolution and inherited or acquired traits remain four separate things. Likewise, **beast grades, individual potential, growth and evolution** are not just human Circles renamed.

Some cultures celebrate teachers and academies; others favour family instruction, the field, independent research or rites. None is a universal price gate. Public stories about high stages describe possibilities, **not** abilities granted to starting characters.

## 5. Commonly recognized beastkind

There are familiar creature **families**, individual species, regional forms and very different minds. Popular ecology guides mention reedfoxes, miregrazers, flinthares, glasswings, echoquills, cinderskins, brineclaws, sunspines, frost-antlers, skystriders, reef polyps, sporecaps, vinehearts and moonmoths.

In the already reachable Reedmarch districts, familiar **common creature names** from the original engine include the **Lanternfen Fox**, **Flint-Ear Hare**, **Glasswing Skimmer** and **Mirehorn Grazer**. Their names and rough ecological roles may be printed in a village guide. Nobody should infer that an individual is present, its powers are confirmed, its evolution is available, or that it will accept an approach.

Beasts can be grazers, predators, pollinators, scavengers, plants, fungi, reef builders, spirits, constructed forms or fully sapient persons. Species biology matters; each has its own habitat, food, organs, social behaviours and possible evolutionary histories. Sapient-beast communities have independent cultural norms and their rights cannot be replaced by human taming customs.

Well-known stories of rare beasts are not reliable field identifications. High-grade legends, hidden evolution branches, protected territories, an unknown individual's disposition and secret techniques belong to the actual world, not to every character's starting knowledge.

## 6. The lives behind the grand names

Most people get up, cook, repair their gear, work a shift, care for someone, ask for assistance, negotiate a price, enjoy a festival or return home. Someone can be impressive in their craft without being a duelist. A beast may never bond anyone and still have a complex life. A family may be important in one town and unknown three valleys away.

Across the continents, the public festival calendar includes **Lantern Drifting**, **First Rain**, **Ice Farewell**, **Tide Parliament**, **High Wind**, **Council of Tracks** and **Cooling Night**. Festivals can create genuine trade, remembrance, technical demonstrations, political discussion and friendly competition. A listed festival does not mean a current event or public prize has already been scheduled in an individual campaign.

Merchants commonly speak of grain, herb, salt, ore, glass, feed, boats and medicines along the established trade routes. The exact stock, safe passage, ownership and offered price are *not* knowable from these traditional trade associations. Similarly, a posted law or famous magistrate is not universal authority across the sea.

Communities compare achievements at many scales: two apprentices, rival ferry crews, city workshops, neighboring houses, travelling scholars, beast-people assemblies and countries. Ranking is culturally important but **never constantly forced** and does not reduce all achievements to the same number.

### Schoolbook historical eras

The **Age of First Paths** associates the earliest roads with rifts and migratory beast routes. The **Estuary Wars** are remembered through the Seven Estuary Treaty and subsequent passage-rights arguments. The **Ash Years** mark a Kareth transition from collapsed volcanic imperial rule to forge-city politics. The **Longwinter Covenant** is remembered for northern rescue and recognition of migrating communities. In the **Current Century**, trade, ecological rights, origin-pattern research and institutional conflict continue reshaping public life.

These are broad historical anchors from the Atlas. An individual family, cultural group or archaeologist may disagree over motives and meaning. The original causal-history and research systems, not this schoolbook primer, decide what can actually be proved.

## 7. An ordinary beginning: the Reedmarch example

**Morrowfen Crossing** is an especially well documented example of what common-knowledge play looks like, **not** the compulsory birthplace or starting position of every character.

A traveller approaching the canal markets might recognize the Naturalists Guild office, ordinary ferry livelihoods and the public sanctuary argument before knowing any guild official personally. They might have heard of House Fenwake's levee obligations and Reedward crossing craft. The name Lumenfall Annex may be familiar to an apprentice physician. Whisperfen, Copperwood, Ashwind and Riftglass are already authored connected regions whose safety, creatures and materials remain dependent on actual visits and live evidence.

Someone raised in the Red Dune Sultanate, Reef Cantons or Skyvillage League should instead learn **their own** local culture first. The GM can introduce that country as the lived-in personal starting world while the broader six-continent map remains real. New unique local scenes still require original GM placement and sourcing; the existing six playable locations are not automatically the whole world.

## 8. How the Starting World works in the engine

This dossier is narrative-facing common lore. The executable companion **`world/starting-world.js`** uses the original Atlas, `world/social-houses.js`, `world/bestiary.js`, original public government and existing beast/species definitions. It **reads** established data without writing a second world state, spending time, rolling dice or awarding knowledge skills.

Public inspection is available as **`world.startingWorld({topic})`** where topic is one of:

| Topic | What it exposes |
|---|---|
| `overview` | Six continents, well-known countries and houses, recognizable systems and beast names |
| `continents` | Common map geography and broad historical associations |
| `polities` | The full established 24-country public gazetteer, recognized political forms and traditional trade routes |
| `families` | Named publicly famous house charters and civic obligations, *not* private persons or inheritance |
| `institutions` | Major publicly recognized organizations and the work they are known for |
| `systems` | Human disciplines, specialist arts and **separate** human-beast partnerships |
| `beasts` | Familiar lineages and common named beasts, not secret powers or direct sightings |
| `life` | Ordinary professions, customs and festival traditions |
| `history` | The established broad historical eras |
| `local` | The **actually established current region**: culture, known local families, institutions and public office |

An in-chat reader can also ask **“starting world”**, **“common knowledge”**, **“world kingdoms”**, **“famous families”**, **“power systems”**, **“common beasts”**, **“world history”**, or **“local common knowledge”** without consuming a turn. The GM may read the general world primer before writing a character's first scene. If that scene has not yet been authored, the engine must **not** misrepresent its temporary technical save location as the player's home.

### What this layer never does

- It never grants affinity, beast identification, technique mastery, life history, a noble connection, faction membership, a prophecy or a starting reward.
- It never creates a new sovereign, new family, generic NPC, guaranteed mentor, encounter or secretly universal cultural rule.
- It never replaces world ticks, independent NPC/beast choices, publication delays, current diplomacy, finance, medicine or actual physical work.
- It does not force the player to begin in Reedmarch, the capital of a famous kingdom, a school, a tournament or a beast-taming tutorial.
- It never claims that all human or nonhuman cultures know *exactly* the same things.

The **Starting World is familiar, stable and usable**. Beyond it, the world is not finished or exhaustively catalogued: the GM and the original source-verified systems continue to develop new places, families, species, careers, discoveries and conflicts naturally through play.
