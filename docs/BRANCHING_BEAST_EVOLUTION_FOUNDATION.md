# Open branching evolution — foundation #06

**Canon:** Unified Master Prompt §§8, 10 and 18; the original `rules/progress.js` registration, maturity, conditions, biological form application, irreversible evolution event and consent authority are **not replaced**.

## GM-authored alternate futures

A GM may author a *possible* species-specific form for a particular original beast with `evolution.authorBranch`. It records the original individual, species and current stage, a named potential branch, anatomical changes, organ/ability limits, a physiological source, actual maturity and non-time prerequisites, targeted ecology, metabolic event costs and incompatible alternative IDs. Plans can be rare, ordinary, hidden, magical or otherwise genuinely supported by the original form and rules; the examples are **illustrations only**, not a species roster or preset transformation menu.

The plan is attached as sealed provenance to the existing `beast.evolutionBranchAssessments[branchId]`, then `evolution.register` installs it into the pre-existing `s.table.evolution[beastId/branchId]` track. **Potential is not a granted form.** No time advances, individual beast appears, skill is learned, assent is supplied or event die is rolled by simply authoring or registering a path.

## Actual evidence, growth, consent and outcomes

Successful **original** `evolution.signs` recognition of a witnessed, source-checked individual biological scene may disclose a previously sealed branch through the existing `s.table.evolutionDiscoveries` privacy rules. A failed recognition does not identify a secret future. The `evolution.branchWebStatus` read view lists only known possibilities, never hidden grades, true readiness, unvisited paths or future rolls.

Original `evolution.advance`, Ripening, Pattern Echo, Crucible, `evolution.event` and the pre-existing consent and Grade rules remain authoritative. Added branch validation checks the committed source-sealed plan, source species and stage, actual regional suitability, actual presence and the animal's own available energy/condition **before any event roll**. No invented GM success, user-selected modifier, instant level, guaranteed consent or bypass of known prerequisites.

Only **after** the real evolution resolver emits a documented attempted result does the creature spend its precommitted energy and condition cost. Failures retain the original stalled, illness, physical complication and branch-closure logic; newly added source receipts preserve the event's actual result. A successful transformation changes anatomy, organs, energy, abilities and biological needs **through the original form application** and closes other pre-existing alternatives for the old form. It does not close forms authored for the newly evolved stage or force a sapient creature to accept a rite.

## Ecological continuation inside one world

The evolved individual retains one `b.evolutionEcology` consequence record linked to the original branch and the **real** regional `s.table.realm.ecosystems` at the event. Later original six-day world ticks examine the creature's own actual recorded location and precommitted habitat tolerances. An unsuited region or damaged habitat can impose modest actual condition/fatigue strain, with dated source-linked history. Fit habitat imposes no invented harm. No population index is adjusted from one beast, no movement or new species encounter is fabricated, and the owner of an animal is not given control over its independent choices.

**Current scope:** This is an expandable, verified-by-suite-when-complete foundation for an authored *possible form → genuine discovery → existing event → physical/ecological consequences*. It is not full creature generation, universal magical technique execution, evolution for unknown unregistered species, cross-species reproduction, free-form organ engineering, or automatic ecological succession. More mechanisms may be attached when genuinely supported by new source facts and the original Master Prompt.
