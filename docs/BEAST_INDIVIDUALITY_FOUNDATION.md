# Beast-specific lives and earned techniques — foundation #05

**Canon:** Original Unified Master Prompt §§7–9, 13–16 and 43, later open-ended GM/engine boundary. Existing owners are the original `s.beasts[beastId]` individual record, `world/wild-lives.js` first-contact and history, `world/beast-ecological-learning.js` source-verified independent ecology practice, and `world/sapient-beast-persons.js` for independent S3+ persons. This is **not another bestiary, species roster, animal creator, pet training menu, or narrative generator**.

## GM-authored originality on an actual individual

The GM can establish a memorable sensory habit, distinctive marking, voice, recurring social response or other personally witnessed detail through `world.beastIndividualObserve`, using an existing individual beast ID and one authentic witnessed life or person-contact record. The observation category and descriptive content are open-ended rather than chosen from a personality list. A direct player-observed statement must still concern a presently witnessed individual; GM-only interpretation and previously established secret notes remain private.

This extends the pre-existing `individualLife` or `sapientLife` record as `individuality`, retaining original source identity and separate append-only receipts. It does not choose the beast's thoughts, attitude, affiliations, anatomy, contract, personality, grade, level or destiny.

## Techniques come from earned individual experience

An **actual non-sapient wild individual** may develop a distinct way of doing a previously source-verified task. The original `beast-ecological-learning` successful receipt is required before `world.beastTechniqueRecord` accepts an original name, biological principle, situation, activation, limitations, counters, environment, energy cost and recovery days. The original event must belong to **that individual**, not merely its species, family, a trainer's claim or an arbitrary player check.

Two current actual bridges demonstrate the mechanism: foraging and existing-source navigation. Their exact names and expressive interpretation are GM-authored without a fixed technique roster. A later different genuinely successful action can reinforce a personally learned method via `world.beastTechniqueDevelop`, with historical source evidence and without duplicated XP. The original ecological proficiency still belongs exclusively to the existing learning ledger.

When the wild individual **independently chooses** an established compatible field action, the existing world tick can let it exercise a developed technique using its private RNG and original ongoing individual history. Usage spends its actual personal energy, respects cooldown, records an individual source receipt and has only a small bounded consequence in the original hunger/fatigue tracks. Real rest can restore modest energy. Neither the GM nor the player selects an animal's roll, cooperation, ability use or hidden schedule.

No technique is inserted as a free combat attack or magical effect. An original learned field method is **not** an invented supernatural spell, evolution, synchronisation skill, human rank or unlimited technique copy. Bigger effects require the relevant original physiology and already existing combat, energy, source and agency adjudication.

## Individual persons remain people

For sapient creatures, GM-authored personally witnessed individuality attaches to their already existing `sapientLife` contact and personhood. Animal ecological practice is not projected onto them, and their independent profession-like practice and decisions remain governed by the existing sapient-person evidence engine. Higher cognitive behaviour, relationships, teaching and voluntary partnership need real decisions and consent. Private motives are never shown just because a person has a character record.

## Integration and limits

- `world.beastIndividualStatus` is a **read-only player-known** view, never a live locator or offscreen progression dashboard.
- Source hashes and original life verification protect identities, observation origins, learning proof and method uses through campaign save/reload and exact replay.
- No active campaign or historical Aerin/Jory save is edited. Disposable tests create source-attested beasts, their distinctive traces, earned technique receipts, later real world use, and sapient contacts.
- The record is extensible with more categories and methods but current **automatically executable** ecological applications are deliberately narrow. It does not simulate all future magical techniques, cross-species senses, advanced cognition or personalised combat arts without verified existing power/technique rules.
- The complete regression inventory includes `tests/beast-individuality.test.js`. Do **not** check #05 or merge the branch until full CI succeeds on its exact latest head.
