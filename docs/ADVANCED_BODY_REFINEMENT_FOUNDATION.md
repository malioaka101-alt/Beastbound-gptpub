# Foundation #13 — advanced Body Refinement and transformed physiques

**Authority:** Master Prompt §§23, 28–30 and established human biological/health records. This is private foundation work, not character progression in an active campaign.

## Current draft: original Nerve–Sense Cycle

`rules/body-nerve-sense.js` adds an initial **original earned Organ Cycle III → individually mapped Nerve–Sense** route within the existing `s.player.body.cycles`, `s.table.bodyRefinement`, combat wound model and original stamina pool. This path is a **first tested example**, not a mandate that every body tradition follow the same order: the Master Prompt explicitly permits other physically compatible schools to refine cycles differently, repeat cycles and specialise their own tissues. Other verifiable anatomical orders will need matching real sources and eligibility/adapters rather than forged prior stage labels.

The GM can author **any genuinely supportable number of independently observed neural tissues/regions**, rather than filling a fixed two-to-eight list: e.g. peripheral sensation, motor nerve response, autonomic integration and later-discovered vestibular function. The initial two-region minimum only prevents a supposed *whole-body* training map from resting on one isolated observation. Each region needs its own observed tissue and function, actual metabolic/fatigue boundary, injury risk, recovery method and independent test. Naming an adaptation grants no new beast sense, reflex technique, immunity, automatic combat action or human-evolution stage.

**Organic development of the same saved physiology:** `body.nerveExtend` appends newly and independently witnessed tissue regions to the original signed neural map **before its whole-body completion**. The engine reconstructs the chronology of the initial map, newly observed structures, paid physical practice and clinical repair. Existing earned progress and original rolls remain intact; newly mapped tissues enter with zero earned integration and must be demonstrated normally. An old observation, duplicated tissue key or altered extension receipt is rejected. A *completed* Cycle IV is not retroactively rewritten by tacking a new organ onto the old certificate; later specialisation needs new genuine evidence in the appropriate existing physiology/practice system. This is the first concrete create → develop → preserve loop, not a separate universal mutation menu.

`body.nerveMap` witnesses anatomy without awarding rank; `body.nerveExtend` records a later independently witnessed discovery without erasing history; `body.nervePractice` consumes real Stamina and time, resolves actual engine checks, earns independently logged tissue integration or physical injury/strain; `body.nerveRepair` requires a separately diagnosed event and a real independent check. A distinct witnessed whole-body proof in `body.nerveComplete` is required before the physical original `Nerve-Sense Cycle` is recorded. Success adds only a small, **actually earned +1 maximum Stamina** through original existing player/combatant resources, **without refilling current Stamina**, while making the source identity and formed anatomy available to later genuinely learned specialist techniques.

The original `Disciplines.organEarned` accepts later compatible cycles without invalidating its source-confirmed original Cycle III history and physical benefits. The private turn route exposes read-only `body.nerveStatus` without advancing time or changing a checkpoint.

**Blood–Essence**, **Adaptive Physique** and unique **Evolved Physique** remain **unfinished**. They must use real blood/energy sources, compatible organism functions, metabolic load, tissue ecology, physical strain and independently earned expression. Stable distinctive human constitutions need reproducible safe maintenance and reversion where physiologically possible, without wiping wounds or inventing biological source/lineage. Source-grounded alternate refinement orders remain a high-priority openness requirement.

The disposable `tests/body-advanced-nerve-sense.test.js` earns Organ III through the actual engine and checks an initially nine-region Nerve–Sense map, a tenth genuinely witnessed region added after real practice, continued integrated work without replaying the original roll, real physical costs, earlier Cycle III proof survival, no free cross-system powers, amendment tamper detection and read-only private replay. It is first in the complete regression inventory. **#13 remains unchecked, draft and unmerged until the full foundation and exact-head full regression have been verified.** No campaign save has been edited.

## Continuing the same body: Blood–Essence V, Adaptive VI, Evolved VII (draft)

The previous separately source-earned Nerve–Sense IV remains the only authority for neural anatomy. A single additional **Body Refinement family adapter**, `rules/body-living-physiques.js`, extends the same `s.player.body.cycles`, `s.table.bodyRefinement`, original Organ III and original health/Stamina. It does not create another body level or universal progression engine.

- **Blood–Essence V:** GM-witnessed original blood circulation and energetically compatible tissue transport, with individually established metabolism and failure limits. Mere mention of an inherited trait is not a donated lineage, a beast power or a free Origin Pattern.
- **Adaptive Physique VI:** GM-witnessed original response to a specific environment, pressure/fatigue tradeoffs and reversibility. A verified source can then support a **short, temporary physical expression** with actual d20, stamina cost, observed safe duration, genuine voluntary reversion and automatic expiry derived from existing world time. No elemental immunity or condition-specific mechanical combat benefit exists unless separately witnessed and implemented.
- **Evolved Physique VII:** a personally coherent, independently tested integrated constitution requiring real verified original Nerve–Sense, Blood–Essence and Adaptive records. A set of names is insufficient. The completed record persists the actual original supporting proof hashes rather than granting a parallel human-evolution stage.

**Flexible development:** Blood V and Adaptive VI can be completed **before or after** separately earned Nerve IV if the authentic individual anatomy supports it. The current proven examples are *not* a fixed catalogue of schools, unique blood essences or environments. Original GM observations provide the form; the engine tracks source, individual tissues, metabolic limit, health, personal effort, serious failures, care, formation and persisted factual consequences. One shared adjudicator resolves V–VII, rather than a different rule for every authored tissue.

**Limits still outstanding:** active expressions are presently safely time-bounded and evidenced; the adapter does **not** yet provide bespoke resistance/combat/medicine handlers, continuous feeding and metabolism for a stable permanent physique, independent sustained biological reproduction, all plausible alternate/repeat tissue histories, or unbounded automatic power invention. Those require actual corresponding physical and causal adapters. All effects beyond the verified existing Stamina gain remain unavailable mechanically. Do not substitute the fictional effect name for a successful check or wound proof.

Disposable `tests/body-living-physiques.test.js` exercises a source-earned Organ III → Adaptive VI → Blood V → separately earned Neural IV → fully proven Evolved VII, retains original source evidence, and checks time-limited activated physiology, reversible states, cessation and altered-save refusal. This test is included in the **full existing regression inventory**. Keep #13 unchecked until exact-head full CI confirms the current branch and missing systems are addressed.

## Verified-life design, new maintenance slice (draft)

**Original §23:** a completed Evolved Physique is an individual's *unique permanent constitution*, not a temporary timed buff or a second Human Evolution stage. Biological maintenance is a distinct **current-condition** observation: it may be supported, overdue for reassessment, injured or individually restored without erasing earned anatomy because a calendar advanced. The canonical health/wound/character records remain authoritative.

`body.physiqueSustain` is a source-constrained **ordinary-food maintenance example**, integrated directly into `rules/body-living-physiques.js` and existing `rules/unified.js` material conservation. It requires earned verified VII, a separately witnessed original physiological demand, compatible genuine **owned clean rations**, original Stamina, real time and an engine-owned check; even failure consumes the provisions and work. A successful check records an *independently observed, source-bound period* of demonstrated constitutional functioning. When real world minutes pass its observed limit, `body.physiqueStatus.constitutionMaintenance.currentlyObserved` becomes false **without deleting the structural Cycle VII or silently inflicting a wound**. New clinical examination may re-establish evidence. The player cannot choose a free support result, compel an NPC to sustain them, or reroll a prior witnessed care event. The save verifier checks individual source, original physical consumption receipt, one d20, witness non-reuse and chronological care history. Read-only status never advances time.

**Do not generalize one diet into a universal progression gate.** Clean rations are only a presently verified material for an ordinary, independently observed human food route. Future diets, alternative metabolism, clinically established support, elemental or origin-derived biology must use actual previously witnessed nutritional/energetic sources and the same resource/evidence systems. A written phrase like *I absorb starlight* is not itself an energy source; conversely, once a genuine distinct physiology exists, the engine must not force it into one merchant's ration menu. A successful maintenance check proves currently supported bodily function, **not** special resistance, a Soul or Mana advantage, immortality, healing or automatic reversion of a permanent constitution.

The integration regression now asserts genuine original physical food inventory movement, stamina/time, no duplicate roll, current-condition observation expiry, permanent earned anatomy, tampering refusal and private read-only saved status. Full current-head CI verification and broader constitutional upkeep remain necessary.

## Individual anatomy continues after the canonical cycle (draft)

The canonical seven Body Refinement cycles measure *whole-body formation*, not a maximum seven-item technique catalogue. `body.physiqueTissueMap`, `body.physiqueTissueWork` and `body.physiqueTissueCare` now append to **each existing V–VII living cycle's own anatomy** rather than minting another rank or copying a separate character system. Newly discovered tissues remain possible **before or after whole-cycle formation**. No universal tissue inventory or numerical roster cap exists.

Each GM-authorised original physical tissue uses a unique **individual** identifier, actual observed tissue, independently observed function, compatible metabolic support and original injury limit, with a separate witnessed clinical countertest tied to that exact tissue identity. Work on a tissue consumes the character's **original Stamina**, advances **the original world clock**, and resolves the actual engine d20. Recorded effort accumulates per individual tissue without one-time unlock; severe mistakes contribute to its persistent strain and may cause **real injuries via the existing wound model**, not fictional numbers. Further legitimate independently witnessed physical care may reduce experimental tissue strain, but cannot erase the character's unrelated Serious/Critical wounds, refund paid materials, fabricate immunity, increase a power stage or clone a beast trait. Source proofs bind the specific tissue to its actual canonical cycle and original clinical records, preserve every previous outcome and reject replay of the same experiment.

**The tests' examples are not rules defining the world's content:** one original blood pathway, one individual adaptive pathway and eleven distinct evolved-constitution tissue observations are used to prove new additions and unbounded *catalogue growth* after formation. Blood pathway work then repeats with a new original source to show continued adaptation rather than a frozen first discovery. The original neural cycle continues to use its already existing `body.nerveExtend` and independent source verifier; no replacement or parallel neural biology system was introduced.

**Remaining limitations:** there is currently no generic safe way for a new witnessed tissue to grant an arbitrary individual gameplay ability. Its actual effect on combat, environmental resistance, clinical medicine, heredity, wound capacity or originating Beast Pattern must be separately implemented and proved in the corresponding **existing** engine system with original sources, risks, resources and rights. Canonical cycles and demonstrated individual tissue history should not be confused with trait acquisition. Full exact-head regression must still confirm this new extension before #13 can be checked.

## First genuinely earned live physiology bridge: original footing checks (draft)

The individual-tissue ledger now has a **source-constrained mechanical application**, not just a collection of descriptive notes. An existing local terrain check, created through `core.prepareSceneCheck` and resolved once through `core.check`, owns the real scene, difficulty, skill, time, roll and ordinary result. A separately observed and already genuinely trained Blood, Adaptive or Evolved tissue (at least two earned progress, without serious experimental strain) can support **one** specific original *footing* check through `body.physiqueTissueFooting`. The GM must establish an individually witnessed mechanical contribution and countertest at the actual place, linked to the precommitted check ID; natural-language claims cannot choose a bonus or change the existing DC.

This operation **does not roll** and does not manufacture a new terrain opportunity. Only when the actual prepared footing action is resolved does `rules/scene-check.js` consult the existing shared body record, apply the engine's single bounded **+1** source-specific physical modifier and spend **one real Stamina**. The same original one-use d20 resolves the terrain outcome and ordinary earned practice. The source-checked receipt includes exact tissue, scene, original roll, physical cost and check ID; it is saved with the corresponding individual tissue. Multiple tissues cannot stack on the same check, and replay of the original challenge is refused. Failed terrain checks still use up the physical effort. Unrelated reading, perception, combat, training, persuasion and medical actions gain **no bonus** from this adapter.

This deliberately limited executable example proves the *connection pattern*, not a fixed catalogue of future physiology. Other actual source-supported uses—reflexes, breathing, weather adaptation, injury tolerance, regeneration, inherited characteristics, or full transformations—must connect to their own **existing** action, risk, anatomy, materials and consent/harm rules. No tissue name, generic master-prompt description or numerical work total automatically constitutes a learned technique. No species traits, soul/mana senses or unlimited resistance are awarded. Broader context coverage and exact-head regression verification remain outstanding while #13 is unchecked.
