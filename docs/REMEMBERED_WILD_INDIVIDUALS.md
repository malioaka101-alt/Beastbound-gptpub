# Remembered wild individuals — genuine local re-encounters

The original Unified Master Prompt §§7, 11, 13–16, 41, 43 and 54 requires **living, independently choosing individual beasts**. A player's previous encounter is a genuine history, not a script forcing friendship or a permanent GPS tag. This adds a narrow but actually playable reunion route on top of `docs/INDIVIDUAL_WILD_BEAST_LIVES.md`.

## Player memory, not omniscience

`world.wildMemoriesStatus({})` shows only **previously watched individuals**, their recorded identity key, last *personally witnessed* location and day, and the visible behaviour from their watch. It does not show the beast's current unobserved location, private personality/needs, individual grade, future evolution or survival. The status is read-only, including file-backed chat turns. It does not invent species knowledge for a failed identification or assume that naming a remembered individual establishes consent.

A freeform search is possible through `world.seekKnownWildBeast({beastId})`, or the natural-language `track beast <id>` / `track animal <id>` commands. It requires a source-verified, formerly watched real wild individual, a currently visitable wildlife habitat, enough stamina and no unresolved confrontation. It does **not** require a named master, a paid licence, membership, a compulsory course or a generic one-day wait after a departed animal. Searches consume **45 actual in-world minutes** and **3 Stamina**, even if the creature no longer lives there. The player's existing Wisdom and Survival determine a normal engine d20 tracking check against the actual local ecological conditions. No caller can supply the result, DC, location, animal reaction or special bonus.

After the real field time and appropriate world ticks, the engine checks the beast's **actually persisted current habitat** and whether it is independently available. Only if it really lives in that local site, the genuine tracking check succeeds and its separate individual choice permits an encounter can the same creature appear again. A favourable player roll never compels the creature to be found. Threat, injury, severe distress, capture and actual moves can preclude a reunion. An inconclusive search says only that no reunion was confirmed. **It does not disclose an absent animal's whereabouts.** Searching a region in which the creature is not present does not advance its independent behaviour RNG or change its future off-screen decisions.

A real reunion preserves the **same persistent animal ID, individuality, lineage, needs and history**. It records a new witnessed encounter with an independently drawn local separation and the creature's current observable mood. That current encounter may be watched through the existing `world.watchWildBeast` system, and the animal still decides whether to forage, watch, rest, reposition or leave. This is **not** automatically an animal's trust, identification, consent, legal contract, loyalty, Grade examination, evolution route, XP or level.

## What is not yet implemented

This is a first return-and-tracking loop for **already genuinely encountered, non-sapient, unowned wildlife**. Sapient beasts need the dignity, negotiation and independent motivations of full persons. The starting location and species lists remain playable example coverage, not a census or prohibition on other life; actual travel corridors and the original world's ecology are the limits of this tested slice. General wildlife search, reinspection of identified species, open-ended footprint evidence, long-term scent trails, new species discoveries, adoption, breeding, generations, mortality and advanced evolution still require source-verifiable world interactions. The player remains free to choose any other activity, skill, discipline or relationship path without being railroaded into tracking.

## Verification and privacy

`tests/wild-reencounters.test.js` proves private source-backed memory views, forged-input refusal without side effects, absent-wildlife searches that expend real effort but do not move the absent creature's RNG, independent success/refusal outcomes even for a powerful tracker, continued unique beast identity, honest witnessed reunion, preservation of separate evolution/bond ledgers, and private file-backed idempotence. The test suite uses disposable local game states, never a real campaign checkpoint. Merge only after the full private GitHub workflow has passed.
