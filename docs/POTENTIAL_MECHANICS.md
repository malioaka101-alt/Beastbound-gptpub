# Potential mechanics — aptitude without destiny
The player's own fourteen generated potential values are visible through
`character.status`, without an expensive in-world examination. Unknown
other people's potential still needs a genuine assessment, evidence or their
choice to share it.

Each score is 1–9 and has a **bounded check modifier**:
1–2: −1; 3–4: +0; 5–6: +1; 7–9: +2.

Potential adjusts checks in the relevant discipline or specialisation;
where supported, a successful study session also gains extra progress equal
to the positive aptitude bonus. The original dice are rolled exactly once,
and the old material, stamina, quality, consent, and learned-knowledge
requirements are unchanged. A low score is not a power stage cap, an
absolute aptitude limit, an automatic failure or a prohibition on learning
more than one discipline.

The actual source of the score is the seeded
`player.capabilityProfile.potential` owned by the player character.
NPC or beast scores are not inferred from a player score. No new dice are
consumed to display potential.

**Compatibility and evolution** retain requirements based on actual anatomy,
witnessed conditions, source rights and biological load. Innate aptitude can
make progress harder, but must never make a valid attainable form or stage
mathematically impossible solely because of a potential ceiling.

This design does not automatically award stages, techniques, skills, beast
contracts, special ancestry, or biological adaptations.
