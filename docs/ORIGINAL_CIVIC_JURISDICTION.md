**Canonical basis:** Beastbound Unified Master Prompt §§33–36, the original open-ended Atlas of 24 evolving polities, `world/government-offices.js`, real independently submitted `world/civic-petitions.js` and `world/public-hearings.js` records, original dated local country governments and original complete `docs/MASTER_PROMPT_DEPTH_CHECKLIST.md`. **This is a narrow source-attested foundation, not a completed universal legal system or a new hardcoded list of possible laws.**

The GM continues to author worlds, political motives, rules, organisations, laws and consequences naturally in context, subject to the original Master Prompt and established actual worlds. The source-checked engine must distinguish *an actual public official whose office has jurisdiction* from an academy head, an influential family, a wealthy resident, a group coordinator or a fabricated monarch. The original government-office registry already preserves independently determined nonhereditary public mandate changes and truthful existing holder anchors for old saves.

## Connected original evidence

`Gov.jurisdictionSnapshot(w,polity,regionId,day)` uses the real original Atlas region's polity, an existing public civil body, current competent public office and actual audited mandate number. It works with all **24 already established polities** without pretending that the existing March Council clerk controls them all. It rejects unrecognised foreign regions, nonexistent territory, unsupported past mandates and a court under unsupported powers.

Today the only *actual original independently reviewed civic source providers* are the **Reedmarch** ordinary citizen petition counter and the **Reedmarch** scheduled public hearings. The new `Gov.attestOriginalCivicProcedure(s,w,{kind,witnessId})` reads the original player witness, the original independently completed sixday civic review, the original actual public docket and the actual officer/mandate/region **itself**. The caller cannot supply a desired decision, ruler, territory, law, penalty, opinion, court vote or treasury debit.

The existing `world/civic-petitions.js` and `world/public-hearings.js` now call this only **after their original independent world review and real docket already exist**, without a second roll or simulated political consequence. The office's existing persistent `w.governance.reedmarch` holds an append-only, source-sealed `civicProcedures` history containing the actual original witnessed claim hash, real original independent review source hash, historical incumbent, mandate sequence, actual jurisdiction, original decision and authentic public-release delay. Old saves with no such record do **not** invent historic hearings, regulations or past votes. Later elections cannot retroactively replace the recorded prior officer; historical source validity is checked even after actual original mandate changes.

**A citizen petition is an unverified claim.** Even when docketed, it neither changes law nor finds anyone guilty. An actual independently reviewed hearing may order a records review, invite voluntary mediation or defer; it does not override local property/rights, compel a beast or resident, grant universal policing authority, authorise taxation, enact statute or punish people. The ledger stores private GM/world source provenance; player-facing news remains subject to the original notices and personal knowledge.

The authority adapter is *extensible*: future personally and legally sourced jurisdictions, elected regional bodies, actual court rulings, enforcement disputes and legitimate foreign instruments should reuse this original public-office and historical-source mechanism when independently built and verified. Do not assume every government has a single universal legal hierarchy, that a family member holds the same office as a head of state, or that every kind of power is licensed by the March Council. The starting public-review providers are examples and currently proven points of attachment, not an exhaustive list of permissible actions, factions, cultures, disputes or institutions.

## Foundation #20 follow-through: original public records review

The order is anchored to the historical officeholder/mandate, original hearing source, original constitutional procedure and the actual Reedmarch household public charters; a citizen cannot request an invented ruling or bind absent parties by naming one. Its authority is strictly institutional: the council can direct its **own public filing and charter inspection**, not punish, fine, seize assets, force witnesses or enact a law.

At the next **actual later sixday tick**, when the original council is active and has sufficient existing administrative capacity, the same docket records a source-hashed inspection of the previously filed resident hearing and already-public charter material. A later dated local public notice is released through the original chronicle; `social.hearingStatus` may expose it only after the corresponding public release. This does not fabricate wrongdoing or private records, and leaves older saved hearings with no recorded administrative order untouched. The two genuinely different published issue cases in `tests/civic-original-records-remedy.test.js` exercise actual independent council decisions, public-realm privacy, old-save compatibility, source-hash tampering, no second ruling and real subsequent world time.

## #20 — public protection, actual local standing and allegations stay distinct

`world/government-offices.js::localRightsContext` reuses the **same** original atlas/public governing-body records at the currently witnessed physical region. It does not guess a local statute from species, rewrite the polity's rights into a universal legal catalogue, or claim that reading a charter enacts legislation. Existing publicly authored `world/atlas.js` `rights` text is the only source returned, bound to its actual polity and region; different polities keep their own descriptions and authorities. This is a **read-only original public/evidence view** accessible as `world.localRightsContext`, not an enactment, offence decision or police action.

An optional `beastId` can be associated only when an **individually encountered, currently present, genuine sapient person** already has an authentic `world/sapient-beast-persons.js` life and origin witness. It never makes a species someone's property, a bond, agreement, interpreted shared language, legal guilt or enforceable judicial classification. A personally witnessed free person's actual ability to decline remains with the existing beast agency systems. An optional `petitionId` checks the player's **actual originally filed local March Council complaint** via `world/civic-petitions.js::sourceCheck`; the complaint remains an allegation, and the existing independently decided reply is visible only when the resident has actually collected it. These two context types cannot be conflated into an invented legal claim about a third party.

The disposable `tests/civic-local-rights-and-sapient-standing.test.js` covers physically reached differing polity charters, actual council filing and withheld reply, authentic nonhuman encounter and later departure, forged or remote sources, no new law/compulsion/currency/RNG, and export/import stability. No campaign, private consent or gameplay ledger is modified simply by asking about the law. **#20 remains OPEN:** enacted jurisdiction-specific law, offence adjudication, enforceable remedies and appeals require further genuine competent sources.

## #20 — original local legislation is distinct from advice or a hearing

A **publicly authored local civic charter** can now have an explicit, freeform `legalCompetence` source with an original authority statement, actual decision procedure, **its own required assent count**, territorial scope, and minimum public-notice interval. These descriptions belong to the **already-established independent beast polity's original civic institution** in `world/beast-civic-life.js`. An arbitrary council name, a drafted charter, an ordinary advisory hearing, a plaintiff's allegation, or a procedural March Council response do **not** become enforceable law.

The previously source-verified `world.beastCivicProceeding` may attach an **actual, personally witnessed enactment** only after authentic independently encountered sapient participants attend the legitimate local council and **the charter's source-established number of named persons separately and freely assent** under that institution's previously recorded procedure. Each actual participant's own recorded position and basis must be present; no one can use an absent person or invent assent. The ordinance's actual region must belong to its own polity, the original venue and historical meeting are source-attested, and its publication date must follow the charter's original minimum notice period. It may define an open-ended original conduct rule, affected rights and limitations, a bounded available remedy and an avenue for response or appeal. These are **authored legal texts**, not a universal menu of preset laws or fixed species restrictions.

No new law ledger is created: original legislative competence stays in the institution's **original charter**, and the source-sealed enactment stays in its **existing, append-only civic proceedings**, under the existing sovereign country. The read-only local `world.beastCivicStatus` reveals genuinely public original rules only after the effective notice date. Existing unamended pre-#20 charters and hearings keep their historical meaning and do not retrospectively acquire lawmaking power. A text mentioning an available remedy cannot seize inventory, levy a fine, impose a contract, create a crime, convict an accused, compel an intelligent beast, or bind another polity without a **separately competent, source-backed case decision and enforcement**.

The independent source tests use two unlike authored local conduct rules, two charters with **different legitimate approval thresholds** (including a recorded dissent), and their own publication and appeal language, real source-witnessed sapient council members, actual travel into the polity, negative cases for fabricated assent/foreign territory/duplicate or retroactive rule, original participant levels, ownership and player money unchanged, immutable original source history, old charters, and export/import replay. The new local-legislation regression is also required in the existing final integration gate.

**Foundation #20 remains open.** A genuine local rule with authored available remedies is not evidence that any specific person broke it, that an eligible court heard their answer, or that a sanction/restitution was legitimately decided. The next connection must bind actual independent case evidence, historically competent adjudicators, opposition/appeal and only proven remedies, with no invented guilt.
