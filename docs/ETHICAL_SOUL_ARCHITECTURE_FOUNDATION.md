# Foundation #12 — Soul higher phases and ethical engineering

**Canon:** Unified Master Prompt §22 and original Table Rules. Soul Cultivation is growth of one's own spiritual stability and identity. Soul Engineering is technical use of learned spiritual structures for ethical medical care, wards, communication, compatible consensual imprints and contracts. **Soul training alone does not read other minds, force contracts, create sentient constructs, erase a person's memory or resurrect the dead.** Established independent people and sapient beasts have separate personhood, privacy, law, religion and consent.

## Current draft bridge — earned Phase III Resonance Lattice

The verified original `rules/soul-threading.js` produces Phase II by real individual `mind`, `body`, `resonance` and integrated soul-thread work. The new `rules/soul-resonance-lattice.js` adds a Phase III ledger at **`s.table.soulArchitecture.lattice`**, without creating a new power stat. It requires the original Phase II `threadProof`, prior reconstructed thread-work hash, old Soul Perception history, actual self-resonance witnesses and a conscious character at an established place.

- `soul.latticeMap` records GM-authored personal resonance geometry, reversible identity boundary, interference, communication limitations, practical recovery method and one genuinely witnessed own-soul response. Recording a plan does not grant a phase, contract, memory manipulation or tool.
- `soul.latticePractice` performs an actual engine check with real Soul Reserve, Stamina and time. The personal structure earns progress or experiences source-recorded interference. Severe failures produce real Soul Stability loss and existing wound consequences. No prescribed style, NPC teacher, tuition or minimum calendar gate.
- `soul.latticeRepair` uses a **new original diagnosis** and a real separate check, paid reserve and physical effort to recover some actual structural strain. A design title cannot erase injuries.
- `soul.latticeComplete` requires earned personal practice and a separately witnessed stable standing-wave / identity-boundary countertest, with an independent engine check. Success raises the existing Soul phase to **III** and uses the established `Build.soulProfile(3)` for Soul Reserve capacity and perception range. It **does not refill current Soul Reserve**.
- `soul.latticeStatus` is read-only and private. The original Phase II threading concentration remains earned and usable after advancing to Phase III. Provenance includes individual witness hashes, actual check rolls, training costs, structural progress, independent formation, and source-preserving save/replay.

These mechanics establish one practitioner's **spiritual architecture**. Phase III patterns may eventually support communication, compatible contracts, memory wards and energy exchange only when a **separate effect action** has genuine participants, an original witnessed origin, real sources, informed ongoing consent, ability to refuse or revoke, and an honest engine outcome. No readout of NPC secrets or automatic spirit-bound item is produced by the phase alone.

## Individually earned Soul IV–VII structures (current draft)

`rules/soul-higher-architecture.js` extends **the same** `s.table.soulArchitecture.higher.records` and existing `s.player.soul` Reserve, Stability, Perception and Phase. There is no parallel soul currency, externally awarded rank, canonical school or fixed teacher fee. The practitioner must already have independently earned prior Phase I, II and III sources. Each subsequent Phase has a distinctive **source-witnessed** original structure, a physically observed safety countertest, real practice checks and Soul Reserve/Stamina/time costs. The GM may freely author the actual geometry, technical explanation, setbacks and social tradition, provided it is compatible with established facts.

- **IV — Soul Forge:** real **non-sentient** source-compatible vessel matrix and local material/energy conditions, explicitly excluding a trapped person's soul, automatic consciousness or an unearned spiritual organ.
- **V — Inner Sanctuary:** individually witnessed interior identity boundary, self-consent and controllable privacy, without memories transferred from third parties.
- **VI — Soul Domain:** individually measured bounded personal spiritual range, separately observed external counters and collapsible self-domain, without automatic mindreading, coercion or territorial claims.
- **VII — Living Soul Architecture:** individually observed connected feedback and explicit maintenance failure/identity limits. Reaching the Phase does not automatically create a permanent self-sustaining ward or grant indefinite life.

Each earned formation requires a separate new original Soul functioning test after authentic structural work, and derives new Soul Reserve and perception capacity from the original `Build.soulProfile` **without refilling current Reserve**. Structural interference is source-recorded, can reduce actual Soul Stability and cause real injuries via the existing health model, and cannot be undone by editing a stage label. Original witnessed repair spends actual Soul Reserve and Stamina and resolves its own independent check.

Crucial source rule: original **Wisdom at the actual breakthrough is sealed on the transition receipt**. Later attributes may legitimately change because of development, health, ageing or conditions; historic earned anatomy cannot be retroactively invalidated by the person's current Wisdom. Current Wisdom does affect **new** checks. Earlier Phase II concentration and Soul Perception remain legitimately earned through later phases.

## Ethical Soul applications now executable in draft

`rules/soul-ethical-applications.js` adds only **bounded, personally owned** applications under `s.table.soulArchitecture.ethicalApplications`:

- `soul.selfStabilize` (at least Phase IV): a source-witnessed real Soul Stability deficit can be treated through an independently resolved check, with actual Soul Reserve/Stamina/time. It restores at most two points on success and cannot invent trauma, heal a different person, rewrite memory or raise the dead. Severe failure can cause one additional point of strain.
- `soul.selfImprint` / `soul.selfImprintRevoke` (at least Phase V): a practitioner can try to preserve **their own witnessed story memory** after an original scene, informed self-consent and privacy check. A genuine d20 result and energy/time costs determine whether it forms. The same person may always revoke their own active imprint; historical source and revoked status remain in the save. These actions cannot access other minds or fabricate a third party's consent.
- `soul.domainPulse` (at least Phase VI): a source-witnessed measured, short, locally contestable personal Soul field, with original domain-range limit, real Reserve/Stamina/time, independent check and possible collapse. It does **not** automatically influence NPCs or beasts or extend private perception into other minds.
- `soul.ethicalStatus` is genuinely read-only and returns only the character's personally known imprints, self-healing counts and bounded domain attempts.

These are genuine effects on self-care, self-owned personal imprints and limited source-observed domain response, not automatically awarded target effects. **Communication with another person, material soul contracts, medical treatment of another, consented imprints shared with another, advanced spiritual devices and restorative repair of severe soul destruction** still need distinct participant sources, informed ongoing consent, legally appropriate context, an actual resolver and failure consequences. Do not treat high Phase as a blank cheque to override them.
