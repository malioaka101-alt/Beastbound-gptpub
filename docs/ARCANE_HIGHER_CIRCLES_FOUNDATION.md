# Foundation #11 — source-earned Arcane architecture and authored spell formulae

**Original source:** Unified Master Prompt §21; the same original Mana Core, Circle I, Circle II dual-channel and Circle III matrix sources remain authoritative. There is no universal teacher, school fee, mandatory sixday period, preset spell menu or independent artificial character level.

## Implemented first bridge: Fourth Circle Dynamic Architecture (draft)

`rules/arcane-higher-architecture.js` requires the **genuinely earned original Circle III source chain** and its stable recorded geometry, including the historically proven First and Second Circles and actual matrix check receipts. Later dynamic changes do not rewrite the Third Circle witness or artificially elevate a Circle counter.

`arcane.dynamicMap` registers an original GM-witnessed fourth return geometry grounded in the existing matrix, measured switching, feedback, load, interference, collapse mode, limitations and observed independent countertest. No Circle or spell is awarded merely for a description.

`arcane.dynamicWork` requires each original distinct physical practice witness, actual Mana and Stamina, engine-resolved checks and elapsed world time. Success earns a bounded structural-integrity track. Severe failures strain Core stability and the separate dynamic channel; the old original Third Circle matrix remains intact. `arcane.dynamicRepair` uses an independently witnessed diagnosis, actual Mana/Stamina/time and its own engine check; it never silently corrects an unstable Core for free. `arcane.dynamicForm` separately tests functioning adaptive switching after earned practice and, on a real success, applies **the same `Build.arcaneProfile(4)`** to channels, Mana capacity, intake, load tolerance and prepared capacity without refilling current Mana or granting a spell.

Actual source checks, costs, witness snapshots, integrity, repairs, stage-formation evidence and capacity changes are sealed for save/replay validation. `arcane.dynamicStatus` exposes only the individual's original recorded state.

## Independent original spell grammar (draft)

`rules/arcane-source-formulas.js` creates authorable spells through `Build.spellCost()`, **not** a replacement engine. The existing grammar determines family, range, area, duration, magnitude, conditions, targets, speed, complexity, minimum Circle, casting difficulty, Mana cost and channel load. The formula must fit the actually earned architecture, available channels and capacity. World-circuit claims cannot invoke unearned Circle IX sources.

The GM records an actually observed original formula principle, geometry, expected limit, measured counter and interference. The design is a source record, not a learned spell. `arcane.formulaWork` earns training from distinct witnesses through real checks, Mana, Stamina and time. Successful or failed `arcane.formulaProbe` independently tests the formula under the exact original spell-grammar Mana budget, with measured casting DC and interference. A severe experimental problem can be repaired only through separate witnessed diagnostic work and real costs. The experimental record can become **validated as a formula**, not automatically licensed as an offensive spell, a permanent enchantment, a summoned creature or an effect on an unconsenting target. Actual spell effects need their own source-specific adapters and independent combat or physical target checks.

All new action and read-only status routes extend the existing `rules/index.js`, `scripts/chat-turn.js`, `rules/power-overview.js` and dual-channel system. They do not alter a live campaign.
