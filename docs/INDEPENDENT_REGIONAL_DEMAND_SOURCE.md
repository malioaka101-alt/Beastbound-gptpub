## This isolated batch

\`world/independent-regional-demand.js\` adds **one optional, source-sealed regional demand observation**, not an alternate economy engine. It is attached to an actual independently living person in \`w.emergentPeople.people\` who independently continues ordinary life, is actually present on a locally mapped original sixday shift, and chooses whether to post a public expression of interest in an existing, locally produced good. The source retains that real person's origin, stated work purpose, region/culture/language, preexisting job identity, genuine physical shift witness, actual ecology at that decision and a replayable individual-specific willingness decision.

- The choice is a **separate independent, source-derived person-bound decision**, not a second roll for earlier original work and not a new draw from the player's, world's, or professional's existing RNG. Neither authored scene, shop quotation nor player action can choose the outcome or reroll that one original world-sixday decision.
- Good candidates come from the established original *current regional* goods, grounded in the actual person's place, living purpose and local environmental situation; this is an **example of open-ended GM-supported economic behaviour, not a catalogue of permitted careers, languages, species, goods or tastes**.
- Both genuine expression and genuine refusal are retained privately; the market-facing local economy view exposes **only an actual publicly posted expression, only after its original publication day, only while current, and only while the player is physically in that exact original region**. Other regions, private refusals, invented requests, imaginary economic identities, or individual cash balances stay hidden. Older valid checkpoints have no added historical requests.
- This is **real interest in possible trade, NOT a sale, physically available buyer cash, guarantee of an offer, movement of goods, transfer of Scales, labour agreement, production result or obligated contract**. An ordinary request cannot alter merchant stock, physically consume regional forage, mint money or force the seller to quote. The existing original signed purchase workflow alone moves stock and currency.
- The ledger uses the **engine's actual sixday tick counter**, not a naive absolute-day modulo, so the five Concord Days and later calendar anchors cannot fabricate time-bound resident decisions. It is optional on older saves, bounded in history, identity- and presence-sealed and checked against the original authored atlas and independently lived person.
- Existing \`world.publicEconomy\` retains the original *local* commodity indices and adds \`locallyPostedGoodsInterest\` for only source-known local published expressions. Foreign economies remain published background goods but do not leak private buyer names, decisions, secret demand or unverified prices.
