# First Muscle-Tendon Cycle — genuine physical combat consequence

**Master Prompt authority:** §§17, 19, 23, 29, 45–46, 49, 51. Body Refinement is a separate, purely human-capable power system: a practitioner does **not** need beast ownership, a teacher, a universal fee, a calendar age gate or a spell to use already-earned physical development.

An individual who has genuinely completed **Body Refinement Cycle I — Muscle-Tendon** using the existing real discipline breakthrough gains its previously authored **M1 natural physical force**, alongside the stamina and Might changes already implemented at breakthrough. A real source-dated Cycle I history, the actual character's body constitution record and the combatant's earned physical Offense Magnitude must agree before that force is expressed in an existing `strength`-based mundane combat action.

`rules/player-combat-action.js` verifies the original equipped and personally recorded mundane strike as before. Its actual Offense Magnitude is the **maximum** of the authentic item's magnitude and the earned M1 natural physical force, **not their sum**. A good M2 weapon does not become M3 merely because a body refiner is physically stronger. Dexterity-based techniques still use their own documented mechanics. The existing wound model resolves the genuine attack die, defender and damage; the player cannot supply additional Damage, Offense Magnitude, strength, discipline progress or the opponent's plan. When a genuine M1 body force is used, the combat result carries a player-visible witnessed note. No extra strike, bonus skill rank, unsupported weapon, new energy pool or unrelated Qi/Mana effect is granted.

Importantly, an old or inconsistent body claim that is not supported by recorded successful personal breakthrough and current body anatomy **does not receive this additional M1 force, but does not prevent ordinary equipped attacks**. This protects free gameplay and keeps the system compatible with older saves. Human and beast advancement remain separate.

**Remaining limits:** Bone-Marrow/Organ/Nerve/Blood-Essence/Adaptive/Evolved Physique cycles, complete injury tolerance, physical exertion and detailed muscle/tendon biomechanics, unarmed source-attested combat actions and noncombat strength demonstrations require separate verified rules. This pass does not declare the full Body system complete.

`tests/combat-player-attack.test.js` uses disposable synthetic characters, the actual source-backed first-body breakthrough check, an equipped source-referenced staff, authentic wound combat, opposing decision and checkpoint replay. It verifies no invented M from forged body counters, no additive scale stacking and no extra dice. The entire 97-file suite remains mandatory under the private single-runner check.
