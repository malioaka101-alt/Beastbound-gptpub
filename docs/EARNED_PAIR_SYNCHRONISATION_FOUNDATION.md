# Foundation #09 — earned, distinct human–beast synchronisation

**Original canon:** Unified Master Prompt §16. Synchronisation has ten **separate** human–beast partnership stages, each potentially novice, stable or mastered. It is not human cultivation, beast Grade, beast Level, Soul Phase, evolution, magic storage, ownership, affection or contract depth. The GM remains free to author unusual signals, exercises, pair techniques, body plans, cultures, settings and combinations. Specific observed means and physiology determine whether an effect can actually happen.

## Extend the existing reciprocal bond

`rules/bond-synchronisation.js` attaches `syncPractice` directly to the **one original** `s.table.bondRecords[beastId]`, retaining its pre-existing `syncStage`, `syncPoints`, `syncState`, trust, respect, resonance, compatibility, communication and coordination. The bond cannot be summoned through a method name: there must be a real present pre-existing consenting partner, with source-classified sapience, living welfare, safe physical condition and sufficient real stamina. An established social arrangement under foundation #08 is not automatically a mutual magical pairing.

`bond.syncPractice` accepts only the known partner ID and an independently witnessed original shared exercise event. The scene must name **both distinct people** and their actual tasks, setting and safety boundaries. It cannot specify the check result, success, bonus, DC or beast assent. The individual beast's private source-stable agency can refuse before a human roll; actual adult sapient persons additionally require verified independent personal life and informed consent, and S2 Protected Minds require separately sourced preferences and guardian consent. Refusal uses real interaction time and modest human preparation/fatigue costs but grants no training points.

Genuine accepted attempts expend separate human Stamina, beast fatigue and, for stages involving actual energy exchange, **the beast's own available energy**. The original engine then performs one check and records its actual degree. Only a successful genuinely applied exercise earns sync practice points. Existing numeric readiness thresholds remain from `rules/bonds.js`, and advanced stage-specific requirements use original witnessed proofs (e.g. genuine energy recognition, a locally real medium, suitable Soul Stability, an individual evolution history or an exceptional joint event). No arbitrary teacher, fee, sixday count, age or mandatory number of sessions. An exceptional valid original success can advance promptly. Skill points alone cannot bypass an actual partner decision, missing medium or incompatible body.

`bond.syncPractice({focus:'refine'})` improves the already earned current stage from **novice → stable → mastered** through its own successfully earned practice, without preventing advancement to another stage for lack of a calendar delay. The stage and its depth are separate from every individual power system. A successful stage promotion persists under the original `syncStage` and mirrors an already established beast field for compatibility.

## Pair-specific method design: no free powers

Stage 5 unlocks a source-verified `bond.syncDesign` description only when that precise human really learned the named underlying technique using its **existing provider**, and the **current beast species** really has the particular innate ability. Each method records its pair identity, actual separate source hashes, witnessed design context, limits and distinctive principle. A name, Grade, genre, ordinary affection or a player's chosen spell cannot grant an unearned new magical phenomenon.

`bond.syncDemonstrate` supports a deliberately limited **noncombat** co-ordination exercise based on that exact saved design, independent fresh willingness, a new original witnessed scene, a real check and separate costs (human Stamina, beast energy and fatigue). It produces no copy of the other person's spell, damage, automatic defence, healing, evolution, Soul breakthrough or general-purpose shared buff. More advanced Stage 6–10 manifestations and body-altering abilities need future source-specific adapters for their real biological, spiritual and power costs, including independent consequences. The ten named stages remain available as earned targets; describing an advanced method does not implement its gameplay effect.

## Source integrity and replay

A sealed receipt chain on the original bond tracks original witness snapshots, single-use attempts, separately observed decisions, actual roll evidence, spent costs, earned points, stage transitions, stage refinement and pair-specific method demonstrations. An individual witnessed scene cannot be re-rolled under a new description. Original history may be archived with linked head hashes, not a lifetime achievement cap. Read-only `bond.syncExperienceStatus` shows only known reciprocal partner facts and explicit readiness; unknown beast identities have a neutral unknown response.

**Test:** `tests/earned-pair-synchronisation.test.js` uses disposable authentic starter partner history; verifies real staged source evidence, independent beast decisions, original bond thresholds, true stamina/energy costs, stage promotion and refinement, no stolen human/animal ranks, repeated-scene rejection, source tampering, exact-turn read-only and no automatic link, plus an original genuinely earned human Arcane technique paired with an actually encountered source-observed individual beast. No live campaign saves are touched.

**Sequential checklist #09 remains unchecked until the exact-head full regression is green and merged.** This foundation does not claim all high-stage effects are executable merely because stages have gained authentic records.
