# Actual voluntary family mediation after a public hearing

## Canon and positive social freedom

The original Beastbound Master Prompt (notably its government, lineage and independent-citizen sections) demands living clans, varied states, individual choice, differing social power, real consequences and equal agency for sapient people and beasts. The Table Rules prohibit the GM from writing the player's wishes, changing an NPC's decision to please the player, manufacturing relations, or treating something an NPC could not know as common knowledge. **No original source requires a resident to become a noble or pay a family to discuss civic problems.** This patch therefore expands already implemented March Council hearings and existing named local households rather than imposing a universal political rank menu.

This specific mediation workflow, deadlines, sixday correspondence and elder participation are **limited NEW Reedmarch civic worldbuilding**. They are *not* claims that the Master Prompt already authored a particular mediation law. Existing laws, sanctuary rights and property are unaffected until further actually supported proceedings.

## Four genuine actions

The existing `social.hearing` allows an ordinary resident to put **their own words** into the March Council record on Workday 4, 10:00–16:00. If the **independent** later public council disposition is `mediation_invite`, and its actual notice has been published, the resident can choose to initiate contact. Other existing avenues (direct family correspondence, another public hearing, leaving politics altogether) remain available; the mediator route is **not** a universal lock on speaking to people.

1. **`social.mediationOffer`** with `{"hearingId":"<actual-hearing-id>","proposal":"<player's own freely written practical suggestion>"}` during real daytime hours in Morrowfen Crossing. There is no mandatory family, guild, wealth, sect, power-rank or tuition gate. Filing takes **12 real minutes**. The engine proves the original hearing witness, issue and full statement, independent published council disposition, local civil docket and house charters. The proposal is *only the player's proposal*, not confirmed truth or a family's agreement. One pending invitation per genuine hearing prevents trying every possible random outcome immediately; revised proposals can be filed after an independent reply and real sixday of reflection.
2. **Independent family replies** occur at the next genuine world sixday no earlier than six days after the offer, via a separate world RNG and real local house standing/legitimacy. **Each publicly named elder decides personally**, as an individual. For river crossings these are **Ilyen Fenwake** and **Mara Reedward**; for wetland reedbeds, **Tamis Reedhand** and **Ilyen Fenwake**. Each may volunteer to attend, ask for revised terms or decline. One willing elder cannot compel the other. A positive reply offers presence at a future conversation, **not a promise to accept the proposal**. The clerk's response is published locally with a two-day delay; `social.mediationStatus` is read-only and does not leak early replies or hidden family finances.
3. **`social.mediationBook`** with `{"mediationId":"<actual-issued-id>"}` after **both** elders independently offered to attend. The clerk books the next real available Workday-4 sitting at least two days ahead, taking **10 actual minutes**; a resident may instead specify an eligible `preferredDay` within 30 days, respecting the real calendar and the five Concord Days. If a response has grown more than 30 days old, fresh consent is required. People need not attend without an actual appointment.
4. **`social.mediationMeet`** with `{"mediationId":"<issued-id>","statement":"<player's own freely spoken words>"}` at the actual booked public sitting, during **10:00–16:00**, taking **45 real minutes**. The meeting is then actually witnessed with two named elders who personally volunteered. **Each elder independently decides again** whether to support a **limited voluntary joint on-site inspection**, request clarification or decline a practical commitment. An ordinary resident's statement is not transformed into signed contract terms or a shared mind. When *both* support a joint site inspection the world records a truthful **proposed, not completed** cooperative visit. Nobody is forced to travel, spend clan money, transfer land or legally enforce the proposal without later actual acts.

The API does **not** offer player-specified NPC approval, heirship, romantic consent, secret clan knowledge, government law, invented witnessing, skill boosts, dice or free bloodline powers. The only fixed recognised procedural cooperation so far is **discussion of a possible joint field inspection**, not blanket agreement with any freeform text or automatic solution to toll and sanctuary disputes. The player may negotiate further in future releases or simply walk away. **A resident may explicitly withdraw an uncompleted offer or unused appointment** through `social.mediationWithdraw({mediationId})` at the same civic desk, spending eight real minutes; this cancels their own future booking without erasing the historical fact of an elder's earlier reply, inventing a family grudge, or permanently forbidding revised approaches. A canceled early invitation cannot be immediately refiled to fish for approval without at least a genuine sixday since the original offer. In the present narrow slice, a later proposed site inspection has *not* actually taken place and is clearly labelled as such.

The source chain spans `publicHearings.records` (signed hearing source witness), `table.realm.publicHearingReviews` (independent published council invitation), `table.realm.governance.reedmarch.publicHearingDockets` (public decision), `table.voluntaryMediation.offers` (source-hashed player proposal), `table.realm.mediationReplies` (separate independent elders) and the actual appointment/meeting event hashes. Only source-supported, player-known information appears in the mediation panel.

## Limits and next steps

This is a first voluntary **person-level** social event, not a complete contract system. Family members can still hold different positions internally; a single elder consenting to **attend a meeting** has not legally bound every relative, created an aristocratic title, or appointed the player to anything. Future genuine steps include individually motivated family-member relationships, independent household succession, records reviews with real inspected documents, material disputes and lasting restitution, craft/barter-based employment, local council lawmaking, mediation over issues that are not limited to two preauthored disputes, and actual follow-through on consented field visits.

All regression tests in `tests/voluntary-mediation.test.js` run with disposable games and real witnessed hearings, random independent invitations, actual sixday delays and later visits. They verify refusal and re-proposal, separate named people, no fast-forwards or forged source results, exact calendar bookings, authentic on-site meetings, one true source RNG stream and no automatic family rank, money or inherited abilities. The full CI workflow remains mandatory. No live campaign save is edited.
