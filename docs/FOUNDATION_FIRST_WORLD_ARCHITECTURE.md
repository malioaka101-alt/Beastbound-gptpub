# Foundation-first living-world architecture (standing Beastbound directive)

**User design decision, 7 November 2026:** Beastbound's task is **not** to map, prepopulate or write out its entire world. The task is to create a robust simulation, generation and persistence **foundation** from which the world is continuously created, maintained and expanded **as the private RPG is played**. This directive governs future engine priorities, design audits and choices between alternatives.

## Runtime world, not an expanding hard-coded atlas

- Author a limited number of coherent **starting seeds and exemplars** (biomes, people, households, cultures, beast taxa, legal patterns, disciplines, histories) only where needed to ground gameplay, demonstrate what is possible or test the system. These are *examples*, not a declaration that every playable person, society, technique, life form, nation or activity has already been written.
- Build **general, composable generators** for new individuals, lineages, family branches, clans, professions, settlements, groups, techniques, research discoveries, institutions, ecological niches, habitats, routes, crises, political structures and emergent relationships. Each generation needs enough causal context to be believable in this particular world; random name combinations or arbitrary species mashups are not sufficient.
- Generate **when causally demanded**: through exploration, birth, migration, commerce, conflict, political change, research, recruitment, discovery, material and social evolution, and other activities—whether initiated by the player or by independent off-screen lives. The world must not need the player to witness an entity before it can exist, act or grow.
- **Persist what actually exists.** Record stable identities, provenance, relationships, household and bloodline ties, location, needs, resources, injuries, capabilities, memories, motives, legal rights and consequences as relevant. Checkpoint, reload and re-encounter the *same* entity; never substitute a template that silently resets its history.
- Advance the world on a **real chronological clock**. Opportunities, mistakes, trade, inheritance, ecology, birth, education, departures, deaths and government changes should affect future possibilities. Institutions may form, divide, reform, decline and disappear. Existing seed data does not enjoy script immunity.
- Protect **individual agency**: NPCs and beasts have independent choices, welfare, consent and rights appropriate to their personhood. Player efforts can influence but cannot force every outcome. No guaranteed teacher, universal fee, fixed career tree, closed rank cap, compulsory family membership or predefined destiny unless actual canon, physiology, law or contextual evidence warrants it.
- **Separate innate inheritance from learned culture.** Exceptional parents can pass substantial real biological, Origin Pattern, magical organ, bloodline and power-aptitude advantages to humans and beasts; that is not automatically a copy of achieved rank, trained techniques, earned XP, a legal appointment or personal property. Real family/clan/organisational traditions can be taught, shared, withheld, lost, reinvented or stolen, subject to causality and actual choices.
- **Keep player knowledge distinct from simulation truth.** Privately created facts are not instantly visible to the player; reveal information through personally observed events, credible witnesses, research, trade, documents or disclosure. Retain source evidence so the GM never invents progression, rolls, off-screen facts or a saved checkpoint.
- Support **open-ended emergence**. Schema and engines should permit new cultures, species, groups, disciplines and unusual but plausible paths without requiring a code change for each individually named example. Constraints exist for ecological, physical, ethical, legal, canonical and technical reasons—not merely to funnel everyone through predetermined content.

## Development acceptance criteria

Before building a new list of fifty named exemplars, ask if a reusable **engine capability** could create and maintain them. Favor mechanics that enable **many future instances** over a one-off catalogue. A good vertical slice uses a small authored example to prove the general mechanism, then removes unjustified assumptions about universal counts, fixed populations, required discoveries or exclusive lists.

Test that entities can (a) emerge without being manually authored, (b) retain distinct identity and relationships through save/reload, (c) experience independent world events and consequences, (d) change, reproduce, adapt, form institutions or exit under credible conditions, (e) interact with players without compulsory outcomes, and (f) preserve source provenance and knowledge boundaries. Avoid fake completions that merely add descriptions, statuses or a larger seeded roster.

The **original Unified Master Prompt and established campaign canon take precedence** when a particular world fact is already defined. Do not overwrite Aerin's game with Jory's campaign details. Do not edit active campaign checkpoints during development. Keep the GitHub engine and saves private.

**Priority implication:** After the current bounded first-generation beast and inheritance slices, prefer generic human-person generation, family/birth/inheritance integration, emergent habitat/population and cultivation discovery frameworks—not manually listing the next set of named clans or species. Extend by believable templates and source-grounded mechanisms as play unfolds.
