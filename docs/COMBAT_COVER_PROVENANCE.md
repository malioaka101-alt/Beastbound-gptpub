# Source-grounded cover — first tactical terrain interaction

Beastbound's wound combat should support credible terrain, positioning and nonlethal decisions, without letting a player manufacture an invisible wall or choose a +100 defense bonus. The verified `combat.exchange` now adds **`intent:"take_cover"`**, taking a full in-world combat round to occupy a previously established physical obstacle at the actual encounter location.

## Currently supported local obstacles

The original authored local descriptions already establish **copper-barked tree trunks in Copperwood Verge** (`forest`) and **ward stones at Riftglass Outpost** (`ruins`). Only these two biomes receive an explicit **solid cover** option at present. Hills (bare ridge), wetlands (reeds/water, concealment but no verified hard barrier), and settlements (crowds/boardwalks, not automatically safe cover) are **not** granted protection without future separately witnessed obstacles. No new obstacle, creature, trait, terrain grade or spell is created by the action.

The engine, not the player, chooses a `kind`, `source`, `location` and `establishedRound` for the obstacle and records it in `battle.playerCover`. A read-only `combat.sceneStatus` reveals only the player's own cover, known local terrain and current round. The compact chat panel displays occupied cover without exposing hidden enemy plans or wound stats.

The limited cover bonus is **+2 to the player's defense against the enemy's independently selected attack**, taken from actual existing authored geography, and applies on the same turn the player uses their action to reach it and while the player stays there. It can stack with a later **Defend** action's active +3 defense, because that uses an additional full action; the existing stamina/brace reaction rules are unchanged. The player leaves cover when choosing a weapon **Attack** or **Withdraw** and must spend another genuine combat turn to occupy a new cover position. Taking cover again while already sheltered is not a new free action. A beast that retreats does so independently; no forced enemy attack is generated by choosing cover.

**Trust boundary:** `combat.exchange` still accepts only `intent`, legitimate equipped `attackId`, `nonlethal` and `playerBrace`, not `coverBonus`, `cover`, `enemyPlan`, `terrain` or `defense`. The source-derived cover status is revalidated against the real current location, the original fight biome, its exact predefined obstacle and the prior action round before using it. A stale or forged status fails before spending dice. The original general `Combat.attack` maths can receive a bounded internal situational bonus, but **direct `combat.attack` remains prohibited in private Project turns**.

This is a **first bounded position/tactics procedure**, not a complete spatial grid, action-point movement system, combat distances, flank geometry, dynamic obstacle destruction, or environmental magic subsystem. An individual battle cannot conjure a ruin or tree not recorded in that location's authored scene. More terrain-specific obstacles require independent source-backed observations rather than general permission to choose them.

`tests/combat-cover.test.js` checks source-backed scene status, real 1-minute round cost, persistent +2 and Defend +3, cleared cover when attacking, unsupported sites, no chosen enemy plans or cover bonuses, forged position rejection, and file-backed once-only turn IDs on disposable campaign saves.
