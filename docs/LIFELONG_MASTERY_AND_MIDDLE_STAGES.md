# Lifelong masteries and three new intermediate milestones per progression (7 November 2026)

## Correction, 7 November 2026 — mastery is performance, NEVER elapsed time

**User clarification overriding any older pacing wording below:** “Lifelong” describes how demanding mastery usually is, not a calendar or age prerequisite. No minimum days, years, number of sessions, rest period, age, fixed teacher or training schedule may be used to *unlock* the degrees or bridges. Progress follows actually resolved difficulty/quality, meaningful applied work, successful method diversity, earned skill, individual aptitude/potential and credible discoveries. Chosen actions do have realistic time/effort costs and the independent world continues ticking, but **idle time alone contributes zero advancement and an exceptional breakthrough can be earned promptly**. A serious failure consumes capacity, not a mandatory sixday wait. This statement takes precedence over earlier time-target descriptions and preserves all original Master Prompt power and consent conditions.

**General professional skill PP is corrected too:** A prior four-PP-per-sixday cap in `engine.skillGain` was a calendar gate. It is removed. Now genuine resolved training and skill checks may earn as many points as their actual outcomes warrant in a single sixday, while mastery/grand trials remain independently necessary and meaningful. Historical sixday counters continue as display metadata only, never an upper bound on personal achievement.

**Difficulty replaces time gating:** As a character demonstrates deeper mastery, the required check becomes harder based on the **earned mastery degree and the current independently verified major stage**, not days trained. This means a brilliant, talented or unusually well-prepared character who actually meets the difficulty can advance rapidly; repeated trivial work does not automatically equal high-tier command. Advanced breadth requires successful relevant methods, not merely choosing an “innovation” label for a failed drill. The real clock still reflects how much time an activity took and the body can still tire, but neither sets a minimum calendar age, seniority or sixday unlock.


**Authority:** Original Unified Master Prompt §§17–27, 28–35, the Table Rules' non-convertible measurements and evolution Maturity rule, and `docs/FOUNDATION_FIRST_WORLD_ARCHITECTURE.md`. The player specifically requested **several extra stages in the middle and substantially deeper progression**, such that the peak normally represents a life's dedication and a truly exceptional practitioner may approach it over a few good decades.

## Canonical major stages stay real

The Master Prompt defines **9 Meridian stages, 9 Arcane Circles, 7 Soul phases, 7 Body cycles, 8 Rift steps, 8 Origin integration procedures, 7 broad human evolution forms, 10 reciprocal Sync stages, 7 specialist ranks and professional skill 0–5**. Renumbering those established milestones would break actual saved physiology and turn one system into a disguised universal power ladder. Instead the engine now has **three new, separately earnable interstitial milestones in the middle of each canonical ladder**. They are substantive training/integration achievements, *not* a stolen tenth Circle or a replacement for an original named milestone.

For beast **individual Grades I–IX**, grade still means the original individual assessment, not another cultivator rank. Three mid-grade *development studies* are available for real lineage expression, adaptation and evolution stability, but they **do not automatically increase individual Grade, species Grade, Level or biological evolution**. Similarly three *material quality verification* milestones may deepen real Grade I–IX items without faking superior material or a magic effect.

### Unique middle milestones

- Meridian: **Gate-Weave Consolidation → Living Circulation Synthesis → Constitutional Resonance** (after original III, V and VII).
- Arcane: **Circle-Lattice Stabilisation → Field-Matrix Coherence → Astral Circuit Synthesis** (after Circle 3, 5 and 7).
- Soul: **Spirit-Thread Tempering → Inner Pattern Consolidation → Sanctuary Resonance Integration** (after Phase II, IV and V).
- Body: **Marrow-Tissue Harmonisation → Sense-Organ Integration → Blood-Essence Consolidation** (after cycles II, IV and V).
- Rift: **Anchored Fold Stabilisation → Boundary-Space Integration → Realm-Pressure Harmonisation** (after steps III, V and VI).
- Origin: **Lineage Acceptance Trials → Trait-Pattern Consolidation → Ancestral Expression Synthesis** (after steps II, IV and VI).
- Evolution: **Awakened Constitution Stabilisation → Transformed Form Integration → Ascendant Adaptation Synthesis** (after the appropriate established forms).
- Sync: **Reciprocal Pattern Alignment → Paired Manifestation Refinement → Shared Evolution Readiness**; each remains the **pair's** earned achievement and needs both beings' free continued involvement.
- Specialists: **School Technique Integration → Independent Method Synthesis → Original Discipline Architecture**. Their named disciplines do not convert to universal combat levels.
- General profession: **Independent Foundations → Applied Judgement → Integrated Masterwork**, with profession-specific competence and independent institutional qualifications.

These are genuine additional intermediate targets, not a mandatory school, political rank, hereditary social privilege, predictable endpoint or fixed planet-wide profession directory.

## Nine individually earned mastery degrees inside any major stage

In `rules/mastery-lifepath.js` the deep progression is:

**Awareness → Reliable basics → Repeatability → Applied fluency → Stabilised practice → Adaptation → Integrated control → Independent innovation → Peak mastery**.

These are separate from grade, Circle, lineage, age, licence and profession class. Each degree requires **90 hard-earned mastery points** from actual successful and increasingly varied work—not from an elapsed number of days, years, birthdays, seasons or training blocks. A genuine training attempt can happen in a focused 15-minute session, with one real engine d20 check; taking longer for the same work does not by itself increase the reward. A success advances according to the actual degree of the check, relevant saved skill rank, attributes and **source-seeded individually latent aptitude**; failure can result in no increase and serious overtraining needs recovery. Most characters progress at the normal x1 rate. Individually exceptional, demonstrably skilled and constitutionally gifted people can achieve x2 or x3 learning rates, but nobody claims talent just by calling themselves a prodigy.

**Breadth matters.** Repetition of one activity cannot establish high integrated mastery; higher degrees require competence across multiple appropriate methods (observation, application, analysis, practice, recovery, innovation or real care, depending on the field). A specific intermediate milestone can be earned at the appropriate original middle stage through genuine **Peak mastery**, demonstrated success across varied relevant methods, and a separate d20 integration trial. It requires **no minimum number of completed days, months, years or counted sessions**. Failure has real activity costs and can be retried whenever actual capacity and circumstances permit. Serious strain affects Stamina and immediate physical condition; no automatically imposed calendar cooldown. None of this silently opens a new Meridian Gate, constructs an Arcane Circle, produces an evolution, awards a Guild licence, copies a contract or grants magical immunity.

**Pacing is not a chronological lock.** Ordinary progression has nine demanding mastery degrees within each original stage plus three interstages. Those degrees reflect difficult achievements and depth of skill, **not a timer that must run down**. For ordinary people with part-time careers and life responsibilities, reaching exceptional breadth is plausibly a lifelong pursuit, often many decades and sometimes beyond one mortal lifetime. Exceptionally gifted, highly trained practitioners can in favourable conditions do the same over a few good decades. An injury, discovery, exceptional parentage, independently gained knowledge, rare opportunity, mentorship or accelerated breakthrough can change that path. A person may accomplish an important individual or professional task without reaching the top. No rule says one must be a particular birthday age to discover an ability. **This is a description of how difficult genuine mastery often proves to be, not a guaranteed or required number of years**. Progress depends on actions, insights, real challenges, earned competence, aptitude and potential, actual opportunities, health, resources and personal agency. **Even a prodigy who makes an authentic breakthrough exceptionally early may advance immediately; neither age nor training duration blocks it.**

The repeatable `rankings.practice({path,skill?,focus,minutes?})` allows a meaningful active session of 15–480 minutes (default 60). The previous optional `days` values (6, 30, 90, 180) remain compatible for players deliberately choosing extended routines, but are **never a minimum prerequisite** and give no elapsed-calendar bonus. The actual check and its quality, relevant independently seeded talent, earned skill and demonstrated methods determine progress. The clock records only how long the chosen activity really took. An intermediate `rankings.bridge({path,skill?})` is a focused 120-minute challenge and requires genuine mastery and proven relevant methods, **not a minimum of thirty sessions or six-day waiting**. Calling the same private turn twice must never repeat the practice or reroll dice. The `rankings.bridge({path,skill?})` operation attempts the appropriately earned middle-stage integration trial. `rankings.detail({})` remains strictly **read-only** and shows original ranks, nine degrees, extra waypoints, personally earned sources and unavailable/unassessed routes separately.

**Coverage honesty:** The current starter engine only has mechanically verified entry breakthroughs for some primary disciplines. It does *not* claim to simulate every advanced circle, specialist surgical operation, final evolution or full bilateral Sync ritual. Mastery training for an unestablished power discipline is correctly refused. New middle stages are an extensible foundation; field-specific achievements, trained results and usable advanced effects require separate source-bound adapters. Existing campaign facts are not overwritten.

## Continued creation

A new `creation.repair({id})` route gives the first **actual maintenance lifecycle** to source-backed experimental instruments. A worn genuine reed surface-water gauge can be repaired for **one actual Marsh Reed, 50 in-world minutes, two Stamina and an honest Crafting check**; failure consumes the replacement material, while success restores bounded durability but **requires recalibration** before another valid reading. Hard-earned professional Crafting depth can grant at most a +1/+2 bonus on relevant work checks, never free material, a higher Grade or an invented magical effect. This is designed to extend to real future civilian and magical inventions, without claiming that all mechanisms are already available.
