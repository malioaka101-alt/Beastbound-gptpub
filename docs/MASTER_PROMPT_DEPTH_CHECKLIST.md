## Foundation principles and stop rule

**Authority:** the original `reference/Beastbound_Realms_Unified_Master_Prompt.md`, the user's later compatible directions, actual saved facts, original engine and verified CI. See [current Project instructions](PROJECT_CHAT_INSTRUCTIONS.md), [cross-system owners and boundaries](SYSTEM_CONNECTION_AUDIT.md) and [GM/engine division](GM_NARRATIVE_AND_ENGINE_BOUNDARY.md).

- Accept an item when its **expandable, connected, persistent, engine-verified *foundation*** meets the written criterion. Do **not** require all future fantasy content, every species, cultural form, craft, policy or unusual power before moving forward. Conversely, do not tick a missing connection on the basis of one hardcoded example.
- **Before coding**, audit existing state owners and genuine tests; identify a missing shared invariant. Reuse original mechanisms, actor identities, source witnesses, independent consent, finite work/resources, public knowledge and world ticks. Don't build duplicate ledgers or a global generic story/permission engine.
- Verify exact original dice and replay, persistence, privacy, independent decisions, source hashes, physical conservation and old-save compatibility. Full successful **exact-head GitHub Actions three-batch regression plus final integration** and a confirmed merge precede acceptance.
- **Respect player freedom:** GM-authored opportunities are open-ended; an unverified proposal is not executable. No compulsory teacher, apprenticeship price, fixed institution, menu of power effects or false universal rank. Mechanical outcomes must come from legitimate source-backed actions.
- Preserve source rules, distinct species/person agency and human power architectures. Never invent old campaign facts, and never modify gameplay saves, requests or results merely to develop or refactor foundations.

The original #19 criterion is met at **foundation level**, rather than as a completed catalogue of world economies:

- **GM-authored observation of real assets and owners:** `rules/material-handoffs.js::attestAsset` registers original freeform descriptions only against source-owned player goods, actually held merchant supplies, a real recipient's accepted material custody, or the existing original physical construction and paid-equipment sources. It neither creates items nor asserts legal title.
- **Genuine inputs/output and persistence:** `rules/physical-work.js`, `rules/physical-production-ledger.js`, `rules/unified.js` and `rules/core.js` conserve real materials and their verified outputs across unlike original medicine and tool work, finite bundles, purchase and sale receipts, rejected attempts, saves and independent world days.
- **Wages and contracts:** `world/person-careers.js` verifies real completed individual work before finite currency can move. `world/merchant-voluntary-work-terms.js` records independent agreement, refusal and settlement; a contract alone neither pays nor forces work.
- **Regional demand and actual trade:** `world/independent-regional-demand.js` records authentic person/source-based interest at world ticks, while `world/independent-local-purchases.js` checks funded purchases and paid demand effects against original seller stock and prices. - **Continuous world:** source hashes, actor identity, independent consent, original RNG/time, cash/stock conservation, save replay, private information and later world ticks are covered by existing regressions and the complete CI.

**Scope of acceptance:** this authorizes no unverified goods, money, titles, licences, prices, new powers or consent. It does **not** claim all trade routes, taxes, credit, insurance, illicit markets, quality grades, dispute enforcement or every future material are mechanically realized. Those specialist consequences belong to their original owner systems and subsequent relevant foundations. Do not reopen #19 simply to add more examples; identify a genuine broken shared invariant if future play demonstrates one.

## #20 accepted — jurisdictional civic foundation, not universal courts

**Merged, exact-head verified evidence:** PRs [#104](https://github.com/malioaka101-alt/Beastbound-gptpub/pull/104) through [#109](https://github.com/malioaka101-alt/Beastbound-gptpub/pull/109) establish bounded source-owned civic jurisdiction, individual sapient personhood and legitimate procedure, real source-authored local enactments, independent opposing case accounts, earlier authentic in-person place observations and integrity-preserving replay. In particular, PRs [#107](https://github.com/malioaka101-alt/Beastbound-gptpub/pull/107), [#108](https://github.com/malioaka101-alt/Beastbound-gptpub/pull/108) and [#109](https://github.com/malioaka101-alt/Beastbound-gptpub/pull/109) are **merged** after their exact-head three-batch regression and final integration checks; their official GitHub merge commits are evidence, not proposed code. The earlier original `world/government-offices.js` / `world/public-hearings.js` March Council source also has independently decided procedural review and a **real source-attested public-records inspection order**, fulfilled on an original later sixday tick. This is an actually executed **noncoercive procedural remedy**, not a punishment.

**Expandable connected foundation:** The GM can author unfamiliar regional laws, offence descriptions, permitted remedies and cultural processes without filling a fixed catalogue; actual **supported** legal authority derives only from a particular grounded polity, original charter/office and individually witnessed consenting/contesting participants. `world/beast-civic-life.js`, `world/government-offices.js`, `rules/public-hearings.js`, `world/lived-places.js` and `world/sapient-beast-persons.js` supply the existing persistent source owners. Original live persons and their consent remain autonomous, public cases retain both accounts, physical observations are distinguished from allegations, locally effective provisions have original notice, and status is limited to genuinely public local facts. Existing identity, inventory, money, health and world-clock mechanisms still own any *real* downstream mechanical change; legal descriptions never fabricate those effects.

**Honest scope/stop rule:** This is #20's **foundation-level acceptance**, not a claim that every offence is legally adjudicated, every jurisdiction uses a council, or fines, convictions, seizures, imprisonment, damages, appeals and compulsory compliance are automatically executable. Allegations and visible terrain are not proof of an individual's liability; a merely described remedy never executes. An unimplemented, unusual cultural procedure or coercive result stays unverified until genuinely established original legal and owner-system witnesses exist. No universal adjudication engine, second ledger or prewritten offence/remedy menu is required. The additional filing-only PR [#110](https://github.com/malioaka101-alt/Beastbound-gptpub/pull/110) was **closed without merge**: an extra per-case challenge API was not a demonstrated missing shared foundation invariant. Reopen #20 only for a proven reusable broken invariant, not to code each fictional step.
