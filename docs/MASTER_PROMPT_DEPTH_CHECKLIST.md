# Beastbound Master Prompt depth — persistent foundation checklist

**Created:** 8 November 2026. **Authority:** `/mnt/data/Beastbound_Realms_Unified_Master_Prompt.md`, the original 57-section Unified Master Prompt, existing verified campaign facts, and the current engine. This is **not** a list of things that must all be finished before play. No campaign is active.

## How to use this checklist

The **Game Master** chooses/creates cosmology, culture, people, histories, powers, events and scenes in imaginative prose, limited by the original Master Prompt and established facts. The **engine** is the mechanical/continuity worker: it records real identities, laws, ownership, sources, time, limits, independent changes, rolls and consequences. The GM supplies examples and starting facts, **not a fixed cap or a prefab roster**. Never design a new subsystem when an existing living-world, mythic, ecology, person, bond, law, career, resource or character record can be extended. Never turn a character's established pre-game biography into a permission roll. 

Each item is checked only when an **expandable, connected, persisted, engine-verified foundation** exists and tests demonstrate that it can create, develop, inspect and maintain instances across real saves. A handful of seeded examples are **illustrations**, not exhaustive content. Detailed mastery beyond a foundation remains future work; record known limitations. Work sequentially, beginning at #01, and update this checklist with commit/CI evidence. Do not pre-emptively tick untested work.

- [x] **03. Species biology and taxonomy** — Master Prompt §§6–9, 18. **Existing bridge:** Species-addressable bestiary and source-backed animal records. **Foundation criterion:** GM creates an individual species' anatomy, reproduction, diet, habitat and Origin Pattern; engine stores structured biological constraints, generation, variants and inheritance within existing taxonomy.
- [x] **04. Ecological webs and populations** — Master Prompt §§2, 5, 11. **Existing bridge:** World ecology, local populations and independent wild lives. **Foundation criterion:** Connect species to real predators, food sources, breeding and migration habitats; update populations from observed resource conditions and world ticks.
- [x] **05. - [ ] **12. Soul higher phases and ethical engineering** — Master Prompt §§22. **Existing bridge:** Soul Perception and Threading I–II. **Foundation criterion:** Extend soul structures/repair/identity systems, witnessed procedures, safety, consent and phase effects without mind-reading by default.

- [ ] **14. Beast-Origin lineage integration** — Master Prompt §§18, 24. **Existing bridge:** Compatibility screening and bounded early research. **Foundation criterion:** Track GM-authored trait origins and eight-step integration/rejection, ethical acquisition and evolving original human biology.
- [ ] **15. Rift higher practice and realm transit** — Master Prompt §§3, 25. **Existing bridge:** Rift Sense and measured Anchor Marking. **Foundation criterion:** Reuse #1 realm/boundary ledger to implement genuinely witnessed Fold Step, Boundary Weaving, pocket realms, Realm Walking with costs and no unearned teleport.

- [ ] **17. Living career and qualification paths** — Master Prompt §§32, 33, 47–48. **Existing bridge:** Independent workers, organisations and physical work. **Foundation criterion:** Make GM-authored jobs, historical credentials, earnings, safety duties, apprenticeship and labour opportunities persist in existing people/group/economy ledgers.
- [ ] **18. Organisational and political depth** — Master Prompt §§33–34, 42. **Existing bridge:** States, family houses, groups, individual NPC initiatives. **Foundation criterion:** Make authored existing offices, membership, succession, finances, diplomacy, command and disagreement mechanically recordable; avoid a fixed list of ranks/organisations.
- [ ] **19. Property, production and deep economy** — Master Prompt §§35, 50. **Existing bridge:** Actual item stock, owned goods, transfers, local market. **Foundation criterion:** Register GM-authored assets and their real owners; use actual input/output conservation, wages, contracts and regional demand; maintain records across independent ticks.
- [ ] **20. Law, civic process and beast rights** — Master Prompt §§12, 15, 36. **Existing bridge:** Petitions, public hearings, mediation and rights lore. **Foundation criterion:** Build flexible jurisdiction-specific legal rules, offences and remedies on real witnesses and protected consent, not universal law menus.
- [ ] **21. Competition and ranking culture** — Master Prompt §§37–38. **Existing bridge:** Independent score registers and calendar. **Foundation criterion:** GM creates events, entrants and qualification sources; engine handles announced criteria, actual eligible performance, judging, appeals, seasons, ranks and rewards.
- [ ] **22. Long-lived families, relationships and life events** — Master Prompt §§39–43. **Existing bridge:** Named origin relatives, worker memories and autonomous household work. **Foundation criterion:** Expand existing individuals, kin, marriage, dependants, succession, collaboration, loss, return and changing voluntary relationships; never manufacture affection.
- [ ] **23. Tactical combat and environmental effects** — Master Prompt §§45–46. **Existing bridge:** Wounds, Ward, contested exchanges and encounter evidence. **Foundation criterion:** Track terrain, distance, positions, cooperative moves, restraint, nonlethal consequences and source-valid abilities without generic fabricated moves.
- [ ] **24. Clinical recovery, equipment and crafting depth** — Master Prompt §§49–50. **Existing bridge:** Existing wound core and source-backed physical work. **Foundation criterion:** Build expandability for authentic injuries, healing, persistent medical recovery, tool wear, material quality, safe creative designs and reproducible recipes.
- [ ] **25. General freeform consequence handoff** — Master Prompt §§41, 44–48, 54. **Existing bridge:** GM interpretation, protected witnessed dice and save replay. **Foundation criterion:** Allow novel actions to use suitable established actor, world, resource and check facts without arbitrary DCs or a duplicate story engine; add a narrow verified consequence mechanism only when play demonstrates need.
