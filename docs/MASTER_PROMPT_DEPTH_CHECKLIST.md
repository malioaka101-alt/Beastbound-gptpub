## Foundation principles and stop rule

**Authority:** the original `reference/Beastbound_Realms_Unified_Master_Prompt.md`, the user's later compatible directions, actual saved facts, original engine and verified CI. See [current Project instructions](PROJECT_CHAT_INSTRUCTIONS.md), [cross-system owners and boundaries](SYSTEM_CONNECTION_AUDIT.md) and [GM/engine division](GM_NARRATIVE_AND_ENGINE_BOUNDARY.md).

- Accept an item when its **expandable, connected, persistent, engine-verified *foundation*** meets the written criterion. Do **not** require all future fantasy content, every species, cultural form, craft, policy or unusual power before moving forward. Conversely, do not tick a missing connection on the basis of one hardcoded example.
- **Before coding**, audit existing state owners and genuine tests; identify a missing shared invariant. Reuse original mechanisms, actor identities, source witnesses, independent consent, finite work/resources, public knowledge and world ticks. Don't build duplicate ledgers or a global generic story/permission engine.
- Verify exact original dice and replay, persistence, privacy, independent decisions, source hashes, physical conservation and old-save compatibility. Full successful **exact-head GitHub Actions three-batch regression plus final integration** and a confirmed merge precede acceptance.
- **Respect player freedom:** GM-authored opportunities are open-ended; an unverified proposal is not executable. No compulsory teacher, apprenticeship price, fixed institution, menu of power effects or false universal rank. Mechanical outcomes must come from legitimate source-backed actions.
- Preserve source rules, distinct species/person agency and human power architectures. Never invent old campaign facts, and never modify gameplay saves, requests or results merely to develop or refactor foundations.
