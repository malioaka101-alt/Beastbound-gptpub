# Foundation: independent co-present relationships and ordinary knowledge encounters

**Status (7 November 2026):** Implemented private world-tick vertical slice in `world/independent-contacts.js`, with dedicated regression test `tests/independent-contacts.test.js`. No active campaign save edited. This is a foundation for relationships emerging naturally from the world, **not** a complete simulated social network, universal teaching system or scripted settlement.

## Actual world events, not a generated friendship directory

After `world/emergent-people.js` has run a sixday of actual movement and work, `world/simulation.js` calls the new independent contact adapter. Only individually generated people with an existing, source-hashed active **local work presence** at the same supported place, on the same world day, can participate. Missing, expired, relocated or forged shifts do not qualify. Their identities persist through saves and are not re-generated for a scene.

A sampled off-screen contact is initiated or not initiated from each person's own stable origin, personality, condition and contemporaneous circumstances. The other person can decline. If a meeting occurs, a continuing pair can cooperate, converse without immediate gains, or disagree. **No friendship, guaranteed goodwill, compelled agreement or romance is created by a timer.** Regards are modest, bounded *internal traces of actual encounters*, not universal personality ratings. The simulator samples a small, changing subset of consequential off-screen encounters at each genuine world tick (growing sublinearly with the number of co-present individuals). This is a performance and relevance budget, **not** a limit on real-world conversations, an invented population ceiling, an age requirement, or a calendar gate on friendships or personally played scenes. Individuals not sampled still live and work independently. There is no world-wide preset count of families, NPCs or social classes.

A genuinely successful ordinary work receipt from `world/person-careers.js` may, if one individual voluntarily offers and the other independently accepts, be passed as a *remembered vocational lead*. Source receipts are validated when offered and preserved within the contact's sealed record. A recipient learns that a particular method has been demonstrated, **not** how to perform it successfully. Individual aptitude, independent work, lawful/safe access and the separate `world/causal-learning.js` procedure still control any actual learning later. The donor's rank, licences, family secrets, magical stages, beast Grades, techniques, property and contracts are never copied.

Shared verbal vocational examples currently require the same established language. Different-language encounters can still occur, including nonverbal cooperation or disagreement, but no imagined instant translation. The original Master Prompt allows many possible speech, gesture, scent, sign and magical communication methods; later trusted adapters may establish these from actual source-backed competence rather than assuming universal human speech.

## Integrity and player knowledge

The world keeps a private identity-bound, per-pair relationship record and an individual record of *heard* methods. Events carry both original physical shift receipts, independent consent outcomes and (when present) the genuine source professional work receipt. A bounded chain of recent events replays into checkpointed state, folding older events into a source-linked anchor. Editing recent outcomes, encounter sites, received knowledge or resulting relationships fails verification. This is a consistency/provenance mechanism, not adversary-proof cryptography against someone rewriting all saved state.

World contacts do not write to the player's personally witnessed conversations, public journals or news. Another person's private relationships, whereabouts, desires and skills **cannot** become player-known merely because a world tick happened. A later actual meeting, conversation, publication, gossip route or other credible information channel would be required.

## Deliberate next adapters, not falsely claimed features

- Independent encounters between established sapient beast persons and humans require separate physical co-location, language/gesture evidence, personhood protections and **individual consent**, not automatic inclusion in a human work-shift generator.
- Ordinary knowledge can eventually inform real recipient experiments and practical work, but a heard method is not yet itself an earned `Practice.record` accomplishment or new spell.
- Family formation, offspring and inherited aptitude must use real lives, choices and parentage; generic coworker meetings do not impose kinship.
- Actual rivalry, reconciliation, cross-cultural exchange, resource bargaining, workplaces, households and mentorship should each come from concrete events and validated adapters, not a hard-coded guaranteed result.

This is intentionally small **functionality with general foundations**, not a list of preauthored NPCs or future plotlines. The engine continues to respect the original Master Prompt and established campaign canon; Aerin's save stays unchanged.
