# Public evolution discoveries and sealed conditions

The source rules prohibit exposing facts that the player has not yet discovered. A privately registered evolution branch can contain hidden conditions, sealed potential limits, and unknown biological gates.

**Public `evolution.status` and `evolution.eligibility` require an earlier source-dated `table.evolutionDiscoveries[branchKey]` record** matching the current beast, species/form and `revealedToPlayer:true`. A guessed undiscovered branch key gives exactly the same `{known:false}` reply as an unknown key, without identifying the branch or explaining its existence.

Even a disclosed branch returns only publicly established name, Maturity, documented progress and known unmet conditions. Its actual `ready`, `eligible`, internal Potential thresholds, gate value and number/text of unknown requirements remain sealed. `knownConditionsMet` means only that **known** requirements and Maturity appear satisfied; it does not promise that an Evolution Event is mechanically possible. The private `rules/progress.js::status` calculation remains authoritative for the genuine source-verified event and isolated formula tests.

No research discovery is fabricated by querying status. The independent discovery-authoring system is still incomplete. Do not claim evolution is confirmed viable or reveal unknown prerequisites based on a read-only request.
