# Finish Line A — Freeform GM intent and source-backed contextual dialogue

**Authority:** Original Unified Master Prompt §§39–45, 51, 54; latest Table Rules and current player instruction that any plausible action can be attempted without arbitrary teachers, fees, parser vocabularies or extra subsystems. The ChatGPT Project narrator is the GM; private `scripts/chat-turn.js` is the **mechanical** executor, not a semantic language model.

## A1 · The player's action is not constrained to enumerated parser commands

An unmatched but syntactically valid `mode:"act"` request now returns a read-only `output.gmAdjudication`: original intent, only genuinely observed local scene and people, `status:"unresolved"`, and explicit guarantees of **no roll, time, material, consent, success, damage or progression**. This is **not** a success roll or a promised eventual outcome. Before, such requests fell through to the old `engine.js` prototype where they might return "I could not map ...", encourage menus, or accidentally enter unverified legacy shortcuts. The executor still only forwards established source-compatible legacy movement, exploratory and departure actions. Existing protected rules retain their specific higher-authority guards.

**GM use:** describe a trivial certainly feasible outcome in ordinary prose without a dice call; when stakes/risk need mathematics, establish the actual source and use its protected rule. For a local terrain clue, the supported pattern is actual witnessed `world.habitatSurvey` → **one original saved `checkId`** → `core.check` only for that context. Unrelated player-authored labels cannot be used to reuse a habitat check as proof of a different act; GM must not assign a new DC, hidden NPC agreement, species discovery, spell or reward simply because an intent has been acknowledged. A future genuinely sourced obstacle can be committed through existing `table.sceneChecks` provenance and resolved through `rules/scene-check.js`, without creating a new per-obstacle minigame. The engine alone cannot judge every unbounded natural-language intention; **that is the GM's role**.

**Open-ended journeys:** An unknown or non-adjacent destination remains a legitimate, read-only unresolved intention. It is neither a disallowed parser command nor instant cross-map travel. A player may seek directions, investigate routes or try to begin such a journey; its movement, risks, elapsed time and costs still require source-bound resolution. Known adjacent places continue through the existing travel engine. This adds no fast-travel system, new fee or universal route restriction.

## A2 · Meaningful follow-ups to an actually known person

The original `rules/npc-conversations.js` now accepts an **optional real question** alongside the canonical person's ID; `scripts/chat-turn.js` passes `talk to <actual person> about <question>` through to it. The same person may answer several different questions that day without an arbitrary "no more conversation" ban. Introductory conversation still takes its original 12 minutes, follow-ups 6, and repeating an identical already-witnessed question costs neither another time step nor another roll. Meeting record, original source hash, player-known speech and day remain in the same saved `npcConversations` journal.

The answer may contain only facts the source actually supports: the NPC's original authored profession statement, real local linked routes, or genuinely observed current weather. Unknown private topics, future promises and character mindreading are marked `unverified`, not supplied as fabricated authoritative answers; the narrator can continue natural dialogue based on genuine witnessed context. A factual public answer becomes player-known evidence, **not** a new power, rank, wage, teaching contract or guaranteed cooperation. Established voluntary work/consent/commerce routes remain the sole source of their associated consequences. Engine-backed conversations remain source-bound to the same actual person-presence and local availability authoritative for ordinary interactions. This is **not** a second relationship system.

**Independently emergent people use the same principle, without a new NPC system.** `world/emergent-people.js` already retains each person's source-backed origin, present region, profession, personal work purpose, disposition, fatigue, lived encounters and independent consent. Its conversation path now draws bounded replies from those facts and the existing `world/atlas.js` cultural customs, language and witnessed local routes, *only when the player actually asks about a relevant subject*. A regional custom does not establish the person's faith, politics, ethics, beliefs or agreement: two people of the same culture or profession can still act differently. For a new subject, an already willing person may continue talking without another consent reroll; repeating an identical question returns the original witnessed answer without advancing time; a recent genuine refusal is not undone by rephrasing. Unknown private answers remain unverified, not fabricated, and real contracts still require their original independent rules. The existing `emergentConversations` journal saves the witnessed topic and answer kind; no second friendship ledger, scripted dialogue tree or cultural stereotype registry was added.

## A4 · Source availability, not universal gift hours

`rules/material-handoffs.js` no longer imposes its own global 08:00–18:00 cutoff. `world/person-presence.js` already establishes true daytime attendance for the original fixed professionals and independent visible duty days for other people. A genuine independently seen person may accept or refuse an ordinary material outside the canonical market shift if that person is actually in the scene. No absent NPC or after-hours merchant is summoned. No gift creates a sale, friendship, contract or chosen response.

## What this pass does NOT claim

No trusted remote GM API to sign arbitrary new challenges has been introduced. The freeform narrator must still establish legitimate facts, consent, obstacles and evidence. No new spell, recipe, ability, voice simulation, personal knowledge, inferred goals or material appears. `tests/open-ended-intent-and-contextual-dialogue.test.js`, `tests/actual-presence-not-universal-hours.test.js` and the revised existing NPC and handoff tests verify these repairs against disposable saves. The active Aerin checkpoint remains untouched.
