# Foundation #18 — source-audited original association operating capacity

**Authority:** Original Unified Master Prompt §33 (budgets, changing fortunes, independent members), §35 (scarce resources, coherent economy) and §36 (jurisdiction and enforceable rights), with the actual original `rules/emergent-groups.js`, `world/emergent-groups.js`, player-owned Talon accounting and original independent living-world month. The GM can create unlimited different organisations, claims, cultures, projects, financial arrangements and people; the engine preserves **only source-verified economic facts**. No compulsory organisation dues, guild monopoly, mandatory fee or closed work catalogue.

## Two quantities must remain distinct

The existing player-facing rule `social.groupContribute({groupId,activity,coins})` already makes a real original personal **Talon** debit, then raises the group's original **limited operating capacity** by the same number. This is a resource conversion for the group's real independent work/upkeep rule. It is **not** evidence that the group holds spendable/redeemable coins in a second bank account, that the founder can freely withdraw the funds, that an NPC owes a wage, that it purchased specific property, or that a hypothetical new fee can automatically transfer money. Keep the quantities, names and physical ownership honest; do not invent an exchangeable asset merely because the values match.

The group monthly world tick already consumes its limited operating capacity for **real source-witnessed 45-minute group work**, determined by **one original existing group random outcome**, and for existing member-count-based upkeep. This change does not add an income source or another cost, but connects **every one of those original balance movements** in the same `e.operatingAccount` journal:

- Original founding groups begin with an honest zero balance and no fabricated donations. Older saves without the journal start at their **actual saved current balance** as an explicit historical anchor: the earlier deposits and spending were not recorded.
- An actual founder contribution stores the existing `C.event` witness and its hash, the original action/day, exact original Talon balance before/after, original group operating capacity before/after, and a signed increase with matching amount. It requires the same original genuine group founder and original 45-minute work rule. A contribution of zero Talons remains valid independent voluntary work, **not** a new fee.
- Original independent monthly work cost attaches the original source-sealed `world/emergent-groups.js::record` review, actual spent operating capacity, original one-roll result, real collective work effort and actual original balance change. It creates **no further work result or dice**.
- Original monthly upkeep attaches the originally computed member-count-based cost only **if the actual existing available operating capacity pays it**, recording exactly the original before/after amount. Unfunded groups still become inactive under the original system; the journal never creates overdrafts, NPC wages, confiscations or forced participation.

The per-group journal verifies chronological unique receipts, internally sealed witnesses, matched original independent review digests, source-scoped member upkeep, existing founding identity, nonnegative balances and **current operating capacity = saved honest anchor + all attested deltas**. The human-readable `originalOperatingHistory` reads without mutating or silently reconstructing earlier saves, and explicitly says the capacity is **not withdrawable Talons and not a claim of material property**.

This extends `world/emergent-groups.js` itself; it is not a second economic engine. The worker-spinoff organisations remain financially linked to `world/agent-initiatives.js`'s original actual funded worker model: this patch deliberately does not copy their balances or double-create resources. Natural future multi-owner group property, source-authorised custody, independent member permission, payroll, trade credit and legal contracts remain separate open foundations.
