# Accountable household stewardship — earned work, consent and the possibility of replacement

This patch continues **living clan and family development** rather than preserving a permanent elder-controlled status quo. It follows the original Beastbound Master Prompt's distinct families, political legitimacy and independent people, the Table Rules' NPC agency and player-known evidence, and the [Living World Change Charter](LIVING_WORLD_CHANGE_CHARTER.md). Its precise committee procedure, resource amounts and review odds are **new, scoped regional simulator mechanics**, not canon inheritance laws for every kingdom, species or clan. The existing 28 household charters identify an elder and a second **relative or chosen kin**; neither surname nor a leadership role proves biological descent, inherited spellcasting, noble status or ownership.

## From decorative coordinator to real bounded authority

The initial person-level `world/family-members.js` allowed an independently accomplished family member to become a *voluntary coordinator of public-duty work* after two successful contributions, greater earned merit and separately consenting incumbent elder and candidate. Their role was previously largely a status flag. Now a real consenting coordinator may **propose and spend from the household's small, actually tracked public-duty reserve** when they independently choose practical household service, *even when the elder chooses not to work on that project*. The elder and second member still make separately sampled decisions. If the second member chooses an **independent public-record review** instead, they must continue to fund that activity from their own real allowance; the work coordinator title doesn't automatically grant unlimited control of family funds.

Each limited project still takes two actual funded world-month sessions and a genuine separate practical check, with the same real costs, failures, beneficial effects and preserved personal merit as before. The role never transfers the family headship, ancestor title, office, land, bloodline, marriage consent, individual beast autonomy or legal succession; the accepted delegation is exclusively about **bounded public-duty stewardship**.

## Earned accountability, actual withdrawal and persistence

Each genuinely consented coordinating appointment now keeps its own **durable source-hashed** mandate record: its original signed public-role transfer, initial real date, later work actually performed *since the appointment*, distinct success/failure counts, and subsequent independent reviews. An accountability review becomes eligible only after **at least two genuine later projects** or a significant **real public-duty crisis involving severe pressure and insufficient reserve**. Reviews cannot repeat every month; a genuine four-month separation is required. The original elder and current coordinator independently decide whether to continue the explicitly voluntary delegation, considering actual work, conditions and prior disagreements.

- If **both** continue, the coordinator keeps their limited responsibilities and the review becomes permanent recorded history.
- If **either withdraws consent**, the voluntary mandate concludes. The historical elder resumes only the public-duty coordination function. The coordinator's merit, kinship, prior service and independently earned standing are never erased; neither character is automatically shamed, expelled, married or deemed disloyal.
- A former coordinator may receive a new separately consented mandate after **new actual successful service** since the previous conclusion; repeating old merits cannot trigger an infinite instant reappointment. Neither legal family succession nor crown status changes.

This means a family member can **rise through practical work, direct real resources, lose an actual limited appointment because circumstances and independent people change, and potentially recover it**. Success cannot simply be predetermined by a timer. This first small model is NOT a comprehensive clan constitution, inheritance court, succession war, generational aging or political coercion engine; these require independently established law, community support and case-specific events.

## Accurate family public history across decades

The prior routine `social-houses.js` report contained a concrete error: it assigned the current report date to `lastChangeDay` **before** checking whether a verified member achievement happened since the previous report. The check therefore usually excluded all genuine past achievements and could present an appeal instead of a real recorded accomplishment.

This patch evaluates the **previous** report boundary, and requires an actual named member's source-hashed verified public-duty work receipt with a matching day, house and successful result. That receipt is stored durably on the household's member ledger, so justified progress can still be announced after a rolling news/history buffer is trimmed. A report with no new verified work cannot simply recycle last cycle's success or proclaim material progress because the family is rich or famous. Attempts to alter the receipt hash cannot create public success. Normal petitions and public appeals remain available.

`world.familyMembersStatus({})` remains strictly **read-only**, displays only locally known published evidence on the regular eight-day notice delay, and never reveals private votes, work allowances, sealed personal motives, inherited traits or future guaranteed outcomes. Existing saves are not edited; new fields are introduced only through genuine future world progression and only after qualifying independent events.
