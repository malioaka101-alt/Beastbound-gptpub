# Organic creation, research and living families — integration contract

**Foundation change, 8 November 2026.** Beastbound remains a private chat RPG. The original Unified Master Prompt and Table Rules govern; the GM writes the scene and interprets unrestricted intentions, while the actual engine verifies numeric changes and maintains independent histories.

## Connected operations already available

Existing `creation.draft`, `creation.personalDevelop`, `creation.personalUse`, `creation.prototype`, and the distinct individual power paths remain separate. A personal Meridian, Arcane, Body, Soul or Rift expression is an *earned personal technique* only with its actual verified structural source; a fancy name is not a source of power. Existing source-proven self-taught ordinary techniques, witnessed school work, limited family correspondence, and independent group creation still operate through their own guarded procedures.

**Source plan creation, not imaginary feasibility:** `research.prepareDesign({designId})` now creates a fixed research plan for one genuinely implemented physical target: a personally recorded mundane reed-based surface-water sensing tool (`tool/mundane/sense/surface_water/magnitude 0/touch/single/1min/variant`). It verifies the original hashed design, real home habitat, scope, author and source and seals its *limited* feasibility **before** `research.start({id})`. The player cannot specify truth, roll, DC, grade, material result or result. `research.designPlansStatus` is read-only. The existing research survey, real sixday study costs, hypothesis, proof and genuine two-person replication remain indispensable. The result does **not** automatically build a device, grant a technique or issue a school licence. Other systems, radically different mechanisms and unknown supernatural inventions need independently established evidence, not a universal “research anything = success” adapter.

**Research changes the world only through an act:** `research.publish({id})` is optional. It requires a genuinely recognised `invention`, `ecology` or `method` research project, its precommitted original plan and two recorded independently used testers. Filing consumes actual 45 minutes and one Stamina. Its source-sealed public report is released after three days in the real regional research registry, then `world/research-publications.js` reviews it on a later real sixday. A local existing institution must be active with available funds; it spends 2 institutional budget units on its own independent check. A successful limited endorsement adds at most one institutional training capacity point and one reputation point; a failed review changes no teaching capacity and still spends money. Even an endorsement never teaches spells, special anatomy, a rank, contracts, titles or a finished artefact. `world.localResearchStatus` and the regional realm summary expose only released, public facts. Distant or unreleased research remains unknown.

**Real NPC-created local methods:** `world/independent-innovations.js` listens to every actual sixday of the already integrated `emergent-people` and `person-careers` worlds. Any verified living worker with at least three distinct successful real work days demonstrating the same ordinary method, earned professional competence and 2 personal funds may conduct a new independently rolled trial. Failure spends resources; another trial requires new real successes. Success records a named, unique, source-sealed modest **mundane vocational technique** and releases a notice after six days. Later work by **that particular creator, in its original region and doing the matching task**, receives a verified +1 practical modifier through the *existing* work procedure. This is the first direct runtime use of an originally generated world technique. Others do not learn it from a title, contact or family link. `world.localInnovationsStatus` and the regional summary expose released local results only.

**Families arise from independent choices:** `world/emergent-households.js` checks existing source-verified, repeated co-present cooperative relationships between two actual generated adults. Each adult independently decides whether to share a voluntary chosen-family household; both must be at the same real place, have enough money, speak a shared language and be willing. Each contributes real funds; monthly household maintenance consumes actual pooled and individual provisions, can ease fatigue modestly and pauses cohabitation benefits if the members live apart. It does **not** fabricate parents, children, marriages, biological ancestry, inheritable constitutions, noble Houses or automatically granted private arts. This is a first *organic social family form*; separate genealogy, birth, marriage, new clan politics and proprietary inheritance mechanics require further genuine consent and source verification. `world.localHouseholdsStatus` and the regional summary reveal published local household facts, not private thoughts.

## What the GM does

The player never needs to ask for an engine operation, follow a fixed list or purchase a compulsory lesson. When real player or NPC activities call for a new invention, field method, family, school or discovery, the GM should read the existing verified design, personal history, independent NPC work receipts, relationships, world locale and meaningful limitations; use an existing protected route where it genuinely covers the intended action. The GM *authentically creates the narrative interpretation* and its participants; the engine determines costs, actual rolls, elapsed world time and stored reality. If an unfamiliar effect cannot be demonstrated by a known adapter, record its plausible research direction and required evidence rather than converting a research title into a working ability.

The source records are **persistent private campaign data**, with read-only local discovery and replay-safe duplicate-turn protection. No existing save or inactive trial is upgraded to a granted power or family by this patch.

## Explicit remaining gaps

This does **not** implement arbitrary on-demand magical technique mechanism generation, high-stage lineages, actual biological family creation, player-founded royal dynasties, instant transfer of NPC-created methods via all trade routes or a general procedural research-lab simulator. The original creation framework remains intentionally bounded where real causation has not yet been modelled. Existing NPC projects and diffusion routes continue; the new dynamic local pathway does not falsely rewrite their independent histories. Expand adapters case by case using actual physical/magical source evidence and end-to-end tests; do not silently pretend full completeness.
