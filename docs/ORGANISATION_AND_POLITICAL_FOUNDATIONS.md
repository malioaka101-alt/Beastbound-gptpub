# Foundation #18 — Organisations, political autonomy and evolving internal decisions (first draft slice)

**Authority:** original Unified Master Prompt §§33–34, 42 and the ordered `docs/MASTER_PROMPT_DEPTH_CHECKLIST.md`. The GM creates unlimited contextual institutions, cultures, offices, alliances, histories and motives. The engine verifies actual existing people, source evidence, real resources, independent decisions and world progression. This is a **first connected building block**, not the complete original political world.

## Reuse actual established structures

`world/emergent-groups.js` already holds an expanding world-native association registry, original founding witness, an independently consenting membership ledger, modest limited finances, monthly effort results and original public knowledge. `world/group-member-agency.js` already supplies its own independent member decision RNG and actual in-person acceptance/departure. `world/person-presence.js` confirms a named participant is **actually present**, rather than inventing travel or witnesses. The original regional Atlas, ecosystems and polity provide contextual physical needs. `world/government-offices.js`, original public hearings, civic petitions and real polity diplomacy govern separate actual sovereign authority; a voluntarily formed association is **not** a government ministry or hereditary ruling family.

## First example: independently reviewed local priorities

A current voluntary association's founder may present an **open-ended** source-witnessed priority relating to a physically real local region. The original real existing individual member must have personally and independently consented to membership earlier, and must genuinely be locally present to hear this new proposal. The actual association member-agency draws **one own persistent independent decision** to support or dissent; the founder cannot supply the answer, vote on behalf of a member, invent a second member, force them to stay or reroll the same question by changing the ID. There are no mandatory profession/house/faction types, meeting fees or secret approval menus. This first adapter operates on witnessed voluntary player-founded groups because they uniquely preserve individually identifiable additional members, rather than pretending the anonymous workforce of an NPC spinoff has named electors.

The original group record retains the precise member consent hash, actor name, genuine local meeting place, original region/ecology/polity conditions, independently chosen support or disagreement, source-sealed draw, unfalsified prior founding charter and the group balance **at the time**. The group may discuss a potential operating-budget allotment not exceeding its saved actual abstract operating `funds`; this is **not** spending, minting, a transfer of Scales or completed group work. Real future work/supplies still require the existing world activity and source-conserving financial systems. A member expressing support does not give them lasting leadership, bind every member, declare war, grant rights or force an NPC into a project. Other groups may preserve dissent and later choose different goals; actual recognition or politics must flow through already existing civic/government/diplomacy mechanisms.

The focused `tests/group-internal-priorities.test.js` uses actual disposable games, original witnessed `social.formGroup`, true in-person individually agreed `social.inviteGroupMember`, an authentic finite player-funded group contribution, original independent NPC RNG for both accepting and dissenting internal reviews, unchanged personal and group money, no forced affiliation, tamper rejection and actual export/import. It joins the complete engine regression as a separate group.
