# Foundation #18 — Organisations, political autonomy and evolving internal decisions (first draft slice)

**Authority:** original Unified Master Prompt §§33–34, 42 and the ordered `docs/MASTER_PROMPT_DEPTH_CHECKLIST.md`. The GM creates unlimited contextual institutions, cultures, offices, alliances, histories and motives. The engine verifies actual existing people, source evidence, real resources, independent decisions and world progression. This is a **first connected building block**, not the complete original political world.

## Reuse actual established structures

`world/emergent-groups.js` already holds an expanding world-native association registry, original founding witness, an independently consenting membership ledger, modest limited finances, monthly effort results and original public knowledge. `world/group-member-agency.js` already supplies its own independent member decision RNG and actual in-person acceptance/departure. `world/person-presence.js` confirms a named participant is **actually present**, rather than inventing travel or witnesses. The original regional Atlas, ecosystems and polity provide contextual physical needs. `world/government-offices.js`, original public hearings, civic petitions and real polity diplomacy govern separate actual sovereign authority; a voluntarily formed association is **not** a government ministry or hereditary ruling family.

## First example: independently reviewed local priorities

A current voluntary association's founder may present an **open-ended** source-witnessed priority relating to a physically real local region. The original real existing individual member must have personally and independently consented to membership earlier, and must genuinely be locally present to hear this new proposal. The actual association member-agency draws **one own persistent independent decision** to support or dissent; the founder cannot supply the answer, vote on behalf of a member, invent a second member, force them to stay or reroll the same question by changing the ID. There are no mandatory profession/house/faction types, meeting fees or secret approval menus. This first adapter operates on witnessed voluntary player-founded groups because they uniquely preserve individually identifiable additional members, rather than pretending the anonymous workforce of an NPC spinoff has named electors.

The original group record retains the precise member consent hash, actor name, genuine local meeting place, original region/ecology/polity conditions, independently chosen support or disagreement, source-sealed draw, unfalsified prior founding charter and the group balance **at the time**. The group may discuss a potential operating-budget allotment not exceeding its saved actual abstract operating `funds`; this is **not** spending, minting, a transfer of Scales or completed group work. Real future work/supplies still require the existing world activity and source-conserving financial systems. A member expressing support does not give them lasting leadership, bind every member, declare war, grant rights or force an NPC into a project. Other groups may preserve dissent and later choose different goals; actual recognition or politics must flow through already existing civic/government/diplomacy mechanisms.

The focused `tests/group-internal-priorities.test.js` uses actual disposable games, original witnessed `social.formGroup`, true in-person individually agreed `social.inviteGroupMember`, an authentic finite player-funded group contribution, original independent NPC RNG for both accepting and dissenting internal reviews, unchanged personal and group money, no forced affiliation, tamper rejection and actual export/import. ## Second connected example — voluntary operational responsibilities (10 November 2026; draft)

Beyond one person's honest support/dissent over an internal priority, a founder of a real *already existing* voluntarily formed group may offer a practical **source-authored, non-exclusive responsibility** to a named member who has personally and independently agreed to membership earlier. A role name and its scope can be created as needed for the world (civic research, shelter coordination, practical witnessing, logistics, scholarship, ecological care or a wholly new future craft); there is **no enumerated job list or hardcoded leader caste**. The member must be independently found at a real original place through the established person-presence rules, in the same polity as their actual group. The original `world/group-member-agency.js` chooses their own willingness, independent of the founder's stated desired result. A refusal is fully valid, and changing the request ID does not reroll an identical same-day offer.

`world/emergent-groups.js` now keeps this historical response directly on the original group's `internalRoles`, retaining the original membership witness hash, genuine present site, actual asking founder, **freely authored title and bounded scope**, member's original decision RNG proof, acceptance/decline, start and end days. This is a modest real operational arrangement; *neither acceptance nor a title establishes completed work, money, allegiance, forced participation, sovereign powers, command over others, a paid appointment, career qualification or access to a magical system*. The member's independent resignation from the organisation ends any current responsibility naturally; an agreed term also ends by world day without inventing a new vote. Later formal political recognition, elections, membership disputes, salaries or resource transactions require original world law, legitimate source and continued independent consent.

`tests/group-voluntary-roles.test.js` exercises actual private disposable social group founding, genuinely independently accepting local NPC membership, personally witnessed actual original NPC presence, valid open role offered as a separate decision, deterministic independent acceptance *and* refusal, preflight without consuming NPC dice, no paid or forced work, no alteration to public rights, preserved Scales and association funds, term expiry, save round-trip, provenance tampering and duplicate-day proposal refusal. ## Third connected example — historical priority attribution to actual work and existing treasury costs (10 November 2026; draft)

A member expressing support for a proposal **does not create successful work or move money**. The original player-founded group's `rules/emergent-groups.js::contribute` retains the actual founder's 45-minute work attempt and their optional, genuinely owned Talon contribution. It may now **optionally** cite an independently personally supported, source-authenticated prior local priority ID, if that member had actually agreed when present. An unsupported or forged ID is rejected **before** advancing time, consuming money or adding an action. Ordinary freeform contributions **still work without any consultation**; dissent from one member is not a universal veto on independent work, just a reason that specific member cannot be named as sponsoring the effort. No hardcoded list of acceptable activities, compulsory committee approval or contract is introduced.

The **existing original group monthly life tick** remains the only source of work success/failure: it validates real `C.event` story witnesses, collects real effort, spends only the actual existing limited group `funds`, and uses its **one already existing independent group d20-equivalent draw**, unchanged. Source-backed `priorityWork` entries on the **same original group** subsequently and only after that genuine tick link the actual original action witness and hash, group-level original independently drawn review and its sealed result, the original month-wide expense (which is NOT charged a second time), and original member support. Attribution does not award a second review, make a member perform the task, retroactively convert a proposed budget to currency, fabricate stock, claim a new member, assert a unanimous policy, or imply a government decree. The expense shown is explicitly the **group-wide original review cost**, not an invented separate expense per priority. Legitimate monthly upkeep remains exactly the original existing charge. Independent priority records remain advisory, and group state retains the actual original success/failure and funding.

The focused `tests/group-priority-real-work-costs.test.js` uses the original rule entrypoint `social.formGroup`, real independent `social.inviteGroupMember` acceptance, actual contributor-owned player coins and 45-minute voluntary activity, the genuinely sourced `deliberateInternalPriority` and one original month `world.advance`. It confirms no free work success or minting from assent, exact independently drawn source result, original group treasury non-increase, no duplicate player charge for reporting, immutable original action+review evidence, original checkpoint export/import, invalid unsupported request anti-mutation and tamper rejection. ## Fourth connected example — voluntary time-limited internal coordination without invented public authority (11 November 2026; draft)

The original `world/emergent-groups.js` founding charter and `founder` remain *historical identity*, not an eternally assumed exclusive mandate. A group may now **offer a strictly limited operational coordination term** to a genuine current, individually consenting member who is personally present at a real original location. The member independently chooses to accept or refuse with the existing `world/group-member-agency.js` persistent individual RNG; the GM may author a meaningful local scope and term but cannot predetermine the response. This preserves dynamic internal leadership without inventing a royal, legal, guild-monopoly or hereditary right. The actual founding person is **never erased or replaced by a new role**.

The original group stores source-sealed `coordinationMandates` with founding charter hash, personally consenting member witness, actual region/site, founder identity, time-bounded practical scope, existing group state, the original independent draw, and dated acceptance/refusal. A read-only `coordinatorStatus` derives the currently active limited coordinator from actual history: an accepted member can temporarily coordinate optional study/research meetings within the recorded term, while the original founder remains the historical founder. If the term expires or the member genuinely withdraws from membership, the default reported state reverts to the original founder without generating a new appointment. Duplicate same-day offers cannot reroll personal consent. Approval **does not** permit unilateral government commands, automatically spend the group treasury, force work, bind dissenting people/beasts, grant magical systems, or confer employment rights.

This is an initial source-backed **operational leadership handoff**, *not yet* a constitutional election, hereditary succession, legal office recognition, executive power transfer, inter-polity authority, fully ratified council or binding majority rule. Other groups can use their own real witnessed cultural rules later, not a universal prescribed president/council structure. The new disposable `tests/group-voluntary-coordination.test.js` uses actual original group founding, independent in-person member assent and a separate acceptance/refusal of the temporary role. It tests honest term expiration, no founder rewriting, no compelled actions or money transfers, save/import, invalid source anti-reroll and tamper resistance.
