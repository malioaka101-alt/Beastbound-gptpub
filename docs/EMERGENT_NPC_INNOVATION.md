# Emergent ordinary-person innovation and institutional rivalry — first playable world loop

**Status:** A deliberately bounded first implemented slice of the [Living World Change Charter](LIVING_WORLD_CHANGE_CHARTER.md), not a claim to complete the original Master Prompt's full generational social simulation. Original canonical biology, human power ladders, intelligent-beast rights and established campaign histories remain controlling. The three worker projects below are **new Beastbound world authoring attached to already existing named NPCs and institutions**, not invented lore from Jory's campaign or a copied manhwa.

## Why this matters

The original prototype already let 48 background professionals gradually accrue training points, but it would not let their concrete work meaningfully change their world. Great houses, official institutions and powerful teachers risked becoming static winners. This extension creates **actual material, independently decided, lasting social mobility from skilled working people**. It also respects failures, losses and reversible gains. A family surname, money paid to a master, a title or high cultivation stage is **not a prerequisite** for ordinary innovation.

The first documented starting practitioners are the existing field specialists `reedmarch_field`, `crownspine_field`, and `saltleague_field`, seeded from `world/notables.js`'s **existing 48 personal records**. Their local practical goals are respectively: a **wetland-crossing inspection fieldbook**, a **mountain refuge rope checklist**, and a **three-language harbour-accounting workbook**. These are modest useful *professional methods* within the original polity crafts, **not** newly verified supernatural Arts, Origin affinities, inheritable constitutions, discovered evolution branches, untested medical cures or abilities the player suddenly receives.

## Actual development rather than elapsed-time achievements

Each real world month, the professionals' projects consume **2–3 actual budget points from their real employer or later independently founded circle**, provided the organisation is operating and can actually afford to support the work. Four or more true months of professional effort and at least eight contributed material-budget points precede one **bounded actual independent d20 field trial**. The roll uses the NPC's **previously earned professional skill rank**, the relevant organisation's real training capacity and the host polity's real stability, plus a modest extra challenge for alpine safety work. The project keeps the roll, difficulty, modifier, exact sponsor, spent resources, initial and resulting capacity, current day/month, actor and method in a **SHA-256 witnessed event record**.

- A successful trial creates one **limited, tested vocational method** and can increase that real organisation's training capacity and public reputation by **one point each**. A release-lagged guild report may carry the named worker's accomplishment to local people, who may subsequently use or compete with the method. It grants no Circle, Meridian Gate, degree, title or beast transformation.
- An unsuccessful trial consumes the actual preparation and resources and produces **no known method**. A rare severe mishap can interrupt the NPC's next month of professional work. Funding collapse, temporary institutional suspension and illness cause stalled work and gradual regression, not arbitrary project success. The same individual can recover and attempt a better version later.
- NPC initiatives draw from a **persistent independent world RNG stream**, never the player's combat RNG. Exactly one month updates a project once, in the world clock's genuine chronological order. Saves contain the particular outcomes; the GM cannot pick a good result after seeing a roll.

## New entrants and a changing status quo

**After at least two successful documented trials**, the practitioner may gradually attract real colleague interest, and existing workers may independently decide to transfer from the employing institution into a newly formed **public vocational circle**. Formation is not guaranteed and does not magically create labour: twelve people actually leave the old organisation and provide a first **twelve budget points of voluntary member dues** to the new group. The new group can teach, conduct further practical work and gain or lose participants as the real local economy and competitor's reputation change. The worker's professional practice shifts to their actual new circle, so it isn't permanently dependent on a single incumbent academy.

The independent circle then has its own **members, dues, upkeep, funds, capacity, reputation, operating status and financial risk**, rather than a decorative reputation flag. People may move between it and the earlier institution (numbers are conserved). The new group may **suspend services** when it cannot afford to operate and **reopen those services** when its dues and reserves genuinely recover; outright legal dissolution is not yet implemented. If its **participation AND tested reputation exceed the former organisation for three consecutive months**, a genuine public notice records an alternative local vocational training provider surpassing its original incumbent. The standing is **reversible** if people later choose differently. This is an important *real first step* toward changing the social status quo; it does **not** instantly confiscate a noble estate, cancel a guild charter, replace the king or force the established school to close.

Progress remains **causal and contingent**, rather than one new great clan each year. Talent, money, access, research reliability, available colleagues, public needs, accidents, economies and competing organisations all matter. The player may join, ignore, oppose or encourage such trends **when future real interactions support those choices**. This implementation does not invent an automatic player-enrolment operation or decree personal relationships.

## Visibility and truthful history

`world.initiativesStatus({})` is **strictly read-only**. It gives a character only the **published local notices** that are supported by original dated study, transfer and competition records **and** the existing world public-news ledger after their actual **eight-day circulation delay**. No unpublished NPC goals, secret institutional budgets, undisclosed failures, roll seeds or absent foreign-city intelligence are disclosed. The private ledger records the exact worker, source institution, method, roll inputs and outcome, and an SHA-256 provenance hash, so time and failure remain real.

Existing saves with no `agentInitiatives` record are not rewritten during a read. They lazily acquire the new project state when an actual later **world month tick** occurs; this does not fabricate prior years of forgotten discoveries. The real `w.notables`, `w.institutions`, `w.countries` and `w.agentInitiatives.entrants` remain consistent. Only three initial source-grounded projects are implemented, not a new mechanical requirement that every NPC must have the same research agenda.

## Important unfinished areas

This first implementation does **not yet provide** universally generative NPC ambitions, original new power disciplines with actual validated anatomy, research truth behind unverified theories, player-accessible invention partnerships, a living local contracting economy, real professional qualification transfers, dynasty births and deaths, emergent kingdoms, new family genealogies, enforceable laws, or complete regional trade-network reaction to innovations. Historical fame may later spread through real witness routes, rivals and apprentices. Those should follow the same evidence, growth, regression and open-ended freedom requirements instead of becoming a new set of arbitrary training locks.

## Tests

`tests/agent-initiatives.test.js` covers 24-state coexistence with three sourced practitioners, real budget depletion and failed checks, published technical successes after actual elapsed time, deterministic world replay and preservation of player resources, a verified route for ordinary practitioners to form new independent organisations, conservation of member transfers, and **both directions of local institutional competition** with no inherited rank or magical power. A separate private file-backed test checks non-mutating public-status requests and rejects hidden-outcome injection. The entire pre-existing CI suite remains mandatory. No live campaign save is altered.
