# Foundation #18 — original association funds: one audited real balance

**Authority:** original 57-section Beastbound Unified Master Prompt §§33–36, established `world/emergent-groups.js` voluntary membership/work/funding and original `rules/emergent-groups.js::contribute`, actual player-owned Talons and the original monthly world work/upkeep processor. The GM writes open-ended organisations, their histories, motives, cultures and scenes; the engine confirms original sources, amounts, noncoercion and conservation. **Draft, not acceptance of #18 and not a complete banking/credit/property system.**

## Reuse the original account rather than minting a second treasury

Existing voluntary player-founded associations already have one actual `e.funds` operating balance. The founder's original `social.groupContribute` operation can transfer a genuine finite integer amount of already-held player Talons, already debiting `s.player.coins` and crediting the **same existing** `e.funds` with no additional currency or source of income. The original independently resolved monthly work already debits only available operating funds, and the normal group month independently incurs a small actual upkeep cost. **This slice does not create new payment permissions, banking, independent contracts, shares, legal title, wages or a group income roll.**

What was missing was a durable, independently inspectable provenance chain after the short original world/group journals rotate.

The original association record now optionally retains `treasuryAudit` with an honest opening `e.funds` anchor and each real balance movement: individual founder payment in actual Talons, original independent real group-work costs, and original monthly upkeep. Every receipt is source-sealed and checked against the preceding closing balance and the association's **single actual current fund balance**. A real contribution preserves the original player story event and event digest, the actual pre/post player purse balances, the exact sum removed/added and genuine group identity. The independent work debit stores the **original** group-month review including its single unchanged independent result/draw and actual expense, not a second roll. The existing month upkeep is recorded from that actual monthly tick/member count, without a fabricated external invoice. Each source can be recorded only once; an attempted duplicate is rejected, and creating a receipt never itself moves money.

Older saves with a nonzero group balance and no audit are **not rewritten with imaginary donations or spending**. At the first later genuinely grounded money movement, a transparent starting anchor preserves the actual preexisting balance, labels previous movements as unreconstructed and attests only subsequent events. A group with no audit can be inspected read-only without automatically inventing one. This is historical honesty, not proof of all previously unobserved funds.

## Actual rule and GM visibility

`social.groupTreasuryStatus({groupId})` is a **read-only** original chat-runner operation. Only the actual local player founder of a source-verified player-founded group can inspect their existing group operating balance, opening anchor and original movement receipts. It cannot instruct other members to pay, withdraw other owners' property, access an NPC spinoff's independent treasury, obtain a royal title or convert accounting units into Scales. The status's sources reflect only the original real operational costs; this is not automatic proof of legal ownership, insurance, creditworthiness, profit or spend authorisation by a public ministry.

There are **no hardcoded supported organisation types**: a GM-authored craft circle, kin group, sanctuary society, academy, trade association or any other future voluntary association can use the same existing founding/consent/work and financial ledger when a genuine original paying actor/source exists. No member is required to hire a mentor, pay membership fees or obey a majority.
