# A self-directed fieldworker journey: signs → reeds → medicine

**Authority:** Unified Master Prompt §§11,35,44–45,49–50,51. The original Table Rules require free-form player intent, original checks, real time, ecological consequences, genuine materials, and save continuity.

## Existing connected procedures, now usable in ordinary ChatGPT actions

A player standing in **actual open wilderness** can say **"survey habitat"** or **"inspect habitat signs"**. The existing `world.habitatSurvey` procedure observes source-authored local terrain, costs real time and stamina, and establishes exactly one current, source-witnessed possible Survival check. It grants no animal identity or loot.

The player can then say **"interpret habitat signs"**, running the **original** `core.check` for the one genuinely pending local observation. A success is not chosen by the player, and an inconclusive check is recorded honestly. **"field notes"** shows only personally observed and interpreted local evidence, without spending time or revealing unseen animals. The interface is not a new Research power.

After a **genuine successful fen survey**, the player may say **"gather reeds"** or **"harvest marsh reeds"**. The original `world.harvestReeds` procedure verifies the **same actual original observed fieldwork and original Survival die**. Each **separate** act of gathering spends another 45 minutes and four stamina, yields **one common marsh reed**, and increases the real fen disturbance. Multiple separate gathers may follow a genuine successful, still-recent survey while **real stamina and habitat conditions permit**. The first-ever harvest for a day remains recorded in its historical field, while every modern attempt has a separate checkpointed source receipt. No fresh dice, magical crystals, minerals, beasts, Grades or currency are created simply by repeating an action. The once-per-day habitat-*survey* preparation safeguard remains in the current bounded observation example to prevent rolling again on the same evidence; it is not a universal ban on investigating or gathering in other genuine ways.

Once two **actually owned** reeds and one ration have been obtained—potentially through **two legitimate harvests from one real field survey** and the original witnessed in-person provisioner trade—the established `craft field poultice` action at **either of two established clean medical facilities**—the Lumenfall Annex clinic-laboratory or Morrowfen Crossing infirmary—still consumes them, spends 70 minutes and resolves **one original Crafting check**, yielding a healing balm *only on genuine success*. This is not a complete recipe system: a GM can recognise other plausible paths while recording a real source procedure before promising a mechanical result.

The same ordinary action can be retried with a unique new turn ID **only where it is a genuinely new action**, not to reroll a consumed survey or a spent set of ingredients. Save-checkpoint replay by the original turn ID remains idempotent. The natural-language interface merely makes the pre-existing engine mechanics usable; it cannot choose original roll, check DC, harvest quantity, tool, recipe ingredients, success or species encounter.

## Verification and open gaps

`tests/reed-harvest.test.js` exercises a disposable real player going from current habitat signs through the original Survival roll, safely repeatable independent gathering, a local status journal, first daily receipt preservation and private file-backed replay. `tests/real-supplies-to-medicine-journey.test.js` verifies an additional complete ordinary gameplay path from **the existing real Orrin provisioner quote and paid ration**, to two independently time- and stamina-funded fen harvests from one genuine survey, to the same authentic Lumenfall laboratory craft with **materials spent even on failure**. A second source-backed full journey at the *original crossing infirmary* is now verified by `tests/shared-physical-work-journeys.test.js`, sharing the very same material-work executor as physical prototype assembly and repair. Old first-of-day records remain readable and later attempts never overwrite them. The full 97-test private single-runner CI gate remains required for any engine merge.

Additional original Master Prompt foundations still outstanding include varied authentic herbs and minerals, realistic nonarbitrary ecological yields, crafting beyond these two clean medical facilities only where actually sufficient clean tools and conditions exist, material quality and durability, stronger permanent provenance under narrative-log rotation, credible local goods markets, more local medical knowledge and real injury treatment. No active Aerin save is used in this development.
