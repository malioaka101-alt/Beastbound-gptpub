## Acceptance invariant

A skill, anatomical stage, witnessed breakthrough, weapon readiness, beast bond, spell or human evolution becomes GM-known **only after its original and current source owners jointly verify it**. Historical source seals and genuine resource costs are necessary but **not automatically sufficient**: identity, place, opportunity, personal consent, clinical countertests and physical exhaustion must also be real.

A full-run result from an earlier commit does not replace the exact-head final acceptance requirement. An unusually unlucky NPC may legitimately stall or withdraw rather than producing a guaranteed high stage. Saved future #189 mechanical rework is **not** an excuse to retroactively change #188's original law.
