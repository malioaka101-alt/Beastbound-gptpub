# Foundation #18 — source-accountable consented coordinator invitations, not command power

**Authority:** original Unified Beastbound Master Prompt §§33–34, 42; current Table Rules. The GM authors a freeform organisation, its real present people, their complex motives, the open-ended work opportunity and the scene. The original game engine, **not GM assertions or dialogue**, resolves who actually consented, time, private independent answers and later real work.

## Build on the same organisation, consent, role, task and time engines

The accepted foundation already supports actual separately consenting members, limited real individual operational coordinator mandates, original-person-present rules, optional work invitations, original world-tick independent volunteer labour and source-hashed group work records. **This extension stays inside those same records.** No second association/task/finance/command engine, no new caste/class menu, no predetermined categories of group or universally required office.

A previously genuinely accepting and still-active operational coordinator may be personally **asked** by the actual founder in an 20-minute local scene to consider making a specific freeform work offer to a different, physically present, previously consenting member. The role is not an appointment to hereditary, legal or civic office; no command over anyone follows. The original `social.coordinatorTaskRequest` action confirms **both people** are actually present, have current active membership, that the coordinator's earlier decision genuinely accepted their freeform bounded mandate, and that its term remains active. Unrelated characters, expired/refused roles and already-reviewed same-day tasks are refused *before* consuming time or a new choice.

The coordinator **independently** chooses whether to make the offer using the established original `group-member-agency.js` RNG. The contextual chance responds to their *authored* genuine role scope, the new freely described task, the group's actual operational state and prior earned results. It is not a hardcoded gate forbidding novel tasks, professional credentials, money or a mandatory teacher. Only if the coordinator actually chooses to offer does the other member **independently** accept, consider, or decline, using the original volunteer-task decision rules. A refused offer is a real decision without falsely forcing the recipient to answer. No one can include desired NPC outcomes in the action parameters.

The original `world/emergent-groups.js` **voluntaryTasks ledger** saves the original 20-minute story witness, both NPCs' previously separately witnessed membership, the actually accepted mandate and immutable scope, physical presence, authored task text, separate original issuer/recipient draws, original member-agency state before and after, real world day and expiry. Replayed history validates against the original source; resealing a chosen NPC answer cannot replace an independently resolved draw. The original existing `eligibleVoluntaryTask`, original monthly volunteered work, and original `retainOriginalTaskService` are reused unchanged, so an accepted invitation **may** later link to genuinely worked 90-minute original service, but does not guarantee time, outcome, knowledge, money, permission or success.

## Realistic starting examples, never a capped content list

One organically founded marsh-survey circle has two individually consenting participants. One independently agrees to a temporary, scoped operational coordinator role. When the founder facilitates a local scene, the coordinator may agree to request shared river-crossing observations from the other member, decline to offer, or hear the other's actual decline. The exact proposal and organisation's purpose can vary freely, and original membership, founder history, treasury balance and public authority remain intact.

The same source and consent structure applies to *any* genuinely developed guild, school, laboratory, community group, sect, sanctuary or other plausible institution; no hard-coded number of types. Future independent nonplayer-founded organisations may use the original existing evidence-adapter extension when their actual people and source-backed decisions exist. No simulated extra NPC identities or invented policies are added by these fixtures.
