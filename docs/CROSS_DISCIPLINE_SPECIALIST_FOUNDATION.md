Authority: **original Unified Master Prompt §§19, 26, 29**. Unlike a universal rank ladder, Meridian anatomy, Arcane Mana Core matrices, Soul identity, living Body tissues, Beast-Origin material, Rift physics and distinct specialist crafts develop independently. ## First observed interaction: an individual's original Meridian–Arcane circulation test

The narrow initial `rules/cross-discipline-coherence.js` adapter connects **the already genuine first Lower Meridian Gate** (`rules/meridian-qi-strike.js::gate`, requiring original anatomical source records) and **the already genuine First Arcane Circle** (`rules/arcane-spark.js::curriculum`, requiring its source-dated learned Core/Circle). Merely possessing Qi and Mana or editing a stage number does not establish these. A real GM independent scene observation must report the specific existing Gate, actual individual Circle geometry, existing tissue/Qi route, Mana Core output route, **separate Qi-only and Mana-only baselines**, measured personal observed phase response and an independent reversal/countertest with heat, metabolic supply, recovery and pressure failure.

`cross.qiManaExperiment` accepts only the prior witnessed event index and derives the original difficulty from actual Gate quality, existing Core stability, independent measured interaction and accumulated individual strain. Exactly **one original engine d20** decides a bounded physical calibration. The first observed example costs **3 existing Qi, 3 existing Mana, 2 actual Stamina and 30 real world minutes**, including on unsuccessful trials, and can cause an actual wound and accumulated circulation strain on severe failure. A successful, independently observed `coherent` interaction creates a **60-minute original locally observed coherent Qi/Mana field**. `interfering` or `unsafe` findings never magically transform into a coherent effect merely because a check succeeded. The field is a **measured transitory physical state**, not a learned spell or a rule-exempt +X to every action; any effect on an existing Ward, Formation, weapon art or spell requires a separately source-supported adapter. A read-only `cross.qiManaStatus` lists actually observed fields and strain without reading thoughts or asserting secrets.

The first documented costs, interaction categories and temporary field are **initial measured examples**, not universal values, a compulsory school, a fixed list of hybrid combinations or an inference that all meridians share the same Mana compatibility. New biological routes, Soul/Arcane couplings, Body/Meridian support, Origin conflict, Rift/formations, runic libraries, alchemy, weapon intent and healing interfaces need their own witnessed physical/cultural sources, interoperable original resource ledgers, actual effects, consent where relevant and independent countertests. Specialists can excel professionally without a corresponding combat tier.

`tests/cross-discipline-qi-mana-coherence.test.js` covers genuinely source-recorded prior Gate/Circle fixture, original Qi/Mana/Stamina costs, real one-roll checks, individual interference modes, refusal of selected guaranteed result/bonus, tamper rejection, source persistence through log rollover and expiration. The test is **first** in the full engine regression list. **#16 remains unchecked** until this and later expandable cross-disciplinary applications are accepted; the first slice is draft and no active campaign saves changed.


## Second measured interaction: independent Meridian distribution supports an already learned Arcane Ward

`rules/cross-ward-interface.js` adds a **separately witnessed source-specific application**, without automatically turning a temporary coherence field into an attack, spell or universal numerical advantage. The user must already possess both genuine individually earned Gate/Circle structural evidence, have actually established an active `coherent` Qi–Mana field, and **independently learned the existing real First-Circle Arcane Ward formula** through the original `rules/arcane-ward.js::profile` verification. Mere core stability or a fabricated Ward name is insufficient.

The GM must first have witnessed a specifically measured Qi-to-Ward distribution, the exact original existing Ward geometry, an independent Ward-only baseline and a separately observed adverse-phase countertest, human tissue/Core limits, and a genuine single-casting bound. `cross.wardCalibrate` then spends **1 real Qi, 1 real Mana, 1 real Stamina and 10 real minutes** and makes **one actual original independent calibration check**. A successfully source-verified calibration lasts only during the observed underlying coherence and the separately measured short time window; an unsuccessful attempt spends real resources and cannot create a support.

On one later real `arcane_ward` combat casting, the already learned original Ward may receive **a source-bound +1 to its existing casting check modifier**, with the same original Ward DC, Mana payment, three maximum Ward steps, single existing casting roll and normal one-exchange expiry. Its one real original Ward cast produces an anchored receipt even if it fails, consuming the calibration. No attacker or recipient can select an interface bonus, replay its source receipt, trigger a second cast, conjure an additional Ward or stack extra physical magnitudes. This effect is tied to this *specific independently observed first Meridian–Ward distribution*. Other credible uses require their own witnessed source and independently tested physical adapter, not a global merit bonus for every skill or spell.

`tests/cross-ward-interface.test.js` is first in mandatory CI, exercising genuine prior first-Gate/first-Circle identity, separately learned Ward, individually witnessed coherence and Ward countertest, separate calibration resource costs, one real source-adjusted original combat Ward casting, one-use handling, no roll duplication, save tamper rejection and original evidence retained after journal rotation. **This second slice is a draft until the full latest exact-head regression passes; #16 remains unchecked** pending wider specialist and intersystem coverage. It does not impose mandatory tuition or a universal hybrid cap.


## Third independently testable specialist link — original layered Runes and Sigils (10 November 2026)

`rules/specialist-rune-sigils.js` begins **Master Prompt §26's distinct symbol-vocabulary specialist discipline** without rebranding ordinary Arcane spellcasting as a rune. A GM must have independently observed the individual symbol vocabulary, one actual physical first binding layer, a *separate* Mana activation layer, mineral compatibility and material ownership, the measured practical output, failed reverse-layer countertest, genuine discharge logic and physical heat/stability limits. A player cannot simply name a new symbol, choose a DC or assert a limitless effect. The engine validates the already earned original First-Circle Mana Core only for **this particular Mana-excited mineral example**, not as a universal requirement on all runic traditions.

This first rune adapter requires a previously possessed `moss_crystal` plus `ironbloom` via the existing shared **physical-work executor**. Original Crafting and Arcana competence and a real d20, world-time and Stamina decide whether an individual layered mineral sigil genuinely forms; its source is retained even on failed construction and the materials are genuinely consumed. The resulting one **physical object** is anchored to the present actual location and its distinctive original observed symbol source. `specialist.sigilActivate` independently consumes original Mana and living Stamina, elapsed time and the original activation check. On successful excitation it emits a **locally visible source-measured light signal** for a limited finite interval. Its mineral discharge cycles are finite; actual failure consumes a cycle rather than granting a free reroll. The signal reveals no hidden actors, does not teleport, summon, grant another spell, add free Mana or award specialist ranks. Long-campaign receipts survive normal rolling narrative journal history; more than one independent inscription and real interleaved activations can coexist without duplicating physical materials.

The demonstrated two-layer `moss_crystal`/`ironbloom` mechanism, local light, three usable discharge cycles and original costs are **one specific materially observed mechanism**, not a fixed runic vocabulary, mandatory academy, guild, purchase, universal cap or model for all crafts. Layered defensive sigils, multi-node formations, alchemical reactions, living biological interfaces, weapon intent, elemental transformations, and healing repair need their **own** independently measured physical effects and appropriate access rights; none can be claimed from the existence of this first local signal. The new disposable `tests/specialist-rune-sigils.test.js` must run explicitly first in the exact-head regression inventory. **#16 remains draft, unchecked and unmerged until test acceptance and further genuine cross-system/specialist foundations.** No active campaign save touched.
