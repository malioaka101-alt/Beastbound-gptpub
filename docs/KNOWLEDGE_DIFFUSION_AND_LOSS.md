# Beastbound — evidence-backed diffusion and loss of ordinary knowledge

## Status and source boundary

This is a first implemented link between *actual NPC innovation*, *existing physical trade routes*, *another named independent professional*, and *a real adopting institution*. It enforces `docs/LIVING_WORLD_CHANGE_CHARTER.md`'s requirements that discoveries can spread unevenly, rivals can emerge, knowledge can stall or be lost, and no institution keeps permanent innovation dominance merely by being famous. The source Master Prompt establishes a changing international world and independent research, but **does not name these three specific cross-border adaptations**. They are new, geographically constrained Beastbound regional authoring—not imported anime or Jory lore. No character inherits a supernatural trait.

The already-implemented `world/agent-initiatives.js` provides **real named NPC field trials** that consume real institutional resources, pass or fail actual checked rolls, and publish date-stamped local notices. This extension *does not* create an imaginary foreign research result. It checks the original successful field-trial event hash, actual d20 result and DC, matching source professional, genuine source institutional work, plus the real originating local journal notice and its **eight-day publication lag**. Only after these prerequisites may the published method travel through an actually connected, functioning trade route.

These uses are adaptations, not proof a foreign technique works unchanged, and do not grant an origin method to everybody. For the initial model, a trader can bring public vocational documents through a live route only when the route is not disrupted, its flow is at least 32, and risk is below 80. There are no universal tuition prices, enforced sect membership, always-friendly teachers, magical knowledge transfer or static academy monopolies.

## Reproduction can work or fail

A receiving professional reads an actually documented published foreign method; the historical witnessed source is recorded once in `table.realm.knowledgeDiffusion` so older source reports need not remain inside a short rolling news buffer forever. **Hearing of a method does not instantly teach it.** At least three genuine separate months of work cost the real receiving institution **2–3 budget units each month**. The adapter then makes one persistent independent d20 practical check against DC 15, using their actual prior professional rank, target capacity, route flow, real nation stability and funding stress. A success may grant **one modest local training-capacity point** and publishes a delayed, named *local adaptation*, not a brand-new magic, cure or universally known technique. Failure costs spent money and time, records a verified negative trial and requires another actual period of preparation.

Public recognition follows the local eight-day publication delay. **`world.diffusionStatus({})` is strictly read-only** and shows only genuinely published local source-hashed adoption, failure or loss reports. It does not reveal the hidden next trial date, research rolls, private institution funds, foreign secret findings, or arbitrarily selected player success. Publication outside the player's current region does not make it immediately known to them. The private project and scientific record persist across future world months.

## Decline, loss and recovery

An actually adopted practice can remain locally viable even if its foreign trade route closes, **provided local professional support and budgets remain healthy**. It may lapse after **two sustained months** of local institute shutdown, severe resource depletion, or a combined trade disruption **and** insufficient local support. Closure of the external route alone does not erase a locally mastered method. At that time the originally gained single capacity point is removed, and a truthful local notice records the lapse. The original method is not retroactively declared never invented, and the other region's actual origin remains history. A local practitioner may, if conditions later permit, invest again and pass **another real independent practical trial** to reestablish the practice. Neither decline nor rediscovery is guaranteed on a preset timer.

The small model does **not** yet implement multi-hop global scholarly networks, autonomous new disciplines, family medical secrets, full technical licensing law, intergovernmental research treaties, large-scale productivity shifts, automatic player memberships or generational knowledge preservation. Those need their own genuine events and consent. It also does not certify actual supernatural progress; the original Meridian, Arcane, Soul and beast-evolution rules remain authoritative.

The regression suite `tests/knowledge-diffusion.test.js` tests genuine earlier published trials, 24-state source coexistence, legal route connectivity, independency and determinism, real foreign funding and re-rolls, inaccessible or disrupted trade, both success and failure, honest capacity loss and verified recovery, no leaked source plans, no unearned family/cultivation power and read-only file-backed private chat turns. No active campaign save is edited.
