# Independently consenting NPCs in open-ended living organisations


## Extended actual person attendance (7 November 2026)

The earlier *five physically authored NPC* limitation is now partly broadened: named independent world professionals may be on real, saved, independently scheduled local duty appointments. `social.localPeopleStatus({})` is read-only and shows only personally available, physically established people here and now; `social.inviteGroupMember({groupId,npcId,proposal})` may offer membership to one who is truly present, without forcing their answer. A public professional list, a distant famous name or a founder's invented story cannot count as presence or consent. Most polities still lack playable local encounters: this is **not** complete character generation, free NPC travel, family adoption or full social agency. See [Witnessed local professionals](WITNESSED_LOCAL_PROFESSIONALS.md). No campaign save is modified just because this capability exists.

## Source scope and freedom

Beastbound's Master Prompt, Table Rules and Living World Change Charter require free choice, individual NPC agency, family formation, consequential development and the right to leave. **The 28 chartered houses and the five currently interactable local NPCs are starting examples, not the total population of families or possible members.** This patch is the first directly playable **NPC consent link** between `world/emergent-groups.js` (PR #86) and the engine's existing actually located canonical people. It is not the full character population, detailed family law, marriage, romantic relationships, adoptive kinship or a generic right to draft any NPC into a group. The exact independent response formulas are new, limited simulator rules, not a universal personality theory or canon reaction for a named NPC.

A character can make a new informal clan, household circle, study group, guild, shelter association, religious fellowship, travelling school or other group through `social.formGroup({name,kind,purpose})` using their own words, no forced category menu or entrance fee. This patch adds `social.inviteGroupMember({groupId,npcId,proposal})` to let the founder personally offer **freeform terms** to a real NPC already physically grounded in the current game location. The NPC must genuinely exist, be at that place, and the player must not be in an unresolved combat/wildlife encounter or incapacitated. There is **no mandatory initial conversation token, guild teacher, noble sponsor, matching job title, fixed wealth gate or universal family category**.

The first source-witness provider covers the five already authored `engine.js` NPCs at Morrowfen Crossing, the Academy and the Ruins. Other individuals can join through the underlying existing independent `registerMemberConsentAdapter` hook after a **new truthful NPC meeting/availability system** establishes their own locations, wishes and ability to consent. **Do not interpret the five-person interface as evidence other families or potential colleagues cannot exist.** No fictional member, heir, teacher or powerful ancestor is introduced merely to fill a missing slot.

## A personal offer is not permission to assign someone a life

Each meaningful in-person invitation takes **18 genuine minutes** and records the player's actual proposal, witnessed location, named individual and their independent answer. The answer is computed using the NPC's real profession/goal, thematic relevance of the freely written proposal, earlier authentic personal meetings, the group's actually completed work and real local stability, with persistent separate **world-affiliation RNG**. Word matching is a soft contextual factor—not a requirement to include a magic keyword; no proposal guarantees acceptance. Earlier repeated refusals can discourage pressure, without permanent exclusion. Someone may:

- **Accept** a clearly limited *nonexclusive voluntary membership*. The record contains the exact personal affirmative reply and a separate original source witness, joined member identity, hash and date. They do **not** automatically quit their existing profession, abandon other responsibilities, move their physical location, surrender secrets, marry, become biological relatives, inherit power or transfer legal rights.
- **Decline**. The founder hears the actual answer; no member is created, their coins are not charged, and professional refusal doesn't mean permanent hatred, expulsion or irreversible enmity.
- **Consider** an offer without joining. This is deliberately not a fabricated promise that a subsequent automatic success will occur. A future genuine approach may be possible as circumstances change.

An actual person can answer one new group-membership proposal per group per in-world day, preventing mechanical reroll-farming of the same instant. The founder may approach again on a later day; no mandatory multi-week registrar cooldown or universal fixed teacher price blocks them. This one-day conversation boundary is about meaningful personal assent, **not** a restriction on other freeform NPC dialogue.

`social.groupMembersStatus({groupId})` gives the group's original founder only witnessed invitation replies and currently *reported* individual affiliations. It never discloses hidden future NPC decisions or remote immediate resignations. The ordinary `social.groupsStatus({})` remains a read-only local entity list and never treats membership as a legal hereditary charter.

## NPC contributions and genuinely voluntary departures

After an NPC genuinely joins, `world/group-member-agency.js` advances their possible involvement on actual **world-month ticks**, even if the player is doing something else. A named member may choose to contribute **90 actual in-world minutes of their own voluntary community service**, but only when their existing NPC location and the group's polity support a genuinely local activity. Their practical success or failure requires an independent source-stamped d20 check using **limited** thematic professional relevance and real host stability. A true modest success can improve the group's existing influence and reduce public-duty pressure slightly, with locally delayed dated notice. Failed effort earns no such benefit. **No phantom NPC salary, magically created funds, skills, tuition, magical techniques or personal XP transfer** is claimed.

A group may also experience a genuine sustained resource/inactivity crisis, repeated documented failed work, or severe regional instability. A member whose actual circumstances warrant reconsideration may **independently choose** to step away. Their earlier original assent is kept, and a separately hashed world-event witness, reason, date and delayed public announcement document the departure. They are never made to leave merely because a calendar birthday passes, nor can a founder force their refusal to be reinterpreted. The original founder can learn about an offscreen departure **after a local public notice**, not through unjustified omniscience; the public association member count may legitimately lag until notice.

Crucially, **a person who freely left can later choose to return**. The prior membership and departure remain in the history; a *new personally witnessed acceptance after the departure date* creates the new valid membership period. Neither earlier refusal nor withdrawal is a permanent "never join this kind of group again" game barrier.

## Provenance and limits

The underlying open registry still supports **arbitrary future source-backed origin and membership adapters**, so a newly met healer, intelligent-beast delegate, adopted relation, professional colleague or local civic volunteer can later become a group participant on their own terms. In this slice, **player-facing invitation eligibility is limited to the five currently physically established engine NPCs** because other named world professionals do not yet have grounded actual scene positions. Generalising that character-presence system is a future task. An NPC's acceptance is not automatically a marriage, an apprentice contract, legal family membership or any hereditary genetic/supernatural assertion. Complex formal admission constitutions, member representation and binding obligations need source-grounded, culturally appropriate mechanisms, not a compulsory generic guild form.

The persistent original agreement remains auditable across engine exports, load/save, and the rolling 90-event player journal; departures retain separate witnesses. Read-only status operations do not save or reroll NPC decisions. `tests/group-membership.test.js` and `tests/group-membership-runner.test.js` check authentic in-person acceptance, refusal and deferral, re-approach without predetermined outcome, voluntary work success/failure, stress-related departure, true re-acceptance, delayed notice visibility, no free rank/ancestry, deterministic independent RNG, file-backed chat-turn idempotence and original seed-world invariants. The existing full GitHub Actions regression suite remains mandatory. No active campaign saves have been edited.
