# Finish Line A — physically owned materials offered to independently present people

**Purpose:** Generalize ordinary material transfer across actual people, not create another one-off merchant. The same proven player inventory source is shared with natural gathering, planting, crafting, medicine and wild-animal feeding. The Master Prompt §45 requires genuine d20 checks only for uncertain **player** feats and §50 tracks actual item ownership; §§35, 43–44 and 47 require independent people and real world choices. A person's voluntary response is not decided by the player's Charisma die, predetermined script or a negotiated fictional reward.

## Ordinary play

The player may say **give clean rations to Orrin Vale**, **offer rations to Orrin**, **hand marsh reed to Professor Sera**, or use **give / offer / hand / pass** with any originally sourced `E.RESOURCES` material and an **actually present** named person. It resolves the intended person against the same real-local presence used by ordinary conversation (authored NPCs, source-verified independent workers and genuinely scheduled named people). **Generic give/offer food to a witnessed wild creature still uses the old species-aware independent wild-animal interaction**, and no human name may be reinterpreted as that creature.

The protected `social.materialHandoff({npcId,item})` action requires authentic co-presence during the real public duty window, no immediate conflict, physical ownership, and one unforced independent decision by that person. The existing `rules/unified.js` item planner removes **exactly one item only if accepted**. Refusal leaves player inventory intact. A true conversation costs six minutes regardless of the person's response; repeating exactly the same proposal with no changed context cannot fish for a different outcome or cost another decision. A later change in situation may make another offer meaningful. No universal quota, mandatory paid intermediary, profession licence, automatic trust boost, friendship, permission, contract or sale is invented.

**Recipient custody matters.** An accepted item now belongs to the independent person as a real world-owned physical holding, separate from the provisioner's commercial shop stock. It is **not** automatically listed for sale, sold back for money or used to curry favour. A later independent sixday can consume a genuinely received ration for that person's ordinary needs. Other goods remain in their custody until another actually supported use arises; their profession alone cannot materialize a recipe. The world uses a distinct, persistent per-recipient RNG, preserving player-roll integrity and actual independent responses. All witnessed offers are provenance-hashed and included in the original private checkpoint; a replayed turn ID retrieves the identical result with no second transfer. The **player-facing** `my handoffs` / `social.materialHandoffsStatus` displays only witnessed outcomes, not private holdings, hidden feelings, later consumption or future offers.

## Safety and honest scope

This does **not** pretend a recipient owns unrelated merchant stock, consented to a contract, will repay the gift, has revealed intentions, or has offered service. Giving food to sapient beasts requires their individually sourced personhood and communication contexts; this current path uses genuine **human** person presence, while original wild-beast food/partner-care remain separate. Future source-verified human-to-human trade and sharing can use the same ownership and agency foundation, rather than duplicating a per-profession economy.

`tests/voluntary-person-material-handoffs.test.js` covers a real Orrin ration quotation and paid purchase followed by donation, a separate actual gathered/raw material offered to a different present professional, independently chosen refusal, no reroll fishing, actual recipient ownership, later world use, multiple ordinary natural phrases, named-absent safeguards, forged identity/source refusal, read-only witnessed memory, and original file-backed turn replay. The full existing test inventory remains required. Development never edits the active Aerin save.
