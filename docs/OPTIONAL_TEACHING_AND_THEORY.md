# Optional teaching, theory, comprehension and progression (standing design rule)

**Player clarification, 7 November 2026:** Treat **teachers, mentors, knowledgeable scholars, demonstrators, manuals, schools, facilities, tools, patrons and related support** as **contextual advantages that may make appropriate learning easier, faster, safer, deeper or more precise**. They are **not universal prerequisites** for discovering, practising, constructing, breaking through or improving power systems. Apply only the restrictions that actually follow from the specific discipline, organism, risk, source, environment, capabilities, rights and immediate circumstances.

This clarification supersedes any older wording that says a generic teacher, tuition fee, school, course duration, age, fixed practice count, institutional approval or rank comparison must be present before a character can try to learn something. A historical source line saying that a multidisciplinary practitioner requires teachers is not read as a mandatory teaching contract: it describes a possible source of effective learning when the contextual world actually provides one. Mastery is achieved through **actual understanding and independently earned results**, not a purchased unlock.

## Two independent axes: knowing and performing

**Practical capability** and **theoretical understanding** must be distinct for each real individual.

- A skilled high-stage practitioner may offer invaluable hands-on demonstrations, precise safety corrections, unusual methods and professional judgement. Their personal stage by itself does not establish patience, teaching ability, knowledge of another tradition or guaranteed comprehension in a student.
- A person with **no actual cultivation stage** may nonetheless be a great anatomist, historian, theory researcher, mathematical architect, translator, archivist or methodologist. They can pass on genuinely held and communicable theory, identify errors and explain principles without claiming to perform the technique themselves.
- A brilliant practitioner who cannot explain their skill may be a poor instructor; a scholar who has studied a broad field may teach foundational concepts more clearly than a narrow high-rank specialist. A guide can also be mistaken, biased or incomplete.
- Students differ in comprehension, aptitude, existing abilities, resources, curiosity, language, health, risk tolerance and personal choice. A strong learner might develop rapidly through direct experimentation, observation, books, collaboration, an expert teacher or a mixture. No route automatically duplicates a source's powers.
- A teacher's **knowledge breadth, depth, validity, ability to communicate, real access and willingness** determine what advice can actually help. Practical rank, when verified, matters where direct demonstrations or supervision are relevant; rank and knowledge are neither synonyms nor universal interchangeable numbers.
- A person who is being helped is still **doing the work**. Information and a demonstrated procedure do not grant a magical Circle, Gate, technique, professional rank, licence, inherited trait or evolved body without the actual compatible personal process.

### Context, not a fixed menu

Sourced help may legitimately alter the difficulty of understanding an individual task, speed of learning, quality of correction, risk of error, choice of experiment, access to materials or achievable technique. No single automatic flat mentor bonus, school fee or mentor-learner rank gap applies across all activities. Different types of instruction provide **different kinds of benefits** depending on the actual problem.

The relationship is reciprocal: the teacher or other person can refuse, ask for compensation, offer a free public explanation, request an exchange, share selectively, teach poorly, leave, change their mind or have competing obligations. Payment and formal permission matter only where an actual access arrangement, law, property, service or agreement applies—not as hard-coded universal world rules. Sapient beasts count as independent persons when their real knowledge, communication and rights permit teaching.

### Concrete implementation — narrow, not a global restriction

The existing `npc.converse` operation records particular **verbatim, personally witnessed professional explanations**. `rules/disciplines.js` now permits an optional `guidanceId` on the supported `discipline.practice`, `discipline.breakthrough` and `meridian.gatePractice` operations. It checks the existing conversation record, individual's preserved player-knowledge entry, witnessed source/event hash, relevant subject and named source; a player cannot invent a mentor, stage, intellect score or dice bonus. A fitting known explanation gives a **modest +1 to the learner's own check**, not instant skill, a new power or mandatory lesson. The check, cost, consequences and learner's progression remain entirely their own.

Two established **starting examples**, *not an exhaustive list of possible experts*, are:
- **Professor Sera**, the genuinely authored Meridian anatomist at Lumenfall Academy. Her recorded public discussion supports ordinary Meridian anatomical guidance **without assuming she cultivates or has any particular stage**.
- **Iskra Fen**, a real Rift surveyor with an established public explanation of spatial boundaries and anchors. Her discussion can inform bounded Rift observation without asserting she has personally attained a supernatural Rift rank.

Knowledge once actually heard can travel with the learner. The optional bonus does not depend on the expert staying on hand forever; the learner's persisted source records must still validate. The small first implementation is intentionally only two source-backed local examples; the world does **not** imply other people are incapable of teaching, only that the engine must not invent them as already known.

A future **genuinely present and willing higher-stage instructor** may give a stronger benefit *when their own ability, subject understanding, communication, consent, materials and the student's comprehension are independently established*. A non-practising scholar's independently proven theoretical expertise can likewise be substantial. These more varied assisted interactions **are not yet fully executable by the private runner**; do not silently claim that the basic +1 models every teacher or that advanced ranks can be copied. The existing more traditional academy route reflects particular currently authored course examples, not mandatory tuition or the only way to practise.

Source verification also matters for social consent: the stored conversation is evidence of a **public theoretical explanation**, **not consent to serve as a permanent tutor**, an offer of private instruction, an assignment of magical power or personal mindreading.

### General subject knowledge, not a fixed list of allowed teachers

The initial test examples remain Professor Sera and Iskra Fen, but the implementation in `rules/disciplines.js` is no longer a hard-coded name whitelist. A genuinely authored individual can have an established public professional subject and personally witnessed relevant explanation, or an author-established `publicExpertise` breadth list whose theory is also substantiated by their actual discussion. The existing `npc.converse` operation records that actual statement, and an optional `guidanceId` must match the preserved conversation, player-known fact and witness hash before applying anything to the learner's real check. This can represent a polymath whose broad theoretical knowledge spans disciplines even if their personal magic rank is completely unknown. It does **not** claim a person's cooperation as a permanent tutor, permit player-defined theory scores, copy powers, or infer their stage from a profession.

The same source-backed modest comprehension aid now works on early `discipline.practice`, appropriate `discipline.breakthrough`, `meridian.gatePractice` and `arcane.circleWork` (Second Circle redesign). Any helper with an actual role beyond a public explanation—for example an in-person advanced practical mentor giving a demonstration—still needs a genuinely established individual, willing action, compatible subject expertise and actual example before granting an appropriately different benefit. Their personal stage is **not** a substitute for knowledge or teaching ability, and the existing +1 public-theory aid is not a universal teaching scale.

`rules/npc-conversations.js` now looks up established canonical NPC identities at the actual time of speaking, rather than keeping a separate frozen name list. A new NPC is never invented or guaranteed to speak on the player's demand merely because the code can recognise an authored individual.

### Real short-session practice, not an imposed training calendar

`discipline.practice` now accepts an optional **`minutes`** value (15–480) representing a real chosen practice period. It spends actual Stamina according to effort, advances real world time and uses the native check for progress and setbacks. The player can work **independently by default** (no special route selector necessary), in an available safe environment, and can optionally benefit from previously heard relevant expert explanations through the existing source-checked `guidanceId`. A well-prepared student can make meaningful progress in a short session; talent and comprehension matter more than a universal number of training weeks. The natural limits are safety, resources, fatigue, method, source, risk, anatomy and independently rolled success—not attendance at a named institution. Rest, travel, relationships and world events continue on the same existing time stream. The old sixday mode remains when `minutes` is omitted for existing saves and protected historical calls; `source` without a route continues to select the old optional established-practice context.

A short visit, working conversation or freely available practice context has **no automatic tuition cost** merely for being described as established or assisted. The old explicitly booked sixday institutional course retains its historical fee. Unverified claims of helpful instruction do not grant the old course's improvement; a genuinely heard relevant explanation can provide its own modest contextual aid.

The short session is **not** a shortcut to an unearned stage or spell. Existing `discipline.breakthrough` still enforces its specific verified anatomy and actual check. It costs the already recorded effort and can fail; simply spending 15 minutes cannot claim a new Circle, Gate, soul phase or body transformation. See `tests/flexible-discipline-practice.test.js`, which includes no compulsory instructor, valid previously heard theoretical help, real fatigue and money, status persistence and duplicate-turn safety. This change handles the first early-discipline practice operation only; other legacy courses still need individual contextual audits, not one arbitrary global timetable replacement.

### Existing practice-rate calculator respects the distinction

The existing pure `rules/progress.trainingPP` calculator now accepts separate, **source-assessed** `teacherKnowledge` and `teachingClarity` values alongside optional `teacherRank` and the learner's own `comprehension`. Thus even a theoretical expert with **no practised rank** can provide effective instruction when their knowledge and explanations are genuinely established. An exceptional practitioner who communicates badly does not automatically produce a teaching advantage when poor clarity is known. This is a mathematical illustration for trusted world adapters, **not a player-controlled request to declare arbitrary knowledge, teaching quality, success or Practice Points**. Older calculations without these newly evidenced fields preserve their historical compatibility; their legacy generic rank estimate is not a claim that rank guarantees real-world teaching ability.

### Living-world honesty about who is actually teaching

The existing background `world/notables.js` profession updater previously inferred a personally higher-ranked instructor whenever an institution had high capacity. That was a false equivalence: a prosperous academy can have rooms, books and equipment without proving a particular advanced mentor is available or willing to teach. It no longer fabricates `teacherRank` from `institution.capacity`. Independent professional growth still occurs through actual work and available context; documented facilities may affect work opportunities, while teacher-specific benefits require a real identified source. `tests/contextual-theory-guidance.test.js` protects this distinction.

### Developer acceptance

For every new training or pedagogy mechanic, ask: **Does this person actually know something useful? Can the learner receive and understand it? Is the action possible without them? What is the particular benefit and cost, if any?** Never require a teacher merely because a stage is higher, or require the teacher to have personally performed what they can accurately explain. Never award effects beyond the student’s own anatomy, work and honest game resolution.

**Tests:** `tests/contextual-theory-guidance.test.js` checks an anatomical scholar with no known stage, an independent learner who needs no teacher, legitimate theory advantage, false/unheard/mismatched source refusals, non-practitioner Rift advice, no automatic rank copy, checkpoint reload and duplicate-turn safety. Earlier power and chat tests must continue to pass. `saves/aerin_marsh.json` is unchanged by development.
