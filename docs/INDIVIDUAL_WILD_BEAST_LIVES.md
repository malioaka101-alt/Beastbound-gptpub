# Individual wild-beast lives — first observed and off-screen ecology slice

This is a **limited, actually playable** application of the Unified Master Prompt §§5, 7, 11–16 and 43. Beastbound's recorded creatures are individuals: they can pursue their own needs, occupy real habitats, keep their distance, leave, forage and rest. They are not automatic companions, guaranteed power resources, a static species trophy list or tame animals waiting for the player.

## What exists

The engine already generates a distinct animal with a persistent ID, species, non-player needs and independent mood when a real local exploration encounters wildlife. The new `world/wild-lives.js` adds an **identity- and source-backed individual-life ledger** for that animal after a genuinely witnessed noninvasive watch. The original encounter and its independent distance are validated; artificial parameter objects cannot choose an animal, mood, location, favourable decision, reward, trust or Grade.

The player may choose **`world.watchWildBeast({})`**, or natural-language **“watch beast”**, **“watch animal”** or **“watch quietly”** in the existing private chat runner. A calm, genuine *current* wild encounter is required, without combat, coercion, an injured/incapacitated observer or a fabricated specimen. The action consumes 12 in-world minutes. The animal independently and privately selects an observable behaviour—looking around, foraging, resting, shifting farther away or actually withdrawing—according to saved local ecological stress, available forage and water, its own needs and individual temperament. Some behaviours materially change its needs or end the encounter. Nothing grants a bond, ownership, a technique, species identity, secret evolutionary knowledge, experience, a magical contract or automatic peace; threatening/sapient creatures need different appropriate procedures. A later distinct watch needs 60 actual minutes since the earlier attempt, to prevent time-free repeated outcome shopping.

`world.wildBehaviourStatus({})` is strictly **read-only** and shows only a currently present known wild animal, original witnessed encounter distance and previously **observed** activity at that scene. It does not reveal a remote wild animal's new location, private disposition, needs, hidden grade, mind or future.

Once a real individual was watched, subsequent **actual sixday world ticks** also advance it independently when the animal is no longer engaged in the player's current encounter and is not a partner/owned being. It may forage, rest, continue territorial life or move to an **existing connected site where that species can really occur**. Habitat deterioration, regional pressure, forage and water scarcity influence whether it stays or seeks elsewhere. The saved history is private and source-linked, with bounded needs and condition; it is not instant public news or player omniscience. This initially simulates **important, already encountered and witnessed animals**, not every individual in the entire world's wilderness, and does not yet support credible births, deaths, age changes, complex predator/prey cycles or an open-ended individual wildlife registry outside local playable routes.

## Constraints and future extensions

The original species spawns and known locations are **implemented coverage, not absolute population limits**. As new regions, species, observed individuals, migration corridors and sapient-beast legal systems become playable, they must have real provenance and ecological constraints, not arbitrary plot appearances. Independent intelligent beasts should be treated as people rather than processed through this ordinary animal watch. More nuanced life histories (long-term reproduction, local social groups, seasonality, ecology feedback, changing lineages, ailments, migration and life-course outcomes) remain open. The GM may depict plausible wider wildlife without falsely narrating an uncomputed mechanical change.

The feature respects Aerin's campaign and the separate Jory campaign: **no active saves are edited by these tests or development commits**. The original Master Prompt and established facts override this prototype. `tests/wild-lives.test.js` verifies genuine encounter witnesses, player inability to select NPC/beast outcomes, privacy, deterministic independent behaviour, source location bounds, voluntary withdrawal, consistent off-screen movement/needs, no free bonds or powers, and idempotent private file-backed turns. Full mandatory GitHub Actions CI must pass before merge.
