# Foundation #18 — voluntary limited coordinator responsibility after real organisational change

## Source-first world continuity

The original Beastbound Unified Master Prompt §§33–34 and 42 calls for changeable organisations with real leadership tensions, people with their own opinions and histories, and voluntary choices. This **is not** a separate faction simulator, a fixed menu of guild types, a compulsory political duty or a player-designed outcome. It extends the existing `world/emergent-groups.js` group's original `coordinationMandates`, `coordinationReviews`, source-authenticated `purposeHistory`/`purposeResponses`, and the **same** independent `world/group-member-agency.js` RNG stream and original world-month tick.

After a member has actually consented to one bounded operational coordinator mandate, the founding character may later publicly change their declared purpose. A genuinely consenting participant can independently learn/consider the original change (in an authentic in-person discussion or the original delayed local announcement), and form an individually sourced view. A leader who **still personally dissents** from the actual current revision after at least six days can privately reconsider their optional coordination role at the next genuine monthly world review; the original member-agency draw independently decides **relinquish or continue**. This is a person's choice, not a founder's command or an automatic faction crisis. An unaware member cannot be presumed to disagree. A personally changed subsequent view cancels the earlier disagreement as grounds for role review.

The witnessed original member consent, role mandate and scope, original public purpose revision, independent member response source, original world day, original live duty term and separate independent RNG before/draw/after are retained in the **existing `coordinationReviews` array**, with a distinct source origin. Existing original failed-work reviews remain intact, and no old historical records are rewritten. A particular response is reviewed at most once; never ask for both a failed-work and purpose review on the same day. On a genuine step-down, the coordinator returns to ordinary **still-consenting membership**. A replacement is not fabricated or appointed, a real prior accepted task is not rewritten, and the original group's funds, work, player resources, law, salaries, partner rights and government offices do not change. They may later be invited back only through independent actual consent and the existing role process.
