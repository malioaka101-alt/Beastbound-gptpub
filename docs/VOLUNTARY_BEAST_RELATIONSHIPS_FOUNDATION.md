# Voluntary beast–human relationships and terms — foundation #08

**Canon:** Unified Master Prompt §§13–15 and established Table Rules on independent animal welfare, Protected Minds, individual personhood and non-ownership. This *extends the actual living beast object and its existing bond/encounter/person source*. It is neither a second magical contract engine nor a universal NPC friendship score.

## One actual person, many possible kinds of contact

`bond.socialDraft` creates a source-sealed **proposal** attached to `s.beasts[beastId].socialArrangements`: a free-form original label, lived purpose, the human's duties, the beast's proposed duties, protections, communication basis, duration and a real termination path. A rescue-care proposal, friendship, temporary collaboration, habitat work, apprenticeship, paid service, equipment lease, negotiated safe passage or another credible arrangement can be described without a finite type list, level gate, purchased teaching session or invented affection. Recording any proposal **grants no acceptance, magical link, ownership, bond stability, human social rank, automatic technique, money, protected legal title or fabricated service**. Particular promised payments/medical treatment require original separate material and care rules.

`bond.socialNegotiate` checks an original in-person witnessed terms discussion with a source-matched exact proposal, real location, adequate ability to understand its chosen communication medium, the source-verified individual and a real freely engaged prior encounter. For **sapient S3+ persons**, the engine resolves that exact individual's own private independent decision to accept, counteroffer or decline, with real personal RNG that cannot be supplied by the player. A counteroffer does not count as a signed agreement; amended duties require another discussed proposal. Actual source-hashed receipts and current life status preserve the distinction. No universal educator, fee or waiting period is required.

For **non-sapient S0–S1 animals**, authentic previously accepted animal interaction and the original independent welfare/agency checks can support a personally witnessed friendly or caring contact, but *never a legal animal labour promise or informed sapient contract*. S2 Protected Minds require genuine independent guardian and preference evidence: unsupported guardian assent cannot be conjured from a simple social operation. Distress, coercion, injuries and physically absent individuals block implied willingness.

These social records are **separate** from `bond.seal` and the original magical-load, Soul capacity, Contract Lore, Grade, informed consent and guardian proofs. Existing `s.table.bondRecords` retains numeric personal trust, respect, communication, affection and full magical contract stability. Forming a social agreement changes none of those measurements automatically. A previous friendship cannot be converted into ownership or instant synchronisation.

## Continuing real obligations

`bond.socialEnd` permits the human to stop participating. If present, their notice is delivered in person; otherwise the saved state distinguishes an unverified remote notice. Ending participation cannot erase money, property damage or care still owed; legal consequences need independent witnesses and actual local law. `bond.socialDispute` records a witnessed specific dispute rather than awarding damages or declaring either party guilty. The same original scene cannot be reused for multiple legal outcomes.

The normal existing sixday world tick can expire a **genuinely time-limited** agreement or note that real serious injury/hunger makes promised performance unsafe. It never compels an independent animal's labour, grants gifts on a calendar, consumes non-existent salary, produces new offspring, manufactures party loyalty or silently resolves an argument. Unwitnessed individual welfare remains private in the read-only `bond.socialStatus` view.

Source-sealed origin details and append-only signed decisions survive export, import, duplicate-turn protection, expiration, refusal, revocation and disputes. A few tests demonstrate independent sapient negotiation and safe animal contact; they are not a fixed list of relationships or professions. This is a **foundation**, not a complete implementation of regional contract law, property remedies, emergency medical care, paid apprenticeships or legal arbitration. Actual consequences must use already sourced inventory, law, training, medicine and person agency. No active campaign save is edited.

Full exact-head regression is mandatory before checking off #08.
