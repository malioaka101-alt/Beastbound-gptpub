# Beastbound design charter — a world that grows, changes, declines and is replaced

## Reusable individual-achievement foundation and sapient beast rights (7 November 2026)

The world should not be pre-mapped or filled with static authored ranks, towns, or family memberships. `world/earned-individual-practice.js` supplies a **domain-neutral private ledger of actually resolved accomplishments**, with individuals' verified origins, aptitude, methods and replayable evidence. Different biology, power paths and institutions need genuine specialised adapters; **an elapsed-year clock or a profession label never improves ability by itself**. A first `world/sapient-beast-persons.js` adapter attaches only after **real local encounters of existing sapient beings**. They remain autonomous people with freedom to decline interaction, choose personal activities, and learn only from real successes. Player status remembers solely witnessed communication and doesn't leak offscreen competence or language. Independent practical observation/resource-care growth cannot manufacture a civil rank, lineage, invented settlement, spell, beast partnership or evolutionary stage. This is a foundation for later meaningful sapient societies as persons form relationships during play, **not** the creation of the whole world today. See `docs/FOUNDATION_INDIVIDUAL_ACHIEVEMENTS_AND_SAPIENT_PERSONS.md`.


## Wild individuals develop genuine ecological competence (7 November 2026)

As play proceeds, **genuinely sourced and independently living non-sapient beasts** can learn limited ecological skills from actual successes at finding food and moving among existing species-compatible habitats. Their own ecological challenges, aptitude and biologically supported Origin Pattern influence learning; sixday ticks only provide opportunities for real action, **not age-based experience**. Source-backed practice receipts are private, checkpoint persistent and distinguish meaningful actions from rest and calendar progression. Practical foraging mastery can marginally improve later nourishment without creating resources. Biology does not automatically become a combat stage, species/individual Grade, contract, sapience or unlocked evolution. S3+ beast persons **must** have independent, voluntary, culturally coherent learning/professions when implemented; no animal-routine command can be imposed on them. See `docs/WILD_EARNED_ECOLOGICAL_SKILLS.md`.


## Independent personal careers through achieved work, not elapsed time (7 November 2026)

Beyond existing authored notable seeds, newly generated ordinary persons now individually **earn professional experience** through actual independently resolved tasks appropriate to their present region, economy and occupation. A personal work receipt records the challenge, individual roll, result and grounded effects; confirmed success and aptitude drive competence, with difficult achievements worth more. Failures may create fatigue or lost income. Relevant successful work modestly improves actual environment/civic state, not merely a narrative story blurb. Existing standing no-calendar rule applies equally to NPCs: no age, sixdays spent, mandatory counted attempts or days-trained ceiling; a prodigy's real exceptional task may accelerate them. Skill does not silently become a professional licence, hereditary family art, human-beast bond or advanced magical school. People can freely decline an in-person work observation, and public memory records only witnessed outcomes—not the entire hidden career. This is a reusable individual-world foundation, not a census, prewritten profession list or finished NPC invention system. See `docs/INDEPENDENT_EARNED_CAREERS.md`.


## Correction, 7 November 2026 — mastery is performance, NEVER elapsed time

**User clarification overriding any older pacing wording below:** “Lifelong” describes how demanding mastery usually is, not a calendar or age prerequisite. No minimum days, years, number of sessions, rest period, age, fixed teacher or training schedule may be used to *unlock* the degrees or bridges. Progress follows actually resolved difficulty/quality, meaningful applied work, successful method diversity, earned skill, individual aptitude/potential and credible discoveries. Chosen actions do have realistic time/effort costs and the independent world continues ticking, but **idle time alone contributes zero advancement and an exceptional breakthrough can be earned promptly**. A serious failure consumes capacity, not a mandatory sixday wait. This statement takes precedence over earlier time-target descriptions and preserves all original Master Prompt power and consent conditions.

**Calendar-independent professional ranks:** The old four-PP-per-sixday cap has been removed from the engine. Reaching a professional rank depends on genuinely earned work and any independently required mastery/trial evidence, not calendar waiting. The existing sixday reporting counters are metadata and should never be used as advancement limits. There must still be real outcomes and consequences; simply requesting an easy roll repeatedly is not a shortcut to genuine competence.


## Deep, individually earned progression over actual lives (7 November 2026)

The player expressly wants **more middle steps and far deeper rankings**: a typical individual's path to the peak should be lifelong, while genuine extraordinary aptitude, understanding and opportunity can shorten the journey to **several good decades**. Add 3 actual interstitial mastery bridges across each system's original canonical stages, and 9 earnable degrees *inside* each major stage. Keep the Master Prompt's original named major milestones, and do not convert professions, biological evolution, beast grade, human cultivation, learned techniques, licences or sync into a single level. The framework requires **actual independently resolved successful work and discoveries**, varied proven methods, relevant aptitude and potential, source-sealed evidence and a genuine bridge trial. Time is recorded only as a consequence of the work, **not a minimum advancement condition**. A meaningful short session counts; idle years do not. It must not create birthday, days-trained, mandatory-sixday, number-of-sessions or fixed-recovery-wait barriers, compulsory paid mentors, automatic unlocking, or player-exclusive shortcuts. Severe strain changes immediate bodily capacity rather than installing a calendar cooldown. NPCs and sapient beasts should eventually live and progress through compatible evidence-based lifepaths outside player observation. The current runtime applies to source-established personal paths and refuses unimplemented advanced magic rather than inventing it. Repairable, wear-limited civilian prototypes also support practical long-term mastery rather than disposable one-scene inventions. See `docs/LIFELONG_MASTERY_AND_MIDDLE_STAGES.md`.


## Depth inside rankings and the first genuine experimental invention (7 November 2026)

Beastbound now distinguishes **source-demonstrated professional experience within a rank** and **potential stability/mastery within each distinct power system's canonical stage**, instead of inventing higher universal numerical ceilings. Each may be novice, stabilised or refined within its actual underlying craft/physiology; only concrete verified work may substantiate quality. `rankings.detail` is a strictly read-only view, never a self-awarded qualification. New original mundane field tools can now go beyond an unproven proposal: a source-bound low-Magnitude **surface-water gauge** may be physically built, fail at real material/time/Stamina cost, receive a real calibration attempt and then read only local approximate surface-water conditions with wear and uncertainty. Each functional scope must have a tested adapter; no prose-selected magical effects, remote secrets or free techniques. This is the tested **foundation for further emergent invention**, not a hand-coded world catalogue. See `docs/RANKING_DEPTH_AND_INVENTION_PROTOTYPES.md`.


## Foundation for generated tools and techniques with credible limits (7 November 2026)

Novel items/techniques should **emerge through existing individuals' real needs, knowledge and experimentation**, rather than a global recipe encyclopedia or automatic player-claimed power. `rules/creation-design.js` now accepts original freeform practical purposes and separately evaluates strictly bounded mechanisms (effect/magnitude/range/area/duration), earned skills, actual discipline stage/evidence, technical research difficulty and resource/grade obligations. A 35-minute draft records **only a source-backed unproven concept**, not an equipped weapon, certified spell, free ability, magic contract or produced item. In particular, simple everyday work need not carry mandatory guild/teacher requirements, while powerful magical devices need source-backed knowledge, materials, costs, risks and testing. Freeform inventions must support agriculture, medicine, shelter, care, transport, communication, culture, trade and social/animal rights—not just combat. This is the **first validator/dossier step**; real novel prototype fabrication, technique use, self-directed experimentation and independent NPC/organisation creations remain priorities. See `docs/BOUNDED_CREATION_FOUNDATION.md`.


## New independently living people emerge from real opportunities (7 November 2026)

The earlier 48-name professional and 28-household charters are **only world seeds**. The runtime now actually creates source-backed *new* independent individual agents when persistent ecology, regional livelihoods, civic stress or trade opportunities warrant them, even far away from the player. Their identities, duties, personal resources, work, fatigue, movements, and significant memories evolve on real world ticks, and survive checkpoint reload. The chance of a fresh local worker responds to existing staff rather than ever declaring the region at a hard population ceiling. Only genuine current presence yields local NPC status and witnessable dialogue; social invitation remains voluntary and source-proven. No unseen migration or private motivation is automatically player-known. People do not inherit noble status, secret powers or family techniques from a generated byname. This is a reusable emergence mechanism, not an encyclopedic roster or completed human generation system. See `docs/CAUSALLY_EMERGENT_PEOPLE.md`.


## Governing architectural priority: simulation foundations, not world mapping

**User clarification, 7 November 2026:** Our job is to build **mechanisms that create, maintain and grow Beastbound's world as play proceeds**—not to finish its geography, history, population, professions, beast taxonomy or all clans in advance. A few authored instances are useful to ground canon and exercise tests, but never define the totality of a living world. Prefer generative, chronologically advanced, save-persistent individual and social systems over additional hand-authored catalogues. Personhood, consent, ecology, inheritance, power-system distinctions and established world facts shape credible emergent outcomes; they are not pretexts for hard-coded paths. The active campaign checkpoint remains untouched during development. **See `docs/FOUNDATION_FIRST_WORLD_ARCHITECTURE.md` for mandatory implementation and testing criteria.**


## A first actual generation now follows individual animal lives (7 November 2026)

Previously only particular watched animals could interact socially. Two independently living real mammalian individuals can now conceive under actual species-specific and environmental conditions during world time, with non-guaranteed gestation and separate naturally-born young recorded through genuine parent IDs and source hashes. Children live and mature without player involvement, and may form their own ecological contacts, potentially enabling genuine multi-generation ancestry. A true born youngster may later be encountered normally and is not assumed named, partnered or known. Unknown original adults never acquire fabricated birth dates. The new shared `world/origin-heredity.js` distinguishes **inherited physique, innate energy potential, system aptitudes and dormant/expressed bloodline** from **skills/techniques learned from family, clan, organisation or teachers**. Stronger parents can transmit real constitutional advantage; children are individuals with variation and no unearned rank. The model also supports humans provided actual biological births are sourced; no human births are yet simulated and no charter's magical reputation becomes verified ancestry by declaration. This is a limited biological slice, not complete ecology, inheritance law, all species or human dynasties. See `docs/REAL_BIRTHS_AND_ORIGIN_INHERITANCE.md`.


## Wildlife can form its own relationships without the player (7 November 2026)

Previously witnessed independent animals may now meet *each other* during real sixday time, in their actual compatible habitat and after independently determined individual movement. Their encounters can involve distance, resource competition, foraging alongside each other and nonexclusive coexistence. Private pair records preserve actual original animal identity and dated histories; neither a tamer, paid master nor the player is needed. The simulation does **not** invent birth dates, biological maturity requirements, guaranteed mating, pregnancy, offspring or species-wide population limits. Social ecology does not convert into Evolution Stage, Level, Grade, Skill or a contract. Cross-species webs, deep reproduction and sapient beast social institutions remain substantive future tasks. See `docs/INDEPENDENT_WILD_NEIGHBOURS.md`.



## Remembered animals can be sought again, but remain free (7 November 2026)

The initial `world/wild-lives.js` individual ecology now supports an **optional, source-backed return search**. The human remembers real prior witnessed meetings, not a magic current-location tracker; re-encounters require current local presence, Survival ability, time/stamina and the animal's separately generated independent response. An unsuccessful search does not reveal where the beast moved or influence its off-screen behaviour RNG. This prevents wildlife from being disposable encounter templates or a guaranteed recruiting queue. The natural `track beast <id>` route does not impose compulsory teachers, licences or artificial daily waiting. The same individual keeps its history, needs, agency and full rights, including refusal. See `docs/REMEMBERED_WILD_INDIVIDUALS.md`.



## Particular wild individuals now have bounded lives beyond the initial encounter (7 November 2026)

`world/wild-lives.js` adds a first causal individual wildlife loop: source-witnessed calm local animals can visibly choose their own foraging, resting, boundary-setting, survey or withdrawal; later actual sixday time updates their needs and limited independent roam along real species-compatible local routes. Habitat scarcity and stress influence choices. The player sees only real local behaviour, not hidden private movements or mental states. Observing is not befriending, taming, trapping, training, evolution, or a guaranteed outcome. This is an **important-individual vertical slice, not a world census**, and cannot yet simulate every beast society, full predator/prey cycle or open-ended population genetics. See `docs/INDIVIDUAL_WILD_BEAST_LIVES.md`.


## Human powers now have bounded uses in the real living world (7 November 2026)

The original disciplines remain distinct and **never become a single character rank**. Earned Soul Phase I and Rift Step I have first actual field applications, source-witnessed checks and real time/resource costs. Soul Perception can sense a genuinely present animal within a **real independently established metre distance**, offering at most +1 on one independently resolved noninvasive wildlife observation, not a forced relationship or hidden beast data. Rift Sense uses the existing independently changing boundary ecology at Riftglass Outpost and reports only broad personally interpreted exterior pressure, not hidden realm access or a map. These abilities are first-stage vertical slices only: further high-tier cultivation, soul ethics, real spatial navigation and richer human-beast relations remain priorities. The world's independent sixday/month choices continue regardless of character advancement. See `docs/EARNED_SOUL_RIFT_FIELD_SENSES.md`.


## Extended actual person attendance (7 November 2026)

The earlier *five physically authored NPC* limitation is now partly broadened: named independent world professionals may be on real, saved, independently scheduled local duty appointments. `social.localPeopleStatus({})` is read-only and shows only personally available, physically established people here and now; `social.inviteGroupMember({groupId,npcId,proposal})` may offer membership to one who is truly present, without forcing their answer. A public professional list, a distant famous name or a founder's invented story cannot count as presence or consent. Most polities still lack playable local encounters: this is **not** complete character generation, free NPC travel, family adoption or full social agency. See [Witnessed local professionals](WITNESSED_LOCAL_PROFESSIONALS.md). No campaign save is modified just because this capability exists.

## Individuals can enter, support, leave and return to future-formed groups (2026-11-07)

The first personal affiliation system now bridges the open-ended social registry and **real, locally present** NPC conversations. Any truly player-founded group—regardless of self-described type—can invite an existing witnessed NPC in open-ended words; the other person independently accepts, refuses or remains undecided without a mandated teacher, family rank, joining fee or caller-selected answer. Real assent can produce subsequent source-checked volunteer efforts that modestly improve group conditions, genuine failed work, and independent withdrawal when sustained material hardship occurs. Earlier relationships remain historical; a later real new invitation can support rejoining. Only honestly witnessed offers and released local departures are player-known. The initial five located NPCs are **source-limited interface coverage, not a closed world population**; future witnessed individuals can join via the same registered membership evidence contract. This improves growth *and regression* without automatic family bloodline grants, noblesse, sect restrictions or calendar-based inevitable loss. See `docs/OPEN_GROUP_MEMBER_AGENCY.md`.


## Original entity lists are SEEDS; the world can invent credible new organisations (2026-11-07)

A fundamental open-world design rule: **the 28 original named households, 48 named professionals and 24 states are starting data, not the world's full population or an implicit maximum**. `world/emergent-groups.js` now stores actual later-born collectives dynamically and `Houses.catalog/lookup` lets social discovery cover established and newly founded groups. A player may self-declare an informal family, clan, guild, research circle or another open-ended association by their **own chosen kind and purpose**, with no automatic rank, membership, spouse, inherited trait or government authority. Real voluntary NPC worker spin-offs from verified independent research join the same registry. World months carry group operation, actual contributed resources and bounded success/failure, while public knowledge respects notice release and founding witnesses. An individual founder does not require the engine to invent a second character just because legacy family work uses a two-person model. This is **an extensible social proof-of-concept**, not a completed generic person/institution/settlement/power ontology. Future systems must allow source-backed new entities and diverse kinds of structures without turning the current authored examples into rigid access gates. See `docs/OPEN_WORLD_ENTITY_FOUNDATIONS.md`.


## Recognised working leaders can gain *and lose* real responsibility (2026-11-07)

The initial person-level family coordinator is no longer merely a status label: a genuinely accomplished, separately consented family member can direct a *limited common public-duty project reserve* while retaining their individual choices. Verified service, failure and real pressure on scarce funds lead to independent, properly spaced accountability reviews. The original elder or the acting coordinator may withdraw consent to a limited voluntary appointment; history, individual merit and legal elder status are not erased, and new genuine achievements can justify renewed cooperation. This is **social progression and regression from actual causes**, not compulsory family loyalty, deterministic hereditary succession or a legal title change. The previous periodic house-report bug also now preserves and rechecks the *prior* report date and original source-hashed family-service witness: only genuine member work supports a claim of material progress. See `docs/ACCOUNTABLE_HOUSEHOLD_STEWARDSHIP.md`.


## Real independent members inside already established families (2026-11-06)

`world/family-members.js` begins the transition from 28 static clan entries into **separately motivated people**: each actual prior named elder and acknowledged relative/chosen kin independently responds to real public obligations and finances, can cooperate on funded practical work or disagree and undertake their own projects, and can fail or earn individual credibility. Limited **public-duty work coordination** can pass to a proven younger or chosen member only with **separate real consent**, not inherited right, manufactured death, or a fixed revolution timer. The legally established elder remains unchanged. Old automatically proclaimed family material progress is no longer accepted without a corresponding actual verified member deed. `world.familyMembersStatus({})` shows only honestly published and source-hashed local outcomes, not private feelings or financial secrets. Work and public reputation can rise or regress under actual circumstances; romantic relationships, new births, marriages and legal succession remain separate unfinished systems. See `docs/LIVING_HOUSEHOLD_PEOPLE.md`.


## First evidence-based professional relationships (2026-11-06)

`world/professional-peers.js` adds actual repeated individual choices between the two previously established professional NPCs in each of the 24 polities. A real source-hashed, publicly released achievement can inspire one independent colleague to propose collaboration, try a competing practical method, or do nothing. Genuine collaboration requires **separate voluntary assent**, real work time, operating institutions, adequate funding and a source-recorded skill check. A tested alternative can gain legitimate local capability without retconning the first discovery or manufacturing a feud. Failures, declined invitations and wasted investments remain historical; later evidence can reopen different possibilities. Only publicly released outcomes are displayed, not private relationships. This is a limited *professional* social-development loop, not complete family, personal, romantic, political or supernatural autonomy. See `docs/PROFESSIONAL_PEER_RELATIONSHIPS.md`.


## Independent existing NPC priorities can now change as the world changes (2026-11-06)

`world/adaptive-ambitions.js` provides a first persistent goal-selection and real-deed cycle for **all 48 already named professional NPCs**, not merely a fixed set of three successful researchers. Their practical priorities respond to changing local ecology, passage hazards, civic need and teaching capacity. Actual personal career goals, institutional resources, preparation and independent checks determine whether they invest, succeed, fail, change focus or abandon work. Effects are modest, field-specific and verifiable; earned outcomes can improve real world metrics without manufacturing new spells, sovereign titles or legendary beast traits. Date-stamped source-hashed deeds become locally public only after ordinary release delays. This is a genuine implemented piece of autonomous development **and regression**, but does not yet simulate all individual motives, generations, relationships or new higher power disciplines. See `docs/ADAPTIVE_NPC_AMBITIONS.md`.


## First implemented cross-border evidence pathway (2026-11-06)

Alongside locally evolving NPC projects and vocational challengers, `world/knowledge-diffusion.js` now models **three geographically grounded, mundane method transfers**: a prior actually successful published trial may travel by an actual connected trade route to another real professional and institution, requiring repeated funded local reproduction and independent trial outcomes. Failure has costs, trade disruption halts transfer, and a sustained loss of actual local support can reverse earlier local adoption; foreign trade interruption alone does not erase a method the local community can still sustain until honestly re-established. Public news remains source-witnessed, dated and locally released. This is a **partial implemented feedback loop**, not already-generalised automatic global scientific diffusion, new power-system invention, worldwide institutional replacement or full intergenerational history. Broaden it only as actual ecology, professional expertise, real communications and verified individual ambition permit.


**Standing player direction, 6 November 2026.** The purpose of Beastbound's simulation is not to preserve a permanent status quo. **The world must feel genuinely alive:** people, beasts, families, sects, clans, countries, institutions, crafts and bodies of knowledge exist independently of the protagonist. They can learn, research, cultivate, discover, fail, recover, age, adapt, gain influence, lose it, disappear or be surpassed. New individuals, techniques, technologies, traditions, organisations and powers can emerge and eventually challenge or replace the old order, **when the world has developed credible reasons for them to do so**.

This document captures a **design requirement and prioritisation rule**, not a claim that every mechanic described here already exists. The original Unified Master Prompt and established campaign facts retain precedence; no Jory-specific campaign lore should be imported into Aerin's world.

## Governing principles

1. **No frozen winners or guaranteed dynasties.** Nobles, great clans, prestigious academies, long-reigning governments and supposedly dominant species do not receive permanent plot armour. A weak or unknown household, citizen, beast lineage or region may gradually become influential. Established powers may reform or decline, withstand challenges or be replaced. None of these outcomes should be forced simply to create dramatic change.
2. **Growth is earned and can be nonlinear.** Training, access to knowledge, practice, talent, resources, ecology, teachers, peer networks, experiments and encounters can each contribute to progression. Breakthroughs can be rare or surprising, but their origin, prerequisites, costs, dangers, failures, evidence and effects need actual grounding. A costly master is *one possible path*, not a universal law; independent research, partnerships, inherited methods, field discoveries and new disciplines can be legitimate when supported by the world.
3. **Regression and failure matter as much as growth.** Injury, lost expertise, habitat depletion, overextended treasuries, political errors, defeated rivals, social isolation, failed research, economic shocks, mortality and institutional rigidity may cause real declines. Rehabilitation, recovery, reform and rediscovery remain possible. Losses should have causal consequences, not arbitrary punitive timers or unearned resets.
4. **Independent people have ambitions and plans.** Named NPCs and sapient beasts should pursue their own goals, research, work, family interests, friendships, rivalries, careers, relationships and risks while the player is elsewhere. People can disagree within the same house or sect. Their plans can succeed, stall, be revised or fail, and their actions can alter other people's opportunities. Use actually established positions and resources; don't invent successful off-screen breakthroughs, promises, romance, consent or powers.
5. **Exceptional deeds leave traces and change names.** When an NPC, player, beast or institution genuinely makes an exceptional discovery, wins an important competition, protects a settlement, builds a useful invention, negotiates a landmark settlement or achieves a breakthrough, the world may remember it: witnesses, contemporaries, local reputation, apprentices, rivals, archives, laws, trade, histories, imitation and lasting consequences. Public renown should follow discoverable evidence and plausible communication; secret deeds remain secret until revealed.
6. **Knowledge, science, technology and institutions evolve.** Researchers can test competing theories, lose experiments, publish methods, be corrected, form schools, invent tools, discover new cultivation paths or shift accepted practice. Knowledge can spread imperfectly; unequal access, culture, trade, law, guilds, sects and ecology affect who adopts it. A new idea can disrupt an existing monopoly without granting automatic instant mastery or universal adoption.
7. **Power can rise from outside privilege.** Ordinary villagers, scholars, artisans, independent practitioners, adventurers and intelligent beasts can build skill, wealth, coalitions and standing through *real paths* rather than only noble birth, fees, patron approval, an academy or a scripted quest. Likewise an heir may fail, lose support or face lawful constraints. Social promotion, institutional membership, leadership and inheritance are separate outcomes requiring real support, consent and procedures.
8. **World reactions form feedback loops.** A successful inventor attracts apprentices and competitors; a weakened kingdom may provoke emigration, reform, alliances or opportunism; ecological overharvest changes species and livelihoods; a new cultivation method changes training markets and political interests. Agents respond to the world as it becomes different, not to a timeless static lookup table. Limit cascading effects by real time, information channels, capacity, geography and constraints.
9. **Timescales and uncertainty must be natural, not fixed destiny.** Some progress is daily, some seasonal, generational or centuries-long. Sudden events can occur if they have a credible catalyst; not every month needs a new great clan, a coup, a breakthrough or a species extinction. A very strong power should remain hard to displace without convincing counterforces, yet never be forever immune to change.
10. **The player is part of the world, not its centre or prisoner.** The protagonist may accelerate, redirect, resist or ignore developments. NPCs act independently, and the same options can open to others when circumstances justify them. Do not force the user to choose a family, sect, profession, research tree or political storyline. Respect choices to leave, refuse, withdraw or pursue unconventional plausible approaches.
11. **Continuity, provenance and partial knowledge.** Record who did what, when, where, by what supported mechanism, who witnessed it, and what changed. Differentiate rumour from demonstrated fact and public achievement from secret activity. Only character-known information belongs in narration and panels. Existing history cannot be overwritten to make a new result convenient. Actual engine outputs—not improvisation—govern rolls, power increases, losses, world ticks and other mechanical results.

## Development acceptance criteria

When expanding any social, research, cultivation, economy, ecology or career system, ask:

- **Who can change?** Not only the player: at least potentially the relevant independent NPCs, beasts, communities and competing organisations.
- **How do they change?** From actual inputs and meaningful decisions, with multiple plausible routes rather than one compulsory fee, teacher, family or preselected quest.
- **What might fail, degrade or end?** Include stakes and real resistance as well as recovery and transformation—not automatic upward progress or a predetermined fall.
- **What responds to success or failure?** Other people's plans, supply, demand, environment, alliances, prestige, opportunities and institutions may evolve where logically warranted.
- **How is it verified and remembered?** Time, source, actor, witness, simulation outcome and player-known vs hidden facts should have durable records; tests should cover both rise and regression, not merely the happy path.
- **Can the status quo eventually be surpassed?** If an old ruler, clan, practice, university, combat technique or technology dominates by script alone, identify the real paths by which someone else could challenge it, adapt it or render it obsolete. Do not promise the replacement will happen regardless of circumstances.

These are **directional tests, not hard numerical quotas**. Don't make every named background NPC a legendary cultivator or fill the world with weekly revolutions. The world should behave plausibly, preserve ecological and social constraints, and allow exceptional results to be genuinely exceptional.

## Current engine reality (as of 6 November 2026)

The private prototype already has **some** independent world sixday/month ticks, evolving state and diplomacy, background professional practice, regional household pressures, named public leaders and procedural civic decisions, plus source-verified player training, research and some real public interactions. That is **not full emergent civilisation**: NPC breakthroughs, credible invented techniques, multi-generation inheritance, true births/deaths, family succession and conflicts, economic innovation, research adoption/diffusion, institution creation/failure, historic reputation, realised political reforms, large-scale replacement of incumbents and dynamic social mobility all still require substantial implementation, validation and testing. Do not narrate those unfinished effects as if the engine already computed them.

The development priority should increasingly shift from *additional static catalogues or artificial restrictions* to **persistent, evolving agent goals, research and technique discovery, changing institutions, observable public achievements, genuine downward transitions and long-term causal feedback**. New worldbuilding should add possibilities and consequences, never create a permanent hierarchy the player or independent agents cannot meaningfully change.
