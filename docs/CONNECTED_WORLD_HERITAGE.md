# Beastbound — renowned world heritage and the work of power paths

**Scope:** a deliberately selective, coherent foundation, **not a complete setting encyclopaedia**. The original Master Prompt §§4, 19–25, 29–38, 45–46, 54–55 is the authority for what the world **must** support. Its descriptions of cultivation, statecraft, religious difference, autonomous beasts, and the many professions are canonical. The named continents, states, houses, sites, founding figures and syllabus titles below are **new world authoring already grounded in** `world/atlas.js`, `world/social-houses.js`, `world/mythic.js`, and `world/renown-and-practice.js`; they are not undisclosed historical facts from the Master Prompt and are **not player-campaign backstory**.

## What the older files already decide

Six connected continents contain 24 authored states, 28 named households/clans, dozens of operating institutions and biomes tied to roads, festivals and creature habitats. The world already has five named historical eras, living governments, public rights, interspecies polities, notables with their own training, independent wildlife, trade routes, temples, seasons, guild reports and profession-based work. This chapter extends that **same** world.

The **Age of First Paths** left routes initially traced by migrating beasts and spatial boundaries. Today's Open Peak independence traditions, Sahriel's First Cup custodians and the Old Tracks beast courts tell distinct and sometimes contradictory stories about that period. None can prove an exact ancient Meridian stage or a miraculous first teacher.

During the **Estuary Wars**, competing river settlements fought over levees and crossing rights. The **Seven Estuary Treaty** still matters because Reedmarch ferrymen, House Fenwake and the Lumenfall Annex disagree over whether river rights, safety measurements and hereditary archives belong to private holders or the commons. **Ila the Floodgate Witness** is historically renowned for the civic testimony, not a verified irresistible technique.

The **Ash Years** ended an old volcanic imperial order and left Kareth with independent Free Forges and the dangerous Ashglass March. **Sorin of the Cooling Hammers**, the Forge University, Emberhand Foundry Kin and later Sealwall wardens belong to this causal line: affordable workshop safety, labour rights, rift pressure measurement and rescue infrastructure, not an invented apex Fire Circle or portal spell. **The Unnamed Sealwall Keepers** are a collective legendary memorial; the living wardens retain ordinary physical exposure risks.

The **Longwinter Covenant** formed Threnor's ice-refuge and nonhuman sea rescue obligations. **Speaker Iri of the Open Refuge** and **the Unclosed Vigil of Starlens** are public legends with disagreeing source accounts. Their legacies survive in Aurora Academy observational ethics, Whitewake rescue crews, early warning signals, and public shelters, not coercive beast command or automatic Soul Domains.

In the **Current Century**, transcontinental trade and legal recognition of nonhuman persons press against private inheritance, military exemptions, habitat conservation and medical access. **The Shellsong Accord Speaker** is renowned for insisting that rescue is not ownership. Treaties, guild grants and disputed evidence—not undisclosed cosmic prophecies—drive today's conflicts.

### Only a few legends, never fake strongest-character statistics

The authored public memory now selectively highlights eight ancient or historical entries, including **the Waterkeeper of the First Cup** (Sahriel's public water access), **the Starlens Vigil** (northern perception and source discipline), and **the Shellsong Accord Speaker** (reef personhood), alongside the existing Ila/Sorin/Longwinter/Many-Tracks/Sealwall accounts. These are **famous, sometimes legendary people and collective traditions**. Their exact names, techniques, ancestral privileges, magical ranks, and survival are not mechanically verified merely because a chronicler speaks confidently.

The existing world also has **14 particularly renowned institutional traditions**, including Lumenfall Annex, Orchard College, Crownspine's Gate Monastery, Forge University, Aurora Academy, Rift Wardens, First Cup water sciences, Reef Consistory, Open Peak, Redwall War College, Copper Stair Route Keepers, Coral Healers College and Storm School. The underlying live simulation decides whether an institution is functioning or strained; a prestigious reputation is neither an immortal combat stat nor guaranteed political stability.

## Four separate layers of techniques

**Common, widely circulated methods:** recognized foundational descriptions that can be learned by anyone with a legitimate route and supporting physiology, such as Qi circulation, Arcane Circle calibration, Soul Perception, Body conditioning, Rift pressure readings, noninvasive Origin mapping, formation surveys or reciprocal beast signalling. Some already have engine-backed execution; others are **public professional theory**. A name alone never grants the method.

**School and guild methods:** a named organisation's historically developed practice, such as **Gate–Rope Rescue Weave**, **Emberhand Cooling-Return Schema**, **Starlens Echo-Source Separation**, **Open Peak Relief Breath**, **Sealwall Three-Marker Protocol**, **Marshglass No-Take Pattern Audit**, **Shellsong Reciprocal Current Signs**, **First Cup Aquifer Assay** or **Redwall Shelter-Node Plan**. These connect the public basics to real institutional purposes, particular houses and local history. They are not fixed universal classes or instantly usable magical powers. Actual instruction, practical observation, ethical consent, research or independent rediscovery can establish personal learning.

**Family, clan and protected methods:** Marshglass private research annotations, Briarhall patient lineage registers, Sealwarden refuge keys, Starlens private optical folios, Openpeak notebooks and the Many Tracks court's consent-sensitive signal forms are historically identified but **not publicly disclosed**. Being born into a famous line neither proves an inherited gift nor conveys lawful access to a private spell, bodily origin trait, patient record or communal nonhuman tradition. Some families make their teachings freely available, some negotiate access, and some keep legitimate safety-sensitive material restricted.

**Individually developed arts:** the existing `creation.draft` / `creation.personalDevelop` structure captures what a person actually designs, observes, trains and invents. **Personal techniques, transformations and domains can diverge from both the common syllabus and family traditions**. Their own source-backed anatomy, original practice, physical cost, design history and stage mechanics govern whether they work. An ancient school anecdote never creates an individual signature ability in a new character.

## World coupling: an example, not a scripted quest

A patient-care worker in the **existing Lumenfall clinic district** can examine real medical preparation quality using Medicine. Their report records actual world time, stamina, the original d20 check and the site. A relevant public institute may later decide to examine it. **Orchard College** receives this cross-polity report only after transport and normal institutional review, not via instantaneous world-wide telepathy. No medicine item, cure or job title materialises from writing the report.

A practitioner at **Morrowfen Crossing** can perform a separate *reciprocal communication* field practicum with a **genuinely willing existing beast companion**. The creature must have its own source-recorded relationship and current welfare. That produces an observation, not compulsory beast synchronisation, a new contract, or automatically recognised reef diplomacy. The remote **Reef Consistory** cannot review the overseas material until enough in-world time has actually passed. The local world and distant institutions continue their own decisions without automatically praising the player.

This contrasts with `world.heritage`, which is a **read-only public knowledge** bridge: the GM can draw from the original regional myths, named families, common methods, school histories and live institutional context when narrating a scene. Searching a catalogue does not cast a spell or reveal secrets. The `world.atlas`, `world.realmStatus`, `world.heritage`, `world.culturalPractice`, and existing time/chronicle loops now refer to **the same records**, not disconnected authored descriptions.

## A school method actually travels between livelihood and defence

**New tested integration: Open Peak Relief Breath.** This is a publicly identified tradition of the actual Open Peak School, of the existing Openpeak Scholar Households in the Skyvillage League; the original `world/atlas.js`, `world/social-houses.js` and `world/renown-and-practice.js` define its history, location, family and method. A person performing an Ashwind foothills *load-and-breath rescue drill* already uses authentic Body III Organ Cycle anatomy through `world.culturalPractice`, with original time, stamina and dice. After **one genuinely strong/exceptional original field report, or two independently successful original reports**, they can try a carefully interpreted **independent equivalent** of the public Open Peak syllabus at that same physical worksite. This does **not** make them an enrolled student, grant protected Openpeak notebooks or require that a distant master be present.

Successful demonstrations retain one authenticated original report within the existing professional archive, even after an ordinary long career rotates the limited report journal; this is durable evidence, not extra skill points or a new rank.

The subsequent trial is **still the original `world.culturalPractice` action**, not a second career system. It requires fifteen more real working minutes, one additional stamina and a single ordinary professional skill check. The original field evidence must exist and is permanently archived in that trial’s report. A supported method yields a bounded **+1 to that one professional work check**; a failed attempt still consumes the normal full work time and energy. The field observation still only creates a reviewable report, not an invented rescue victim, automatic medical cure, wage or licence. Other established school names with matching authentic civilian methods use the same public-syllabus-reconstruction logic; a school where no actual applicable work or original source exists remains theory.

**The same original physical learning now matters in combat.** If a Body III practitioner *successfully demonstrates* the actual Open Peak method, the already-existing wound-combat `Recover` action gains **one further stamina**. This reuses the original one-action recovery limit, the genuinely earned Organ Cycle and the original report verification. It cannot be awarded by merely viewing the public `world.heritage` entry, inventing a relative, editing a development counter or calling oneself an alumnus. It does not create extra turns, instantly heal wounds or replace personal techniques. A practitioner without current verified Organ Cycle anatomy cannot claim the live benefit even if they have an older report.

The report follows the original world publication and courier rules. The remote Open Peak institution can independently accept, ignore or decline it after its geographically plausible review delay; **that response is not necessary to practise an independently discovered equivalent**, and it is not a conferral of institutional reputation or patronage. The GM should naturally narrate a person practising breath timing under a real load, watching their recovery and later using it while winded in a fight—not offer a UI list of school perks. The historical syllabus supplies the cultural context; the particular character and their original experience supply the technique’s real provenance.

`tests/heritage-school-applied-practice.test.js` uses temporary characters with authenticated Body III organ work, genuine physical travel, real public field attempts, an independent syllabus adaptation with original checks and time costs, persisted archival evidence, combat Recover reuse, remote institutional review, anti-forgery and private duplicate-turn protection. No real campaign save is touched.

## What remains deliberately unfinished

This is **not the entire world history**; the histories of most households, states and figures still need development. Schools' protected techniques are not yet full executable combat/industrial procedures, and the existing public ranks do not certify living peak individuals. Next worthwhile work is gradual source-verified **institutional curricula and professional demonstrations** plus distinctive, independent living masters whose actual personal stages, career acts, diplomacy and limitations are earned in the same world simulation. Do not create an omnipotent roster, inherit power from a surname, make tutoring compulsory, or turn freeform text RPG play into an ability menu.
