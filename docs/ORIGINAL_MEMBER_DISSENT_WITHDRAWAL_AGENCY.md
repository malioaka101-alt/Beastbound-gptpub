# Foundation #18 — independent individual withdrawal after documented living dissent

**Authoritative scope:** original Beastbound Unified Master Prompt §33 (organisations' internal factions, changing fortunes, recruitment, goals and struggles) and Table Rules for independent NPCs, consent, time, delayed public information and historically accurate saves. This feature deliberately reuses the original `world/emergent-groups.js` open-ended priorities and individual stances, and existing `world/group-member-agency.js` independent world-month affiliation decision. It is **not** a finite faction list, an exile/expulsion system, an election, a fixed belief system or a new group work or political RNG.

A genuinely founded voluntary association can already have multiple **freeform actual issues** proposed by real independently accepted local members, with distinct actual personally witnessed and revisable support/dissent from other genuine members. These positions each contain the original member, original group and issue, elapsed day, evidence and replayable original independent-agency result. They do not bind the group, take funds, cause a completed project or compel third parties. Positions remain their own living person's views, not a collectively recorded majority or invented faction.

### A person may leave for sincere disagreement, not automatically

The existing autonomous member monthly review previously evaluated possible **personal departure** under genuinely persistent group inactivity, repeated original failed efforts or actual severe local instability. This extension adds one *possible* pressure: a member has independently and **currently** dissented from at least two **distinct original group priorities**, with each latest personal evidence at least six days old but no older than 180 days. Only original existing `internalPriorities` and `internalPositions` with intact actual source hashes qualify. If the member later changes one view, its latest individually verified stance supersedes that past dissent for deciding whether there is sustained current disagreement.

A qualifying difference of views is **not** an automatic schism or a rule banning disagreement. It merely allows the **same existing once-per-month personal affiliation review** to ask whether that member prefers to continue or independently withdraw. Most answers can remain membership, and an NPC can disagree continuously without being expelled or stripped of rights. No player can pass `accepted`, choose the departure, dictate political allegiances or compel someone into a faction. Actual local membership and environmental realities still matter.

If and only if the original real person independently opts to leave, the **same existing** `G.departMemberFromEvidence` path withdraws their single original active membership, preserves their former participation as history, emits its existing local notice with the normal **six-day release delay**, and does not transfer the group's one existing account, a relative's inheritance, a public office, their profession or power to anyone. This is a personal withdrawal, not formal disciplinary proceedings or proof of criminal wrongdoing.

### Additional source authenticity

Only the newly added dissent-based withdrawal reason stores an expanded independent witness: the exact **two or more current issue and personal stance source hashes** with their issue IDs, original last dissent days, actual world month, recorded member-agency RNG state before and after the existing single original withdrawal decision draw, exact modelled chance and contextual unfunded-months and local-stability snapshots. The existing personal departure adapter verifies those references against the same source-sealed original group positions and replays the **original** single random draw; it rejects forged votes, fake source hashes, invented consent and a chosen departure with no prior relevant disagreement.

Older original departures and group saves remain source-valid: they are not backfilled with imaginary dissent or changed probabilities. A member's voluntary withdrawal can happen for real motives beyond those covered by this *initial* simulation trigger; the GM must not treat this check as an exhaustive list of lawful reasons to depart, or an enforced institutional rule.
