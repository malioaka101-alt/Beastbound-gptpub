# Foundation #18 — actual personal consequences of changing group direction

**Canonical basis:** Unified Beastbound Master Prompt §§33–34 and 42; original Table Rules. The GM invents freeform changing organisations, independent people and specific reasons for conflict. The SAME world group/member state records what was really established and resolves consequences through the existing independent agency and original world ticks. There is no faction catalogue, extra organisation economy or second NPC decision engine.

## What is now connected

Merged [PR #76](https://github.com/malioaka101-alt/Beastbound-gptpub/pull/76) adds real separately source-proven later public declarations of a founder's purpose while retaining their immutable founding charter. Merged [PR #77](https://github.com/malioaka101-alt/Beastbound-gptpub/pull/77), full 193/193 exact-head [CI #43163828369](https://github.com/malioaka101-alt/Beastbound-gptpub/actions/runs/43163828369) and GitHub merge `5b679baa0efea898f862cb36ccfc95d4baed03e9`, gives existing genuinely consenting, actually present group members their own source-verified supportive, dissenting or uncertain personal views. Their answer is never supplied by the founder.

A member can now voluntarily **consider withdrawing** after sustained personally witnessed disagreement with the organisation's *changing purpose*. This extends, not duplicates, the earlier original `world/group-member-agency.js` mechanism that already handles personal departures after repeated dissent from concrete internal proposals. The original `world/emergent-groups.js` holds all declaration and individual response records.

A current pressure reason requires at least two **separate genuine original purpose revisions**, each with an actually dissenting last personal response, six days of lived world time and the source history of those revisions intact. The most recent individual response must still be dissent: a real later change of mind to support or uncertainty removes this *current* pressure. The system does not label every new disagreement an automatic faction, construct a rival organisation or force a permanent schism. Other existing genuine hardship reasons still apply on their original evidence.

At the original monthly world review, after an existing thirty-day membership and under the pre-existing interval limit, the **original independent member agency** can choose to leave or stay. No caller controls the choice. An actual departure reduces the same group and verified member count through the existing consent-revocation adapter. Original private source records include the exact two source hashes of disputed freeform directions, original individual decisions, prior authenticated membership, independent original RNG before/draw/after, real day and original recorded political/financial conditions. Members who stay remain independent. Departure becomes publicly reportable after the existing six-day delay. Nothing gives the founder a power of expulsion, other members' assets, state legal office, compulsory work, or an inherited rank.

A new prospective member's original invitation alignment now considers the organisation's **current declared purpose**, rather than only a potentially obsolete founding declaration. This does not make their acceptance automatic or confer rights. The original founding purpose remains historical fact.

## Examples are tests, not playable required choices

A freeform local river observation circle changes its declared activity twice. A genuinely consenting member independently disagrees both times, then after a real month either leaves or stays according to the original agency. A similar member with only one disagreement stays without a manufactured persistent conflict, while a third path demonstrates genuine later independent reconciliation. The same mechanics extend to any plausible future school, guild, sanctuary, temple, collective, laboratory or politically involved community the GM actually creates.
