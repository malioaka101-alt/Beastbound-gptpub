# Beastbound — Living household people, independent choices and earned public standing

## Original source versus new authored simulation

The Beastbound Unified Master Prompt demands living families, clans, councils, nonhuman kin, government, succession customs, personal autonomy, research, service and consequences. The established regional atlas and the earlier `world/social-houses.js` contain **28 actual named households across 24 countries**, each with a separately recorded elder, a known relative or chosen kin, and a **public duty and disputed obligation**. These names are new regional Beastbound authoring from earlier work, not text supplied by the original Master Prompt. The original source does not specify their bank accounts, inner attitudes, annual elections or compulsory household obedience. This patch makes that **boundary explicit** and does not replace family canon, legal inheritance, or individual consent.

`world/family-members.js` is the first implemented **person-versus-person household dynamics** layer. Every previously named elder and acknowledged relative/chosen kin is an independent living actor. These two people may cooperate to meet their documented household public obligations, disagree over the use of resources, pursue separate limited public-service efforts, or choose to do nothing. Neither is forced to share the other's position. The word *relative* is not rewritten as biological descent where the existing charter explicitly records **chosen kin**. Family surname, descent, special-family status, court connections, inherited biology and actual technical skill remain separate facts.

## Real constraints, choices, gains and setbacks

Each household has a newly authored **small public-duty working reserve** and its secondary member has a modest independently maintained **work allowance**. These are *new limited simulation bookkeeping*, not declarations of exact personal wealth, tax receipts, kingdom ownership, a player's purse, inheritance or clan treasure. They respond to the local economy: household contributions and upkeep vary with genuine host prosperity and stability; allowances grow only as local work permits. Every household's private deliberation is on a **separately staggered actual monthly calendar**, with new consideration no more frequently than every three months unless an existing project must continue. No world-wide scripted day forces 28 families to meet together.

An actual individual choice can be:
- The elder supports a limited documented contribution toward the household's already filed public duty, or protects constrained working reserves.
- The second household member independently supports the same work, seeks a separate public-record/service review (spending their *own* allowance), or prioritises other responsibilities.
- A shared practical project begins **only when both selected the same form of practical duty work**. The engine does not reinterpret disagreement as consent, love or an inherited oath.
- Where they disagree, each can undertake credible separately funded work or decline to work. The disagreement is counted but does **not automatically become a feud, marriage dispute or loss of family membership**.

A person or agreed pair must complete **two separately funded actual months**, spending two, three or four units per work month depending on which reserves and participants are involved. The result uses a real independent persistent world d20, current house influence, verified merit from previous local work, local stability and the project type. Successful effort can modestly reduce the existing house's public-service pressure and improve its influence by at most a point. That is a limited actual **practical public contribution**, not a decree, adjudication, legal restoration or an assertion that disputed allegations were true. The specific earlier work and before/after numbers are recorded. Failure costs real resources and accomplishes nothing; financial shortage blocks, erodes preparation and can ultimately end an unfinished project.

The **actual people** receive individual merit only from verified practical work. A member whose independent contributions substantially exceed those of the established elder can eventually be considered for a role as **voluntary coordinator of public-duty projects**. That requires a history of at least two successful documented projects, sufficient relative merit **and two separate current consents**, from both the elder and the candidate, rather than an automatic age/wealth/surname timer. Repeated unsuccessful ballots are separated by at least six real world months. This is **not the head of family**, not a throne, not succession, property transfer, electoral appointment, adoption, new marriage or birth. Existing charter elders and the public government register are never overwritten.

## Historical truth and what the player knows

Each actual action has its own `table.realm.familyMembers` persistent ledger entry naming real people, documented household duty, internal choices, money/time spent, independent d20, actual results and prior/after house state, all with a SHA-256 receipt. Internal decisions, unperformed work, private disagreements and budgets are **not freely revealed to the player**. A completed actual contribution (and certain genuine failed projects) can appear as a locally published **public notice after eight real days**. `world.familyMembersStatus({})` is strictly read-only and returns **only source-hashed, genuinely released notices in the player's current region**. Being physically present in that region does not mean a family representative is standing next to the player or is personally offering patronage.

This patch also corrects a prior social world simulation shortcut: a wealthy/influential household's old four-month routine status notice could claim **material progress** merely because its influence rose. That claim is now reserved for **actual earlier verified member-level practical work**. Otherwise the normal report is an appeal or a request for a public hearing, not a fictitious accomplishment. A truly reported completed project can contribute to the wider house's historical standing while later scarcity, failed work and changing agendas still matter. The world never automatically awards a legendary inherited constitution, a Merlin-tier teacher, a Meridian Gate or an Arcane Circle from a family's surname.

## Real limitations and future development

This is a **first family-person dynamics slice**, not comprehensive lifetimes, parent-child psychology, legal succession, marriage, children, marriages of convenience, marital consent, heirs, divorces, inheritance law, autonomous recruitments, treaty marriages or each relative's combat/power profile. A later political challenger must still demonstrate legal candidacy, real evidence and genuine political process, rather than automatically usurping an elder after a successful public contribution. The player's own choices remain open: joining, investigating, helping, opposing or ignoring any household are separate source-grounded paths and are **not** gated by an arbitrary mandatory noble tutor.

Regression tests `tests/family-members.test.js` and `tests/family-members-longrun.test.js` exercise all 28 original named pairs and nonhuman chosen-kin definitions, separate opinions, real reserves, independent competence rolls, honest setbacks, earned merit, work-coordination consent versus automatic inheritance, deterministic multi-year household development, dated source-hashed public news and strict file-backed read-only status. Existing campaign saves are **not edited**.
